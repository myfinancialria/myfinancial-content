# Market Wrap — 03 July 2026

**TL;DR**
- Nifty 50 ended the day with modest gains, while Bank Nifty saw a slight dip.
- Midcap and Smallcap indices presented a mixed picture, with Midcap 100 closing lower and Smallcap 100 marginally up.
- Realty, IT, and Pharma sectors were the top performers, showing strong buying interest.
- PSU Bank, Energy, and Media sectors faced selling pressure, ending in the red.
- Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) continued their selling streak, while Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) remained net buyers.

## How the market closed

The Indian equity markets witnessed a day of mixed sentiment, with the benchmark Nifty 50 closing in positive territory, while the Bank Nifty edged lower. The Nifty 50 managed to gain 0.39%, ending the session at 24270.85, largely supported by gains in select sectors like Realty and IT. In contrast, the Bank Nifty closed at 57938.50, down 0.16%, reflecting some profit booking in banking counters.

| Index | Close | Change | High | Low |
| :---------------- | :---------- | :------- | :--------- | :--------- |
| Nifty 50 | 24270.85 | +0.39% | 24378.15 | 24252.35 |
| Bank Nifty | 57938.50 | -0.16% | 58343.25 | 57799.05 |
| Midcap 100 | 62190.30 | -0.19% | 62556.55 | 62136.50 |
| Smallcap 100 | 19175.10 | +0.04% | 19277.35 | 19153.70 |

The India VIX, a measure of market volatility, eased by 3.99% to settle at 11.80, indicating a slight reduction in market apprehension.

## Sectors — winners and losers

Today's trading session saw a clear divergence in sectoral performance, with some sectors demonstrating robust strength while others succumbed to selling pressure.

**Top 3 Gainer Sectors:**
*   **REALTY:** +2.19% - The sector witnessed strong buying interest, buoyed by positive news such as Raymond Realty reporting a more than two-fold jump in Q1 pre-sales to ₹700 crore, indicating heightened demand for housing.
*   **IT:** +1.76% - Technology stocks gained momentum, potentially benefiting from global cues and a renewed focus on AI infrastructure, as seen with Nokia's AI comeback story.
*   **PHARMA:** +1.72% - The pharmaceutical sector also posted healthy gains, suggesting investor confidence in the defensive nature of the segment amidst broader market movements.

**Top 3 Loser Sectors:**
*   **PSU BANK:** -1.54% - Public Sector Banks faced significant selling pressure today, with some counters experiencing sharp declines.
*   **ENERGY:** -1.33% - The energy sector saw profit booking, despite some individual company updates like Vedanta Power's Q1 sales rise.
*   **MEDIA:** -0.45% - Media stocks also ended in the red, albeit with a relatively smaller decline compared to other losing sectors.

## Market breadth

The market breadth for the day was slightly negative, with 237 advances against 262 declines out of a total of 499 stocks. This indicates that while the Nifty 50 managed to close higher, the broader market saw more stocks losing ground than gaining, suggesting a cautious sentiment beneath the headline index performance.

## FII / DII flows

Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) continued to be net sellers in the Indian equity market today, offloading shares worth ₹312 crore. Conversely, Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) remained strong buyers, injecting ₹1,784 crore into the market.

| Investor Type | Net Position (₹ crore) |
| :------------ | :--------------------- |
| FII | -312 |
| DII | 1,784 |

Looking at the 5-day history, FIIs have been consistent net sellers for the past four trading sessions, with today marking their smallest net outflow in this period. Meanwhile, DIIs have consistently provided strong buying support, offsetting FII selling and maintaining liquidity in the market. This trend highlights the continued resilience of domestic money in supporting Indian equities.

## What moved markets today

Several factors influenced market sentiment and specific sector movements today:

*   **Positive Momentum in Realty and IT:**
    *   **Raymond Realty Q1 pre-sales jump over two-fold to Rs 700 cr on better housing demand.** Raymond Realty's impressive Q1 sales bookings, soaring to ₹700 crore, significantly boosted the Realty sector, which emerged as the top gainer.
    *   **Nokia: How a forgotten stock from Finland doubled investors wealth in AI comeback story.** Global cues, particularly the narrative around Nokia's resurgence as an AI infrastructure player, likely contributed to the positive sentiment in the Indian IT sector.
    *   **IDFC First Bank Q1 update: Loan book grows 21% YoY to Rs 3.05 lakh crore; shares rise 2%.** IDFC First Bank reported strong provisional Q1 FY27 business growth, with loans and advances rising over 20% year-on-year, providing some positive momentum within the broader financial services space.

*   **Weakness in Capital Goods and PSU Banks:**
    *   The significant declines seen in Capital Goods stocks like GE Vernova T&D India, Hitachi Energy India, and Siemens Energy India suggest sector-specific profit booking or concerns, despite the broader market's mixed performance.
    *   **Union Bank of India** was among the top losers, contributing to the overall decline in the PSU Bank sector. Analysts have also suggested preferring private banks and NBFCs over PSU banks, which might have influenced today's selling pressure.

*   **Global Market Optimism and IPO Buzz:**
    *   **Global Market Update: Europe's STOXX 600 hits record high, eyes best weekly gain since mid-May.** Positive global cues, particularly from European markets, helped underpin overall market sentiment, preventing a sharper correction.
    *   **Bitcoin climbs above $61,000 as weak US jobs data fuels Fed rate cut hopes.** The rally in cryptocurrencies, driven by hopes of US Fed rate cuts, also reflected a broader risk-on sentiment in global markets.
    *   **Kusumgar IPO price band fixed at Rs 398–419; Rs 650-crore issue opens July 8, GMP at 33%.** The ongoing activity in the primary market, with new IPOs and allotment dates, kept investor interest alive, although its direct impact on the broader indices was limited.

## Setup for tomorrow

For tomorrow's session, the market will likely continue to digest today's mixed cues. The resilience of the Nifty 50 despite negative breadth and FII selling suggests underlying domestic strength. Investors will be watching for further Q1 updates from companies and any fresh global developments. The capital goods sector's significant decline today warrants close observation to see if it's a one-off correction or a start of a broader trend.

Globally, the USD/INR closed at 95.20, while the Dollar Index was marginally down at 100.77. Crude oil prices (Brent at $71.57, WTI at $68.32) saw slight declines. Gold and Silver, however, posted strong gains, with Gold up 1.83% and Silver up 3.32%. Data for the US 10Y Yield is unavailable. These global commodity and currency movements will provide overnight pointers for the Indian market.
