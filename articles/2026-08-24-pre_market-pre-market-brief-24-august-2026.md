# Pre-Market Brief — 24 August 2026

**TL;DR**
*   Global markets presented a mixed picture, with US indices closing higher while most Asian markets traded lower.
*   GIFT Nifty is signalling a positive start for the Indian benchmark indices.
*   Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) continue to be strong net buyers, offsetting persistent selling by Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs).
*   Crude oil prices have slipped ahead of a US announcement on new sanctions against Iran, potentially easing inflation concerns.
*   Nifty's immediate rally is seen hinging on a decisive break above the 24,350–24,400 resistance zone.
*   Key risks for the day include continued FII selling and the evolving global geopolitical landscape impacting crude oil.

## How global markets traded — and what it means for India
Overnight, US markets closed on a positive note, with all three major indices registering gains. The Dow Jones led the pack, rising nearly 1%, while the Nasdaq and S&P 500 also saw moderate upticks. However, Asian markets this morning are largely trading in the red, with Hong Kong's Hang Seng index experiencing a significant decline of over 2% and Shanghai also down. Japan's Nikkei saw a marginal dip. This mixed global sentiment sets a cautious tone for the Indian markets, despite positive cues from GIFT Nifty.

## Currency, commodities and yields
The Indian Rupee (USD/INR) strengthened slightly against the US Dollar, trading at ₹95.64, a decline of 0.14%. The Dollar Index, which measures the dollar's strength against a basket of major currencies, saw a marginal increase of 0.03% to 98.83. Crude oil prices witnessed a notable decline, with Brent Crude falling 1.32% to $93.14 and WTI Crude dropping 1.57% to $85.69. Gold prices surged by 1.50% to $4693.60, hitting a three-month high, while Silver saw a modest dip of 0.86% to $68.87. The US 10-Year Treasury Yield rose by 0.89% to 4.74%.

**Interpretation:** A slightly stronger Rupee (USD/INR down) offers marginal relief for importers and could be a slight headwind for export-oriented sectors like IT and pharma. The decline in crude oil prices is a significant positive for India, potentially easing inflationary pressures and benefiting sectors like oil marketing companies (OMCs), paint, and aviation. The rally in gold suggests increased safe-haven demand amidst global uncertainties and ahead of crucial US inflation data and the Fed Chair's speech. The rise in US 10-year yields could make US bonds more attractive, potentially influencing FII flows away from emerging markets like India.

## GIFT Nifty — what it's signalling
The GIFT Nifty is trading at 24356.50, up by 0.20%. This indicates a positive start for the Indian Nifty 50 index, pointing towards a potential gap-up opening today.

## FII/DII flows — who's driving the tape
Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) remained net sellers in the Indian equity market yesterday, offloading shares worth ₹543 crore. In contrast, Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) continued their strong buying spree, pumping ₹2,124 crore into the market.

| Date         | FII Net (₹ crore) | DII Net (₹ crore) |
| :----------- | :---------------- | :---------------- |
| 2026-08-21   | -543              | +2,124            |

Over the last five sessions, FIIs have been net sellers in three out of five days, with a cumulative net outflow. DIIs, on the other hand, have been consistent net buyers, providing crucial support to the market.

**Interpretation:** The persistent selling by FIIs suggests a cautious stance from foreign investors, potentially due to global factors or profit-booking. However, the robust and consistent buying by DIIs is acting as a strong counter-balance, absorbing the FII outflows and providing a crucial floor to the market, indicating strong domestic investor confidence.

## Option-chain signals (OI) — where support & resistance sit
**Nifty:** The Put-Call Ratio (PCR) for Nifty is 0.95, indicating a neutral-to-slightly cautious sentiment. The highest Call Open Interest (OI), acting as resistance, is seen at 24300 (13,469,800 contracts), followed by 24500 (13,028,900 contracts). On the Put side, the highest OI, indicating strong support, is at 24000 (17,739,500 contracts), followed by 24200 (15,048,000 contracts).
**Bank Nifty:** The PCR for Bank Nifty is 0.73, suggesting a cautious or slightly bearish bias. The highest Call OI is at 58000 (2,181,450 contracts), followed by 59000 (1,246,500 contracts). Key Put OI support is at 57000 (1,326,660 contracts), followed by 57500 (1,005,210 contracts).
**Sensex:** The PCR for Sensex is 0.91, indicating a neutral-to-slightly cautious outlook. Resistance from Call OI is highest at 77600 (732,340 contracts) and 77700 (630,900 contracts). Support from Put OI is strong at 77500 (841,420 contracts) and 77000 (570,120 contracts).

**Interpretation:** For Nifty, the 24300 level is a crucial immediate resistance, aligning with the technical R1/R2 zone. A break above this could see the market target 24500. On the downside, 24000 and 24200 are strong support levels that buyers are defending. Bank Nifty looks more cautious with a lower PCR, indicating that 58000 will be a tough resistance to cross. Sensex also faces immediate resistance around 77600-77700, with 77500 acting as a key support. The PCRs across indices generally suggest a neutral to slightly cautious market sentiment, with more aggressive call writing (resistance) than put writing (support) in some cases. Traders should respect these OI-derived levels in conjunction with the technical pivot points and moving averages today.

## Stock & sector news that matters

*   **Oil Price Today (August 24): Crude oil slips below $93 ahead of US sanctions announcement on Iran.**
    Crude oil prices declined as investors awaited an announcement from the US Treasury Secretary regarding new sanctions on Iran. These sanctions are expected to be "the toughest in history" and could disrupt oil supply routes.
    **Why it matters:** A fall in crude oil prices is generally positive for the Indian economy, as India is a major oil importer. This could ease inflation concerns and improve the margins for oil marketing companies (OMCs), paint manufacturers, and aviation companies. However, the actual impact will depend on the severity of the sanctions and their effect on global supply.

*   **Lalithaa Jewellery Mart shares to debut today; GMP signals 37% premium ahead of listing.**
    The ₹1,700-crore IPO of Lalithaa Jewellery Mart, which closed on August 19, was subscribed an exceptional 62.97 times.
    **Why it matters:** A strong listing for Lalithaa Jewellery Mart could boost market sentiment for new issues and the broader jewellery sector. High subscription rates and a significant grey market premium (GMP) often indicate strong investor demand and potential for listing gains.

*   **Horizon Industrial Parks shares to list today. Check GMP ahead of debut.**
    The ₹2,600-crore IPO of Horizon Industrial Parks comprised an entirely fresh issue of 43.34 crore shares, with the price band fixed at ₹57–60 per share.
    **Why it matters:** This listing, alongside Lalithaa Jewellery, will test investor appetite for new offerings. A positive debut could signal healthy liquidity and investor confidence in the primary market, potentially impacting other upcoming IPOs.

*   **Nifty’s rally hinges on a break above the key 24,350–24,400 resistance zone.**
    Nifty is currently oscillating within a wide trading range, and analysts suggest a breakout is needed to gain significant momentum.
    **Why it matters:** This highlights a critical technical level for Nifty. Traders will be closely watching this range. A decisive move above 24,400 could trigger further upside, while a failure to cross it might lead to consolidation or a pullback.

*   **Gold hits over three-month high ahead of US inflation data, Fed chair speech.**
    Gold prices have surged, driven by investor anticipation for upcoming US inflation data and a speech by Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh, which are likely to influence monetary policy expectations.
    **Why it matters:** Rising gold prices indicate increased safe-haven demand amidst global economic uncertainty and expectations around interest rate movements. This could reflect investor caution and a shift towards non-yielding assets.

*   **JBM Auto among 5 stocks showing bullish RSI upswing.**
    On August 21, JBM Auto was identified among five Nifty500 stocks that gained over 4% and showed a bullish RSI upswing, with their RSI value crossing above 50.
    **Why it matters:** Stocks showing bullish technical indicators like an RSI upswing could see increased buying interest and momentum in today's session. Investors might watch JBM Auto and other similar stocks for potential trading opportunities.

Markets will closely watch the details and potential impact on global oil supply and prices. Such geopolitical events typically introduce volatility, especially in energy-related stocks and the broader market sentiment.

These events are crucial for global interest rate expectations. Markets typically react with increased volatility around such announcements, as they provide clues on future monetary policy, impacting currency movements, bond yields, and equity flows.
