# Market Wrap — 30 July 2026

**TL;DR**
- Nifty 50 closed marginally higher, extending its gains above 24,300.
- Broader markets, including Midcap and Smallcap indices, ended in the red, indicating a mixed sentiment.
- The Auto sector was the standout performer, driving much of the Nifty's positive momentum.
- Realty, FMCG, and Pharma sectors faced selling pressure, ending as top losers.
- Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) turned significant net buyers, injecting ₹2,982 crore into the market.
- Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) continued their buying streak with ₹998 crore.
- F&O positioning data for today is unavailable and will populate from the next session.

## How the market behaved today
The Indian benchmark indices witnessed a day of mixed performance, with the Nifty 50 managing to close in positive territory, extending its gains above the 24,300 mark. The index opened with a slight gap up and largely traded within a range, eventually closing at 24317.15, up by 0.28%. In contrast, the Bank Nifty ended marginally lower, while the broader market, represented by the Midcap 100 and Smallcap 100 indices, saw declines.

| Index | Close | Change | High | Low |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nifty 50 | 24317.15 | +0.28% | 24342.95 | 24187.10 |
| Bank Nifty | 57147.50 | -0.10% | 57236.65 | 56768.60 |
| Midcap 100 | 62641.75 | -0.35% | 62829.40 | 62412.45 |
| Smallcap 100 | 19254.45 | -0.56% | 19366.50 | 19232.90 |

India VIX, the volatility index, edged up by 1.25% to 12.16, suggesting a slight increase in expected market fluctuations.

## What moved the market — the factors
The market's performance today was primarily driven by a combination of global cues, strong FII inflows, and sector-specific movements. The US Federal Reserve's decision to keep interest rates unchanged provided a positive global backdrop, which helped benchmark indices like the Nifty 50 to close higher. This positive sentiment was further bolstered by significant buying from Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs), who turned net buyers to the tune of ₹2,982 crore after several days of net selling.

Domestically, the Automobile sector emerged as the clear leader, with heavyweights like M&M and Maruti Suzuki contributing to the Nifty's upward trajectory. Stocks like Balkrishna Industries, Sona BLW Precision Forgings, and Exide Industries from the Auto and Auto Components sector were among the top gainers, reflecting strong investor interest. However, the broader market witnessed profit booking, with the Midcap and Smallcap indices declining, and sectors like Realty, FMCG, and Pharma facing selling pressure. This indicates a selective buying approach by investors, focusing on specific sectors and large-cap stocks while remaining cautious on the broader market.

## Market breadth
The market breadth for the day was negative, with 153 advances and 344 declines out of a total of 497 stocks. This indicates that for every stock that gained, more than two stocks declined, suggesting that the positive movement in the Nifty 50 was not broad-based and a majority of stocks faced selling pressure.

## FII / DII flows
Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) turned significant net buyers today, injecting ₹2,982 crore into the Indian equity market. Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) also continued their buying spree with a net inflow of ₹998 crore.

Looking at the 5-day history, FIIs have turned net buyers today after three consecutive sessions of net selling. This shift from FIIs, coupled with consistent buying from DIIs, suggests renewed foreign investor confidence in the Indian market, which is a positive sign for liquidity and market sentiment.

## Event breakdown — what happened today and how it hit the market
Today's session was influenced by a combination of global and domestic factors. A key global event was the US Federal Reserve's decision to maintain interest rates, which provided a sense of stability and likely contributed to the Nifty 50's positive close. This global cue helped offset some of the domestic selling pressure seen in broader markets.

On the domestic front, the ongoing Q1 earnings season continued to drive stock-specific movements. Strong results from companies like Vedanta and Bajaj Finance provided a boost, particularly to their respective sectors. The Automobile sector, in particular, saw significant buying interest, with several auto stocks featuring among the top gainers, indicating robust performance and investor optimism in this space. Conversely, sectors like Realty saw profit booking. The substantial net buying by FIIs, after a period of outflows, was a significant development, providing crucial liquidity and support to the market.

## Setup for tomorrow
Looking ahead to tomorrow, global cues will continue to play a role. The US Dollar Index is slightly down, while crude oil prices (Brent and WTI) are also showing a minor decline. Gold and Silver, however, have seen a notable uptick. The US 10-year yield is marginally higher. These mixed signals suggest a cautious start. Investors will continue to monitor global developments, especially any further news regarding the US tech sector given the "AI Big Short" concerns. Domestically, the momentum in the Auto sector and the performance of other key sectors like Financial Services (post Bajaj Finance results) will be crucial. Key levels for Nifty 50 around 24,300 will be watched closely, with broader market participation needing to improve for a sustained rally.
