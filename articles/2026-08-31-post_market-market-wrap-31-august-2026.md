# Market Wrap — 31 August 2026

**TL;DR**
- The Nifty 50 closed marginally lower, while the Bank Nifty showed strong gains.
- Heavy selling pressure in Adani group stocks, triggered by MSCI index rebalancing, was a significant drag on the broader market.
- Ather Energy Ltd. emerged as the top Nifty 500 gainer, rising over 6%.
- Adani Energy Solutions Ltd. was the biggest Nifty 500 loser, plummeting over 10%.
- Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) were net sellers, while Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) provided strong buying support.
- F&O build-up data is unavailable for today's session and will be included from the next wrap.

## How the market behaved today
Indian equities witnessed a mixed trading session today, with the benchmark Nifty 50 closing slightly in the red, while the Bank Nifty surged higher. The Nifty 50 ended at 24080.40, down 0.39%, after trading in a narrow range. In contrast, the Bank Nifty closed strong at its day's high of 58024.95, gaining 0.92%. Midcap and Smallcap indices also saw divergent moves, with Midcap 100 closing higher and Smallcap 100 ending lower.

| Index | Close | Change | High | Low |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nifty 50 | 24080.40 | -0.39% | 24128.70 | 23993.60 |
| Bank Nifty | 58024.95 | +0.92% | 58024.95 | 57187.35 |
| Midcap 100 | 64224.75 | +0.24% | 64230.75 | 63347.50 |
| Smallcap 100 | 19931.65 | -0.74% | 20068.60 | 19830.55 |

The India VIX, a measure of market volatility, rose by 3.75% to 11.08, indicating a slight increase in market apprehension.

## What moved the market — the factors
The Indian market today was largely influenced by a significant sell-off in Adani group stocks, which faced heavy selling pressure following recent alterations in the MSCI Global Standard Index. This development acted as a major drag on the broader indices, particularly impacting the Metals & Mining and Power sectors where Adani group companies have a strong presence.

Globally, rising US bond yields and hawkish signals from the US Federal Reserve also pressured sentiment, as did renewed US-Iran tensions and crude oil prices above $90. Despite these headwinds, the robust buying by Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) provided crucial support, offsetting the net selling by Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs). The strong performance of the banking sector, as reflected in the Bank Nifty's gains, also helped to cushion the overall market decline.

## News that moved stocks
- **Adani Group Stocks Face Heavy Selling Pressure:** Shares of the Adani group experienced notable downward pressure, with Adani Enterprises declining by nearly eight percent amid overall market instability. This was largely attributed to recent alterations in the MSCI Global Standard Index.
- **NSE Records Historic Turnover in Closing Auction Session:** The National Stock Exchange (NSE) registered a substantial ₹39,718 crore turnover in its Closing Auction Session, accounting for 22% of the total cash market turnover today. This surge was primarily driven by the MSCI index review and rebalancing.
- **GRT Jewellers to Acquire Controlling Stake in TBZ:** GRT Jewellers will acquire a 74.12% controlling stake in TBZ for up to ₹1,033.71 crore. This deal aims to expand GRT's store network and national footprint, combining two longstanding jewellery brands.
- **Blackstone's Knowledge Realty Trust OFS Subscribed 1.4 Times:** Blackstone's offer for sale (OFS) of Knowledge Realty Trust units saw strong demand from non-retail investors on Day 1. The OFS, valued at ₹11,988 crore, is the largest in India's private sector.
- **Deepa Jewellers Raises ₹138 Cr from Anchor Investors Ahead of IPO:** Deepa Jewellers Ltd successfully raised ₹138 crore from anchor investors today. Their initial public offering is set to open for subscription on September 1.

**Interpretation:**
Long build-up indicates fresh buying with conviction, often suggesting a continuation of the upward trend. Short build-up signifies fresh selling pressure, potentially leading to further price declines. Short covering occurs when traders who had bet on a price fall buy back to close their positions, leading to a price bounce that may not reflect underlying strength. Long unwinding implies profit-taking or weakness, as traders exit their long positions.

## Market breadth
The market breadth was negative today, with 180 advances against 319 declines out of a total of 499 stocks. This indicates that more stocks fell than rose, suggesting a broad-based weakness despite the Nifty 50's marginal decline.

## FII / DII flows
Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) were net sellers today, offloading Indian equities worth ₹5,040 crore. In contrast, Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) continued to be strong buyers, injecting ₹5,184 crore into the market.

| Investor Type | Net Flow (₹ crore) |
|---|---|
| FII | -5,040 |
| DII | 5,184 |

Looking at the 5-day history, FIIs have turned net sellers for the last two sessions after being net buyers earlier in the week. DIIs, however, have consistently been net buyers throughout the period, providing crucial support to the market. This consistent DII buying has helped absorb some of the selling pressure from FIIs.

## Event breakdown — what happened today and how it hit the market
Today's session was significantly influenced by the **MSCI Global Standard Index rebalancing**. This event led to heavy selling pressure on several Adani group stocks, including Adani Energy Solutions, Adani Enterprises, Adani Green Energy, and Adani Power, which all featured prominently among the top losers. The rebalancing also resulted in a historic ₹39,718 crore turnover in the National Stock Exchange's Closing Auction Session, representing 22% of the total cash market turnover. This high turnover underscores the impact of the index adjustments on market activity. While the Adani group stocks faced headwinds, the banking sector showed resilience, with the Bank Nifty closing higher, indicating a rotation of funds or selective buying in other segments of the market.

## Setup for tomorrow
Looking ahead to tomorrow, global cues will play a significant role. The US 10-year yield closed higher at 4.76%, which could keep global sentiment cautious. Brent Crude saw a decline, while WTI Crude rose, suggesting mixed signals from the energy markets. The Dollar Index was slightly down, and the USD/INR closed lower at 95.15. Investors will be watching for any further developments regarding US bond yields and the Federal Reserve's stance. Domestically, the performance of the banking sector and any follow-through on the Adani group stocks will be key. The IPO of Deepa Jewellers opens tomorrow, which could attract some investor interest.
