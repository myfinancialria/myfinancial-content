# Market Wrap — 31 July 2026

**TL;DR**
- Indian equity benchmarks extended their gains for a third consecutive session, with both Nifty 50 and Bank Nifty closing in positive territory.
- Positive global cues, particularly rising US futures, and robust FII buying were key drivers for the market's upward momentum.
- Bajaj Finance Ltd. emerged as the standout gainer among Nifty 500 stocks, surging over 8%.
- Aptus Value Housing Finance India Ltd. was the top loser, declining nearly 6%.
- Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) were significant net buyers, pumping ₹3,624 crore into the market, while Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) were net sellers.
- F&O positioning data for today is unavailable and will be populated from the next session.

## How the market behaved today
Indian equity markets continued their upward trajectory for the third straight day, with the Nifty 50 closing above the 24,380 mark. The day saw a positive opening, with indices maintaining gains throughout the session, reflecting a broad-based positive sentiment.

| Index | Close | Change | High | Low |

| Nifty Midcap 100 | 62915.30 | +0.44% | 63072.40 | 62569.20 |
| Nifty Smallcap 100 | 19339.65 | +0.44% | 19416.30 | 19274.25 |

The India VIX, a measure of market volatility, declined by 3.29% to 11.76, indicating a further easing of investor apprehension.

## What moved the market — the factors
The Indian market's positive close today was primarily driven by encouraging global cues and strong buying interest from Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs). US stock futures rose, with Amazon's surge outweighing an Apple slump, setting a positive tone for global markets. This sentiment spilled over into Indian equities, supported by FIIs who were net buyers to the tune of ₹3,624 crore.

Heavyweight stocks like Bajaj Finance Ltd. and Hyundai Motor India Ltd. saw significant gains, contributing to the overall index rise. Sectoral rotation was also evident, with Media, Auto, and Energy sectors leading the charge, while IT and FMCG faced selling pressure. The decline in India VIX suggested reduced market uncertainty, further bolstering investor confidence. Despite mixed Q1 earnings from some major companies like ITC and Maruti Suzuki, the broader market maintained its positive momentum.

## Sectors — winners and losers
The day witnessed a clear rotation in sectors, with Media, Auto, and Energy leading the gains.

**Top 3 gainer sectors:**
- MEDIA: +2.09% - The sector saw strong buying interest today.
- AUTO: +1.64% - Boosted by positive sentiment and strong performance from key auto players like Hyundai Motor India and Balkrishna Industries.
- ENERGY: +1.09% - Gained amidst rising crude oil prices.

**Top 3 loser sectors:**
- IT: -1.56% - Faced selling pressure, possibly due to global tech sector dynamics or profit booking.
- FMCG: -1.05% - Dragged down by underperformance from some constituents, including ITC's Q1 results.
- METAL: +0.14% - Despite being listed among the top 3 'loser' sectors in the data, the Metal index actually closed with a slight gain.

## News that moved stocks
**Global Market Sentiment & Commodities**
- **US futures rise as Amazon surge outweighs Apple slump.** US stock futures showed strength, with the S&P 500 and Nasdaq futures extending their rally on robust tech earnings, particularly Amazon's strong performance. This positive sentiment from global markets provided a tailwind for Indian equities.
- **India bonds log first monthly loss in four as surging oil, Treasury yields sting.** Indian government bonds experienced their first monthly loss in four months, primarily due to rising oil prices and increasing U.S. Treasury yields. This indicates pressure on the fixed-income market.
- **Rupee posts strongest week since March on RBI support.** The Indian rupee concluded Friday with its most significant weekly gain since March, attributed to continuous support from the central bank amidst a weakening dollar. This strength in the rupee can be a positive for the broader economy.

**Corporate Earnings & Sector Specific News**
- **Aditya Birla Capital Q1 Results: Profit jumps 40% YoY to Rs 1,175 crore.** Aditya Birla Capital reported a substantial 40% year-on-year increase in its first-quarter profits, reaching ₹1,175 crore, indicating strong performance in the financial services sector.
- **ITC Q1 Results: Consolidated profit drops 16% YoY to ₹4,394 crore; cigarettes revenue surges 81% YoY.** ITC reported a 16.2% year-on-year decline in consolidated profit for Q1FY27, despite a significant 27.6% rise in revenue from operations. The standalone profit also fell by 27% YoY to ₹3,579 crore. This news likely contributed to the FMCG sector's underperformance.
- **Maruti Suzuki Q1 Results: Net profit drops 9% as margin pressure offsets revenue growth.** Maruti Suzuki reported an 11% year-on-year decline in Q1 standalone net profit to ₹3,352 crore, despite a 36% rise in revenue to ₹52,456 crore. Higher material costs weighed on earnings, leading to margin pressure. Despite this, the broader Auto sector performed well today.
- **State Bank of India's perpetual bond demand seen spurring more issuances, bankers say.** State Bank of India's successful perpetual bond sale is expected to encourage other banks to issue similar bonds, potentially impacting the financial sector.
- **Urban Company Q1 Results: Firm posts Rs 92 crore loss vs profit year ago; revenue rises 44% YoY.** Urban Company reported a net loss of ₹92 crore in Q1 FY27, a reversal from a profit a year ago, despite a 44% year-on-year rise in revenue. Aggressive expansion of its InstaHelp business impacted profitability.

The market breadth was positive today, with Advances at 302 and Declines at 196 out of a total of 498 stocks. This indicates that more stocks gained than lost, suggesting a relatively broad-based positive sentiment across the market.

## FII / DII flows
Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) were strong net buyers in the cash market today, while Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) were net sellers.

| Investor Type | Net Flow (₹ crore) |
|---|---|
| FII | +3,624 |
| DII | -1,864 |

Looking at the 5-day history, FIIs have been net buyers for the last three consecutive sessions, accumulating a significant amount of capital. This consistent buying by FIIs, especially after being net sellers earlier in the week, indicates renewed foreign investor confidence in the Indian market. DIIs, on the other hand, have shown a mixed trend, being net sellers today after two days of net buying.

## Event breakdown — what happened today and how it hit the market
Today's session was primarily driven by a combination of positive global cues, particularly the uptick in US stock futures, and robust buying activity from Foreign Institutional Investors. While several companies, including ITC and Maruti Suzuki, announced their Q1 earnings, the market's overall direction was more influenced by broader flows and sentiment rather than a single defining event. The decline in India VIX also contributed to a more stable trading environment.

## Setup for tomorrow
Looking ahead to tomorrow, global cues will continue to be a significant factor. The positive momentum from US futures today suggests a potentially strong opening for Asian markets. Investors will keep an eye on crude oil prices, which have been on the rise, and US Treasury yields, which have also increased. Domestically, the ongoing Q1 earnings season will continue to provide stock-specific triggers. Key levels for Nifty 50 and Bank Nifty will be closely watched, along with the performance of sectors like Auto and Energy that showed strength today.
