## Currency, commodities and yields
The Indian Rupee (USD/INR) saw a marginal depreciation against the US Dollar, trading at ₹95.67, up 0.07%. The Dollar Index also edged higher by 0.12% to 100.92. Crude oil prices, both Brent and WTI, saw slight declines, with Brent at $90.65 (-0.10%) and WTI at $84.36 (-0.12%). Gold prices surged significantly by 1.66% to $4101.60, while Silver dipped by 0.83% to $57.38. The US 10-year Treasury Yield rose by 0.39% to 4.62%.

**Interpretation:**
*   **USD/INR:** A slight rise in USD/INR indicates a marginal weakening of the Rupee, which could be a slight tailwind for IT and pharma exporters but could put pressure on importers.
*   **Dollar Index:** The stronger Dollar Index suggests global risk aversion, which typically leads to capital outflows from emerging markets like India.
*   **Crude Oil:** The marginal dip in crude prices is a positive for India, which is a net oil importer, potentially easing concerns for oil marketing companies (OMCs), paint, and aviation sectors.
*   **Gold:** The significant jump in gold prices suggests increased safe-haven demand amidst global uncertainties, reflecting investor caution.
*   **US 10Y Yield:** A rise in US bond yields makes dollar-denominated assets more attractive, potentially drawing FII flows away from emerging markets and increasing borrowing costs globally.

## Stock & sector news that matters

*   **US Federal Reserve's Hawkish Tilt & Dissent:** New Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh faces early policy disagreements, with three policymakers pushing for a rate hike despite the central bank's decision to keep rates unchanged. President Trump also supports Warsh despite policy differences.
    **Why it matters:** This news highlights ongoing global monetary policy uncertainty. A hawkish Fed stance raises the odds of future rate hikes, which can lead to higher US bond yields, strengthen the dollar, and potentially trigger FII outflows from emerging markets like India, impacting overall market sentiment.
*   **Redington Shares Rally 15% on Strong Q1 Results:** Redington Ltd. shares surged after reporting a 77% YoY jump in Q1 FY27 net profit to ₹486 crore and a 35% rise in revenue, driven by broad-based growth.
    **Why it matters:** This strong performance from a technology distribution company could set a positive tone for the broader IT and tech-related sectors. Strong earnings can attract investor interest and lead to sector-specific rallies.
*   **MTAR Technologies Shares Hit 5% Upper Circuit After Q1 Results:** MTAR Technologies shares rose 5% following robust Q1 FY27 results, with net profit surging to ₹50.2 crore and revenue doubling to ₹360 crore, reflecting improved operational efficiency.
    **Why it matters:** This positive outcome for a precision engineering company, often linked to defence and aerospace, could boost sentiment for manufacturing and capital goods sectors, indicating strong order books and execution capabilities.
*   **Adani Ports Shares Decline 3% Despite Strong Q1 Results:** Adani Ports shares fell 3% after reporting a 9% YoY rise in Q1 FY27 net profit to ₹3,620 crore and an 18.5% increase in revenue to ₹10,821 crore. Brokerages like Nomura still see significant upside.
    **Why it matters:** This highlights a "sell on news" reaction, where despite good results, investors might book profits or the results might have already been priced in. It suggests that even strong fundamental performance might not always lead to immediate stock appreciation, especially in a cautious market.
*   **Asian Paints Shares Decline 3% After Q1 Results:** Asian Paints shares fell despite better-than-expected Q1 FY27 earnings and multiple target price hikes from brokerages.
    **Why it matters:** Similar to Adani Ports, this indicates profit booking or high expectations from a blue-chip company. It could signal caution in the consumer discretionary space, where even good results might not be enough to sustain rallies.
*   **Bank of Japan (BOJ) May Shift to Inflation-Fighting Mode by December:** A former BOJ official suggests the central bank could accelerate interest rate hikes as early as December 2026, shifting to an aggressive inflation-fighting stance.
    **Why it matters:** A hawkish shift by the BOJ would be a significant global monetary policy event, following the US Fed's stance. It could lead to increased volatility in global currency and bond markets, potentially impacting capital flows and investor sentiment towards risk assets worldwide, including India.

## Events & data to watch — and how markets usually react
*   **US Federal Reserve's Hawkish Stance:** The ongoing discussion and dissent within the US Fed regarding interest rates will remain a key global driver. Markets typically react with increased volatility to central bank hawkishness, as it impacts global liquidity and risk appetite. Higher rates in the US can lead to a stronger dollar and potential capital outflows from emerging economies.
*   **Bank of Japan (BOJ) Policy Outlook:** The potential for the BOJ to shift to an inflation-fighting mode by December will be closely watched. Such a move from a major global central bank can cause significant shifts in currency markets (especially JPY) and global bond yields, creating ripples across international financial markets.
*   **Ongoing Q1 FY27 Earnings Season:** While no large-cap results are due today, the market will continue to react to results declared yesterday (e.g., Redington, MTAR, Adani Ports, Asian Paints, Vedanta Oil & Gas). Earnings season typically brings stock-specific movements and can set the tone for sectors. Strong results can lead to rallies, while misses or profit booking despite good numbers can cause declines.
*   **Manipal Health Enterprises IPO:** The second day of subscription for Manipal Health Enterprises IPO will be monitored. IPO subscriptions and Grey Market Premium (GMP) often reflect broader market sentiment and investor appetite for new listings.
