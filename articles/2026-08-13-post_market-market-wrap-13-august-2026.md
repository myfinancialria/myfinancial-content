# Market Wrap — 13 August 2026

**TL;DR**
*   Indian benchmark indices, Nifty 50 and Bank Nifty, closed in negative territory today, while the broader market indices, Midcap 100 and Smallcap 100, managed to eke out gains.
*   The market's cautious mood was primarily influenced by sustained selling by Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) and lingering geopolitical concerns surrounding US-Iran uncertainty.
*   Astral Ltd. emerged as the top gainer among Nifty 500 stocks, surging by 8.74%.
*   Aditya Infotech Ltd. was the biggest laggard in the Nifty 500 pack, declining by 5.00%.
*   FIIs were net sellers with an outflow of ₹1,002 crore, while Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) provided strong support with net buying of ₹5,842 crore.
*   F&O positioning data for fresh build-up is unavailable for this session and will be reflected from the next wrap.

## How the market behaved today
Indian equities witnessed a mixed trading session today, with frontline indices closing lower while the broader market showed resilience. The Nifty 50 ended below the crucial 24,400 mark, closing at 24395.85, down 0.16%. Similarly, the Bank Nifty also saw declines, settling at 57635.25, a drop of 0.43%. In contrast, the Nifty Midcap 100 and Nifty Smallcap 100 indices managed to post gains of 0.15% and 0.27% respectively, indicating underlying strength in the broader market. The day was largely range-bound for the main indices after a cautious start.

The India VIX, a key gauge of market volatility, declined by 2.31% to 11.42, suggesting a slight easing of immediate market apprehension.

## What moved the market — the factors
The Indian market today was primarily influenced by a cautious global sentiment, exacerbated by geopolitical uncertainties surrounding US-Iran relations, which kept investors on edge. This led to Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) turning net sellers, pulling out ₹1,002 crore from the Indian equity market. The selling pressure from FIIs contributed to the decline in benchmark indices like the Nifty 50 and Bank Nifty.

However, Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) continued to provide strong support, injecting ₹5,842 crore into the market, which helped cushion the fall and propelled the Midcap 100 and Smallcap 100 indices into positive territory. Sector-wise, the Metal sector was a significant drag, declining by 1.05%, with stocks like National Aluminium Co. Ltd. seeing notable drops. Conversely, sectors like Realty, FMCG, and IT showed resilience, contributing to the positive breadth in the broader market.

Specific corporate earnings also played a role. Solar Industries India Ltd. surged over 8% after reporting a stellar 93% year-on-year rise in Q1 FY27 net profit. In contrast, Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles reported an over 80% year-on-year decline in consolidated net profit for Q1 FY27, which likely weighed on investor sentiment for the auto sector. The Indian Rupee also weakened slightly, influenced by dollar outflows and derivative maturities.

## Event breakdown — what happened today and how it hit the market
Today's session was not dominated by a single, major economic event or policy announcement. Instead, the market's movements were a confluence of several factors. The primary drivers included the continued outflow of funds by Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs), which put pressure on the benchmark indices. Simultaneously, lingering geopolitical concerns, particularly the uncertainty surrounding US-Iran relations, contributed to a cautious sentiment among investors.

On the domestic front, a slew of corporate earnings announcements influenced individual stock movements. Strong Q1 results from companies like Solar Industries India Ltd. led to significant stock rallies, while weaker-than-expected performance from others, such as Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles, weighed on their respective sectors. The robust buying by Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) acted as a crucial counterweight to FII selling, preventing a sharper decline in the broader market and helping the Midcap and Smallcap indices close higher. The slight weakening of the Indian Rupee also reflected the dollar outflows seen today.
