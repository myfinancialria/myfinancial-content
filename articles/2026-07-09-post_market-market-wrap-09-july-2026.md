## How the market behaved today
The Nifty 50 closed at 23,962.80, up 0.34%, while the Bank Nifty gained 0.90% to 57,252.45. The market opened with a gap and remained range-bound throughout the session, with the Nifty 50 oscillating between 24,134.70 and 23,925.70. The Nifty Midcap 100 and Smallcap 100 outperformed, gaining 1.38% and 1.80%, respectively.

India VIX fell 8.99% to 13.36, indicating a decline in market volatility.

## What moved the market — the factors
The market's positive performance today was driven by a combination of factors. Positive global cues, particularly from the US, where stock futures showed modest gains, contributed to the upbeat sentiment. The decline in oil prices and the rupee's modest appreciation against the US dollar also supported the market. Additionally, strong performances from the realty, media, and PSU bank sectors, along with robust gains in midcap and smallcap stocks, added to the positive momentum. On the flip side, the IT and auto sectors saw some profit-taking, which limited the gains.
