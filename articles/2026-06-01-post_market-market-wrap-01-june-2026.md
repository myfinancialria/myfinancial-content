# Market Wrap — 01 June 2026

**TL;DR**
- Indian benchmark indices, Nifty 50 and Bank Nifty, closed lower for the day.
- Midcap and Smallcap segments also witnessed declines, indicating broad-based selling pressure.
- IT, Media, and Metal sectors bucked the trend with gains, while FMCG, PSU Bank, and Realty were the top losers.
- Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) were significant net sellers, while Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) provided support.
- Elevated crude oil prices and ongoing geopolitical tensions continued to weigh on market sentiment.

## How the market closed

Indian equities concluded the trading session on a negative note, extending losses for the fourth consecutive day. The Nifty 50 shed 0.70%, closing at 23,382.60, while the Bank Nifty fell 1.10% to settle at 53,643.10.

| Index | Close | Change | High | Low |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nifty 50 | 23382.60 | -0.70% | 23733.70 | 23357.95 |
| Bank Nifty | 53643.10 | -1.10% | 54582.75 | 53470.00 |
| Nifty Midcap 100 | 60827.95 | -1.45% | 62062.00 | 60735.85 |
| Nifty Smallcap 100 | 17979.80 | -0.88% | 18295.65 | 17949.70 |

The India VIX, a measure of market volatility, rose by 2.16% to 16.54, indicating an uptick in market uncertainty.

## Sectors — winners and losers

The market saw a mixed bag across sectors, with a few pockets of strength amidst broader weakness.

**Top 3 Gainer Sectors:**
- **IT (+2.66%)**: The Information Technology sector witnessed strong gains, driven by optimism stemming from robust US software earnings and an improved sentiment towards the sector.
- **MEDIA (+1.37%)**: The Media sector also performed well, with some individual stocks seeing significant upside.
- **METAL (+0.49%)**: The Metal sector showed resilience, with global aluminium prices hitting a four-year high on renewed Middle East supply risks, providing a tailwind.

**Top 3 Loser Sectors:**
- **FMCG (-2.30%)**: The Fast Moving Consumer Goods sector was among the top laggards, reflecting broader defensive selling.
- **PSU BANK (-1.85%)**: Public Sector Banks faced selling pressure, contributing to the overall market decline.
- **REALTY (-1.83%)**: The Realty sector closed in the red despite news of Godrej Properties acquiring land for a new project.

## Market breadth

The market breadth was overwhelmingly negative today, with 365 declines against just 135 advances out of 500 stocks. This indicates that selling pressure was widespread across the broader market, affecting a large number of stocks beyond the benchmark indices.

## FII / DII flows

Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) continued their selling spree, registering a significant net outflow of ₹21,106 crore today. Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs), however, provided crucial support by being net buyers to the tune of ₹16,764 crore.

| Investor Category | Net Cash Position (₹ crore) |
|---|---|
| FII | -21,106 |
| DII | 16,764 |

Over the past five trading sessions, FIIs have been consistent net sellers, with only one day of net buying on May 25th. In contrast, DIIs have consistently been net buyers, helping to cushion the market impact of FII outflows.

## What moved markets today

Several factors contributed to the negative sentiment and market movements today:

**Geopolitical Tensions and Crude Oil Prices**
Benchmark indices extended losses for a fourth straight session amid elevated crude oil prices and ongoing geopolitical tensions. Brent crude closed significantly higher at $97.25, up +5.65%, while WTI crude also surged +7.78% to $94.16. This volatility in fuel prices has prompted airlines globally to consider hiking passenger fares and cutting flight frequencies.

**Persistent FII Selling Pressure**
Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) continued to offload Indian equities, contributing to the persistent selling pressure observed in the market. Today's substantial FII net outflow of ₹21,106 crore was a key factor in the market's decline.

**Sector-Specific Developments**
The **IT sector** saw a boost, jumping over 2.5% as strong US software earnings revived optimism for domestic technology stocks. Meanwhile, **Ola Electric Mobility** launched its Qualified Institutional Placement (QIP), setting a floor price of ₹37.74 per share to raise funds. In the **Real Estate sector**, Godrej Properties announced the acquisition of a 23-acre land parcel in Greater Noida for ₹500 crore, with an estimated revenue potential of over ₹7,000 crore from a planned group housing project.

## Setup for tomorrow

The Indian markets are likely to remain under pressure tomorrow, influenced by the continued FII selling and elevated crude oil prices. Geopolitical tensions, particularly in the Middle East, will be a key factor to watch, as they directly impact crude prices and global sentiment. Investors will also be looking for cues from global markets, especially how US stocks balance AI optimism with ongoing international concerns.

Globally, US stocks opened near record highs today, with AI optimism countering worries about the US-Iran situation. However, the significant rise in Brent and WTI crude prices overnight could weigh on sentiment. The Dollar Index also saw a slight uptick.
