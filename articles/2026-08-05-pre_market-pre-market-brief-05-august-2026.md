# Pre-Market Brief — 05 August 2026

**TL;DR**
*   Global markets ended on a strong positive note, with US indices rallying and Asian markets, especially Japan's Nikkei, seeing significant gains.
*   GIFT Nifty is indicating a slightly negative start for the Indian market, pointing to a potential gap-down.
*   Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) were net buyers yesterday and have shown consistent buying over the last five sessions, while Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) were net sellers yesterday.
*   The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) maintained the repo rate at 5.25% in its latest policy meeting, while revising FY27 growth projections upwards and inflation downwards.
*   Nifty's key level to watch is 24600, which has significant Call and Put Open Interest, suggesting it could act as a crucial pivot for the day.
*   The sharp decline in crude oil prices and the RBI's policy stance will be key factors influencing market sentiment today.

## How global markets traded — and what it means for India
Global markets displayed a strong positive momentum overnight, largely driven by a rally in AI-related stocks in the US and robust corporate earnings. The Dow Jones, Nasdaq, and S&P 500 all closed significantly higher, with gains exceeding 1.7%. This positive sentiment extended to Asian markets this morning, with Japan's Nikkei surging over 3% and China's Shanghai Composite also posting a healthy gain.

## Currency, commodities and yields
The Indian Rupee strengthened against the US Dollar, with USD/INR trading at 95.06, down 0.28%. This comes as the Dollar Index saw a marginal decline of 0.04% to 99.85. Crude oil prices witnessed a sharp decline, with Brent crude falling 5.68% to $79.01 and WTI crude dropping 6.35% to $75.24. Gold and Silver prices, often considered safe-haven assets, saw significant gains, with Gold up 4.02% to $4195.80 and Silver up 5.92% to $61.08. The US 10-Year Treasury Yield also moved lower, declining 1.26% to 4.63%.

**Interpretation:** A stronger Rupee (USD/INR down) is generally positive for India as it reduces import costs and can attract FII flows. The sharp fall in crude oil prices is a significant positive for India, potentially easing inflationary pressures and benefiting oil marketing companies (OMCs), paint, and aviation sectors. The rise in gold and silver suggests some underlying caution or safe-haven buying globally. A lower US 10-Year Yield typically makes emerging markets like India more attractive for foreign capital, potentially supporting FII inflows.

## GIFT Nifty — what it's signalling
The GIFT Nifty is currently trading at 24707.50, down 0.19%.

**Interpretation:** This indicates a potential slight gap-down opening for the Nifty 50 today, suggesting a cautious start despite the overall positive global cues.

## Stock & sector news that matters

*   **RBI Monetary Policy: Repo Rate Unchanged, Growth/Inflation Revised**
    The RBI's Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) decided to keep the policy repo rate unchanged at 5.25% and maintained a 'neutral' stance in its August 2026 policy meeting. The central bank revised its FY27 GDP growth forecast upwards to 6.7% and lowered its inflation forecast to 5% for FY27.
    **Why it matters:** The unchanged repo rate provides stability and continuity for borrowing costs, which is generally positive for economic activity. The upward revision in growth projections signals confidence in domestic economic resilience, while the downward revision in inflation could ease concerns about future rate hikes. This overall dovish-neutral stance is supportive for equity markets.

*   **HSBC Predicts $25 Billion FII Comeback for India**
    HSBC analysts suggest India could attract up to $25 billion in foreign equity inflows if global emerging-market funds increase their allocations to neutral. This potential influx is attributed to improving earnings, resilient domestic demand, and a favourable policy environment.
    **Why it matters:** This news provides a strong fundamental tailwind for the Indian market, reinforcing the positive FII flow trend observed recently. It indicates that global investors see significant upside potential in India, which could lead to sustained buying interest across various sectors.

*   **Deepak Nitrite Shares Jump 4% on Strong Q1FY27 Results**
    Deepak Nitrite reported a more than threefold jump in its June-quarter net profit (209% YoY) and a 36% rise in revenue. The company also announced a ₹2,500 crore capital expenditure plan.
    **Why it matters:** This strong performance and significant capex plan are very positive for Deepak Nitrite, indicating robust operational growth and future expansion. The stock is likely to see continued investor interest and could impact sentiment for other specialty chemical companies.

*   **Ola Electric Mobility Shares Rally 7% on Battery Storage MoU**
    Ola Electric shares surged after the company signed an MoU with Axis Energy to deploy up to 20 GWh of battery energy storage systems by 2032. This is a major tie-up for Ola's battery manufacturing plans.
    **Why it matters:** This partnership is a significant development for Ola Electric, highlighting its expansion into the battery energy storage segment. It could boost investor confidence in the company's long-term growth prospects and potentially impact other players in the EV and renewable energy ecosystem.

*   **Bharti Airtel Shares Rise 4% Post Q1 Results; Brokerages Bullish**
    Bharti Airtel reported a 37% year-on-year jump in June-quarter net profit and an 18% increase in revenue. Brokerages like Jefferies and CLSA maintained bullish views with positive target prices.
    **Why it matters:** Strong earnings from a telecom major like Bharti Airtel are positive for the sector. Brokerage upgrades and positive outlooks can drive further buying in the stock and potentially uplift other telecom players.

*   **Rupee Strengthens Past 95 to One-Month High**
    The Indian Rupee hit a one-month high, sailing past 95 against the US Dollar, driven by lower oil prices and a weaker dollar. Forward premiums also dropped ahead of the RBI's policy decision.
    **Why it matters:** A stronger Rupee is beneficial for India's import bill, particularly for crude oil, and can help in managing inflation. It also makes Indian assets more attractive to foreign investors.

## Events & data to watch — and how markets usually react

*   **RBI Monetary Policy Outcome:** The RBI has already announced its policy, keeping the repo rate unchanged and revising growth/inflation forecasts. Markets typically react positively to stability in interest rates and optimistic growth outlooks, especially when accompanied by lower inflation projections. However, the initial reaction might be tempered by profit-booking or global cues.
*   **Crude Oil Price Movement:** The sharp decline in Brent and WTI crude prices is a significant positive. Any further volatility in global crude prices will be closely watched, as sustained lower prices are beneficial for India's economy and corporate earnings, particularly for sectors like OMCs, paints, and airlines.
*   **Global Market Cues:** The strong performance of US and Asian markets overnight will provide a positive backdrop. However, any fresh developments or data releases from major global economies could influence sentiment.
*   **FII/DII Flow Trends:** While FIIs have been net buyers, any significant change in their stance or DII activity could influence market direction.

## What to watch in today's session
1.  **Nifty's reaction to 24600:** This level is crucial, with significant Call and Put OI. A sustained move above or below this could dictate the day's trend.
2.  **Crude Oil Prices:** Monitor for any rebound in crude oil prices after yesterday's sharp fall, as this could impact sentiment for oil-sensitive sectors.
3.  **FII Flows:** Continue to watch FII activity for sustained buying or any signs of profit booking, which could influence overall market direction.
4.  **Corporate Actions:** Stocks going ex-dividend or undergoing splits today, such as Brigade, Hyundai, Goodyear, Tembo, and Ajanta Pharma, might see price adjustments and trading interest.

## Bottom line
The Indian market is poised for a cautious start, indicated by GIFT Nifty, despite strong positive cues from global equity markets and a significant drop in crude oil prices. The RBI's decision to maintain the repo rate and its optimistic growth outlook provide a stable domestic backdrop. FIIs have been consistent buyers, offering strong support. The key for Nifty today will be its ability to hold above or break decisively from the 24600 level, which is a significant pivot point based on option chain data. Investors should remain vigilant to global market movements and crude oil price volatility.
