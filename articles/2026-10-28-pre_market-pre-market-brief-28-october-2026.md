# Pre-Market Brief — 28 October 2026

**TL;DR**
- Global markets closed mixed to positive, with US tech leading gains and Asian indices mostly up.
- GIFT Nifty signals a positive opening bias for the Indian markets.
- FIIs turned significant net buyers yesterday, while DIIs were net sellers.
- Jio Platforms' strong listing has propelled the Reliance Group to become India's most valued conglomerate.
- Key Nifty technical and option chain levels are unavailable, suggesting reliance on live price action.
- Rising US 10-year yields and global inflation concerns, potentially exacerbated by the Iran war, remain a key risk.

## How global markets traded — and what it means for India
Overnight, US markets closed on a positive note, with the tech-heavy Nasdaq leading the charge, gaining over 1%. The S&P 500 also saw healthy gains, while the Dow Jones remained largely flat. In Asia this morning, the Nikkei 225 posted strong gains, and the Hang Seng and Shanghai indices also traded higher, indicating a generally upbeat sentiment across the region. European markets, however, closed slightly in the red.

**Interpretation:** The predominantly positive global cues, especially from the US and most of Asia, suggest a favourable starting point for Indian markets today. The strong performance of tech stocks globally could provide a tailwind for Indian IT names.

The Indian Rupee (USD/INR) remained stable against the US Dollar. The Dollar Index saw a slight uptick. In commodities, Brent Crude declined significantly, while WTI Crude saw a rise. However, the rising US 10-year yield is a point of concern, as higher global yields can make emerging markets less attractive, potentially impacting FII flows into India.

## GIFT Nifty — what it's signalling
GIFT Nifty is currently trading at 22766.50, up by +0.54%. This indicates a positive bias and suggests a likely gap-up opening for the Nifty 50 today.

**Interpretation:** The strong positive signal from GIFT Nifty points towards a firm start for the Indian benchmark index, aligning with the broadly positive global sentiment.

However, DIIs taking some profits suggests a cautious approach domestically. Over the last five sessions, FIIs have shown a mixed trend, while DIIs have largely been supportive, except for yesterday.

## Option-chain signals (OI) — where support & resistance sit
Option-chain data for Nifty, Bank Nifty, and Sensex is unavailable for the nearest expiry.

**Interpretation:** Without current option chain data, it's difficult to pinpoint immediate psychological support and resistance levels from open interest. High call OI usually indicates where sellers expect a ceiling, while high put OI suggests where buyers are defending a floor. A Put-Call Ratio (PCR) above 1 typically leans supportive, while below 0.7 leans cautious. Traders should monitor price action closely for clues on where buying and selling interest is concentrated, especially around significant round numbers.

## Stock & sector news that matters

**Adani Enterprises Q2 Results: Revenue spikes 75% YoY to Rs 37,216 crore, profit at Rs 1,492 crore**
Adani Enterprises reported a 55% year-on-year drop in consolidated net profit to ₹1,492 crore for Q2 FY27, despite a significant 75% surge in revenue to ₹37,216 crore.
**Why it matters:** This mixed bag of results (revenue up, profit down) could lead to volatility in Adani Enterprises shares today. Investors will be scrutinising the reasons behind the profit decline despite robust revenue growth.

**Adani Ports Q2 Results: Net profit jumps 17% YoY to Rs 3,622 crore; revenue up 24%**
Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone (APSEZ) announced a 17% year-on-year increase in Q2 FY27 net profit to ₹3,622 crore, with revenue growing 24% to ₹11,340 crore. The company also raised its FY27 revenue and EBITDA guidance.
**Why it matters:** Strong results from Adani Ports, coupled with an upward revision in guidance, are positive for the stock and could provide support to the broader Adani Group shares. This indicates robust operational performance.

**Jio effect! Reliance beats Tata to become India's most valued conglomerate**
The listing of Jio Platforms has propelled the Reliance Group to become India’s most valuable conglomerate, with a combined market capitalisation exceeding ₹28 lakh crore, surpassing the Tata Group.
**Why it matters:** This is a significant development for the Indian corporate landscape. It highlights the market's valuation of Reliance's diverse businesses, particularly its digital arm. Reliance Industries (RIL) shares could see positive sentiment, and the newly listed Jio Platforms stock might continue to attract investor interest.

**Jio Platforms delivers biggest listing gain among India’s 5 largest IPOs, jumps 21% on debut**
Jio Platforms shares listed at a 21.35% premium to its IPO price of ₹1,119, marking the largest listing-day gain among India's five biggest IPOs by issue size.
**Why it matters:** The strong debut of Jio Platforms underscores investor confidence in India's digital and telecom space. This positive sentiment could extend to other telecom-related stocks and technology companies.

**US Market: Fed expected to hold rates as Iran war fuels inflation concerns**
The US Federal Reserve is widely anticipated to maintain current interest rates, as rising energy prices, potentially linked to the Iran war, are intensifying inflation concerns.
**Why it matters:** The US Fed's stance on interest rates is crucial for global markets, including India. A pause in rate hikes could be seen as positive, but the underlying inflation concerns, especially from geopolitical events like the Iran war, could keep global bond yields elevated and influence FII flows.

## Events & data to watch — and how markets usually react
-   **US Federal Reserve Policy Decision:** While the news suggests a rate pause, the official announcement and accompanying commentary later this week will be closely watched. Markets typically react with volatility around central bank policy decisions, especially regarding future rate outlooks.
-   **Global Inflationary Pressures (Iran war impact):** The ongoing geopolitical situation and its potential to fuel energy prices and global inflation will be a key monitorable. Higher inflation concerns can lead to increased bond yields and potentially impact equity valuations globally.
-   **FII/DII Flow Trends:** The direction and magnitude of institutional flows remain critical for market momentum. Sustained FII buying typically provides strong support, while consistent selling can exert downward pressure.

## What to watch in today's session
1.  **GIFT Nifty's lead:** The positive signal from GIFT Nifty will set the initial tone for the Indian open.
2.  **Adani Group Stocks:** Adani Enterprises and Adani Ports will be in focus following their Q2 results. Their performance could influence broader market sentiment.
3.  **Reliance Industries & Jio Platforms:** The news of Reliance becoming India's most valued conglomerate and Jio Platforms' strong listing will keep these stocks in the spotlight.
4.  **Global Yields & Crude Prices:** The movement in US 10-year yields and Brent crude prices will be crucial, given their impact on FII flows and corporate input costs.
5.  **Institutional Flows:** FII and DII activity will be closely monitored to gauge conviction levels.
6.  **Geopolitical Developments:** Any further news regarding the Iran war and its potential impact on global energy prices and inflation will be a key risk factor.

## Bottom line
Indian markets are poised for a positive open today, largely driven by favourable global cues and a strong signal from GIFT Nifty. The significant FII buying yesterday also lends support. However, rising US bond yields and persistent global inflation concerns, potentially exacerbated by geopolitical tensions, remain underlying risks that could cap upside or induce volatility. Traders should watch the initial market reaction to the positive open and monitor institutional flows closely.
