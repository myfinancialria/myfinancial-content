# Market Wrap — 02 June 2026

**TL;DR**
- Indian markets ended the day in positive territory, with Nifty 50 closing near 23,500.
- IT stocks were the star performers, leading the sectoral gains.
- Broader markets also saw positive momentum, albeit with smaller gains.
- Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) remained net sellers, while Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) provided strong buying support.
- Volatility, as measured by India VIX, saw a significant decline.

## How the market closed
Indian equities closed higher today, snapping a four-session losing streak, with the Nifty 50 gaining 0.43% to settle at 23,483.55 and the Bank Nifty rising 0.13% to close at 53,714.65.

| Index | Close | Change | High | Low |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| Nifty 50 | 23483.55 | +0.43% | 23556.95 | 23229.15 |
| Bank Nifty | 53714.65 | +0.13% | 53933.55 | 53121.85 |
| Nifty Midcap 100 | 60942.00 | +0.19% | 61018.55 | 60170.35 |
| Nifty Smallcap 100 | 18052.30 | +0.40% | 18073.80 | 17787.90 |

The India VIX, a measure of market volatility, declined by 7.13% to 15.36, indicating a reduction in market uncertainty.

- **IT:** +4.23%. The sector witnessed a strong surge, with heavyweight stocks like TCS and Infosys leading the charge, partly driven by expectations of AI expanding opportunities for Indian IT.
- **FMCG:** +0.76%.
- **AUTO:** +0.72%.

**Top 3 Loser Sectors:**
- **PHARMA:** -0.86%.
- **ENERGY:** -0.16%.
- **MEDIA:** +0.12%. (Note: While listed as a loser, it closed marginally positive. This indicates it was among the weakest performing sectors despite a positive close).

## Market breadth
Market breadth was positive today, with 297 advances against 201 declines out of 498 traded stocks. This suggests that the broader market participated in the upward move, indicating a healthy sentiment beyond just the frontline indices.

## FII / DII flows
Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) continued their selling streak, offloading ₹3,912 crore from the Indian equity markets today. In contrast, Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) provided significant buying support, with a net inflow of ₹5,109 crore.

| Investor Type | Net Position (₹ crore) |
|:---|:---|
| FII | -3,912 |
| DII | +5,109 |

Looking at the 5-day history, FIIs have been net sellers for the past four trading sessions, indicating sustained outflows. DIIs, however, have consistently been net buyers, absorbing the FII selling pressure and providing crucial stability to the market.

## What moved markets today

**1. IT Sector Shines on AI Optimism**
- **AI will expand opportunities for Indian IT; luxury housing demand remains strong: BofA Securities**. BofA Securities highlighted that AI presents significant new revenue streams for Indian IT services, moving beyond just coding into broader business processes. This positive outlook contributed to the strong performance of the IT sector today.
- **Market Impact:** The Nifty IT index surged over 4%, with major IT stocks like TCS and Infosys seeing substantial gains, driving the overall market higher.

**2. Government Divestment and IPO News**
- **NHPC OFS subscribed nearly 3.5 times on Day 1, Govt to exercise green shoe option**. The government's offer to sell up to 6% stake in NHPC at a floor price of ₹71 per share saw robust demand on its first day.
- **Oyo-parent Prism secures Sebi's nod to launch Rs 6,650-crore IPO**. Oyo's parent company, Prism, received SEBI approval for its substantial ₹6,650 crore IPO, which is expected to value the company between USD 7-8 billion.
- **Market Impact:** While NHPC was among the top losers due to the OFS, the strong subscription indicates underlying demand. The IPO news for Prism adds to the pipeline of new listings, potentially boosting market sentiment.

**3. Global Cues and Volatility Reduction**
- **India bonds end flat as RBI policy caution offsets cooler oil**. Indian bond markets remained flat as the Reserve Bank of India's cautious policy stance counteracted the easing of Brent crude and U.S. Treasury yields.
- **India VIX: 15.36 (-7.13%)**. The significant drop in India VIX suggests a decrease in market fear and uncertainty, contributing to a more stable trading environment.
- **Market Impact:** Reduced volatility and stable bond markets, despite FII outflows, provided a conducive environment for domestic equities to recover.

## Setup for tomorrow
Indian markets will likely take cues from global developments and the continued FII/DII flow dynamics. The resilience shown by mid and small-cap stocks, driven by structural growth drivers, could continue to attract investor interest. Investors are advised to reset return expectations and avoid extreme positions in equities, focusing on balanced asset allocation.

**Global Cues:** The USD/INR closed higher at 95.25, while the Dollar Index saw a marginal dip. Brent crude and WTI crude both closed slightly up. Gold and Silver saw positive moves. US 10Y Yield declined. US futures and Asian markets data is unavailable, as are specific key data releases overnight. Sectors like IT and those with strong domestic growth stories could remain in focus.
