# Market Wrap — 20 August 2026

**TL;DR**
- Indian benchmark indices, Nifty 50 and Bank Nifty, closed higher today, rebounding after recent losses.
- Broad market participation was seen, with Midcap and Smallcap indices also ending in the green.
- Balrampur Chini Mills Ltd. was the top gainer in the Nifty 500, surging nearly 18%.
- Himadri Speciality Chemical Ltd. was the top loser, declining over 6%.
- Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) continued their strong buying, supported by FIIs turning net buyers today.
- India VIX cooled off significantly, indicating a decrease in market volatility.
- F&O positioning data for today is unavailable and will populate from the next session.

## How the market behaved today
Indian equities witnessed a positive session today, with the Nifty 50 closing above the 24,200 mark and the Bank Nifty also ending in the green. The market opened with a slight gap-up and maintained an upward trajectory throughout the day, with both benchmarks closing near their day's highs.

| Index | Close | Change | High | Low |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nifty 50 | 24231.85 | +0.64% | 24265.15 | 24184.55 |
| Bank Nifty | 57495.90 | +0.45% | 57702.95 | 57431.80 |
| Midcap 100 | 63671.55 | +0.41% | 63938.85 | 63631.70 |
| Smallcap 100 | 19841.45 | +0.68% | 19919.40 | 19810.75 |

The India VIX, a measure of market volatility, dropped by 4.95% to 10.76, suggesting a reduction in investor anxiety.

## What moved the market — the factors
The Indian market staged a healthy rebound today, primarily driven by strong buying interest from Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) who infused ₹2,579 crore into equities. This was complemented by Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) turning net buyers with an inflow of ₹1,652 crore, providing a significant boost to sentiment after a mixed trend in recent sessions. The positive market breadth, with advances outnumbering declines, indicated broad-based participation. Sector-specific strength was observed in Media, Realty, and FMCG, which contributed to the upward momentum. The overall positive sentiment also stemmed from the market bouncing back after seven consecutive days of losses, as highlighted by Livemint.

## Sectors — winners and losers
The day saw a clear preference for certain sectors, while others lagged.

**Top 3 gainer sectors:**
- **MEDIA:** +2.13% - This sector showed strong upward momentum today.
- **REALTY:** +1.41% - The realty sector saw significant gains, potentially buoyed by reports of robust commercial demand.
- **FMCG:** +0.82% - Fast Moving Consumer Goods stocks also performed well, with Balrampur Chini Mills being a standout performer.

**Top 3 loser sectors:**
- **PSU BANK:** -0.01% - Public Sector Banks ended marginally in the red.
- **ENERGY:** +0.07% - The Energy sector closed with minimal gains, effectively flat for the day.
- **METAL:** +0.28% - Metal stocks also saw muted positive movement.

## News that moved stocks
- **Jefferies favours two-wheeler stocks over four-wheeler stocks as earnings gap widens**
  Jefferies has expressed a preference for two-wheeler stocks like TVS Motor Company and Eicher Motors over passenger vehicle makers, citing stronger volume growth and resilient margins. This outlook could influence investment decisions in the auto sector.
- **BSE shares fall over 3% from day’s high after report says NSE weighs trading shares on own platform**
  Shares of BSE declined following a report suggesting that NSE might seek permission to trade its own shares on its platform after its proposed IPO listing on BSE. This news created uncertainty for BSE's stock.
- **Indian realty market remains divided amid affordability pressures and GCC-led commercial demand**
  The commercial real estate sector in India is experiencing strong demand for leasing, particularly from Global Capability Centres (GCCs), leading to a decline in vacancy rates. This positive sentiment likely contributed to the gains seen in realty stocks like Brigade Enterprises.
- **Axis Direct recommends power & utility stocks to buy after Q1; infra stocks also in focus**
  Axis Direct has recommended buying power and utility stocks such as NTPC, Skipper, and JSW Energy, citing strong capacity additions and order books. Additionally, they highlighted infra stocks like Kalpataru Projects, GR Infraprojects, and RITES after their Q1FY27 results. These recommendations could drive investor interest in these specific companies and sectors.

## Market breadth
The market breadth was positive today, with 303 advances against 192 declines out of a total of 495 traded stocks. This indicates that the upward movement was fairly broad-based across the market.

## FII / DII flows
Today, both Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) and Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) were net buyers in the Indian equity market.

| Investor Type | Net Flow (₹ crore) |
|---|---|
| FII | +1,652 |
| DII | +2,579 |

Looking at the 5-day trend, DIIs have been consistent net buyers, injecting significant capital into the market every day. FII flows have been mixed, with two days of net selling and two days of net buying in the last five sessions, but today they turned net positive, contributing to the market's strength. This consistent domestic support, coupled with FII buying today, provided a strong foundation for the market's rebound.

## Event breakdown — what happened today and how it hit the market
Today's session was primarily driven by broad-based positive flows and a rebound in sentiment after a period of consolidation. There was no single defining event like a major policy announcement or economic data release that singularly dictated market direction. Instead, the market reacted positively to the combined buying efforts of both FIIs and DIIs, coupled with sector-specific strength in areas like Media, Realty, and FMCG. The cooling off of India VIX also suggested a return of confidence among investors.

## Setup for tomorrow
Looking ahead to tomorrow, global cues will play a role. The US 10-year yield has shown a slight dip, while crude oil prices (Brent and WTI) have seen an increase overnight. Gold and Silver also moved higher. The Dollar Index and USD/INR have seen minor declines. Investors will continue to monitor global economic indicators and any further commentary from central banks. Domestically, the momentum from today's session, coupled with sustained DII buying, could provide support. Sectors like Realty and FMCG, which performed well today, might remain in focus, along with stocks highlighted by research houses.
