# Market Wrap — 17 June 2026

**TL;DR**
- Indian benchmark indices, Nifty 50 and Bank Nifty, closed higher, extending gains for the day.
- Broader markets, represented by Nifty Midcap 100 and Smallcap 100, outperformed the frontline indices.
- PSU Bank, Metal, and IT sectors led the gains, while Auto, Realty, and FMCG sectors saw declines.
- IDBI Bank and UCO Bank were among the top Nifty 500 gainers, while Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles was a significant loser.
- Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) were net sellers for the day, while Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) remained neutral.

## What moved markets today

**Anticipation Builds Ahead of US Fed Meeting**
Global markets, including India, remained watchful ahead of the US Federal Reserve's first interest rate decision under new Chair Kevin Warsh. US stock indexes opened higher, with chip stocks rebounding, as investors awaited the Fed's policy decision, which is widely expected to keep the federal funds target rate steady. This global cue influenced investor sentiment in India, leading to some caution and a pause in the rally of Indian government bonds.

**Indian Brokerages Get Nod for US Stock Investments via GIFT City**
Indian retail investors may soon find it easier to invest in US stocks as Zerodha, Groww, Angel One, and Upstox have secured regulatory approvals from GIFT City’s IFSCA. These licenses will enable the brokerages to facilitate investments in US equities, potentially opening new avenues for diversification for Indian investors.

**BRND.ME and Manipal Hospitals Eye IPOs**
BRND.ME, formerly Mensa Brands, has converted into a public company and is preparing for a potential IPO within the next 12-18 months. Separately, Manipal Health Enterprises is reportedly planning a $1 billion IPO as early as July, targeting a valuation of around $10 billion, having completed investor roadshows. These developments indicate a robust pipeline for public listings in the Indian market.

**SEBI Cautions Against Unlisted Securities Trading**
The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) issued a warning to investors against trading unlisted securities through unauthorized websites and electronic platforms. The regulator highlighted that such platforms operate outside its oversight, leaving investors without regulatory recourse, underscoring the importance of regulated channels for investments.

## Setup for tomorrow

Tomorrow's session will likely be influenced by the outcome of the US Federal Reserve's policy decision, which is expected overnight. While US stock futures edged higher today ahead of the Fed announcement, any surprises in the Fed's commentary could impact global sentiment. Brent Crude prices saw a modest increase today, which could be a factor for oil-sensitive sectors. Investors will also be watching for any further developments on the IPO front, with Turtlemint Fintech Solutions' IPO set to open on Friday.
