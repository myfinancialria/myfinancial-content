## What this means in plain terms

June is often a pivotal month for personal finance in India, marked by several important deadlines and policy announcements that can directly influence your taxes, investments, and even your monthly household budget. For those earning ₹15 lakhs or more annually, whether salaried or self-employed, understanding these events is not just about compliance; it's about smart financial management. This month, you'll need to keep an eye on advance tax instalments, potential shifts in interest rates decided by the RBI, and any new guidelines from SEBI that could impact your investment portfolio. Missing deadlines can lead to penalties, while staying informed about policy changes allows you to adapt your financial strategy for better returns and savings.

## Advance Tax Deadlines: Don't Miss the First Instalment

For many taxpayers, June marks the deadline for the first instalment of advance tax for the financial year 2026-27. Advance tax is essentially paying a part of your income tax liability in advance, rather than at the end of the financial year. This system applies if your estimated tax liability for the year, after deducting TDS (Tax Deducted at Source), is ₹10,000 or more.

### Who needs to pay advance tax?

While salaried individuals often have their taxes deducted at source (TDS) by their employer, those with significant income from other sources – such as business profits, professional fees, capital gains, rental income, or interest income – are usually liable to pay advance tax. Self-employed professionals, freelancers, and businesses are particularly impacted. Even salaried individuals with substantial other income not covered by TDS may need to pay. Senior citizens (60 years or above) not having income from business or profession are exempt from paying advance tax.

### Calculating your advance tax liability

The first instalment, due by June 15th, requires you to pay 15% of your total estimated tax liability for the financial year. To calculate this, you need to estimate your total income for the year, factor in all eligible deductions and exemptions (like Section 80C, 80D, HRA, etc.), and then apply the applicable income tax slab rates. From this estimated tax, you subtract any TDS that is expected to be deducted. The remaining amount, if ₹10,000 or more, becomes your advance tax liability, with 15% due by June 15th. It's crucial to make a realistic estimate to avoid underpayment penalties later. You can use the e-pay tax facility on the Income Tax India portal to make payments.

## RBI Monetary Policy Review: Interest Rates and Your Loans

The Reserve Bank of India's (RBI) Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) typically meets every two months to review the economic situation and decide on key interest rates. A meeting is usually scheduled in June, and its outcome can have far-reaching implications for your personal finances, especially if you have loans or significant savings.

### Understanding the MPC's role

The MPC is a six-member committee, with three members nominated by the RBI and three by the Central Government. Their primary objective is to maintain price stability while keeping in mind the objective of growth. They do this by setting the policy repo rate, which is the rate at which the RBI lends money to commercial banks. Changes in the repo rate influence other interest rates across the economy.

### How rate changes affect you

If the MPC decides to increase the repo rate, commercial banks typically follow suit by increasing their lending rates. This means your home loan EMIs (Equated Monthly Instalments) linked to external benchmarks (like the repo rate) or MCLR (Marginal Cost of Funds-based Lending Rate) could go up, making your loans more expensive. Conversely, a rate cut could lead to lower EMIs. For savers, a hike might mean better interest rates on fixed deposits (FDs) and recurring deposits (RDs), while a cut could lead to reduced returns. Changes also impact bond markets and debt mutual funds, as bond prices generally move inversely to interest rates.

## SEBI Regulations and Investor Protections

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) is the regulator for the securities market in India. It constantly introduces new regulations or modifies existing ones to enhance investor protection and ensure market integrity. June could see new circulars or guidelines that directly affect how you invest in mutual funds, stocks, and other market-linked instruments.

### New rules for mutual funds

SEBI often issues directives concerning mutual funds, ranging from disclosure norms to investment limits and fee structures. For instance, there might be updates on how fund houses categorise schemes, calculate Net Asset Values (NAVs), or manage risks. Any new rule related to expense ratios could impact the returns you get from your mutual fund investments. Investors should regularly check AMFI India and SEBI websites for the latest updates.

### Impact on stock market investors

For direct equity investors, SEBI's regulations can cover aspects like trading margins, disclosure requirements for listed companies, insider trading rules, and protection against market manipulation. Changes in these areas can affect trading strategies, market liquidity, and overall investor confidence. For example, stricter disclosure norms can lead to more transparent company financials, aiding better investment decisions.

## GST Filings and Business Compliance

For self-employed individuals, freelancers, and small business owners in our audience, Goods and Services Tax (GST) compliance is a recurring monthly event. June brings deadlines for various GST filings, which are critical to avoid penalties and maintain good standing with tax authorities.

### Key GST deadlines for businesses

Typically, the 11th of each month is the deadline for filing GSTR-1 (details of outward supplies) for taxpayers with aggregate turnover above ₹5 crores or those who have opted for monthly filing. The 13th is for the Invoice Furnishing Facility (IFF) for quarterly filers. The 20th is the deadline for GSTR-3B (summary of outward supplies, input tax credit, and tax payment) for monthly filers. For those opting for the Quarterly Return Filing and Monthly Payment of Taxes (QRMP) scheme, the 22nd or 24th of the month following the quarter is for GSTR-3B.

### Ensuring timely compliance

Missing GST deadlines can lead to late fees and interest charges. It's essential to reconcile your sales and purchase data, ensure all invoices are properly recorded, and have the correct Input Tax Credit (ITC) claimed. Using reliable accounting software and, if necessary, consulting a tax professional can help streamline the process and ensure accuracy. Timely compliance not only avoids penalties but also ensures smooth business operations and proper credit flow.

## FAQ

### What is advance tax?
Advance tax is the income tax paid in advance during the financial year itself, rather than as a lump sum at the year-end. It applies if your estimated tax liability, after TDS, is ₹10,000 or more.

### How often does the RBI Monetary Policy Committee meet?
The RBI's Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) is mandated to meet at least four times a year, but typically meets six times a year, i.e., bi-monthly.

### What is the role of SEBI?
SEBI (Securities and Exchange Board of India) is the regulatory body for the securities market in India. Its primary role is to protect the interests of investors in securities and to promote the development of, and to regulate, the securities market.

### Will new SEBI regulations affect my existing mutual fund investments?
Yes, new SEBI regulations can affect existing mutual fund investments, especially concerning aspects like expense ratios, disclosure norms, or scheme categorisation, which can indirectly impact your returns or risk profile.

### What are the consequences of missing GST filing deadlines?
Missing GST filing deadlines can lead to late fees and interest on the unpaid tax amount. Persistent non-compliance can also result in penalties and legal action from tax authorities.

### Where can I find official information on income tax deadlines?
Official information on income tax deadlines and regulations can be found on the Income Tax Department's official website, incometax.gov.in.

### How does a repo rate change impact home loan EMIs?
When the RBI increases the repo rate, banks typically increase their lending rates, which can lead to higher EMIs for home loans linked to external benchmarks or MCLR. Conversely, a rate cut can reduce EMIs.
