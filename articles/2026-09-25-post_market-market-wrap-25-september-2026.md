# Market Wrap — 25 September 2026

**TL;DR**
- Indian benchmark indices, Nifty 50 and Bank Nifty, ended the session with modest gains.
- Global cues, particularly optimism around AI in US markets, provided a positive undertone.
- Whirlpool of India Ltd. emerged as the top Nifty 500 gainer, while Ola Electric Mobility Ltd. was the biggest loser.
- Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) were net sellers, while Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) provided strong buying support.
- Realty and Auto sectors led the gains, while Media and IT faced some selling pressure.
- F&O positioning data is unavailable for this session and will be populated from the next wrap.

## How the market behaved today
Indian equity benchmarks closed higher today, with the Nifty 50 advancing by 0.34% to settle at 23,140.50, after trading in a narrow range for most of the session. The Bank Nifty also saw a positive close, gaining 0.26% to end at 55,580.40. Both indices opened with a slight gap up and maintained their positive momentum, albeit with limited volatility. The broader markets, however, presented a mixed picture, with Midcap 100 declining while Smallcap 100 registered marginal gains.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nifty 50 | 23140.50 | +0.34% | 23162.70 | 23020.95 |
| Bank Nifty | 55580.40 | +0.26% | 55762.60 | 55373.75 |
| Midcap 100 | 60906.00 | -0.14% | 61117.45 | 60595.45 |
| Smallcap 100 | 19715.25 | +0.15% | 19754.00 | 19594.90 |

India VIX, the volatility gauge, eased by 4.18% to 12.16, indicating a reduction in market apprehension for the day.

## What moved the market — the factors
Today's market movement was primarily influenced by a combination of global cues and domestic institutional buying. US stocks traded higher overnight and continued their positive momentum, driven by optimism in artificial intelligence, which helped ease worries over higher oil prices and rising Treasury yields. This positive sentiment from Wall Street provided a supportive backdrop for Indian equities.

Domestically, while Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) continued their selling spree, Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) stepped in with significant buying, cushioning the market's fall and helping the benchmarks close in the green. Sectoral rotation was also evident, with Realty, Auto, and FMCG sectors performing well, while Media, IT, and Pharma saw some profit-booking. The decline in India VIX also suggested a calmer trading environment.

## News that moved stocks
-   **IPO Activity Remains Robust:** Several companies are in various stages of their Initial Public Offerings (IPOs). Liqvd Digital India's IPO was subscribed 5.08 times on its final day, indicating strong investor interest. Data center operator DayOne is pushing ahead with its IPO plans, while lab-grown diamond manufacturer Anjali LabTech re-submitted papers for a ₹1,225 crore IPO. Kian-M Export also filed IPO papers to raise ₹500 crore. This continuous stream of IPOs reflects ongoing primary market activity.
-   **Regulatory Actions by SEBI:** The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) banned Omaxe and five other entities for violating minimum public shareholding norms. Such actions underscore SEBI's commitment to market compliance and governance.
-   **Adani Group Block Deal:** Adani Group promoter entities swapped 86 lakh shares of Adani Enterprises in a significant block deal worth ₹2,498 crore. This internal transaction can influence the stock's trading dynamics.
-   **US Federal Reserve and Banking Oversight:** The US Federal Reserve plans to propose an increase in asset thresholds for banking regulations, potentially impacting the US financial sector. While not directly affecting Indian banks, such moves from global central banks are closely watched for broader market sentiment.
-   **Microsoft's AI Push Boosts US Markets:** Microsoft shares jumped 4% after a major Copilot overhaul aimed at office workers, enhancing AI capabilities. This AI enthusiasm contributed to higher US stock market openings, providing a positive global cue for Indian markets.
-   **Embassy REIT Refinances Debt:** Embassy Office Parks REIT raised ₹1,000 crore via non-convertible debentures to refinance existing debts. This move strengthens the REIT's financial position and can be positive for the real estate sector.

## Market breadth
The market breadth was slightly negative today, with 243 advances against 255 declines out of a total of 498 traded stocks. This indicates that while benchmark indices closed higher, the broader market saw a nearly even distribution of gaining and losing stocks, suggesting a lack of broad-based participation in the upward move.

## FII / DII flows
Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) were net sellers in the Indian equity market today, offloading shares worth ₹5,027 crore. In contrast, Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) provided strong support, with net purchases amounting to ₹4,301 crore.

| Investor Type | Net Flow (₹ crore) |
|---|---|
| FII | -5,027 |
| DII | 4,301 |

Over the last five trading sessions, FIIs have largely been net sellers, with significant outflows on three days (₹-5,027 cr, ₹-3,810 cr, ₹-576 cr) and modest inflows on two days (₹+1,617 cr, ₹+600 cr). DIIs, on the other hand, have been consistent net buyers throughout this period, providing crucial support to the market. This trend suggests that domestic money is actively absorbing the selling pressure from foreign investors.

## Event breakdown — what happened today and how it hit the market
Today's session was primarily driven by broad flows and global cues rather than a single defining domestic event. The positive sentiment from US markets, fueled by optimism around AI, played a significant role in lifting Indian benchmarks. The consistent buying by Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) also acted as a strong counter-balance to the selling by Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs), preventing a sharper decline and helping the Nifty and Bank Nifty close in positive territory. Sector-specific news, such as Embassy REIT's debt refinancing, provided some tailwinds to the Realty sector, while regulatory actions by SEBI highlighted ongoing governance efforts. Overall, it was a day where global sentiment and domestic liquidity dictated the market's direction.

## Setup for tomorrow
Looking ahead to tomorrow, global cues will continue to be important. US markets closed higher today, driven by AI enthusiasm. Asian markets will likely take cues from this overnight performance. Key global indicators to watch include the Dollar Index (currently at 101.01, down 0.28%) and Brent Crude prices (at $99.48, down 6.68%), which could influence sentiment. Domestically, the continued FII selling versus DII buying trend will be crucial. Traders will also monitor the performance of sectors like Realty and Auto, which showed strength today, and watch for any further developments in the ongoing IPO pipeline.
