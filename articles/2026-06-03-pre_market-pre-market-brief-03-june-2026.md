# Pre-Market Brief — 03 June 2026

**TL;DR**
*   Global markets presented a mixed picture, with US indices closing positive, while Japan's Nikkei surged and Hong Kong's Hang Seng declined.
*   Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) continued their selling spree, offloading ₹8,363 crore, countered by robust Domestic Institutional Investor (DII) buying of ₹9,589 crore.
*   Crude oil prices saw a significant jump, and the Indian Rupee weakened against the US Dollar, potentially impacting import costs and inflation.
*   Indian banking stocks experienced a notable downturn yesterday, with the Nifty Bank index tumbling and specific lenders facing declines amid fresh concerns.
*   The Reserve Bank of India's Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) meeting begins today, with market participants closely watching for any cues on interest rates.

## Currency, commodities and yields

The **USD/INR** pair closed at ₹95.79, up 0.25%, indicating a weakening Rupee against the US Dollar. A depreciating Rupee makes imports more expensive and could contribute to inflationary pressures in India. The **Dollar Index** also saw a slight uptick, rising 0.09% to 99.31, reflecting a broader strengthening of the US Dollar.

In the commodities market, crude oil prices surged significantly. **Brent Crude** was up 1.78% at $97.71 per barrel, and **WTI Crude** climbed 1.93% to $95.57 per barrel. This sharp increase in oil prices is a concern for India, a major oil importer, as it could inflate the country's import bill and exacerbate inflation. Gold saw a minor gain, with **Gold** at $4496.00, up 0.15%, often considered a safe-haven asset. Silver, however, dipped slightly by 0.80% to $74.71.

The **US 10-Year Yield** decreased by 0.45% to 4.45%. A fall in US bond yields can sometimes signal a flight to safety or expectations of a less aggressive monetary policy stance by the US Federal Reserve, but this move was relatively minor.

## Overnight news that matters

**Nifty Bank tumbles 600 points; IndusInd Bank, Yes Bank, SBI and other stocks fall up to 3%. What lies ahead?**
Indian banking stocks experienced a significant downturn yesterday, with the Nifty Bank index dropping over 1.2%, and specific banks like IndusInd Bank seeing steeper declines. This selloff was partly attributed to a fresh whistleblower complaint against IndusInd Bank. This matters to Indian markets as the banking sector holds substantial weight in benchmark indices, and continued weakness could drag overall market sentiment.

**Rupee could fall to 98/USD by July amid energy crisis, BofA Securities exec says**
The Indian Rupee faces a potential record low of 98 against the dollar by July, according to a BofA Securities executive, primarily due to the Middle East energy shock impacting domestic assets. This forecast is significant for Indian markets as a depreciating Rupee makes imports, especially crude oil, more expensive, potentially fueling inflation and widening the current account deficit.

**AI investment boom reshaping global capital flows, leaving India temporarily on the sidelines: Jonathan Garner**
Foreign investors are reportedly shifting capital from India to North Asian markets like South Korea and Taiwan, driven by the AI investment boom and extraordinary earnings growth in those regions. This trend is crucial for Indian markets as it helps explain the persistent FII outflows and suggests a potential structural shift in global capital allocation away from emerging markets like India in the short term.

**Government denies new report stating RBI may have sold $12 billion gold to rescue rupee**
The Indian government has denied reports claiming the Reserve Bank of India sold gold to support the Rupee, stating that official data shows India's gold reserves have actually increased. This clarification is important for maintaining investor confidence in the RBI's management of foreign exchange reserves and its ability to defend the Rupee without resorting to drastic measures.

**RBI MPC meeting begins today: Will RBI announce a rate hike as Middle East conflict threatens to drive inflation?**
The Reserve Bank of India's Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) meeting commenced today, with market participants keenly awaiting its outcome. While some concerns about inflation persist due to the Middle East conflict, expectations are that the central bank is unlikely to announce a rate hike immediately, possibly opting for a step-by-step approach. The MPC's stance on interest rates and inflation will be a key determinant for market direction and economic outlook.

## What to watch in today's session

1.  **RBI MPC Meeting Outcome**: The central bank's commentary on inflation, growth, and its forward guidance on interest rates will be the most significant event for the day.
2.  **Banking Sector Performance**: Following yesterday's sharp decline, particularly in IndusInd Bank due to a whistleblower complaint, the banking sector's ability to recover or any further weakness will be closely monitored.
3.  **Crude Oil Price Movement**: The sustained rise in Brent and WTI crude oil prices will continue to be a critical factor, impacting India's economy and inflation outlook.
4.  **FII/DII Flow Dynamics**: The ongoing trend of FII selling being cushioned by DII buying will remain crucial in determining market sentiment and direction.
5.  **Rupee-Dollar Trajectory**: Any further significant depreciation of the Indian Rupee against the US Dollar will be watched closely for its implications on import costs and overall economic stability.

## Bottom line

The Indian market is poised for a cautious opening, influenced by continued foreign institutional outflows and the significant rise in global crude oil prices. While domestic institutional investors have consistently provided support, the recent weakness in the banking sector and concerns surrounding the Rupee's depreciation could weigh on sentiment. Investors should pay close attention to the outcome of the RBI's MPC meeting and global commodity price movements for cues today.
