# Market Wrap — 16 July 2026

**TL;DR**
- Indian benchmark indices, Nifty 50 and Bank Nifty, closed largely flat to marginally negative today.
- Geopolitical concerns, specifically the Iran-US conflict, kept market optimism in check.
- Mangalore Refinery & Petrochemicals Ltd. (MRPL) emerged as the top gainer among Nifty 500 stocks.
- ICICI Lombard General Insurance Company Ltd. (ICICIGI) was the biggest loser, falling over 10%.
- Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) continued their selling streak, while Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) provided support.
- F&O positioning data is unavailable for today's wrap and will be included from the next session.

## How the market behaved today
Indian equity markets witnessed a largely flat to negative session today, with the Nifty 50 hovering around the 24,050 mark. The day saw a range-bound movement, with indices struggling to gain significant traction amidst mixed cues.

## What moved the market — the factors
The Indian market today was largely influenced by a cautious sentiment stemming from the ongoing Iran-US conflict, which kept broader optimism in check. Despite this, some positive global cues, such as easing US rate-hike fears and stable oil prices, provided a floor for Indian government bonds.

Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) remained net sellers, offloading ₹736 crore from the cash segment, continuing a trend of net outflows over the past few sessions. Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs), however, provided crucial support by net buying ₹705 crore, preventing a sharper decline.

Sectoral action was mixed. The IT sector saw gains, buoyed by positive Q1 results from companies like Tech Mahindra and Wipro, which declared an interim dividend. Conversely, the Realty and Financial Services sectors witnessed selling pressure, with stocks like Signatureglobal and ICICI Lombard General Insurance among the top losers. Heavyweight stock movements, particularly in the IT space, contributed to the Nifty's resilience, while broader markets, including Midcap and Smallcap indices, saw slight declines.

## News that moved stocks

**Q1 Earnings Season in Focus**
- **BHEL shares jump 4% after Maharatna PSU posts net profit of Rs 377 crore in Q1, revenue jumps 40%**: Bharat Heavy Electricals Ltd. (BHEL) saw its shares rise significantly after reporting a consolidated net profit of ₹377 crore for Q1 FY27, a turnaround from a year-ago loss, driven by a 40% revenue growth. This positive news directly impacted BHEL's stock.
- **Tech Mahindra Q1 Results: Net profit rises 28% YoY to Rs 1,465 crore, revenue up 18%**: Tech Mahindra announced a strong consolidated net profit of ₹1,465 crore for the first quarter, marking a 28% year-on-year increase, with revenue also growing by 18%. This positive earnings report contributed to the IT sector's overall gains.
- **Wipro Q1 Results: Profit flat YoY at Rs 3,352 crore; co declares Rs 2/share dividend**: Wipro reported a modest 1% year-on-year rise in its June-quarter net profit to ₹3,352 crore and declared an interim dividend of ₹2 per share. While profit growth was flat, the dividend declaration and overall IT sector sentiment provided some support to the stock.
- **Reliance Q1 Results Preview: O2C, Jio to drive earnings - Key things to watch out for**: Ahead of its Q1 FY27 results announcement tomorrow, Reliance Industries is anticipated to see consolidated growth, with the O2C segment and Jio expected to be key drivers. This news sets expectations for a major index-heavy stock.

**Global Market & Economic Cues**
- **Indian bonds rise on steady oil, easing US rate-hike fears**: Indian government bonds saw an upturn today as global oil prices stabilized and expectations for further US rate hikes eased, improving investor confidence in the bond market.
- **Global Market: TSMC to invest additional $100 billion in Arizona, raises capex on booming AI demand**: Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (TSMC) announced a massive additional investment in its Arizona facilities and raised its capital expenditure, signaling strong confidence in future artificial intelligence demand. This news highlights the robust global tech outlook.
- **Rupee closes at 96.34 vs USD; firmer Asia FX, oil dip offer little comfort as NDF maturities pinch**: The Indian rupee experienced a slight decline against the dollar, primarily due to dollar demand linked to expiring non-deliverable forward contracts, despite some firmer Asian currencies and a dip in oil prices.

**Market Sentiment & Investment Philosophy**
- **Warren Buffett issues new warning, says tough to find value in market when everybody prefers gambling**: Legendary investor Warren Buffett warned that value investing is becoming increasingly challenging as speculative trading gains popularity, suggesting a market environment driven more by "gambling" than fundamental value. This reflects broader market sentiment concerns.
- **Larry Page, Sergey Brin get $15 billion richer as Warren Buffett says Alphabet bet was his call**: Warren Buffett's revelation that he personally initiated Berkshire Hathaway’s investment in Alphabet led to a nearly 4% jump in Alphabet's stock, adding significant wealth to its co-founders. This news underscores the impact of major investor endorsements.

## FII / DII flows
Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) were net sellers in the Indian equity market today, offloading ₹736 crore. Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs), however, provided strong counter-support, with net purchases of ₹705 crore.

| Investor Type | Net Flow (₹ crore) |
|---|---|
| FII | -736 |
| DII | 705 |

Looking at the 5-day history, FIIs have been net sellers for four out of the last five sessions, with a significant outflow of ₹3,062 crore on July 13. In contrast, DIIs have been consistent net buyers throughout this period, absorbing the FII selling pressure and providing a crucial cushion to the market. This trend suggests domestic investors are finding value in the market despite foreign outflows.

## Event breakdown — what happened today and how it hit the market
Today's session was primarily driven by a combination of global geopolitical concerns and ongoing Q1 earnings. The lingering "Iran-US conflict" acted as a dampener on overall market sentiment, preventing any significant upward momentum.

However, the earnings season provided specific stock-level catalysts. BHEL saw a strong rally after reporting a turnaround to net profit and robust revenue growth in Q1 FY27. Similarly, Tech Mahindra's impressive 28% year-on-year profit increase in Q1 FY27 boosted the IT sector. Wipro also declared an interim dividend alongside its Q1 results, which, despite flat profit growth, contributed to the positive sentiment in the IT space.

On the other hand, the Realty sector experienced weakness, with Signatureglobal among the top losers, while financial services stocks like ICICI Lombard General Insurance also faced selling pressure. The market's overall range-bound movement suggests a tug-of-war between these specific stock and sector-driven moves and broader cautious sentiment.

## Setup for tomorrow
As we head into Friday, the market will likely continue to monitor global cues, including the performance of US and Asian markets overnight. The US 10-year yield closing lower at 4.55% and stable crude oil prices (Brent at $85.10, WTI at $79.97) could offer some support. Key focus tomorrow will be on the Q1 FY27 results of Reliance Industries, an index heavyweight, which are expected to be driven by its O2C and Jio segments. Traders will also keep an eye on the 24,050 level for Nifty 50 for immediate support and resistance.
