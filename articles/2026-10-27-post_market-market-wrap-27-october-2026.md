## What moved the market — the factors
The Indian market today was largely influenced by a combination of global factors and institutional flows. The benchmark indices, Nifty and Sensex, closed marginally lower, primarily due to elevated crude oil prices and ongoing uncertainty surrounding the US Federal Reserve's two-day meeting, which began today. Markets are widely expecting the Fed to hold interest rates steady.

Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) continued their selling streak, offloading ₹1,462 crore from the cash market. This selling pressure was largely absorbed by Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs), who were net buyers to the tune of ₹2,610 crore, providing crucial support to the market. Despite the cautious sentiment at the index level, the strong performance of Midcap and Smallcap segments, which gained over 1% and 2% respectively, indicated a healthy appetite for broader market stocks. The market breadth also favoured bulls, suggesting that many individual stocks saw positive movement. Easing crude oil prices in the US session later in the day could offer some relief for tomorrow's trade.

## News that moved stocks
- **Tata Capital Q2 net profit rises 51% on higher income**
  Tata Capital reported a significant 51% year-on-year increase in its second-quarter net profit to ₹1,660 crore, driven by robust interest income and loan growth. This positive earnings report could boost sentiment for the company and potentially other financial services stocks.
- **Waaree Energies Q2 Results: Profit falls 15% YoY to Rs 719 crore, revenue rises 24%**
  Despite a 23.9% rise in revenue, Waaree Energies saw its Q2 FY27 profit decline by 14.7% year-on-year to ₹718.68 crore, primarily due to margin pressure from acquiring data centre subsidiaries. This mixed result could lead to cautious investor sentiment towards the stock.
- **US Fed opens two-day meeting with markets expecting rate hold**
  The US Federal Reserve commenced its two-day policy meeting today, with market participants largely anticipating a decision to hold interest rates steady. This event is a key global cue, and its outcome will significantly influence global markets, including India, tomorrow.
- **RIL to announce Q2 results on October 30, board to also consider fundraising**
  Reliance Industries is set to announce its September-quarter results on October 30, and its board will also evaluate fundraising options through non-convertible debentures. This upcoming event makes RIL a stock to watch, with its performance and fundraising plans potentially impacting the broader market given its heavy weightage.
- **JetBlue shares surge 12% as airline seeks to calm financial concerns**
  JetBlue Airways shares surged over 12% after the airline addressed financial concerns, despite reporting a larger quarterly loss influenced by high fuel prices. This news highlights the impact of company-specific updates and global commodity prices on stock performance.
- **OneSource Specialty Pharma shares jump 14% after Q2 results; recover 50% from recent lows**
  OneSource Specialty Pharma's shares climbed nearly 14% following strong Q2 results, which showed a 37% year-on-year revenue growth and an adjusted profit after tax of ₹93.7 crore. The company also maintained its FY28 targets, indicating strong investor confidence.
- **PVR Inox among stocks that hit 52-week highs and rallied up to 20% in a month**
  PVR Inox was among several BSE 500 stocks that touched fresh 52-week highs today, having rallied significantly in the past month. This suggests positive momentum for the entertainment sector and specific companies within it.
- **JSW Infrastructure Q2 Results: Net profit falls 11% to Rs 328 crore**
  JSW Infrastructure reported an 11% decline in consolidated net profit for the September quarter, falling to ₹328.23 crore from ₹368.81 crore last year. This result could lead to negative sentiment for the stock.
- **Sebi extends deadline for debenture trustees to comply with norms till April 2027**
  SEBI has granted a six-month extension, till April 27, 2027, for debenture trustees to comply with regulatory requirements. This extension provides more time for compliance and reduces immediate pressure on the entities involved.
- **Coca-Cola raises profit forecast as premium drinks, sodas hold strong**
  Coca-Cola has increased its annual profit forecast, citing robust demand for its range of beverages, especially zero-sugar options. This positive outlook from a global consumer giant can signal broader consumer spending trends.

## FII / DII flows
Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) were net sellers in the Indian equity market today, offloading shares worth ₹-1,462 crore. In contrast, Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) continued to be net buyers, injecting ₹2,610 crore into the market.

Over the last five trading sessions, FIIs have shown a mixed trend, being net sellers on three out of five days, including today. Their cumulative net outflow over these five days stands at ₹-5,160 crore. Conversely, DIIs have been consistent net buyers throughout this period, with a cumulative net inflow of ₹13,551 crore. This consistent DII support has been crucial in cushioning the market against FII selling pressure.

## Event breakdown — what happened today and how it hit the market
Today's session was largely driven by a combination of global cues and ongoing corporate earnings. The primary global event was the commencement of the US Federal Reserve's two-day meeting, with markets widely anticipating a decision to hold interest rates steady. This uncertainty, coupled with elevated crude oil prices (though easing later in the US session), contributed to the marginal decline in benchmark indices.

On the domestic front, corporate earnings continued to trickle in. Tata Capital reported a strong 51% rise in Q2 net profit, which could positively impact the financial services sector. However, Waaree Energies and JSW Infrastructure reported declines in their Q2 net profits, which might weigh on their respective stocks. OneSource Specialty Pharma, on the other hand, saw its shares jump significantly after strong Q2 results. The news of Reliance Industries considering fundraising alongside its upcoming Q2 results also kept investors watchful. The consistent buying by DIIs helped absorb FII selling, preventing a sharper market downturn.

## Setup for tomorrow
Looking ahead to tomorrow, global cues will remain critical, especially the outcome of the US Federal Reserve's policy meeting. US stocks saw an uptick today, with the S&P 500 and Nasdaq advancing as oil prices eased, which could provide a positive lead for Asian markets. Brent crude and WTI crude both closed lower, which is a favourable development for India. The Dollar Index strengthened slightly, and USD/INR also closed higher. Investors will closely watch the Fed's commentary for any signals on future monetary policy. Domestically, the continued FII and DII flow dynamics, crude oil trajectory, and any further corporate earnings announcements, particularly Reliance Industries' results on October 30, will be key factors influencing market direction.
