# Market Wrap — 27 May 2026

**TL;DR**
-   Indian benchmark indices, Nifty 50 and Bank Nifty, closed largely flat to negative, snapping a recent upward trend.
-   Broader markets, represented by the Nifty Midcap 100 and Smallcap 100, showed resilience, ending the day in positive territory.
-   Media, Energy, and Metal sectors emerged as top performers, while Banking, IT, and FMCG lagged.
-   Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) were net sellers for the fifth consecutive session, though Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) provided crucial support.
-   Global cues, including a significant drop in crude oil prices and continued optimism around AI in US markets, influenced sentiment.

## How the market closed

Indian equities witnessed a mixed trading session today, with the frontline Nifty 50 closing marginally lower by 0.03% at 23,907.15, while the Bank Nifty shed 0.43% to settle at 54,853.85. In contrast, the broader market indices displayed strength, with the Nifty Midcap 100 gaining 0.42% and the Nifty Smallcap 100 rising 0.15%.

The India VIX, a measure of market volatility, cooled down significantly, dropping 7.13% to 14.98, indicating a reduction in immediate market uncertainty.

## Sectors — winners and losers

Today's session saw a clear divergence in sectoral performance, with Media, Energy, and Metal leading the gains, while Banking, IT, and FMCG faced selling pressure.

**Top 3 Gainer Sectors:**
*   **MEDIA:** +3.05% - This sector saw strong buying interest, partly buoyed by specific stock movements like Zee Entertainment Enterprises Ltd.
*   **ENERGY:** +1.77% - The sector benefited from a significant drop in global crude oil prices, which typically reduces input costs for many energy companies.
*   **METAL:** +1.67% - Metal stocks witnessed a positive day, reflecting broader market sentiment in specific pockets.

**Top 3 Loser Sectors:**
*   **BANK:** -0.43% - The banking sector was the biggest drag on the market, primarily influenced by concerns surrounding a major private sector bank.
*   **IT:** -0.25% - The information technology sector continued to face headwinds, closing in the red.
*   **FMCG:** -0.17% - Fast-Moving Consumer Goods companies also ended the day with marginal losses.

## Market breadth

The market breadth leaned positive today, with 280 advances against 218 declines out of a total of 498 stocks. This indicates that while frontline indices were subdued, a larger number of individual stocks managed to close in the green, suggesting underlying strength in the broader market.

## FII / DII flows

Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) continued their selling spree, offloading Indian equities worth ₹2,408 crore today. This marks the fifth consecutive session of net outflows from FIIs. However, Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) provided strong counter-support, injecting ₹1,361 crore into the market, cushioning the impact of FII selling.

| Investor Type | Net Cash (₹ crore) |
| :------------ | :----------------- |
| FII           | -2,408             |
| DII           | +1,361             |

Over the past five trading sessions, FIIs have been consistent net sellers, with outflows totalling over ₹8,000 crore, while DIIs have consistently been net buyers, providing a crucial liquidity buffer to the Indian markets.

## What moved markets today

Several key developments influenced market sentiment and specific sectors today:

**Global Oil Prices Plunge on Mideast Hopes**
Oil prices dropped sharply by 5% today, with Brent crude falling below $95, on renewed hopes for progress in US-Iran talks aimed at de-escalating tensions and potentially reopening shipping through the Strait of Hormuz. This development significantly impacted energy stocks, benefiting some companies due to lower input costs, but leading to declines in upstream players like ONGC.

**Adani Group Companies Extend Rally, Recouping Hindenburg Losses**
Adani Group companies continued their strong upward momentum, with stocks like Adani Total Gas Ltd. (ATGL) seeing significant gains. The group has now recovered nearly $150 billion in market value lost after the 2023 Hindenburg report, driven by strong operational performance and easing investor concerns. This rally provided a boost to the Energy and Infrastructure sectors where Adani companies have a significant presence.

**HDFC Bank Shares Fall Amid Governance Concerns**
Shares of HDFC Bank, a heavyweight in the banking sector, declined today following a report alleging the bank internally investigated ₹45 crore in payments linked to MSRDC deposits. This news renewed investor concerns around governance standards and contributed to the overall weakness observed in the broader banking sector.

**Reliance Industries Ltd. Announces AGM Date**
Reliance Industries Ltd. (RIL) announced its 49th annual general meeting (AGM) date on June 19, 2026. Investors will be keenly watching for updates on the much-anticipated Jio IPO and other strategic announcements from the conglomerate, which could provide future direction for the market.

**Global AI Optimism Boosts Chipmakers**
Globally, the AI boom continues to drive enthusiasm, pushing chipmakers like Micron and SK Hynix past the $1 trillion market cap club. While this is primarily a US market phenomenon, it contributes to overall positive global sentiment towards technology and innovation, indirectly supporting investor confidence.

## Setup for tomorrow

Looking ahead to tomorrow's session, Indian markets will likely take cues from global developments overnight. The significant drop in Brent crude prices to $93.24 could continue to benefit sectors sensitive to oil, such as paints, airlines, and certain manufacturing industries, while potentially impacting upstream oil producers. US stock futures are pointing higher, extending a rally driven by AI optimism, which could provide a positive global backdrop.

Key global data releases or any further updates on the Middle East situation will be crucial. Domestically, the consistent FII selling remains a point of caution, though DII support has been robust. Investors should watch out for any further news related to the banking sector and the Adani Group's continued performance. The USD/INR closed higher at 95.68, indicating some pressure on the rupee.
