## What moved the market — the factors
The Indian equity market's downturn today was primarily influenced by rising crude oil prices and persistent geopolitical tensions in the Middle East, which dampened investor sentiment. Globally, US stocks experienced a choppy session as gains in chipmakers were offset by declines in megacap tech stocks, ahead of a key Federal Reserve meeting.

On the domestic front, Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) showed strong buying interest, pumping in a net ₹4,240 crore into Indian equities. This significant inflow, however, was partially counteracted by Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) who turned net sellers, pulling out ₹1,250 crore. This mixed flow indicated a cautious approach by domestic players.

Several companies announced their Q2 earnings, with positive results from Hero MotoCorp, Cholamandalam Investment, Satin Creditcare, and Vedanta Aluminium, which likely provided some support to their respective stocks despite the broader market weakness. Regulatory developments from SEBI, including a review of FPI compliance norms and an MoU with the Bank of England, also kept market participants engaged.

## News that moved stocks
- **Strong Q2 Earnings Boost Key Companies**
  Several companies reported robust second-quarter results. Hero MotoCorp's consolidated profit surged 41% to ₹1,861 crore, driven by strong scooter and EV sales. Cholamandalam Investment and Finance saw its net profit rise 41% to ₹1,630 crore on strong income growth and lower loan losses, with plans for a ₹5,000 crore capital raise. Satin Creditcare Network's Q2 profit more than tripled to ₹176 crore due to improved asset quality and portfolio growth. Vedanta Aluminium doubled its Q2 net profit to ₹6,187 crore, supported by record production and higher realisations. These positive earnings reports likely provided a boost to their respective stocks.

- **SEBI Initiatives to Enhance Market Oversight**
  The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) announced plans to review compliance requirements for Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPIs) in India, aiming to align them with global standards. Additionally, SEBI signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with the Bank of England to strengthen regulatory cooperation and information sharing concerning clearing corporations, which is expected to enhance cross-border supervision. These regulatory moves aim to improve market integrity and attract foreign investment.

- **Ola Electric's Rights Issue Fully Subscribed**
  Ola Electric's ₹1,000-crore rights issue was fully subscribed ahead of its closing date, leading to an 11% jump in its stock over the week and a 4.95% rise today. This indicates strong investor confidence in the company's growth prospects.

- **British Homebuilders Slump on Budget Uncertainty**
  Shares of British homebuilders plummeted following the UK finance minister's budget speech, which lacked clarity on the funding and launch of a crucial first-time buyer housing initiative. This highlights how policy uncertainty can significantly impact sector-specific stocks.

## Event breakdown — what happened today and how it hit the market
Today's market session was primarily driven by a combination of global cues and domestic earnings. The overarching sentiment was cautious due to rising crude oil prices and ongoing Middle East tensions, which led to a broad market decline.

However, several Q2 earnings announcements provided company-specific positive momentum. Hero MotoCorp, Cholamandalam Investment, Satin Creditcare, and Vedanta Aluminium all reported strong profit growth, which likely supported their stock performance and prevented a steeper market fall. The news of Ola Electric's fully subscribed rights issue also indicated strong investor confidence in the EV sector. Regulatory actions by SEBI, focusing on FPI compliance and international cooperation, aimed at strengthening market frameworks.

## Setup for tomorrow
Looking ahead, global cues will continue to play a significant role. While Brent crude saw a slight dip, WTI crude rose, and US 10-year yields edged higher. US markets closed choppy, indicating mixed sentiment. Investors will be watching for further developments in crude oil prices and geopolitical events. Domestically, the momentum from Q2 earnings will likely continue to influence individual stock movements.
