# Upcoming IPOs, Explained Simply — October 2026

**TL;DR**
- Three new Initial Public Offerings (IPOs) are opening for subscription this week: HD Fire Protect Limited, Vaibhav Vyapaar Limited, and Ravita Engineering Services Limited.
- You will learn what an IPO is, how companies offer their shares to the public, and how to understand key terms like price band and lot size.
- We will explain the step-by-step process of applying for an IPO and how shares are allotted.
- This guide aims to help you understand the numbers and the process, so you can make informed decisions.

## First, what is an IPO — in one minute?

Imagine a popular local bakery that has been doing very well. Now, the owner wants to open more branches and buy new ovens. To get the money needed for this expansion, the owner decides to sell a small part of the bakery itself to the public. This is what an **Initial Public Offering (IPO)** is.

An IPO is when a private company offers its shares to the public for the very first time. This is done in the **primary market** (where new shares are sold directly by the company). Once these shares are sold, they then get **listed** (start trading) on a stock exchange like the NSE or BSE. This allows anyone to buy or sell them later in the "secondary market" (the regular stock market).
