# Pre-Market Brief — 06 November 2026

**TL;DR**
*   Global markets saw a significant sell-off overnight, particularly in US tech and Asian indices.
*   GIFT Nifty is signalling a positive start for Indian markets, defying the global weakness.
*   Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) were strong net buyers yesterday, while Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) were net sellers.
*   Concerns over AI chip demand and high valuations for AI companies are driving a global tech sector correction.
*   Nifty 50's key pivot level for the day is 24759.93, with 25131.82 as immediate resistance and 24570.02 as support.
*   The primary risk today is the negative global sentiment, especially in technology, potentially overriding local positive cues.

## How global markets traded — and what it means for India
Global markets experienced a broad-based decline overnight, with US indices leading the fall amid concerns over AI infrastructure spending and valuations. The Nasdaq plummeted over 5.5%, while the Dow Jones and S&P 500 also saw significant corrections. Asian markets followed suit this morning, with the Nikkei 225 dropping nearly 6% and Hang Seng and Shanghai also closing lower. This widespread global weakness suggests a cautious opening for Indian equities, despite some domestic positive factors.
