# Pre-Market Brief — 27 July 2026

**TL;DR**
*   Global markets presented a mixed picture overnight, with US indices split and Asian markets showing varied performance.
*   GIFT Nifty is signalling a positive open for Indian equities, pointing to a gap-up.
*   Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) remained net sellers, while Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) provided strong buying support.
*   A significant drop in crude oil prices, driven by easing Middle East tensions, is a major positive for India.
*   Nifty 50's 24000 level is a crucial resistance to watch, with strong Call Open Interest there.
*   The continued FII selling trend remains a key risk factor for market direction.

## How global markets traded — and what it means for India
Global markets displayed a mixed performance overnight, with US indices closing on a split note. The Dow Jones and S&P 500 managed marginal gains, while the Nasdaq Composite saw a slight dip. In Asia, the Nikkei 225 experienced a notable decline, but the Hang Seng posted gains. China's Shanghai Composite closed lower, despite a strong debut by memory chipmaker CXMT Corp which lifted sentiment in the broader Chinese market. This mixed global sentiment suggests a cautious yet potentially positive start for Indian markets, especially with positive cues from GIFT Nifty.

## Currency, commodities and yields
The Indian Rupee strengthened against the US Dollar, with USD/INR trading at ₹96.21, down 0.69%. The Dollar Index also weakened by 0.28% to 101.19. A significant development was the sharp decline in crude oil prices, with Brent Crude falling 5.28% to $91.67 and WTI Crude down 5.49% to $84.41, primarily due to easing Middle East tensions. Gold saw a modest gain of 0.55% to $4090.00, while Silver was up 1.59% to $59.59. The US 10-year Treasury Yield rose by 0.99% to 4.70%.

**Interpretation:** The strengthening Rupee and weakening Dollar Index are generally positive for India, potentially easing import costs. The sharp fall in crude oil prices is a significant positive for the Indian economy, as it reduces import bills, inflationary pressures, and could benefit sectors like oil marketing companies (OMCs), paints, and aviation. However, the rise in US 10-year yields could make emerging markets, including India, less attractive to foreign investors, posing a risk to FII flows.

The GIFT Nifty is trading at 23957.50, up 0.81%.

**Interpretation:** This indicates a positive bias for the Indian market open, suggesting a potential gap-up for the Nifty 50 today.

Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) were net sellers in the cash market yesterday, offloading equities worth ₹3,893 crore. In contrast, Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) continued to provide strong support, emerging as net buyers with an inflow of ₹5,454 crore.

## Events & data to watch — and how markets usually react
*   **Crude Oil Price Movement:** The sharp decline in Brent and WTI crude prices due to easing Middle East tensions is a major event. Markets typically react positively to lower crude prices, as it reduces inflation concerns and improves the current account deficit for India. Continued stability or further declines would be beneficial.
*   **FII/DII Flow Trends:** The persistent FII selling and DII buying trend will be crucial to monitor. Markets tend to be volatile when FIIs are net sellers, but DII support often cushions the downside. A reversal in FII flows could provide a significant boost.
*   **Q1FY27 Earnings Impact:** While no Nifty 500 results are scheduled today, the market will continue to digest the impact of recent Q1 results from companies like Laurus Labs, Dr Lal Path Labs, and Zen Technologies. Strong results typically lead to stock-specific rallies, while weak results can cause declines.
*   **Corporate Actions:** A large number of companies going ex-dividend today will see their stock prices adjust. This is a routine event, but for individual shareholders, it's important to be aware of the ex-date to be eligible for the dividend.
*   **Global Market Sentiment:** Any further developments in global markets, particularly regarding US bond yields or geopolitical tensions, could influence investor sentiment in India.

## What to watch in today's session
1.  **Crude Oil Prices:** Monitor for any further movements in crude oil, as the recent sharp fall is a key positive driver.
2.  **Nifty 50's 24000 Level:** This level, with high Call OI and technical resistance, will be critical to watch for directional cues.
3.  **FII vs DII Flows:** Observe if FII selling persists and if DIIs continue to provide strong buying support.
4.  **Individual Stock Reactions:** Keep an eye on stocks like Laurus Labs, Dr Lal Path Labs, Sterlite Technologies (for upside) and Zen Technologies (for downside) based on their recent news and earnings.
5.  **Global Cues:** Any significant shifts in US or Asian markets could influence the domestic sentiment.
6.  **Corporate Actions:** Be aware of the numerous stocks trading ex-dividend today, which will see price adjustments.

## Bottom line
Indian markets are poised for a potentially positive open, largely buoyed by the sharp decline in crude oil prices and a positive signal from GIFT Nifty. However, persistent FII selling remains a factor to watch, with DIIs continuing to provide crucial domestic support. The Nifty 50's ability to sustain above the 23900 support and challenge the 24000 resistance will be key to determining the day's trajectory, amidst mixed global cues.
