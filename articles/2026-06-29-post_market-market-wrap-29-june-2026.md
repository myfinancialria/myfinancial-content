# Market Wrap — 29 June 2026

**TL;DR**
- Indian benchmark indices, Nifty 50 and Bank Nifty, closed in the red today.
- Broader markets, Nifty Midcap 100 and Smallcap 100, also saw declines.
- Volatility, as measured by India VIX, edged higher.
- Pharma, Metal, and Energy sectors were the top gainers, while Auto, Media, and IT were the biggest losers.
- Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) were net buyers, alongside strong buying from Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs).

## How the market closed

Indian equities ended the session on a negative note, with the Nifty 50 shedding 0.46% and the Bank Nifty declining by 0.77%. Broader markets also followed suit, indicating a cautious sentiment across the board.

| Index | Close | Change | High | Low |
| :---------------- | :---------- | :--------- | :---------- | :---------- |
| Nifty 50 | 23946.25 | -0.46% | 24120.00 | 23924.55 |
| Bank Nifty | 57727.35 | -0.77% | 58318.05 | 57637.25 |
| Midcap 100 | 61567.30 | -0.37% | 61890.55 | 61198.70 |
| Smallcap 100 | 18673.10 | -0.62% | 18797.30 | 18596.35 |

The India VIX, a measure of market volatility, rose by 4.29% to close at 13.61, suggesting an uptick in expected market swings.

## Sectors — winners and losers

Today's session saw a mixed sectoral performance, with defensive and commodity-linked sectors showing resilience.

**Top 3 Gainer Sectors:**
- **PHARMA:** +1.03%. The sector witnessed buying interest, with some mid-cap pharma stocks like Aurobindo Pharma hitting 52-week highs, signalling robust investor confidence in the healthcare space.
- **METAL:** +0.80%. The sector gained momentum, possibly buoyed by news of Eswari Global Metal Industries filing for a significant IPO, indicating growth prospects in the metals industry.
- **ENERGY:** +0.20%. Energy stocks managed to close in positive territory amidst overall market weakness.

**Top 3 Loser Sectors:**
- **AUTO:** -2.08%. The automobile sector faced significant selling pressure today, despite some individual players like Ather Energy showing strong gains. Bajaj Auto's upcoming share buyback might have influenced some movements within the sector.
- **MEDIA:** -1.32%. Media stocks experienced a notable decline, contributing to the broader market's negative close.
- **IT:** -1.07%. The Information Technology sector was among the top losers, dragged down by sharp corrections in several key stocks.

## Market breadth

The market breadth was decidedly negative today, with 327 declines significantly outweighing 172 advances out of a total of 499 stocks. This indicates that the selling pressure was broad-based and affected a larger number of stocks across the market.

## FII / DII flows

Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) were net buyers today, injecting ₹384 crore into the Indian equity market. Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) showed even stronger conviction, with net buying of ₹5,748 crore, providing crucial support to the market.

| Investor Type | Net Cash Position (₹ crore) |
| :------------ | :-------------------------- |
| FII | 384 |
| DII | 5,748 |

Looking at the 5-day history, FII flows have been mixed, with two days of net buying and three days of net selling. However, DIIs have been consistent net buyers throughout this period, indicating strong domestic institutional support for Indian equities.

## What moved markets today

Here are some of the key developments that influenced market sentiment today:

**Global Tensions Ease, Boosting Sentiment**
*   **US stocks open higher as US, Iran halt attacks.** Wall Street's main indexes opened higher today following reports of easing tensions in the Middle East, specifically a halt in hostilities between the U.S. and Iran. This positive global cue helped lift overall market sentiment, though Indian indices couldn't sustain gains.
*   **India bonds rise as US-Iran halt strikes; monsoon risk limits gains.** Indian bonds surged for the fifth consecutive session, with the 10-year yield falling to its lowest since March, primarily driven by the de-escalation of U.S.-Iran tensions and robust foreign investment.

**Strategic Investments and Fundraising**
*   **Himadri Speciality Chemicals bets big on EV battery materials with 100 GWh manufacturing facility.** Himadri Speciality Chemicals announced ambitious plans to establish India's largest battery materials manufacturing facility, projecting a revenue boost of ₹30,000 crore over five years. This significant investment highlights the growing focus on the electric vehicle ecosystem.
*   **Yes Bank to raise up to $1.7 billion via equity, debt issue.** Yes Bank's board has approved a plan to raise up to ₹160 billion (approximately $1.7 billion) through a combination of equity and debt offerings. This substantial fundraising initiative could strengthen the bank's capital base.
*   **Omaxe to invest Rs 6,200 cr to build 19 hotels in 5 states.** Real estate developer Omaxe announced a major investment of ₹6,200 crore over five years to develop 19 hotels across five states, signalling robust expansion plans in the hospitality sector.

**IT Sector Under Pressure**
*   **Persistent Systems and Netweb Technologies witness sharp declines.** The Information Technology sector saw significant selling pressure today, with major players like Persistent Systems and Netweb Technologies India recording double-digit percentage losses. This contributed to the IT sector being one of the top losers.

## Setup for tomorrow

With global tensions easing and US markets opening higher, Indian markets might see some positive spillover effects tomorrow. However, the negative market breadth and rising India VIX today suggest underlying caution. Domestic institutional buying remains a strong support, but FII flows will be crucial to watch.

Global cues indicate US stock futures are up, and crude oil prices have also risen. The USD/INR closed higher at 94.53. Investors will be keeping an eye on these global developments and any further news on corporate actions or macroeconomic data.
