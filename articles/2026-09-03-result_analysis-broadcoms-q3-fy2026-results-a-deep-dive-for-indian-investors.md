# Broadcom's Q3 FY2026 Results: A Deep Dive for Indian Investors

**TL;DR**
- Broadcom reported strong Q3 FY2026 results, surpassing revenue and adjusted EPS expectations.
- Revenue reached $29.6 billion, exceeding estimates, driven significantly by AI semiconductor demand.
- AI semiconductor revenue surged to $16.7 billion, demonstrating 221% year-over-year growth.
- The company also reported robust cash flow from operations at $14.2 billion and free cash flow of $13.7 billion.
- Despite strong numbers, the stock experienced a slight dip, indicating high investor expectations for future guidance.

## What this means in plain terms

When a company like Broadcom releases its quarterly results, it's like opening a report card for its business performance over the last three months. For investors, especially those in India looking at global tech giants, these numbers reveal how well the company is executing its strategy and what its future prospects might be. Broadcom's recent Q3 FY2026 earnings showed impressive growth, particularly in its AI (Artificial Intelligence) related semiconductor business. This means the company is successfully capitalising on the global boom in AI technology.

However, simply beating expectations isn't always enough to make the stock soar. Sometimes, investors have even higher hopes, and if the company's future outlook or "guidance" doesn't meet those elevated expectations, the stock might react negatively, even with strong current performance. Understanding these nuances helps Indian investors gauge not just the past, but also the potential future trajectory of their investments.

## Q3 FY2026 Performance: Exceeding Estimates

Broadcom Inc. (NASDAQ: AVGO) announced robust financial results for its third quarter of fiscal year 2026, ending August 2, 2026, showcasing significant growth across key metrics. The company reported total revenue of $29.6 billion, comfortably surpassing the analyst consensus estimate of approximately $29.519 billion. This represents an impressive 86% year-over-year increase in consolidated revenue.

Adjusted earnings per share (EPS) also exceeded expectations, coming in at $3.32 against an estimated $3.25. This strong performance underscores Broadcom's ability to drive profitability alongside its top-line growth. The market had anticipated strong results, with some analysts forecasting revenue of around $29.43 billion and GAAP EPS of $2.55, reflecting sharp year-over-year growth driven by AI semiconductor demand.

### Key Financial Highlights:
*   **Total Revenue:** $29.6 billion (Actual) vs. $29.519 billion (Expected)
*   **Adjusted EPS:** $3.32 (Actual) vs. $3.25 (Expected)
*   **Consolidated Revenue Growth:** 86% year-over-year
*   **Non-GAAP Operating Income Growth:** 92% year-over-year

## Segmental Breakdown and AI Momentum

Broadcom's growth narrative in Q3 FY2026 was largely dominated by its Artificial Intelligence (AI) semiconductor segment. The company reported a substantial $16.7 billion in AI semiconductor revenue, significantly outperforming its own guidance of $16 billion. This figure marks an extraordinary 221% year-over-year growth and a 54% quarter-over-quarter increase in AI semiconductor revenue.

President and CEO Hock Tan highlighted the "very strong" demand for custom AI accelerators and networking solutions as the primary driver behind these results. This indicates Broadcom's critical role in providing the underlying hardware infrastructure necessary for the burgeoning AI industry. The company had previously guided for approximately $29.4 billion of total revenue for Q3, with $16.0 billion from AI semiconductors, $4.5 billion from non-AI semiconductors, and $8.9 billion from infrastructure software. The actual results demonstrate an overperformance in the crucial AI segment.

### Infrastructure Software and VMware Integration:
While AI semiconductors captured the spotlight, the infrastructure software segment, which includes the recently acquired VMware business, also contributed to the overall performance. Broadcom's strategy involves integrating VMware to drive recurring enterprise demand and durable growth. The company's diverse portfolio, spanning cloud, data centre, networking, and enterprise software, positions it well to address complex, mission-critical needs of global organisations.

## Profitability, Cash Flow, and Outlook

Broadcom's Q3 FY2026 results not only demonstrated top-line growth but also strong profitability and cash generation. The company achieved record operating profit and free cash flow during the quarter. Non-GAAP operating income saw a remarkable 92% year-over-year growth, aligning with the consolidated revenue growth.

The cash flow generation was particularly impressive, with $14.2 billion in cash from operations. After accounting for $0.5 billion in capital expenditures, Broadcom reported a robust $13.7 billion in free cash flow for the quarter. This significant free cash flow provides the company with substantial financial flexibility for future investments, debt reduction, and shareholder returns.

In terms of shareholder returns, Broadcom's Board of Directors approved a quarterly cash dividend of $0.65 per share. This dividend is payable on September 30, 2026, to stockholders of record as of September 21, 2026.

Looking ahead, management commentary on the earnings call also provided insights into future expectations. While the company delivered strong Q3 results, the market's reaction, with the stock falling 6.5% post-results, suggests that investors are closely scrutinising the long-term guidance and the sustainability of AI-driven growth. Broadcom had previously teased massive FY 2027 and FY 2028 AI revenue leaps, with targets such as approximately $115 billion in FY 2027 and $230 billion in FY 2028 for AI revenue. The ability to meet these ambitious targets and manage potential margin risks amid evolving enterprise IT spending will be crucial for investor confidence.

## A real example

Consider **Priya, 42, a software architect in Pune, earning ₹35 lakhs per annum**. Priya has a diversified investment portfolio, including some exposure to global technology stocks through an international mutual fund or directly via a brokerage that allows foreign stock investments. She holds units in a fund that has Broadcom (AVGO) as one of its top holdings.

When Broadcom announces its Q3 results:

1.  **Initial Reaction:** Priya sees the headline that Broadcom beat revenue and EPS estimates. Her fund's NAV might initially show a positive bump, reflecting the strong numbers.
2.  **Deeper Dive:** She then reads the detailed analysis, noting the impressive 221% year-over-year growth in AI semiconductor revenue to $16.7 billion. This reinforces her belief in the long-term potential of AI and Broadcom's position in it. She also notes the substantial free cash flow of $13.7 billion, indicating financial health.
3.  **Market Nuance:** However, she also observes that despite beating estimates, the stock dipped by 6.5%. This prompts her to understand the market's high expectations and the focus on future guidance. She realises that even excellent current performance might not satisfy an overly optimistic market if the future outlook isn't equally spectacular.
4.  **Portfolio Impact:** While her direct fund value might slightly fluctuate in the short term due to the stock's reaction, Priya, as a long-term investor, focuses on the underlying business strength – the consistent revenue growth, strong profitability, and leadership in a high-growth segment like AI. She decides to hold her investment, confident in Broadcom's strategic direction, but will keep an eye on subsequent guidance and industry trends. The dividend of $0.65 per share also adds to the total return, a small but steady income stream.
