## What moved the market — the factors
Today's market action was a tale of two halves: strong domestic tailwinds battling negative global sentiment. The primary driver for the Indian market's resilience was the robust buying by Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs), who pumped in a significant ₹7,411 crore. This consistent foreign interest, which has seen over $12 billion of foreign investments in Indian equities in November, also led to a sharp appreciation of the Indian Rupee, climbing past the 93 per US Dollar mark for the first time since April.

Adding to the positive sentiment, heavyweight HDFC Bank shares continued their strong recovery, having risen 22% in November and adding ₹2.38 lakh crore in market value. This rebound, fuelled by easing leadership concerns and improved business performance, likely contributed to a rally in bank stocks.

On the regulatory front, positive news emerged as SEBI proposed easing disclosure rules for Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPIs), aiming to remove constraints absent in other markets and enhance India's appeal to global investors. This move signals a commitment to simplifying regulations and attracting more overseas capital.

However, global cues were largely negative. The US Nasdaq Composite index tumbled significantly, falling over 3% at one point, as concerns deepened around the Artificial Intelligence (AI) trade. Shares of Anthropic, a prominent AI firm, plunged on their second day of trading, reflecting growing skepticism and investor caution amidst high valuations in the AI sector. This global selloff could potentially weigh on future gains for Indian equities.

## News that moved stocks
Here's a look at the key news items that influenced market sentiment today:

**SEBI proposes easing FPI disclosure norms**
India's market regulator, SEBI, is set to release a consultation paper aimed at simplifying regulations for foreign portfolio investors, proposing to remove certain compliance restrictions to make India more attractive to global capital. This move is seen as a significant positive for attracting foreign investment into Indian equities.

**Rupee strengthens past 93/USD, FIIs pour in funds**
The Indian Rupee climbed past the 93-per-dollar mark, strengthening significantly due to over $12 billion in foreign investments in Indian equities during November. While a stronger rupee is generally positive for the economy, a report suggests that this sharp appreciation could cut Indian IT firms' FY27 earnings by up to 13%. This could create headwinds for the IT sector.

**HDFC Bank shares rebound, add ₹2.38 lakh crore in market value**
Shares of HDFC Bank have recovered 22% in November, adding a substantial ₹2.38 lakh crore to its market value. This rally, attributed to easing leadership concerns and improved business performance, provided a significant boost to the banking sector and the broader market.

**Global AI trade concerns deepen as Nasdaq tumbles**
The US Nasdaq Composite index tumbled significantly, with shares of AI firm Anthropic falling as much as 10% after its blockbuster IPO. This global market selloff and growing skepticism around high valuations in the AI sector could create a cautious sentiment for technology stocks globally, including in India.

**SEBI proposes limiting tenure for chief executives, independent directors at exchanges**
In a move to enhance governance, SEBI has proposed crucial adjustments to governance norms for market institutions, including limiting the tenure of chief executives and independent directors at exchanges. This regulatory development aims to bring greater transparency and accountability to India's financial market infrastructure.

## Event breakdown — what happened today and how it hit the market
Today's session was primarily driven by a confluence of strong foreign capital inflows and positive domestic developments, which largely offset negative global cues. There was no single defining event like a major policy decision or economic data release.

The significant net buying by FIIs (₹7,411 crore) acted as a strong pillar of support for the market. This sustained foreign interest also propelled the Indian Rupee to strengthen considerably against the US Dollar. The banking sector received a boost from the continued recovery in HDFC Bank shares, which have added substantial market value in November. Furthermore, SEBI's proposals to ease disclosure norms for FPIs were seen as a positive step towards attracting more global investment.

Globally, however, the mood was cautious, especially in the US tech sector. The Nasdaq tumbled due to deepening concerns around the AI trade, with shares of a prominent AI firm, Anthropic, falling sharply. Despite these global headwinds, the Indian market managed to extend its winning run, primarily on the back of robust FII flows and positive sentiment around key domestic stocks and regulatory reforms.

## Setup for tomorrow
Looking ahead, global cues will continue to play a role. While the Indian market showed resilience today, the significant tumble in the US Nasdaq due to AI trade concerns could lead to some cautious opening for Indian tech stocks tomorrow. The US Dollar Index remained relatively stable, but the Indian Rupee's strong appreciation today (USD/INR closed at 92.67, down 0.85%) will be closely watched, particularly for its potential impact on export-oriented sectors like IT.

Commodity prices saw notable moves, with Brent Crude down 2.88% to $89.33 and Gold and Silver experiencing significant drops of 4.89% and 7.97% respectively. The US 10-year yield also declined by 2.14% to 4.94%. Investors will monitor these global trends. Domestically, the sustained FII inflows and the performance of banking heavyweights like HDFC Bank will remain key factors. Any further updates on SEBI's regulatory proposals for FPIs could also influence sentiment.
