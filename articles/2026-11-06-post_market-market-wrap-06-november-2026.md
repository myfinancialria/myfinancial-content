## What moved the market — the factors
Today's market sentiment was primarily shaped by a confluence of strong foreign institutional investor (FII) inflows and positive global cues, particularly a rebound in US chip stocks. FIIs injected a substantial ₹14,975 crore into Indian equities, marking a continuation of their buying spree over the past five sessions. ## News that moved stocks
- **US stocks rebound as chip stocks recover from steep losses, rate fears persist**
  The Nasdaq rebounded by 1.73% as chipmakers like Nvidia, AMD, Intel, and Micron recovered from Thursday's AI-led sell-off. This positive sentiment from the US market likely provided a boost to global and potentially Indian equity sentiment, especially in technology-related sectors.

- **India's 10-year bond posts best week in 7 years, leads emerging Asia as oil slides**
  Indian government bonds saw benchmark yields drop significantly, outperforming other Asian markets. This positive development for the bond market is linked to falling crude prices following a Democratic win in the US midterms, and it signals improved economic stability and investor confidence in India.

- **Indian rupee strengthens past 94/$ as oil slides, foreign inflows return**
  The Indian rupee gained significant strength, moving past 94 per dollar. This appreciation was driven by substantial foreign inflows into Indian equities and a sharp decline in crude oil prices, indicating a positive outlook for India's external accounts and potentially easing import-related inflation pressures.

- **Siemens Q2 Results: Profit grows 5% to Rs 504 crore**
  Siemens Limited reported a consolidated net profit of ₹504 crore for the quarter ending September 30, 2026, a 4.78% increase year-on-year. This positive earnings report could have influenced Siemens' stock performance today.

- **BMW Industries Q2 Results: Net profit rise 43% YoY to Rs 21.62 crore**
  BMW Industries Ltd announced a significant 42.7% increase in net profit for the quarter ending September 30, with total income surging by 33%. Such strong quarterly results typically lead to positive investor sentiment for the company's stock.

- **Gold, silver turn volatile after strong US jobs data, but remain on track to snap multi-week losing streaks**
  Gold and silver prices fell today as stronger-than-expected US jobs data increased expectations of a Federal Reserve rate hike in December. This news impacted commodity markets globally, including India, and reflects the ongoing sensitivity of precious metals to US monetary policy expectations.

## FII / DII flows
Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) were significant net buyers today, injecting a robust ₹14,975 crore into Indian equities. In contrast, Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) were net sellers, pulling out ₹10,445 crore.

| Investor Category | Net Flow (₹ crore) |
|---|---|
| FII | 14,975 |
| DII | -10,445 |

Looking at the 5-day trend, FIIs have been consistent net buyers, with today's inflow being the highest in this period. Their net buying has steadily increased from ₹3,599 crore on October 30 to ₹14,975 crore today. DIIs, on the other hand, have been net sellers throughout this period, with their outflows also increasing significantly today. This trend suggests strong foreign confidence in the Indian market, potentially driven by factors like falling crude oil prices and a strengthening rupee, while domestic investors might be booking profits or reallocating funds.
