## What moved the market — the factors
Today's market rally was primarily driven by a confluence of positive global and domestic factors. The biggest cheer came from the US Federal Reserve, which held its interest rates steady, easing concerns about further tightening of monetary policy globally. This decision provided a significant boost to investor sentiment. Adding to the positive mood, crude oil prices eased considerably, which is a favourable development for India, a major oil importer.

Domestically, both Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) and Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) turned net buyers in the cash market, providing strong institutional support to the rally. FIIs infused ₹872 crore, while DIIs added ₹643 crore, indicating a broad-based buying interest.

However, global markets did present some mixed signals. While European stocks inched up after the ECB held rates steady, the Nasdaq Composite in the US slid over 1% following Alphabet's disappointing earnings report, which sparked concerns about AI spending. This could potentially be a factor to watch for future sessions.

## News that moved stocks
Here are some of the key news items that influenced market sentiment and specific stocks today:

**Indian Equities Rally on Global Cues and Institutional Buying**
Indian equities surged over 1% as the US Federal Reserve maintained interest rates, crude oil prices softened, and institutional investors turned net buyers, collectively strengthening market sentiment. This broad positive momentum impacted the overall market.

**Alphabet Shares Plunge 13% on AI Spending Fears**
Google's parent company, Alphabet, saw its shares crash by 13% after its third-quarter earnings missed expectations, primarily due to heavy spending on Artificial Intelligence and a slowdown in search advertising growth. This significant drop in a global tech giant could weigh on broader technology sector sentiment globally and potentially in India.

**Ambuja Cements’ Profit Slumps 90% in Q2 FY27**
Ambuja Cements reported a sharp 90% year-on-year decline in its Q2 FY27 profit to ₹236 crore. The significant drop was attributed to higher fuel, logistics, and operating costs, which squeezed margins. This news is likely to have a negative impact on Ambuja Cements stock.

**Brigade Group Reports 29% Growth in Q2 Net Profit**
Brigade Group announced a 29% year-on-year increase in its net profit for Q2 FY27, reaching ₹219 crore. The growth was driven by strong residential bookings, improved operating profit, and robust performance in its leasing and hospital segments. This positive earnings report is expected to be favourable for Brigade Group's stock.

**SEBI Amends REIT Regulations**
The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) introduced amendments to REIT regulations, revising unitholder voting thresholds and introducing exit options for dissenting investors during specific sponsor exits. These changes aim to enhance governance and investor protection in the Real Estate Investment Trust (REIT) sector, potentially making REITs more attractive to investors.

**Jio Platforms Q2 Results Due Tomorrow**
Jio Platforms is set to announce its Q2 FY27 results tomorrow, October 30. This will be the company's first earnings report since its bumper listing, and investors will be closely watching key metrics like profit growth, revenue, and Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) for insights into its performance.

## Event breakdown — what happened today and how it hit the market
Today's market session was primarily driven by a combination of favourable global cues and strong domestic institutional flows. The key event was the US Federal Reserve's decision to maintain interest rates, which reassured global markets and reduced uncertainty. This, coupled with a notable easing in crude oil prices, provided a significant tailwind for Indian equities. Domestically, the robust buying by both FIIs and DIIs further amplified the positive sentiment, leading to a broad-based rally. There was no single defining domestic event that dominated the session; rather, it was a collective response to these macro and flow-driven factors.

## Setup for tomorrow
Looking ahead to tomorrow, global cues will continue to play a crucial role. While the US Federal Reserve's decision to hold rates was positive, concerns about US inflation heating up in September and the significant 13% crash in Alphabet shares due to AI spending fears could introduce some caution. On the commodity front, the sharp decline in Brent crude (-4.43%) and WTI crude (-1.37%) is a positive development for India and could provide further support. The US 10-year yield also saw a slight dip.

Investors will be keenly watching the Q2 FY27 results of Jio Platforms, which are due tomorrow, as this could impact Reliance Industries and the broader telecom sector. Global technology stock performance, especially after Alphabet's slump, will also be on the radar.
