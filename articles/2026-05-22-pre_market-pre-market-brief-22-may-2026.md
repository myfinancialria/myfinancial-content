# Pre-Market Brief — 22 May 2026

**TL;DR**
- Global markets largely ended positive, with Asian indices showing strong gains, setting a firm tone.
- The Indian Rupee strengthened against the US Dollar, while crude oil prices saw a decline.
- Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) continued their selling streak, while Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) provided robust buying support.
- LIC announced a significant 1:1 bonus issue and a final dividend, a key development for shareholders.
- SEBI has proposed changes to the price discovery mechanism for IPOs and re-listed stocks, aiming for greater efficiency.

## Where global markets closed
Global markets largely closed in positive territory, indicating a generally optimistic sentiment. Asian markets, in particular, demonstrated strong performance, with Japan's Nikkei 225 leading the gains. US indices also edged higher, albeit with more modest increases. This broad-based positivity could provide a favourable backdrop for the Indian markets today.

## Currency, commodities and yields
The Indian Rupee (USD/INR) strengthened by 0.35% to 96.19 against the US Dollar, which is a positive sign for India's import bill and could ease inflationary pressures. Conversely, the Dollar Index saw a slight uptick of 0.16% to 99.27, indicating broader strength for the greenback globally, possibly due to uncertainty surrounding US-Iran talks and strong US economic data.

In commodities, both Brent Crude and WTI Crude oil prices declined by 0.85% and 0.92% respectively, settling at $104.13 and $97.36. This fall in crude oil prices is a significant positive for India, a major oil importer, as it helps manage current account deficit and inflation. Gold prices dipped by 0.21% to $4521.90, possibly reflecting a shift towards riskier assets or concerns over potential interest rate hikes. Silver, however, gained 0.82% to $76.47.

The US 10-Year Yield rose by 0.31% to 4.59%. A rising US yield can make dollar-denominated assets more attractive, potentially leading to capital outflows from emerging markets like India.

## Overnight news that matters

**LIC board approves 1:1 bonus issue, also declares final dividend; check record dates**
Life Insurance Corporation (LIC) has announced a 1:1 bonus share issue and a final dividend, following a 41.63% rise in its Value of New Business and strong net profit.
*Why it matters:* This is a significant positive for LIC shareholders, indicating strong financial health and a commitment to shareholder returns. It could lead to increased investor interest and positive price action for LIC shares.

**SEBI proposes changes in price discovery mechanism for IPOs, re-listing stocks**
SEBI has proposed modifications to the price discovery process for Initial Public Offerings (IPOs) and re-listed stocks, citing concerns that the current framework might be distorting prices.
*Why it matters:* This move by the market regulator aims to enhance efficiency and fairness in the primary and secondary markets. Any changes could impact how new listings are valued and traded, affecting both companies going public and investors participating in these offerings.

**Rs 420 crore share buybacks: Welspun Living, CMS Info Systems turn ex-record date today. Tracking any?**
Welspun Living and CMS Info Systems are trading ex-record date today for their respective share buyback programmes, collectively valued at ₹420 crore.
*Why it matters:* Share buybacks typically signal management confidence in the company's valuation and can support share prices by reducing the number of outstanding shares. Investors in these companies will be watching for details on entitlement ratios and offer windows.

**Oil prices gain as investors doubt breakthrough in US-Iran peace talks**
Crude oil prices saw an increase amid lingering doubts about a breakthrough in US-Iran peace talks, leading to concerns about Middle East instability and potential supply disruptions.
*Why it matters:* While crude oil dipped yesterday, the underlying geopolitical tensions and uncertainty around the US-Iran deal remain a key factor. Any escalation or lack of resolution could quickly reverse the recent dip in oil prices, which would be a negative for India's economy.

**Dollar perched near six-week high on uncertainty over US-Iran deal**
The US Dollar maintained its position near a six-week high, supported by ongoing uncertainty regarding the US-Iran peace deal and robust US economic data.
*Why it matters:* A stronger US Dollar can put pressure on the Indian Rupee, making imports more expensive and potentially impacting India's trade balance. It also influences FII flows, as a stronger dollar makes dollar-denominated returns more attractive.

## What to watch in today's session
1.  **FII vs DII Flows:** Continue to monitor the dynamic between FII selling and DII buying. Sustained DII support will be crucial in absorbing foreign outflows.
2.  **Crude Oil Movement:** Despite yesterday's dip, geopolitical tensions around US-Iran talks could cause volatility in crude oil prices. Any significant upward movement would be a concern for Indian markets.
3.  **Corporate Actions Impact:** Keep an eye on the companies trading ex-date for dividends and buybacks, especially LIC after its bonus issue announcement, and Welspun Living and CMS Info Systems for their buybacks.
4.  **Global Cues:** While global markets were largely positive, any fresh developments in international trade, geopolitical events, or central bank commentary could influence sentiment.
5.  **SEBI's Proposed Changes:** While not an immediate market mover, the proposed changes to IPO price discovery could spark discussions and impact investor sentiment towards upcoming public issues.

## Bottom line
The Indian market is likely to open with a positive bias, supported by largely favourable global cues and strong domestic institutional buying. However, the persistent selling by FIIs and the underlying geopolitical risks impacting crude oil and the US Dollar warrant caution. Investors should remain vigilant about these external factors and their potential impact on market sentiment.
