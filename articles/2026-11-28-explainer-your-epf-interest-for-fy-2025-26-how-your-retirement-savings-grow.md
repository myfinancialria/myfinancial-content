# Your EPF Interest for FY 2025-26: How Your Retirement Savings Grow

**TL;DR**
- The Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO) has credited 8.25% interest for Financial Year (FY) 2025-26 to over 7 crore member accounts.
- This interest rate was approved by the government after a recommendation from the EPFO's Central Board of Trustees.
- While interest is calculated monthly, it is credited to your EPF account once a year, usually after the financial year ends.
- You can check your updated balance and interest credit through the EPFO Member e-Sewa portal, UMANG app, SMS, or a missed call.
- It's crucial to keep your Universal Account Number (UAN) active and KYC details updated for smooth access to your EPF services.

## What's this about?
The Employees' Provident Fund (EPF) is a mandatory savings scheme for salaried employees in India, managed by the Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO). Both you and your employer contribute a portion of your salary to this fund every month. This money earns interest, which helps your retirement savings grow over time. Recently, the EPFO finished crediting the annual interest for the Financial Year (FY) 2025-26 to the accounts of over 7 crore members.

## Why it's in the news right now
The government had ratified the EPF interest rate at 8.25% for FY 2025-26 back in June 2026. Following this approval, the EPFO began the process of crediting this interest to subscribers' accounts. Union Labour and Employment Minister Mansukh Mandaviya announced in July 2026 that the interest would be auto-processed and credited by July 15, 2026, to approximately 34 crore member accounts. This process has now largely completed, and many members are checking their passbooks to see their updated balances. This annual crediting of interest is a significant event for millions of salaried individuals, as it directly impacts their retirement savings.

## How it actually works
1.  **Contributions:** Each month, both you (the employee) and your employer contribute 12% of your basic salary plus dearness allowance to your EPF account. A small part of the employer's contribution (8.33% of a pensionable salary up to ₹15,000) goes to the Employees' Pension Scheme (EPS), while the rest goes to your EPF account.
2.  **Interest Rate Declaration:** Every year, the EPFO's Central Board of Trustees (CBT) recommends an interest rate for the financial year. For FY 2025-26, the CBT recommended 8.25% in March 2026, which was later approved by the government. This 8.25% rate has been maintained for the third consecutive year.
3.  **Interest Calculation:** Even though the interest is credited annually, it is calculated every month based on your "monthly running balance." This means the balance at the end of each month earns a portion of the annual interest rate.
4.  **Annual Crediting:** The total calculated interest for the entire financial year (April 1 to March 31) is then credited to your EPF account once a year, typically after the financial year ends and the government officially notifies the rate. For FY 2025-26, this crediting process was largely completed by July 15, 2026.
5.  **Checking Your Balance:** You can check if the interest has been credited and view your updated balance through various online and offline methods using your Universal Account Number (UAN). Your UAN is a unique 12-digit number provided by EPFO that stays with you throughout your working life, linking all your EPF accounts from different employers.

## A simple way to think about it
Imagine your EPF account is like a special piggy bank that your employer also helps you fill. Every month, you both put money in. At the end of the year, the bank (EPFO) looks at how much money was in your piggy bank each month. Then, it adds extra money, like a bonus, based on a fixed percentage (the interest rate) for the whole year. This extra money makes your piggy bank grow bigger, helping you save for your future, especially retirement.

## Why it matters to you
1.  **Retirement Savings Growth:** The interest credited directly adds to your retirement corpus, helping your savings grow significantly over the long term due to the power of compounding.
2.  **Financial Security:** EPF is a government-backed scheme, offering stable and reliable returns, which is crucial for your financial security after retirement.
3.  **Tax Benefits:** Generally, EPF contributions, interest earned, and withdrawals (under certain conditions) are tax-exempt, making it a tax-efficient savings instrument for most salaried individuals. However, interest on contributions exceeding ₹2.5 lakh annually became taxable from FY 2021-22.
4.  **Transparency and Access:** With the interest credited, checking your updated passbook allows you to track your savings accurately and plan your finances better.
5.  **Digital Convenience:** The EPFO has been working on improving its digital services, including the new Centralised IT Enabled Services (CITES) system, to make checking balances and processing claims faster and more efficient.

## Common confusions cleared up
*   **Myth:** EPF interest is credited monthly.
    *   **Fact:** While the interest is calculated on a monthly running balance, the actual credit to your account happens only once a year, at the end of the financial year (March 31st), after the government's approval of the rate.
*   **Myth:** If the interest is delayed in reflecting, you lose out on earnings.
    *   **Fact:** No, you do not lose out. The interest is calculated for the entire financial year based on your monthly balances. Any delay in the interest showing in your passbook is merely an administrative processing delay; your earnings are still calculated from April 1st of the financial year.
*   **Myth:** Your UAN changes with every job.
    *   **Fact:** Your Universal Account Number (UAN) is a permanent, 12-digit number assigned to you by the EPFO for life. While your PF account number (Member ID) changes with each employer, all these Member IDs are linked to your single UAN.

## FAQ

### How can I check my EPF balance and see the interest credit?
You can check your EPF balance and passbook online via the EPFO Member e-Sewa portal (passbook.epfoindia.gov.in), through the UMANG app, by sending an SMS to 7738299899 from your registered mobile number, or by giving a missed call to 9966044425.

### What is the EPF interest rate for FY 2025-26?
The Employees' Provident Fund interest rate for the financial year 2025-26 has been set at 8.25% per annum.

### Do I need to apply to get the interest credited?
No, you do not need to apply. The EPFO automatically calculates and credits the interest to eligible member accounts once the rate is approved and notified by the government.

### What is a Universal Account Number (UAN)?
A UAN is a unique 12-digit number issued by the EPFO to every EPF member. It acts as a permanent identifier, linking all your Provident Fund accounts from different employers under one number throughout your working life.

### What if my EPF interest is not reflecting in my passbook yet?
If the interest is not immediately visible, it simply means the credit process is still underway for all member accounts. The EPFO updates these balances in phases. You can check again after some time.
