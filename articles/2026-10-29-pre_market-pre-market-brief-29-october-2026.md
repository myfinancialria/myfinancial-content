# Pre-Market Brief — 29 October 2026

**TL;DR**
*   Global markets ended mixed, with US and European indices closing positive, while some Asian markets saw declines.
*   GIFT Nifty signals a strong positive opening bias for Indian equities.
*   Both FIIs and DIIs were net buyers in the cash segment yesterday, indicating broad support.
*   The US Federal Reserve held rates steady, but bond market concerns over inflation persist, while the ECB rate decision is awaited.
*   Nifty's key levels to watch are 23000 as immediate resistance and 22700 as crucial support.
*   Rising US bond yields and potential global inflation concerns remain key risks for today's session.

## How global markets traded — and what it means for India
Overnight, US markets closed with modest gains, with the Dow Jones, Nasdaq, and S&P 500 all inching higher. European markets also saw positive momentum, led by the FTSE 100. However, Asian markets presented a mixed picture this morning, with Japan's Nikkei 225 rising, but Hong Kong's Hang Seng and China's Shanghai Composite declining, largely due to a tech selloff in China.

## Currency, commodities and yields
The Indian Rupee weakened slightly against the US Dollar, with USD/INR trading at ₹95.33, up 0.16%. The Dollar Index also saw a marginal gain of 0.06% to 102.25. In commodities, crude oil prices saw a notable decline, with Brent Crude down nearly 4% to $110.73 and WTI Crude falling over 1% to $101.60. Gold and Silver also traded lower. The US 10-year Treasury yield rose by over 1% to 5.05%.

However, the rise in US 10-year yields could make global investors more cautious about emerging markets, potentially impacting FII flows.

Conversely, the highest Put OI at 22700 and 22500 indicates robust support, where buyers are likely to defend the market from falling further. Traders should respect these option-chain derived levels in conjunction with the technical levels for today's trading range.

    **Why it matters:** This news is highly positive for ACME Solar, potentially driving its stock performance today. It also highlights strong growth in the renewable energy sector, which could positively impact other players.

    Dolat Capital suggests Jio Platforms is evolving beyond traditional telecom, driven by tariff hikes, 5G monetisation, home broadband, and enterprise digital services.
    **Why it matters:** This outlook provides a positive long-term narrative for Jio Platforms, potentially influencing investor sentiment ahead of its Q2 results (due tomorrow) and for the broader telecom and digital services sector.
*   **US Market: Fed holds rates steady, but bond investors remain concerned over inflation**
    The US Federal Reserve maintained interest rates, but the Treasury yield curve steepened as investors weighed inflation risks, fiscal concerns, and the future rate outlook.
    **Why it matters:** While a rate pause is generally seen as positive, persistent inflation concerns in the US bond market could lead to higher US yields, potentially impacting FII flows into emerging markets like India.
*   **HUL shares rise 3% after Q2 results. Jefferies, Nomura cut target price but forecast 29% upside potential**
    Hindustan Unilever reported a 4% year-on-year increase in Q2 FY27 net profit to ₹2,790 crore and 15% revenue growth to ₹18,226 crore, despite margin pressures from rising commodity prices.
    **Why it matters:** HUL's results provide insights into the FMCG sector's health. While revenue growth is strong, margin compression due to commodity prices is a key concern that could affect other FMCG players.
*   **Indian bonds track US Treasuries higher amid oil-price relief**
    Indian government bonds saw gains, supported by a rally in US Treasuries and a drop in crude oil prices, although a large upcoming bond sale and a weak rupee limited the extent of the gains.
    **Why it matters:** Lower crude oil prices are a significant positive for the Indian economy and bond markets, potentially easing inflation and fiscal concerns. This could lead to a more stable interest rate environment domestically.
*   **Vedanta Aluminium more than doubles profit; cost cuts in focus to drive earnings**
    Vedanta Aluminium's September-quarter net profit more than doubled, with revenue up 41% and EBITDA surging 83% year-on-year, driven by higher production volumes and cost efficiencies.
    **Why it matters:** This strong performance from Vedanta Aluminium could set a positive tone for the metals sector, highlighting the impact of operational efficiencies and commodity price movements.

## Events & data to watch — and how markets usually react
*   **European Central Bank (ECB) Rate Decision:** The ECB is expected to hold interest rates steady. Central bank policy announcements are typically met with increased market volatility as investors react to the decision and accompanying commentary on the economic outlook and future policy path.
*   **US Bond Market Sentiment:** Continued concerns over inflation and US Treasury yields could influence global risk appetite. Markets often react to rising US yields by re-evaluating investments in emerging markets, potentially leading to FII outflows or increased caution.
*   **Jio Platforms Q2 Results (tomorrow):** While not today, the anticipation of results from a major listed entity like Jio Platforms can influence market sentiment, particularly for the telecom and digital services sectors.

Indian markets are poised for a positive start today, largely driven by a strong GIFT Nifty signal and supportive global cues from the US and European markets, coupled with a significant drop in crude oil prices.
