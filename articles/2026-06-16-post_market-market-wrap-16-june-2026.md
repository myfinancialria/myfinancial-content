# Market Wrap — 16 June 2026

**TL;DR**
- Indian equities extended their recovery today, with Nifty 50 closing above 23,989.
- Broader markets, including Midcap and Smallcap indices, also posted positive gains.
- Realty and IT sectors led the charge, while Metal and Auto stocks saw some selling pressure.
- Sonata Software was a standout performer, surging nearly 20% in today's session.
- Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) continued their strong buying, while Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) were net buyers after a streak of outflows.

## What moved markets today

Several key developments influenced market sentiment today, both domestically and globally.

**Easing Geopolitical Tensions and Falling Oil Prices**
A significant driver for today's market optimism was the news of an expected peace deal between the United States and Iran.
-   **Brent oil price drops under $80 on US-Iran deal.** Oil prices fell to a three-month low, with Brent crude dropping under $80 a barrel, on optimism surrounding a potential peace deal between the US and Iran and the anticipated reopening of the Strait of Hormuz.
-   **Rupee extends winning streak to 3rd day, settles at 94.56 vs USD amid oil slide.** The Indian rupee strengthened for the third consecutive session, gaining 15 paise against the US dollar, supported by easing geopolitical tensions and lower crude oil prices.
-   **Dawn of peace in West Asia: What can be the implications for investors?** The potential end of conflict in West Asia, leading to falling crude prices and a stabilising rupee, could shift dynamics for Indian markets, potentially benefiting banking stocks while capital goods and power sectors might see changes.
*Market Impact:* Lower crude oil prices are generally positive for India, a major oil importer, as they can ease inflationary pressures and improve the current account deficit. This boosted investor confidence, leading to a stronger rupee and overall positive market sentiment.

**Strong Manufacturing Sales Data**
Domestic economic indicators also provided some cheer.
-   **Automobiles, electrical machinery push sales of listed private manufacturing companies in Q4: RBI data.** Indian manufacturing companies recorded a 14.5% jump in sales in early 2026, with automobiles, electrical machinery, and metals leading this growth, according to RBI data.
*Market Impact:* Strong manufacturing sales data indicates robust economic activity, which is a positive sign for corporate earnings and overall market health.

**SEBI's Regulatory Updates**
Regulatory developments aimed at improving market efficiency also made headlines.
-   **Sebi eases winding-up norms for AIFs, introduces 'inoperative fund' framework.** SEBI introduced a new framework allowing Alternative Investment Funds (AIFs) to retain liquidation proceeds beyond their fund life under specific conditions, addressing practical challenges.
*Market Impact:* These regulatory changes aim to streamline operations for AIFs, potentially making the investment landscape more efficient and attractive.

Global cues suggest a mixed to positive start for tomorrow's session. Investors will continue to monitor global oil price movements and any further updates on geopolitical situations. Sectors like IT and Realty, which performed well today, could remain in focus, along with banking stocks that may benefit from a stable rupee and lower crude prices.
