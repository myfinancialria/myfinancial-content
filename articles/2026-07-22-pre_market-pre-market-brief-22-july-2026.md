# Pre-Market Brief — 22 July 2026

**TL;DR**
- Global markets presented a mixed picture overnight, with US indices closing higher but Asian markets showing divergence.
- GIFT Nifty indicates a cautious start for Indian markets, pointing to a potential gap-down open.
- FIIs were net buyers yesterday after a streak of selling, while DIIs were net sellers, though DIIs have largely supported the market recently.
- Indian markets saw a significant fall yesterday, with the Nifty 50 dropping below the crucial 24,000 mark.
- The 24,000 level for Nifty 50 will be a key battleground, acting as both a technical and option-chain support/resistance.
- Rising crude oil prices and the overall FII flow trend remain significant risks for today's session.

## How global markets traded — and what it means for India
Overnight, US markets closed on a positive note, with all three major indices gaining. European markets also ended in the green. However, Asian markets this morning are showing a mixed trend, with Chinese stocks advancing on AI and chip rallies, while Hong Kong shares slipped. This mixed global sentiment suggests a cautious opening for Indian equities.

**Interpretation:** The positive close in US and European markets provides some tailwind, but the mixed performance in Asian markets, particularly the decline in Hang Seng, indicates a lack of strong directional cues. This suggests Indian markets might open with some caution, potentially consolidating after yesterday's sharp fall.

## Currency, commodities and yields
The Indian Rupee saw a marginal appreciation against the US Dollar, with USD/INR at 96.45, down 0.04%. The Dollar Index also edged lower by 0.05% to 101.13, indicating a slight weakening of the greenback globally. Crude oil prices, however, surged significantly, with Brent Crude up 1.52% to $92.39 and WTI Crude gaining 0.61% to $85.43. Gold and Silver also saw notable gains, rising 1.41% and 1.75% respectively. The US 10-year Treasury Yield increased by 0.65% to 4.63%.

**Interpretation:** A slightly stronger Rupee and a weaker Dollar Index are generally positive for the Indian economy. However, the sharp rise in crude oil prices is a significant concern, as it could fuel domestic inflation and put pressure on sectors like oil marketing companies (OMCs), paints, and aviation. The increase in US 10-year yields could also make foreign institutional investors (FIIs) more cautious about emerging markets like India. The rise in precious metals suggests some safe-haven demand in global markets.

## GIFT Nifty — what it's signalling
GIFT Nifty is currently trading at 23982.50, down 0.50%.

**Interpretation:** This indicates a negative bias for the Indian market open, suggesting a potential gap-down for the Nifty 50, likely below the 24,000 mark.

## FII/DII flows — who's driving the tape
Yesterday, Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) turned net buyers in the cash segment, infusing ₹1,650 crore into the Indian markets. Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs), however, were net sellers, offloading equities worth ₹-657 crore. Over the last five sessions, FIIs have been net sellers in four out of five sessions, while DIIs have consistently provided support by being net buyers in four out of five sessions.

## Option-chain signals (OI) — where support & resistance sit
For Nifty, the highest Call Open Interest (OI) is concentrated at the 24200 (1.66 crore contracts) and 24000 (1.40 crore contracts) strikes, acting as immediate resistance levels. On the Put side, the highest OI is at 24000 (1.20 crore contracts) and 24200 (89 lakh contracts), indicating support. The Put-Call Ratio (PCR) for Nifty is 0.78.

For Bank Nifty, the 58000 (23.66 lakh contracts) and 58500 (12.43 lakh contracts) strikes hold the highest Call OI, suggesting strong resistance. Key Put OI is seen at 56000 (11.95 lakh contracts) and 58000 (11.60 lakh contracts), providing support. The PCR for Bank Nifty is 0.9.

For Sensex, the highest Call OI is at 77500 (42.85 lakh contracts) and 77000 (40.84 lakh contracts), forming resistance. On the Put side, 76000 (20.38 lakh contracts) and 76800 (18.87 lakh contracts) have the highest OI, indicating support. The PCR for Sensex is 0.67.

**Interpretation:** Nifty's PCR below 1 (0.78) leans cautious, with 24000 being a critical level where both calls and puts have significant OI, suggesting a battleground. A sustained move above or below this level will be crucial. For Bank Nifty, a PCR of 0.9 is neutral-to-cautious, with 58000 acting as a strong resistance and 56000 as key support. Sensex's PCR of 0.67 indicates a cautious sentiment, with 77000-77500 as immediate hurdles and 76000 as a strong floor. Combining with technical levels, traders should respect 24000 for Nifty, 57000-58000 for Bank Nifty, and 76000-77000 for Sensex as key levels today.

## Stock & sector news that matters
- **Triveni Engineering shares adjust 38.5% as stock turns ex-demerger.**
  Triveni Engineering shares saw a sharp decline yesterday, but this was a technical price adjustment due to its power transmission business demerger, not a fundamental fall.
  **Why it matters:** This news is crucial for existing shareholders of Triveni Engineering, as the price adjustment reflects the corporate action. It's important for investors to understand this is a technical adjustment and not a negative market reaction to the company's performance.
- **Vedanta Oil & Gas shares fall over 3% after firm discloses two legal matters involving ONGC, GoI.**
  Vedanta Oil & Gas shares slipped after the company revealed two legal disputes, one involving the Directorate General of Hydrocarbons (DGH) seeking approximately $35 million plus interest, and another with ONGC.
  **Why it matters:** This news is negative for Vedanta Oil & Gas, as legal disputes can create uncertainty and potential financial liabilities, impacting investor sentiment and the stock price.
- **Bandhan Bank shares crash 15% after Q1 results; lender cuts RoA guidance.**
  Bandhan Bank's shares fell sharply after the bank reduced its Return on Assets (RoA) guidance, overshadowing a 35% year-on-year rise in Q1 FY27 net profit. Motilal Oswal downgraded the stock to 'Neutral'.
  **Why it matters:** This is a significant negative for Bandhan Bank and could weigh on sentiment for other private sector banks, especially those with similar asset quality or growth concerns. A cut in guidance often signals challenges ahead.
- **Rupee falls 11 paise to 96.36 against US dollar in early trade.**
  The Indian Rupee weakened against the US Dollar in early trade yesterday, influenced by rising crude oil prices and increased demand for safe-haven assets.
  **Why it matters:** A depreciating rupee can make imports more expensive, potentially impacting inflation and corporate input costs. For export-oriented sectors like IT and pharmaceuticals, a weaker rupee can be a tailwind, boosting their rupee-denominated earnings.
- **Global Market: Chinese stocks advance on AI, chip rally; Hong Kong shares slip.**
  Chinese mainland stocks gained, driven by AI, semiconductor, and metal shares, while Hong Kong equities declined due to profit-booking in technology stocks.
  **Why it matters:** This highlights the divergent trends within Asian markets. While China's tech and manufacturing sectors show strength, profit-booking in Hong Kong tech could signal broader caution in the region, which India often tracks.
- **Cube Highways Trust InvIT subscription opens today.**
  The Cube Highways Trust InvIT is launching its ₹5,000-crore public issue today, offering investors exposure to a diversified portfolio of operational infrastructure assets.
  **Why it matters:** This provides a new investment avenue for investors looking for exposure to infrastructure assets and potentially stable returns. The subscription response will indicate investor appetite for InvITs.

1.  **Nifty's reaction to the 24,000 level:** After yesterday's fall and GIFT Nifty's negative signal, how Nifty 50 trades around the crucial 24,000 mark (both a technical pivot and significant OI level) will be key.

## Bottom line
Indian markets are likely to open with a cautious tone, indicated by GIFT Nifty's negative signal and yesterday's sharp domestic fall. The 24,000 level for Nifty 50 will be critical to watch, acting as a potential pivot for the day's direction. Traders should remain vigilant about rising crude oil prices and the overall FII flow trend, which could introduce volatility.
