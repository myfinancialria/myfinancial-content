# Pre-Market Brief — 01 July 2026

**TL;DR**
*   Global markets ended mostly positive, with US tech indices showing strong gains.
*   FIIs were net sellers for the fourth time in the last five sessions, while DIIs continued their strong buying trend.
*   KPIT Technologies shares plunged significantly after a weak Q1 FY27 outlook, potentially impacting the broader IT sector.
*   Indian government successfully divested ₹2 billion from state-run companies, indicating continued focus on asset monetisation.
*   Indian bond yields saw gains on hopes of Bloomberg index inclusion, despite rising US yields.

## Where global markets closed
Global equity markets largely closed in positive territory on Tuesday, with US indices leading the charge. The technology-heavy Nasdaq recorded a robust gain of 1.52%, while the S&P 500 advanced by 0.79%. The Dow Jones Industrial Average also saw a modest rise of 0.26%. Across Europe, the FTSE 100 in London closed up by 0.12%. In Asia, the Nikkei 225 in Japan gained 0.59%, and China's Shanghai Composite was up 0.17%. However, Hong Kong's Hang Seng bucked the trend, closing down by 0.63%. The overall global tone appears cautiously optimistic, primarily driven by US tech resilience.

## Currency, commodities and yields
The Indian Rupee (USD/INR) strengthened slightly against the US Dollar, closing at ₹94.74, a marginal gain of 0.05%. This comes as the Dollar Index saw a slight uptick of 0.13% to 101.32, indicating a mixed sentiment for the greenback globally.

In the commodities market, Brent Crude oil edged up by 0.12% to $73.01 per barrel, while WTI Crude saw a minor dip of 0.01% to $69.49. The slight rise in Brent could exert some pressure on India's import bill and inflation outlook. Gold prices declined by 0.93% to $3985.30, and Silver also saw a significant drop of 2.52% to $57.98, suggesting a shift away from safe-haven assets.

The US 10-year Treasury yield decreased by 0.46% to 4.37%. A softening in US yields can sometimes reduce the attractiveness of the dollar and potentially ease some pressure on emerging market currencies, including the Rupee.

## Recent FII/DII flows
Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) continued their selling streak, offloading Indian equities worth ₹2,557 crore on June 30th. Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs), however, remained strong buyers, pumping in ₹6,842 crore into the market.

Over the last five trading sessions, FIIs have been net sellers in four out of five sessions, indicating a cautious stance. In contrast, DIIs have consistently provided strong buying support, absorbing the FII outflows.

## Overnight news that matters

**KPIT Tech shares sink 17%, see worst plunge since 2020 Covid crash. Time to buy or more pain ahead?**
KPIT Technologies shares plummeted to a 52-week low after the company warned of weaker-than-expected revenue and margin performance for Q1 FY27 due to a sudden slowdown in European orders. This news is significant for Indian markets as KPIT is a prominent IT services player, and its weak guidance could cast a shadow over the broader IT sector, potentially leading to sector-wide corrections or cautious sentiment.

**RITES shares rocket 16% on Rs 175 crore consultancy order from Ambedkar University**
Shares of RITES surged 16% after the company secured a ₹175 crore project management consultancy order from Babasaheb Bhimrao Ambedkar University for campus infrastructure development. This development is positive for RITES and other companies in the infrastructure and project consultancy space, highlighting continued government spending and order inflows in the sector.

**India's $2 billion state stake sales buck equity market slowdown**
The Indian government successfully raised $2 billion (approximately ₹16,600 crore) from stake sales in state-run companies last quarter, primarily from Coal India. This substantial divestment is a positive for government finances and demonstrates continued efforts towards asset monetisation, which could support fiscal stability and investor confidence in public sector undertakings (PSUs).

**India bonds rise as index-entry hopes counter rise in US yields**
Indian government bonds saw gains, bolstered by anticipation of Bloomberg index inclusion and improved liquidity, despite rising US yields and oil prices. This is a crucial development for the Indian fixed-income market, as inclusion in global bond indices could attract significant foreign capital inflows, potentially strengthening the Rupee and lowering borrowing costs for the government.

**Reliance Power share price soars 18% as AI push sparks rally; stock snaps 4-day losing streak**
Reliance Power's share price surged over 18% after the company announced plans to integrate AI and new technology within its subsidiaries. This highlights the market's strong positive reaction to companies embracing AI and new technologies, suggesting that the AI theme could continue to drive interest and investment in related sectors or companies making such strategic shifts.

## What to watch in today's session
1.  **IT Sector Performance:** Following KPIT Technologies' weak guidance, monitor how other IT services companies react and if there's a broader sector-wide impact.
2.  **PSU Stocks:** Keep an eye on public sector undertakings, especially those in the infrastructure and energy sectors, given the government's successful stake sales and continued focus on divestment.
3.  **Bond Market Movement:** Watch for further developments in Indian government bonds, particularly concerning foreign investor interest and any news related to global index inclusion.
4.  **Specific Stock Reactions:** Observe stocks like RITES and RailTel, which have recently secured significant orders, and Reliance Power, which is riding the AI sentiment.
5.  **Global Cues:** Continue to monitor global market sentiment, especially from US tech, as it often influences FII flows and overall market direction.

## Bottom line
Indian markets are likely to see a cautious open today, influenced by mixed global cues and significant FII outflows. While DIIs continue to provide strong domestic support, the weak guidance from KPIT Technologies could weigh on the IT sector. Investors should remain watchful of sector-specific news and global developments.
