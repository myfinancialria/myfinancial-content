# Market Wrap — 25 May 2026

**TL;DR**
- Indian equities extended their winning streak, with benchmark indices Nifty 50 and Bank Nifty closing significantly higher.
- Banking and PSU Bank sectors led the charge, while FMCG and IT saw muted performance.
- Midcap and Smallcap indices also posted healthy gains, indicating broad market participation.
- Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) continued their selling trend, while Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) provided strong buying support.
- Volatility, as measured by India VIX, cooled down considerably, suggesting reduced market apprehension.

## How the market closed

Indian benchmark indices witnessed a robust session today, with the Nifty 50 surging past the 24,000 mark to close at 24,031.70, registering a gain of 1.32%. The Bank Nifty outperformed, climbing 2.29% to settle at 55,293.65, driven by strong buying interest in banking stocks. Broader markets also participated in the rally, with both Midcap and Smallcap indices closing in the green.

| Index | Close | Change | High | Low |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nifty 50 | 24031.70 | +1.32% | 24054.45 | 23922.85 |
| Bank Nifty | 55293.65 | +2.29% | 55405.20 | 54590.70 |
| Midcap 100 | 61966.60 | +0.94% | 62034.70 | 61777.00 |
| Smallcap 100 | 18202.70 | +1.37% | 18216.85 | 18118.55 |

The India VIX, a key gauge of market volatility, dropped by 6.76% to 16.70, indicating a significant easing of investor anxiety and a more stable market outlook.

## Sectors — winners and losers

The banking sector was the clear outperformer today, with PSU Banks leading the gains.

**Top 3 Gainer Sectors:**
*   **PSU BANK:** +2.90% - This sector saw strong buying interest, potentially buoyed by news such as LIC increasing its stake in Central Bank of India.
*   **BANK:** +2.29% - The broader banking index also performed exceptionally well, contributing significantly to the Nifty's upward movement.
*   **AUTO:** +1.71% - Automobile stocks saw healthy gains, reflecting positive sentiment in the sector.

**Top 3 Loser Sectors:**
*   **FMCG:** -0.18% - This defensive sector saw a marginal decline, indicating a shift towards more cyclical sectors.
*   **IT:** +0.23% - The IT sector posted a modest gain, largely remaining flat compared to the broader market rally.
*   **MEDIA:** +0.28% - The Media sector also saw minimal positive movement, underperforming the broader indices.

## Market breadth

The market breadth was overwhelmingly positive today, with 375 advances against 124 declines out of a total of 499 stocks. This indicates that the rally was broad-based, with a significant majority of stocks participating in the upward movement, reflecting strong underlying bullish sentiment.

## FII / DII flows

Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) continued their selling spree for the fourth consecutive session, offloading Indian equities worth ₹-4,440 crore today. However, Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) provided robust counter-support, pumping in ₹6,004 crore into the market, more than offsetting the FII outflows.

| Investor Type | Net Cash Position (₹ crore) |
|---|---|
| FII | -4,440 |
| DII | 6,004 |

Over the past four trading sessions, FIIs have consistently been net sellers, with cumulative outflows of approximately ₹10,385 crore. In contrast, DIIs have been consistent net buyers, providing strong support to the market with cumulative inflows of around ₹14,266 crore during the same period, indicating domestic resilience against foreign selling pressure.

## What moved markets today

Several factors contributed to today's market dynamics, ranging from global optimism to domestic regulatory and corporate developments.

*   **Global Optimism on Geopolitical Front:**
    European shares climbed to over two-month highs, driven by optimism regarding potential peace between Iran and the US. This positive global sentiment likely spilled over into Indian markets, contributing to the overall bullish mood.

*   **Rupee Strength and RBI Intervention:**
    The Indian rupee extended its winning streak for the third day, closing stronger against the dollar. This was primarily attributed to the Reserve Bank of India's market interventions and a significant drop in crude oil prices, which is a positive for India's import bill and inflation outlook.

*   **SEBI's Proposal for Dynamic Options Strike Framework:**
    The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) proposed a new framework to manage options strike prices during volatile trading sessions. This move aims to improve strike availability and ensure trading continuity, which could enhance market efficiency and investor confidence in the derivatives segment.

*   **Inflation Concerns and RBI's Stance:**
    Nithin Kamath, a prominent market voice, warned about potential inflation spikes due to a weak monsoon (El Niño) and rising global oil prices from the Iran conflict. He suggested that the RBI might not maintain a soft stance on interest rates, a factor that could influence future monetary policy decisions and market expectations.

*   **Corporate Actions and IPO Buzz:**
    Life Insurance Corporation (LIC) increased its stake in Central Bank of India to 6.06%, which likely contributed to the strong performance of PSU Bank stocks. Separately, quick-commerce firm Zepto is reportedly planning to file papers for a $1 billion IPO in June, signaling continued vibrancy in India's IPO market.

## Setup for tomorrow

Indian markets are likely to carry forward the positive momentum into tomorrow's session, supported by easing global geopolitical tensions and a stronger rupee. However, investors will keep a close watch on crude oil prices and any further commentary on inflation, especially in light of Nithin Kamath's recent remarks. The sustained FII selling trend, though offset by DII buying, remains a factor to monitor.

Globally, US 10-year yield has softened, and Brent Crude has seen a significant decline, which are generally positive for emerging markets like India. Asian markets will likely react to overnight global cues. Key data releases or corporate earnings announcements could also influence specific sectors and stocks tomorrow.
