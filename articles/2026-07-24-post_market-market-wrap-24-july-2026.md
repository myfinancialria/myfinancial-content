# Market Wrap — 24 July 2026

**TL;DR**
- Indian equities saw a mixed closing today, with the Nifty 50 declining while Bank Nifty posted gains.
- Global cues, particularly concerns over rising oil prices and stagflation fears, weighed on overall sentiment.
- Action Construction Equipment Ltd. (ACE) was the standout gainer among Nifty 500 stocks.
- Go Digit General Insurance Ltd. (GODIGIT) emerged as the biggest loser in the Nifty 500 pack.

## How the market behaved today
The Indian market witnessed a day of mixed performance, with the benchmark Nifty 50 closing in the red, while the Bank Nifty managed to eke out gains. The Nifty 50 opened with a slight gap down and traded with a negative bias for most of the session, eventually closing at 23767.45, down 0.43%. In contrast, the Bank Nifty showed resilience, closing at 56693.50, up 0.18%, after trading within a broader range. Midcap and Smallcap indices also ended with minor losses, indicating a cautious mood across broader markets.

The India VIX, a measure of market volatility, rose by 4.08% to 14.03, suggesting an increase in expected market fluctuations.

## What moved the market — the factors
Today's market movement was largely influenced by a combination of global concerns, domestic institutional buying offsetting foreign selling, and a mixed bag of corporate earnings. Global markets were cautious, with European shares remaining flat amidst rising oil prices and escalating Middle East tensions, which revived fears of stagflation. The significant plunge in Tesla shares overnight also contributed to a cautious global sentiment.

Domestically, Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) continued their selling streak, offloading ₹2,999 crore in the cash market. This selling pressure was largely absorbed by Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs), who bought equities worth ₹2,947 crore, providing crucial support and preventing a sharper decline in the broader indices. The RBI's intervention to shield the rupee from plunging to record lows also played a role in maintaining some stability.

Sector-wise, the IT and Media sectors saw buying interest, with companies like KPIT Technologies and PVR INOX featuring among the top gainers. Conversely, the Auto, Energy, and Realty sectors faced selling pressure, likely influenced by the global concerns around oil prices and broader economic outlook. A slew of Q1 corporate earnings also dictated stock-specific movements, with some companies reporting strong growth while others saw profit declines.

## News that moved stocks
- **ICICI Bank prices $1 billion debt in largest dollar issue by Indian private lender**: ICICI Bank successfully issued a five-year dollar bond worth $1 billion, marking the largest such offering by an Indian bank in nearly fourteen years. This news is positive for **ICICI Bank** and the broader **banking sector**, showcasing confidence in Indian private lenders.
- **RBI Intervention shields rupee in face of oil, hedging strain**: The Reserve Bank of India's decisive action, with state-run banks selling dollars, prevented the Indian rupee from hitting record lows today. This intervention helped stabilize the **Indian rupee** and averted potential negative sentiment across the **broader market**.
- **ACC Q1 results: Profit falls 61% YoY to Rs 147 crore as revenue, volumes decline**: Cement major ACC reported a significant 61% year-on-year drop in its June-quarter profit due to lower cement volumes and weaker operating margins. This news is negative for **ACC** shares.
- **Tata Consumer Q1 Results: Net profit rises 28% YoY to Rs 427 crore, revenue up 12%**: FMCG major Tata Consumer Products posted a strong 28% year-on-year rise in consolidated net profit for Q1 FY27, driven by a 12% increase in revenue. This is positive news for **Tata Consumer Products** and the **FMCG sector**.
- **SBI Life Q1 results: Profit rises 22% YoY to Rs 720 crore on strong premium growth**: SBI Life Insurance reported a robust 22% year-on-year increase in its June-quarter profit, fueled by strong growth in new business and renewal premiums. This is positive for **SBI Life Insurance** and the **insurance sector**.
- **Sterlite Tech Q1 Results: Profit skyrockets 870% YoY to Rs 197 crore as AI data centre orders lift earnings**: Sterlite Technologies announced record quarterly revenue and an astounding 870% year-on-year profit increase, driven by strong AI-led demand for optical connectivity and data centre solutions. This news is highly positive for **Sterlite Technologies** and highlights the potential in the **IT and telecom infrastructure sectors**.
- **Hindustan Zinc Q1 Results: Net profit spikes 145% YoY to Rs 5,469 crore, revenue jumps 77%**: Hindustan Zinc reported a substantial 145% year-on-year surge in net profit and a 77% rise in revenue for Q1 FY27. This is very positive for **Hindustan Zinc**.
- **Bank of Baroda Q1 results: Profit comes at ₹1,278 crore; NII rises, NIM shrinks YoY**: Bank of Baroda reported a profit of ₹1,278 crore for Q1, with net interest income rising but net interest margin shrinking year-on-year. This news provides a mixed picture for **Bank of Baroda**.
- **Kalind shares jump 5% on ex-bonus, ex-split day; investors see 86% price adjustment**: Kalind's shares saw a 5% jump today, though a technical adjustment due to a 1:5 stock split and 1:2 bonus issue resulted in an 86% decline in demat accounts. This event is specific to **Kalind** and its shareholders.
- **NSE to launch India's first domestic benchmark-based natural gas futures on July 27**: The National Stock Exchange (NSE) announced the launch of India's first cash-settled, domestic benchmark-based natural gas futures contract. This development is significant for the **energy market** and **commodity traders** in India, aiming for transparent price discovery.

## Event breakdown — what happened today and how it hit the market
Today's session was primarily driven by a confluence of corporate earnings announcements, persistent global concerns, and active participation from domestic institutional investors. Several companies released their Q1 results, leading to stock-specific movements. For instance, **Sterlite Technologies** surged on stellar AI-led demand, while **ACC** saw its profit decline. The **banking sector** was in focus with ICICI Bank's successful debt issue and Bank of Baroda's mixed Q1 results, contributing to the Bank Nifty's positive close.

Globally, renewed fears of stagflation due to escalating Gulf conflict and rising oil prices (though Brent crude saw a significant drop today after earlier concerns) kept investors cautious. The RBI's intervention to support the rupee also underscored the ongoing vigilance required in the face of global economic headwinds. The net selling by FIIs against the strong buying by DIIs highlighted the contrasting views between foreign and domestic participants on the current market valuations and outlook.

## Setup for tomorrow
Looking ahead to tomorrow, global cues will continue to be important. While Brent Crude and WTI Crude saw significant declines today, the broader concerns around oil prices and stagflation could still linger. The US 10-year yield saw a slight increase. Investors will be watching for further developments in global markets, particularly how US and Asian markets react overnight. Domestically, the ongoing Q1 earnings season will continue to drive stock-specific action. Key levels for Nifty 50 and Bank Nifty will be crucial to watch for directional cues, with support from DIIs expected to remain a key factor.
