# Market Wrap — 18 June 2026

**TL;DR**
- Indian benchmark indices, Nifty 50 and Bank Nifty, closed higher today, extending their positive run.
- Broader markets also saw gains, with both Nifty Midcap 100 and Smallcap 100 ending in the green.
- Realty and Banking sectors led the gains, while the IT sector experienced a notable decline.
- Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) turned net buyers for the day, while Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) continued their strong buying spree.
- India VIX cooled off, indicating a reduction in market volatility.

## What moved markets today

**1. Indian Bond Rally Defies Hawkish Fed on Oil Rout**
Indian government bonds extended their rally for a sixth consecutive day. This was primarily driven by falling oil prices, which boosted investor confidence despite a hawkish outlook from the US Federal Reserve. The decline in crude oil prices is generally positive for India, a major oil importer, as it helps in managing inflation and current account deficit.

**2. Sebi Board to Consider Reintroducing Open-Market Window for Buybacks**
The Securities and Exchange Board of India (Sebi) board is set to meet on Friday to discuss several proposals, including the reintroduction of open-market share buybacks with a quicker turnaround. This potential regulatory change could offer companies more flexibility in capital allocation and potentially provide support to stock prices through buyback programs.

**3. Accenture Cuts Revenue Outlook, Impacts IT Sector Sentiment**
Global IT consulting giant Accenture lowered its annual revenue growth forecast, citing continued caution in technology spending by companies, even amidst investments in AI and cybersecurity. This news likely contributed to the significant decline observed in the Indian IT sector today, as it signals potential headwinds for the broader technology services industry.

**4. Rupee Posts Longest Winning Run in a Year on Exporter Flows**
The Indian rupee strengthened against the U.S. dollar for the fifth consecutive day, marking its longest rally in a year. This appreciation was attributed to aggressive dollar selling by lenders and exporters. A stronger rupee can be beneficial for controlling imported inflation and improving India's terms of trade.

**5. NSE Eyes ₹5 Trillion IPO Valuation; Boosts Insurance Stocks**
News of the National Stock Exchange (NSE) eyeing a ₹5 trillion IPO valuation highlighted the eye-popping returns for its early investors. Notably, New India Assurance (NIACL) and National Insurance stand to gain significantly. This news likely provided a boost to NIACL shares, which were among today's top gainers.

## Setup for tomorrow

Indian markets are likely to take cues from global developments and domestic institutional flows. The consistent buying by DIIs provides a strong floor, while FII sentiment will be crucial. Investors will also keep an eye on the upcoming Sebi board meeting outcomes, particularly regarding buyback rules.

Globally, US futures have shown recovery, while crude oil prices have fallen significantly, which is a positive for India. However, the strengthening dollar and the hawkish stance of the US Federal Reserve will remain key factors to monitor overnight.
