# Market Wrap — 21 October 2026

**TL;DR**
- Indian equities witnessed a broad-based decline today, with all major indices closing in the red.
- The market was primarily weighed down by significant selling pressure in mid and small-cap stocks and sustained FII outflows.
- India Cements emerged as the top gainer among Nifty 500 stocks, surging over 12%.
- Himadri Speciality Chemical was the biggest loser, dropping nearly 11%.
- Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) remained net sellers, while Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) provided crucial buying support.
- F&O positioning data is unavailable for today and will be included from the next wrap.

## How the market behaved today
Indian benchmark indices closed lower today, reversing early gains to end the session in negative territory. The Nifty 50 opened higher but saw selling pressure throughout the day, closing at 22811.35, down 0.34%. The Bank Nifty also followed suit, shedding 0.92% to settle at 54976.90. The broader market experienced a sharper correction, with the Nifty Midcap 100 declining by 1.58% and the Nifty Smallcap 100 falling by a significant 2.37%, indicating widespread profit-booking.

| Index | Close | Change | High | Low |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nifty 50 | 22811.35 | -0.34% | 23120.90 | 22649.90 |
| Bank Nifty | 54976.90 | -0.92% | 55867.45 | 54696.45 |
| Nifty Midcap 100 | 57565.85 | -1.58% | 58713.25 | 57133.70 |
| Nifty Smallcap 100 | 18487.30 | -2.37% | 19009.75 | 18373.05 |

The India VIX, a measure of market volatility, rose by 6.16% to 15.68, signalling increased investor apprehension.

## What moved the market — the factors
Today's market action was largely influenced by a combination of factors. Global cues were mixed, with US stocks opening higher on positive results from SpaceX and Super Micro, particularly boosting AI-related stocks, though Netflix's slowing sales growth raised concerns. Domestically, the market witnessed sustained outflows from Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs), who were net sellers for the fourth time in five sessions. This selling was largely absorbed by Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs), who continued to provide strong buying support.

The broader market, especially the Nifty Midcap 100 and Nifty Smallcap 100, bore the brunt of the selling, indicating profit-booking after recent rallies. Sectorally, the IT sector was a standout performer, gaining nearly 3%, likely driven by positive sentiment around global tech and specific stock recommendations. Conversely, sectors like Metal, Auto, and Energy faced significant selling pressure. The increase in India VIX also suggested heightened caution among investors. The ongoing Jio Platforms IPO, which saw strong institutional demand on its first day, was a key event, with Moody's terming it credit positive for Reliance Industries.

## Sectors — winners and losers
The market saw a clear divergence in sectoral performance today, with Information Technology leading the gains while Metal and Auto sectors lagged.

**Top 3 gainer sectors:**
- **IT:** +2.88%. The sector saw strong buying interest, potentially buoyed by positive global tech sentiment and specific stock recommendations.
- **PHARMA:** -0.19%. Despite a slight dip, the pharmaceutical sector showed relative resilience compared to the broader market.
- **PSU BANK:** -0.51%. Public sector banks also managed to limit their losses, outperforming the overall market decline.

**Top 3 loser sectors:**
- **METAL:** -1.71%. The metal sector faced significant selling pressure today.
- **AUTO:** -1.54%. Automobile stocks witnessed profit-booking, contributing to the market's decline.
- **ENERGY:** -1.39%. Energy stocks also ended the day with notable losses.

## Market breadth
The market breadth was overwhelmingly negative today, reflecting the broad-based selling pressure. Out of 495 stocks traded on the NSE, only 80 advanced, while a significant 415 declined. This indicates that the selling was not confined to a few pockets but was widespread across the market.

## FII / DII flows
Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) continued their selling streak today, while Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) provided strong counter-buying.

| Investor Category | Net Flow (₹ crore) |
|---|---|
| FII | -1,149 |
| DII | 3,201 |

Looking at the 5-day history, FIIs have been net sellers for four out of the last five trading sessions, indicating a cautious stance from foreign investors. In contrast, DIIs have been consistent net buyers throughout this period, absorbing the foreign outflows and providing crucial support to the Indian markets.

## Event breakdown — what happened today and how it hit the market
Today's session was influenced by a mix of corporate earnings, the ongoing Jio Platforms IPO, and mixed global cues.

The **Jio Platforms IPO** commenced today, receiving a strong response on its first day, with the issue subscribed 3.16 times, primarily driven by robust institutional demand. This event is seen as credit positive for Reliance Industries, potentially enhancing its financial flexibility.

Several companies announced their **Q2 earnings**, which had stock-specific impacts. Mahindra Finance reported a 55% surge in net profit, while PC Jewellers saw a 58% jump and declared itself debt-free. Indian Hotels Company Ltd (IHCL) also posted a 19% rise in net profit. These positive results provided some individual stock strength amidst the broader market weakness.

**Global cues** were mixed. While US stocks opened higher, buoyed by strong results from SpaceX and Super Micro Computer, particularly in the AI sector, concerns over Netflix's slowing sales growth tempered the overall sentiment. This mixed global picture, combined with consistent FII outflows, contributed to the cautious mood in the Indian market.

## Setup for tomorrow
Looking ahead to tomorrow, the market will likely continue to monitor global cues and FII flow trends. The strong institutional participation in the Jio Platforms IPO could provide some underlying support, but the broader market sentiment remains cautious given the recent selling in mid and small caps. Key levels for Nifty 50 and Bank Nifty will be crucial to watch.

Globally, the USD/INR closed at 94.45, down 0.57%, while the Dollar Index was marginally up at 101.77. Brent Crude was at $108.58, down 0.19%, and WTI Crude saw a significant drop of 4.85% to $96.85. Gold and Silver showed slight gains, while the US 10Y Yield edged lower to 5.10. These global commodity and currency movements will provide overnight pointers for tomorrow's Indian trading session.
