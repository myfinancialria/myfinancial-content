# Market Wrap — 26 May 2026

**TL;DR**
- Indian benchmark indices Nifty 50 and Bank Nifty closed in the red, impacted by selling pressure in banking stocks.
- Midcap and Smallcap indices, however, showed resilience, ending the session with decent gains.
- Metal and Energy sectors were among the top performers, while Realty and PSU Bank sectors dragged.
- FIIs turned net buyers after several days, injecting ₹822 crore, while DIIs continued their strong buying spree.
- Global cues were mixed, with crude oil rebounding amid geopolitical tensions, but US futures pointing to a higher open.

## How the market closed
Indian equity benchmarks witnessed a subdued session today, with the Nifty 50 shedding 0.49% to close at 23,913.70, while the Bank Nifty also ended lower by 0.36% at 55,092.90. Midcap and Smallcap segments, however, defied the broader market trend, posting gains.

| Index | Close | Change | High | Low |
| :---------------- | :---------- | :------- | :--------- | :--------- |
| Nifty 50 | 23913.70 | -0.49% | 24089.80 | 23885.45 |
| Bank Nifty | 55092.90 | -0.36% | 55536.80 | 54979.75 |
| Midcap 100 | 62298.90 | +0.54% | 62365.25 | 61875.85 |
| Smallcap 100 | 18267.20 | +0.35% | 18393.10 | 18217.15 |

The India VIX, a measure of market volatility, cooled off by 3.41% to 16.13, indicating a slight reduction in market apprehension.

## Sectors — winners and losers
The session saw a mixed sectoral performance, with Metal and Energy leading the gains, while financial sectors faced headwinds.

**Top 3 Gainer Sectors:**
- **METAL:** +1.10% - The sector saw buying interest today.
- **ENERGY:** +0.58% - Energy stocks gained amidst a rebound in crude oil prices globally.
- **FMCG:** +0.14% - The fast-moving consumer goods sector ended marginally higher.

**Top 3 Loser Sectors:**
- **REALTY:** -0.52% - The realty sector witnessed some profit booking.
- **PSU BANK:** -0.46% - Public sector banks faced selling pressure.
- **BANK:** -0.36% - The broader banking sector was among the top losers, contributing to the Nifty's decline.

## Market breadth
The market breadth was slightly negative today, with 238 advances against 258 declines out of a total of 496 stocks traded. This indicates that more stocks declined than advanced, suggesting a cautious sentiment across the broader market, despite the midcap and smallcap indices closing in the green.

## FII / DII flows
Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) turned net buyers today, injecting ₹822 crore into the Indian markets. Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) continued their strong buying trend, with a net inflow of ₹3,857 crore.

| Investor Type | Net Cash Position (₹ crore) |
| :------------ | :-------------------------- |
| FII | +822 |

Looking at the 5-day history, FIIs have been net sellers for the past four sessions, making today's positive inflow a notable shift. DIIs, on the other hand, have consistently been net buyers, providing crucial support to the market.

## What moved markets today

**1. Banking Stocks Falter, Mid & Smallcaps Shine**
The Nifty and Sensex closed lower today, primarily dragged down by banking and financial stocks. This downturn in heavyweight banking counters contributed significantly to the benchmark indices ending in the red. In contrast, the midcap and smallcap segments showed robust performance, indicating a rotation of capital towards broader market segments.
*Market Impact:* Negative for Nifty and Bank Nifty, positive for Midcap and Smallcap indices.

**2. Rupee Slips as Geopolitical Tensions Escalate**
The Indian rupee weakened against the dollar today, mirroring its Asian peers. This was largely attributed to fading hopes of an imminent U.S.-Iran peace deal, which led to a surge in crude oil prices. State-run banks reportedly intervened to cushion the rupee's fall.
*Market Impact:* Negative for the rupee, potential inflationary concerns due to rising crude oil prices.

**3. SEBI Chief Emphasises India's Diversified Market and Bond Market Development**
SEBI chief Tuhin Kanta Pandey highlighted India's diversified market structure, contrasting it with Taiwan's market, which is heavily concentrated due to the AI boom. He also advocated for deeper development of India's corporate bond market, proposing bond ETFs and tokenisation to support long-term economic growth as debt fundraising nears ₹9 lakh crore.
*Market Impact:* Positive for long-term market sentiment and development of the corporate bond market, reinforcing confidence in India's economic fundamentals.

**4. Mixed Corporate Earnings Reports**
Today saw a few corporate earnings announcements. Brainbees Solutions, the parent company of Firstcry, narrowed its Q4FY26 net loss to ₹30.30 crore with a 12% revenue uptick. IFB Industries also reported a significant profit increase, with its net profit doubling to ₹42.67 crore in the March quarter. Conversely, Pine Labs was among the top losers after its quarterly earnings.
*Market Impact:* Stock-specific movements based on individual company performance; overall, a mixed bag for corporate results.

## Setup for tomorrow
Tomorrow's session is likely to be influenced by global cues and the continuation of the domestic institutional buying trend. While geopolitical tensions continue to keep crude oil prices volatile, US stock futures are pointing to a higher open, driven by optimism around AI. Investors will be closely watching for further developments on the global front.

**Global Cues:** US stock futures are positive, suggesting a potentially upbeat start for global markets. Brent Crude closed lower at $96.30, while WTI Crude also saw a decline to $92.90, despite earlier reports of a rebound. The USD/INR closed at 95.69.
