# Market Wrap — 16 October 2026

**TL;DR**
- The Indian equity benchmarks, Nifty 50 and Bank Nifty, closed higher today, marking a positive end to the week.
- A strong rally in the IT sector, driven by positive earnings news and global tech sentiment, was the primary catalyst for the Nifty's gains.
- HBL Engineering Ltd. emerged as the top gainer in the Nifty 500, surging over 17%.
- R R Kabel Ltd. was the biggest laggard among the Nifty 500 stocks, declining more than 8%.
- Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) continued their selling streak, offloading ₹5,285 crore, while Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) provided support with net buying of ₹3,319 crore.
- F&O positioning signal: Data unavailable for today's build-up, will be available from the next wrap.

## How the market behaved today
The Indian market witnessed a positive close on Friday, with the benchmark Nifty 50 recovering from an early dip to end the day with a gain of 0.65% at 23,146.15. The index opened lower but saw a steady upward trend throughout the session, reaching its day's high just before closing. The Bank Nifty also closed in the green, albeit with a modest gain of 0.06% at 55,844.00. In contrast, the broader markets, represented by the Nifty Midcap 100 and Nifty Smallcap 100, saw marginal declines, indicating a somewhat selective buying interest.

| Index | Close | Change | High | Low |
| :------------------ | :---------- | :---------- | :---------- | :---------- |
| Nifty 50 | 23146.15 | +0.65% | 23149.85 | 22913.80 |
| Bank Nifty | 55844.00 | +0.06% | 55989.55 | 55526.30 |
| Nifty Midcap 100 | 59129.75 | -0.11% | 59302.15 | 58775.90 |
| Nifty Smallcap 100 | 19129.80 | -0.01% | 19194.95 | 19069.85 |

India VIX, the volatility gauge, saw a slight increase of 0.21% to 14.06, suggesting that market participants anticipate a relatively stable, though not entirely calm, environment in the near term.

## What moved the market — the factors
Today's market movement was primarily driven by a robust performance from the Information Technology (IT) sector, which surged nearly 4%. This strong sectoral tailwind helped the Nifty 50 overcome persistent selling pressure from Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs). Despite FIIs offloading over ₹5,200 crore, strong buying by Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) provided crucial support, preventing a sharper decline.

Heavyweight IT stocks like Tech Mahindra and Wipro were significant contributors to the Nifty's upward trajectory, buoyed by positive earnings reports from LTM and a general positive sentiment around the sector, even as global cues from US markets showed some "tech jitters" related to AI stocks. The strengthening Indian Rupee, which gained 1.9% this week to close below 95 per dollar, also contributed to overall market confidence. However, the broader market, including Midcap and Smallcap indices, remained subdued, indicating that the rally was concentrated in specific sectors and large-cap stocks. The ongoing Q2 earnings season also played a pivotal role, with several companies announcing their results and guiding market sentiment.

## Sectors — winners and losers
The IT sector led the charge today, while FMCG, Realty, and Pharma sectors faced some headwinds.

**Top 3 Gainer Sectors:**
- **IT:** +3.92% - The sector saw strong buying interest, likely fueled by positive earnings reports and a generally optimistic outlook despite global tech concerns.
- **MEDIA:** +1.92% - Media stocks witnessed a healthy bounce, indicating renewed investor interest.
- **METAL:** +0.95% - The Metal sector also closed in positive territory, benefiting from broader market sentiment.

**Top 3 Loser Sectors:**
- **FMCG:** -0.39% - Fast Moving Consumer Goods stocks saw some profit booking today.
- **REALTY:** -0.33% - Despite positive news from DLF and Oberoi Realty, the sector ended marginally lower.
- **PHARMA:** -0.14% - The Pharma sector experienced a slight dip, indicating cautious investor sentiment.

## News that moved stocks
Here's a look at some of the key news items that influenced market sentiment and specific stocks today:

**Q2 Earnings Season in Focus**
- **DLF Q2 profit rises to Rs 1,301 crore as luxury project sells out**: DLF reported an 11% YoY increase in net profit and strong sales bookings, with its Aureva luxury retirement project selling out. This positive news likely supported the stock, even as the broader Realty sector saw a slight decline.
- **Federal Bank Q2 profit jumps 45% to record Rs 1,386 crore**: Federal Bank posted record Q2 profit, driven by strong net interest income and improved asset quality. This robust performance contributed to positive sentiment in the banking sector.
- **LTM Q2 Results: PAT remains flat at Rs 1,380 crore; revenue rises 14% YoY; co declares Rs 25 per share dividend**: LTM reported a marginal profit decline but strong revenue growth, especially in AI segments, and declared a dividend. This news likely contributed to the overall positive sentiment in the IT sector, benefiting stocks like Tech Mahindra and Wipro.
- **Bajaj Housing Finance Q2 Results: PAT rises 18% YoY to Rs 757 crore, AUM grows 25%**: Bajaj Housing Finance reported strong growth in profit and assets under management, reflecting a healthy performance in the financial services sector.
- **Oberoi Realty Q2 results, dividend 2026: Check amount, record date, yield, history, stock returns**: Oberoi Realty declared an interim dividend and reported a slight increase in Q2 net profit, providing some support to the realty space.

**IPO and Fundraising Activities**
- **Cult.fit receives Sebi approval for proposed IPO**: Fitness major Cult.fit received SEBI's nod for its IPO, aiming to raise up to ₹950 crore. This development highlights ongoing activity in the primary market.
- **Ujjivan SFB to raise Rs 409 crore from IFC, three insurers**: Ujjivan Small Finance Bank plans to raise capital through a preferential share allotment and reported a significant jump in Q2 net profit. This news indicates growth and investor confidence in the small finance bank segment.

**Sector-Specific Developments**
- **DEE Development Engineers signs framework pact with domestic EPC firm for nuclear power sector**: This agreement strengthens DEE Development Engineers' presence in the nuclear power sector, potentially boosting investor confidence in the capital goods segment.
- **This small-cap AI stock has surged 250% in 2026: What is behind its stellar market performance?**: E2E Networks shares have seen a significant surge, driven by demand for AI infrastructure and NVIDIA-related deals. This highlights the strong investor interest in AI-related themes.

**Macroeconomic Cues**
- **Rupee rises 1.9% in a week, closes below 95 per dollar**: The Indian rupee strengthened significantly, supported by aggressive RBI measures and a weaker dollar index. A stronger rupee is generally positive for the economy and can attract foreign investment.
- **Aggressive RBI measures hand India bonds a ninth weekly loss**: Indian government bonds continued their losing streak due to the RBI's monetary tightening measures, indicating the central bank's focus on controlling inflation.

## Market breadth
The market breadth was largely balanced today, with 251 advances against 242 declines out of a total of 493 stocks. This indicates that while the headline indices moved up, the gains were not broad-based, and a nearly equal number of stocks saw positive and negative movements.

## FII / DII flows
Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) continued their selling spree today, offloading a significant ₹5,285 crore from the Indian equity markets. This marks the fifth consecutive day of FII outflows. In contrast, Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) remained net buyers, providing crucial support by investing ₹3,319 crore.

| Investor Type | Net Flow (₹ crore) |
| :------------ | :----------------- |
| FII | -5,285 |
| DII | 3,319 |

Looking at the 5-day history, FIIs have been consistent net sellers, with outflows totaling ₹21,600 crore over the last five trading sessions. This sustained selling pressure from foreign investors has been a notable factor in the market dynamics. DIIs, on the other hand, have consistently been net buyers, absorbing a substantial portion of the FII selling, which has helped cushion the market from sharper declines. This trend suggests that domestic money is actively supporting the market amidst foreign outflows.

## Event breakdown — what happened today and how it hit the market
Today's session was largely influenced by the ongoing Q2 earnings season and a strong sectoral rotation towards Information Technology. Several companies, including DLF, Federal Bank, LTM, Bajaj Housing Finance, and Oberoi Realty, announced their Q2 results, providing specific triggers for their respective stocks and sectors. The IT sector, in particular, saw a significant rally, with stocks like Tech Mahindra and Wipro leading the gains, possibly buoyed by LTM's revenue growth in AI and overall positive sentiment despite "tech jitters" in US markets.

The persistent selling by FIIs, which has been a consistent theme this week, was largely offset by robust buying from DIIs. This domestic support prevented a more significant market correction, especially in large-cap stocks. On the macroeconomic front, the Indian Rupee's continued strengthening against the dollar, reaching its strongest weekly gain in seven months, was a positive development, indicating improved currency stability. Conversely, the bond market continued to face pressure due to the RBI's aggressive monetary tightening measures, leading to a ninth consecutive weekly loss for Indian government bonds.

## Setup for tomorrow
As we head into the weekend, global cues will be closely watched for tomorrow's session. US markets opened muted today with lingering tech jitters, which could influence Indian IT stocks. However, crude oil prices saw a decline (Brent Crude -0.19%, WTI Crude -2.38%), which is generally positive for India as an oil importer. Gold and Silver saw gains, while the Dollar Index weakened and the US 10-year yield edged higher. Investors will continue to monitor Q2 earnings announcements and FII flow trends. Key levels to watch for Nifty 50 will be around its day's high of 23,149.85 for upward momentum and the 22,900-23,000 zone for support.
