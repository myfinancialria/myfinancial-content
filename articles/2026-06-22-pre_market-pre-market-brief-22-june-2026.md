# Pre-Market Brief — 22 June 2026

**TL;DR**
*   Global markets presented a mixed picture, with US tech indices showing strong gains, while European markets remained largely flat.
*   Asian markets, particularly Japan and Shanghai, closed significantly higher, driven by AI enthusiasm and positive sentiment.
*   Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) were significant net buyers yesterday, injecting nearly ₹4,859 crore into Indian equities, while Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) were net sellers.
*   The Indian Rupee strengthened against the US Dollar, while crude oil prices saw a decline.
*   NSE's new MoU with Bharat Metal Exchange aims to boost the non-ferrous metal derivatives market, potentially impacting the metals sector.

## Where global markets closed
Global markets closed with a mixed but largely positive sentiment. US indices, particularly the Nasdaq and S&P 500, posted strong gains, driven by persistent AI euphoria. European markets remained largely muted as investors weighed ongoing U.S.-Iran talks. In Asia, the Nikkei 225 surged past 72,000, also buoyed by AI investment, while Shanghai Composite also closed significantly higher. The Hang Seng, however, saw a modest decline.

The overall global tone suggests a cautious optimism, with technology and AI-driven sectors leading gains, while geopolitical considerations keep some markets subdued.

## Currency, commodities and yields
The Indian Rupee (USD/INR) strengthened against the US Dollar, trading at ₹94.69, a gain of 0.38%. This could provide some relief on the import bill front. The Dollar Index saw a marginal increase of 0.10% to 100.95, indicating a slight strengthening of the dollar against a basket of major currencies.

In the commodities market, crude oil prices eased, with Brent Crude down 0.41% to $79.52 and WTI Crude falling 1.32% to $75.59. This decline in crude prices is generally positive for India, a major oil importer, as it can help manage inflation and reduce import costs. Gold prices also saw a slight dip of 0.39% to $4207.50, while Silver declined by 0.52% to $65.91. The US 10-year Treasury Yield decreased by 0.27% to 4.45%, which could signal a slight shift towards safer assets or expectations of stable interest rates.

## Recent FII/DII flows
Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) turned significant net buyers on Friday, injecting ₹4,859 crore into the Indian equity market, marking a strong reversal after a mixed week. Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs), however, were net sellers, offloading equities worth ₹1,160 crore.

| Date         | FII Net (₹ crore) | DII Net (₹ crore) |
| :----------- | :---------------- | :---------------- |
| 2026-06-19   | +4,859            | -1,160            |

Over the last five sessions, FIIs have shown a mixed trend with two days of net buying and three days of net selling, resulting in a net inflow of ₹3,397 crore. DIIs, on the other hand, have been net buyers in three of the last five sessions, with a total net inflow of ₹7,107 crore, indicating consistent domestic support.

## Overnight news that matters
**NSE signs MoU with Bharat Metal Exchange to boost non-ferrous metal derivatives market**
NSE has partnered with Bharat Metal Exchange to promote non-ferrous metal derivatives in India. This collaboration aims to enhance price risk management and increase market participation in the metals sector. This development could lead to increased activity and liquidity in metal-related derivative products on the exchange, potentially benefiting companies in the non-ferrous metals industry.

**Japan's Nikkei rises past 72,000 as AI euphoria persists**
Japan's Nikkei index reached a new high, surpassing 72,000, driven by strong investor enthusiasm for Artificial Intelligence (AI) investments and positive developments in U.S.-Iran peace talks. This reflects a broader global trend of AI-driven market rallies, which could influence investor sentiment towards Indian technology and AI-related stocks.

**11,983% profit! HFCL promoter’s Rs 10 Jio bet may turn into Rs 5,800 crore windfall**
Jio Platforms has formally initiated its IPO process by filing its Draft Red Herring Prospectus (DRHP) with SEBI. This highlights the substantial wealth creation for early investors, with HFCL's promoter being a major beneficiary. The impending Jio Platforms IPO, anticipated by October-November, is a significant event for the Indian market, particularly for Reliance Industries, as it could unlock substantial value and attract considerable investor interest.

**Reliance, financials and defence top investment picks as market leadership broadens: Rahul Shah**
Market expert Rahul Shah suggests that Reliance Industries is poised for a turnaround, citing strong earnings, the upcoming Jio Platforms IPO, and investments in AI and energy. He also highlights small banks and defence stocks as attractive investment opportunities. This expert view suggests potential sector-specific movements, with Reliance and financial stocks likely to be in focus.

## What to watch in today's session
1.  **FII/DII Flows:** Monitor the FII and DII activity closely, especially after yesterday's significant FII inflow, to gauge sustained institutional interest.
2.  **Metal Sector Stocks:** Keep an eye on non-ferrous metal companies and related derivatives, following the NSE's MoU with Bharat Metal Exchange, which aims to boost this market segment.
3.  **Reliance Industries:** Any further news or developments regarding the Jio Platforms IPO could influence Reliance Industries' stock performance.
4.  **Currency Movement:** The movement of the Indian Rupee against the US Dollar will be crucial, given its recent strengthening and its implications for import costs and inflation.
5.  **Global Crude Oil Prices:** Continued softening of crude oil prices would be a positive for Indian markets, impacting inflation expectations and the profitability of oil marketing companies.

## Bottom line
Indian markets are likely to open with a positive bias today, influenced by strong closes in US tech and most Asian markets, coupled with significant FII buying on Friday. The easing of crude oil prices and the strengthening Rupee also provide a favourable backdrop. Investors should remain cautious and monitor global cues, institutional flow trends, and any updates on key corporate developments like the Jio Platforms IPO.
