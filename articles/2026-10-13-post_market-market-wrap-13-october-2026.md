# Market Wrap — 13 October 2026

**TL;DR**
*   Indian equities witnessed a robust rally, with the Nifty 50 closing at its day's high, up 1.60%.
*   Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman's comments on hearing investors regarding Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG) tax provided a significant boost to market sentiment.
*   Bandhan Bank Ltd. emerged as the top Nifty 500 gainer, surging over 9%.
*   RBL Bank Ltd. was the biggest Nifty 500 loser, declining over 7%.

## How the market behaved today
Indian markets staged a strong recovery today, with the Nifty 50 closing firmly in the green and marking its day's high at the closing bell. The benchmark Nifty 50 surged by 1.60% to settle at 22721.95, while the Bank Nifty also posted healthy gains, closing up 1.03% at 55481.05. The broader markets also participated in the rally, with the Nifty Midcap 100 gaining 1.06% and the Nifty Smallcap 100 rising 0.11%. The session saw a positive trend throughout the day, indicating broad-based buying interest.

The India VIX, a measure of market volatility, cooled off significantly, dropping by 7.41% to 14.12. This decline suggests a reduction in market uncertainty and a more stable outlook among investors.

## What moved the market — the factors
Today's market rally was primarily driven by positive domestic cues and strong buying by Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs). A key catalyst was Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman's statement indicating that the government would hear investors' concerns regarding Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG) tax, which significantly boosted market sentiment. This assurance helped alleviate some investor anxieties and encouraged fresh buying across sectors.

The Information Technology (IT) sector was a standout performer, surging over 5%, possibly buoyed by the broader global narrative around the AI boom driving wealth creation for tech billionaires, despite some concerns over US chip stocks. The Realty sector also saw robust gains, supported by strong Q2 sales bookings reported by major players like Prestige Estates and Puravankara.

While Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) continued their selling streak, DIIs stepped in with substantial buying, absorbing the FII outflows and providing crucial support to the market. The decline in India VIX also contributed to a more confident trading environment.

## Sectors — winners and losers

## News that moved stocks
*   **Finance Minister's Comments on LTCG Tax Boost Market Sentiment:** Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman stated that the government would hear investors' concerns regarding Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG) tax, as both Indian and foreign investors had raised questions about it. This statement provided a significant positive trigger, contributing to the broad market rally and adding approximately ₹5 lakh crore to the market capitalisation of listed companies.
*   **Realty Sector Sees Strong Q2 Sales Bookings:** Prestige Estates Projects Ltd. reported a 20% increase in Q2 sales bookings to ₹7,225 crore, driven by better housing demand and new residential project launches. Similarly, Puravankara Ltd. saw its Q2 sales bookings surge by 62% to ₹2,136 crore. These strong performance indicators contributed to the Realty sector's significant gains today.
*   **AI Boom Fuels Tech Billionaire Wealth, Mixed Signals for Chip Stocks:** Technology billionaires added a record $845 billion to their fortunes in the first nine months of 2026, primarily driven by the artificial intelligence boom. While this generally positive sentiment might have supported the Indian IT sector, US chip manufacturers like Micron and SK Hynix faced pressure due to competitive advancements from China's CXMT and concerns over AI spending.
*   **SEBI Extends IPO Flexibility and Proposes Review of Issue Proceeds:** SEBI extended the flexibility for companies to revise fresh issue sizes by up to 50% without a new draft prospectus, a move aimed at supporting primary market activity. Additionally, SEBI invited public comments on recommendations to review rules governing the monitoring and disclosure of issue proceeds by listed companies. These regulatory developments aim to streamline market processes and enhance transparency.
*   **NSEIX and London Stock Exchange Sign MoU:** NSE IFSC and the London Stock Exchange signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) to explore deeper market links, including dual listings, cross-listings, new financial products, and cross-border trading connectivity between GIFT City and London. This pact signals potential for increased collaboration and growth opportunities for Indian financial markets.

Over the past five trading sessions, FIIs have been consistent net sellers, offloading a total of ₹28,333 crore from the Indian markets. This trend highlights the domestic institutions' confidence in the Indian market, effectively absorbing foreign outflows.

## Event breakdown — what happened today and how it hit the market
The most significant event impacting the market today was Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman's statement regarding the government's openness to hear investor concerns on Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG) tax. This news acted as a major sentiment booster, as it signaled a willingness to address a long-standing point of contention for both domestic and foreign investors. The market reacted swiftly and positively, with the Nifty 50 rallying strongly to close at its day's high. The positive sentiment was broad-based, benefiting almost all sectors, though IT and Realty saw particularly strong gains. The Realty sector also received an additional boost from strong Q2 sales bookings reported by major developers like Prestige Estates and Puravankara.

## Setup for tomorrow
Looking ahead to tomorrow, global cues will continue to play a role, although today's domestic strength was notable. Overnight, the USD/INR closed lower at 95.27, and the Dollar Index also saw a slight dip. Brent crude closed lower at $105.55, while WTI crude was marginally up. US 10-year yield softened to 5.22%. While US chip stocks faced pressure due to competitive concerns from China, major US banks reported strong Q3 profits, supported by resilient consumer spending and AI investment. Investors will closely monitor global market movements and any further updates on the LTCG tax front. Q2 earnings season will also remain a key focus. Key levels to watch for the Nifty 50 will be around today's high, with support around the 22,500 mark.
