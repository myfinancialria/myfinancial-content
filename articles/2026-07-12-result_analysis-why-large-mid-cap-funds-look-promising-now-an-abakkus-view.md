# Why Large & Mid Cap Funds Look Promising Now: An Abakkus View

**TL;DR**
- An Abakkus Mutual Fund study suggests Large & Mid Cap Funds are an attractive investment category currently.
- The study highlights improving corporate earnings across large and mid-cap companies as a key driver for potential growth.
- Recent market corrections have created reasonable valuations and favourable entry points for long-term investors.
- These funds offer a balanced portfolio, combining the stability of established large-cap companies with the growth potential of mid-cap businesses.
- With stock prices lagging earnings growth, there's potential for valuations to catch up with strengthening fundamentals.

## What this means in plain terms

When you invest in mutual funds, you're essentially pooling your money with other investors to buy a basket of stocks or other securities. Large & Mid Cap Funds are a specific type of equity mutual fund that invests in a mix of India's biggest companies (large-caps) and those that are still growing but are well-established (mid-caps). Think of large-caps as the stable, blue-chip companies that are market leaders, while mid-caps are the next tier, often with higher growth potential.

According to a recent study by Abakkus Mutual Fund, this particular category of funds is looking quite appealing right now. The study suggests two main reasons: firstly, the earnings of these companies are improving, meaning they are becoming more profitable. Secondly, despite this improvement, their stock prices haven't fully caught up, leading to what are called "reasonable valuations" or good prices for their current and future earning potential. This situation, especially after some market corrections, presents an opportunity for investors looking for long-term growth.

## Understanding Large & Mid Cap Funds

Large & Mid Cap Funds are a distinct category of equity mutual funds, defined by the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) to ensure uniformity and transparency in the investment landscape. These funds are mandated to invest a minimum of 35% of their total assets in large-cap stocks and at least 35% in mid-cap stocks. This structure ensures a diversified exposure to both the stability of larger, established businesses and the growth potential of emerging, mid-sized companies.

### What are Large Caps?
Large-cap companies are typically the top 100 companies in India, ranked by their full market capitalisation. These are generally well-established businesses with strong market positions, robust balance sheets, and a proven track record across various economic cycles. Investing in large-caps usually offers a degree of stability and can provide a cushion during market downturns due to their inherent resilience and liquidity. They are often seen as the anchors of an equity portfolio.

### What are Mid Caps?
Mid-cap companies are those ranked from 101st to 250th by market capitalisation in India. These companies are typically in a growth phase, expanding their operations, product ranges, and market presence. While they may carry higher volatility compared to large-caps, they also offer the potential for higher growth and can outperform large-caps over extended periods as they scale up. Mid-caps represent the next generation of potential large-cap leaders.

### The Hybrid Advantage
The core benefit of a Large & Mid Cap Fund lies in its hybrid nature. By combining a significant allocation to both large and mid-cap segments, these funds aim to provide investors with a balanced risk-return profile. The stability offered by large-cap holdings can help mitigate the higher volatility sometimes associated with mid-cap stocks, while the mid-cap exposure ensures participation in the faster-growing segments of the market. This blend seeks to deliver long-term capital appreciation by leveraging the strengths of both market segments.

## The Abakkus Study: Key Findings

A recent internal study by Abakkus Mutual Fund has shed light on the current attractiveness of Large & Mid Cap Funds, particularly in the evolving Indian equity market landscape. The study suggests that the market is transitioning into an "earnings-driven phase," where strong company fundamentals are increasingly being rewarded.

### Earnings Growth and Valuations
One of the most significant findings from the Abakkus study is the widening gap between corporate earnings growth and stock price performance within the large and mid-cap universe. Over the past two years, companies in this segment have delivered earnings growth of 14-16%, while their share prices increased by only 1-2%. This divergence indicates that current valuations have not yet fully reflected the improving corporate fundamentals. Essentially, companies are earning more, but their stock prices haven't adjusted proportionally, suggesting they are trading at more reasonable valuations than their underlying performance might warrant. Vaibhav Chugh, CEO of Abakkus Mutual Fund, noted that "With valuations becoming more reasonable and earnings outlook improving, we believe the category offers an attractive avenue for long-term wealth creation through disciplined investing."

### Market Correction as an Opportunity
The study also highlights that recent market corrections have played a crucial role in creating attractive entry points for long-term investors. Approximately 59% of stocks within the large and mid-cap universe are currently trading more than 20% below their all-time highs. This moderation in stock prices, coupled with resilient earnings growth, has improved valuations across the segment. For investors with a long-term horizon, such periods of correction, where valuations become more appealing, are often considered opportune times to invest, anticipating that stock prices will eventually align with the underlying earnings growth.

## Why This Blend Works for Indian Investors

For Indian investors seeking a balanced approach to equity investing, Large & Mid Cap Funds offer a compelling proposition. Their mandated allocation strategy ensures a strategic mix that can navigate various market conditions effectively.

### Diversification Benefits
A key advantage of these funds is the inherent diversification they provide. By investing in a broad spectrum of companies – from the top 100 to the next 150 by market capitalisation – investors gain exposure across different industries and growth stages. This diversification helps reduce concentration risk, as the portfolio is not overly reliant on the performance of a single market segment or a few mega-cap stocks. The stability of large-caps can act as a buffer during periods of market volatility, while mid-caps offer the potential for higher returns, thus smoothing out the overall portfolio performance over time.

### Potential for Outperformance
Large & Mid Cap Funds are designed to capture growth opportunities across both established and emerging companies. In market cycles where mid-caps might be poised for strong growth, these funds can benefit significantly from their mandatory exposure to this segment. Conversely, when large-caps are leading the rally, the fund's allocation to them ensures participation. This dynamic allocation, often managed actively by fund managers, aims to outperform pure large-cap or pure mid-cap funds over a full market cycle by strategically picking stocks from both segments. The Abakkus study itself points to active stock selection becoming a significant differentiator, with many companies in this universe demonstrating strong compounded annual growth rates (CAGRs) over the last five years.

### Regulatory Framework and Clarity
SEBI's clear categorisation for Large & Mid Cap Funds, mandating at least 35% allocation to each segment, provides investors with transparency and predictability regarding the fund's investment universe. This regulatory clarity, overseen by AMFI (Association of Mutual Funds in India), ensures that fund houses adhere to specific investment mandates, preventing "style drift" where a fund might deviate significantly from its stated objective. This structured approach helps investors make informed decisions, knowing exactly what kind of companies their money is being invested in.

## FAQ

### What is a Large & Mid Cap Fund?
A Large & Mid Cap Fund is an equity mutual fund that, as per SEBI regulations, must invest a minimum of 35% of its assets in large-cap stocks and at least 35% in mid-cap stocks. This structure aims to combine the stability of large companies with the growth potential of mid-sized companies.

### How do Large & Mid Cap Funds differ from Multi-Cap Funds?
The key difference lies in their mandatory allocation. Large & Mid Cap Funds must allocate at least 35% each to large-cap and mid-cap stocks. Multi-Cap Funds, on the other hand, are mandated to invest a minimum of 25% each in large-cap, mid-cap, and small-cap stocks, making them structurally more aggressive.

### Are Large & Mid Cap Funds suitable for conservative investors?
While they offer more stability than pure mid-cap funds due to large-cap exposure, they are still equity funds and carry market risk. They are generally suitable for investors with a moderate to high-risk appetite and a long-term investment horizon (typically 5-7 years or more).

### What are the risks involved in these funds?
Like all equity mutual funds, Large & Mid Cap Funds are subject to market volatility. While large-cap exposure provides some stability, mid-caps can experience sharper drawdowns and liquidity issues in certain market conditions. Fund manager risk and allocation risk are also factors to consider.

### How is taxation handled for Large & Mid Cap Funds?
These funds are taxed as equity-oriented mutual funds. If units are held for more than 12 months, gains are considered Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG) and are taxed at 10% on gains exceeding ₹1 lakh in a financial year. If held for 12 months or less, gains are Short-Term Capital Gains (STCG) and are taxed at 15%. Dividends, if distributed, are added to your taxable income and taxed as per your applicable income tax slab rate.

### What is the ideal investment horizon for Large & Mid Cap Funds?
Given their equity exposure and growth orientation, Large & Mid Cap Funds are best suited for investors with a long-term investment horizon, typically 5 to 10 years or more, to fully realise their potential and ride out market fluctuations.
