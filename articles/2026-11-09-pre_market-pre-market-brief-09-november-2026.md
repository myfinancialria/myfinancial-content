# Pre-Market Brief — 09 November 2026

**TL;DR**
*   Global markets presented a mixed picture overnight, with US tech showing strength despite specific stock concerns, while Asian markets largely opened positive.
*   GIFT Nifty indicates a slightly positive open for the Indian benchmark indices.
*   Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) have been significant net buyers, while Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) have been consistent net sellers over the past five sessions.
*   MakeMyTrip's India arm receiving SEBI nod for its IPO is a key domestic development, while global tech sentiment is impacted by the Nvidia CEO probe.
*   Nifty 50's CPR pivot at 24989.15 and option chain resistance around 25050-25100 will be crucial levels to watch for today's trading range.
*   The primary risks for the day include potential spillover from global tech sector volatility and investor caution ahead of upcoming inflation data from the US and India.

## Currency, commodities and yields
The Indian Rupee (USD/INR) strengthened against the US Dollar, trading at 93.93, a decline of 0.56%. The Dollar Index also saw a decrease of 0.44% to 101.85. In commodities, Brent crude oil futures eased by 0.98% to $97.36 per barrel, and WTI crude fell by 0.57% to $90.31 per barrel. Gold prices rose by 0.78% to $4106.80, while Silver saw a sharper increase of 1.50% to $52.58. The US 10-year Treasury yield edged up by 0.45% to 4.86%.

**Interpretation:** A stronger Rupee and a weaker Dollar Index are generally positive for the Indian market, potentially easing import costs and making FII investments more attractive. Falling crude oil prices are a significant tailwind for India, benefiting oil marketing companies (OMCs), paint, and aviation sectors by reducing input costs. Rising gold and silver prices indicate some safe-haven demand or inflationary expectations. The slight uptick in US 10-year yields could be a minor concern for global equity flows, but its impact is somewhat offset by the weaker dollar.

## Option-chain signals (OI) — where support & resistance sit
*   **Nifty:** The Put-Call Ratio (PCR) stands at 0.89. The highest Call Open Interest (OI) is observed at the 25050 strike (15,547,480 contracts) and 25100 strike (14,969,240 contracts), indicating strong resistance. On the Put side, the highest OI is at the 25000 strike (18,821,465 contracts) and 24900 strike (14,872,845 contracts), suggesting robust support.
*   **Bank Nifty:** The PCR is at 0.57. Major Call OI is concentrated at 60500 (913,950 contracts) and 60000 (809,910 contracts), acting as resistance. For support, the highest Put OI is at 59000 (738,210 contracts).
*   **Sensex:** The PCR is 0.71. Resistance is seen at 81000 (1,042,060 contracts) and 82000 (873,280 contracts) based on Call OI. Strong Put OI is visible at 81000 (782,460 contracts) and 80000 (742,600 contracts), acting as support.

**Interpretation:**
For Nifty, the PCR of 0.89 suggests a neutral to slightly cautious sentiment, leaning towards a balanced market. High Call OI at 25050 and 25100 means option sellers expect the Nifty to face a ceiling around these levels. Conversely, significant Put OI at 25000 and 24900 indicates strong buying interest and a floor being defended by option buyers. Combining with technicals, Nifty's immediate resistance is around 25050-25100, while 24900-25000 should act as strong support.

For Bank Nifty, a PCR of 0.57 indicates a more cautious or bearish-to-neutral sentiment, with Call writers being more aggressive. The 60000-60500 zone is a clear resistance, while 59000 stands as the immediate support.

For Sensex, a PCR of 0.71 also points to a cautious-to-neutral bias. The 81000-81500 range will be a battleground, with 81000 acting as both a psychological resistance (Call OI) and a strong support (Put OI). A decisive break on either side of 81000 would be significant.

Overall, traders should respect the 25000-25100 zone for Nifty, 59000-60000 for Bank Nifty, and 80000-81000 for Sensex as key levels today.

## Events & data to watch — and how markets usually react
*   **US Inflation Data (CPI):** Expected later this week, this is a critical global data point. Markets typically react with volatility, as inflation figures heavily influence the US Federal Reserve's monetary policy decisions. Higher-than-expected inflation could lead to concerns about interest rate hikes, potentially impacting global equity flows, including FIIs into India.
*   **India Inflation Data:** Also due this week, domestic inflation numbers will be closely watched by the RBI and market participants. Higher inflation could dampen sentiment for interest rate-sensitive sectors and raise concerns about the RBI's stance. Conversely, cooling inflation could be seen as positive.
*   **Global Market Themes:** Investors will continue to assess the AI-led technology sell-off (triggered by Nvidia news), oil price movements, and ongoing geopolitical risks. These themes often dictate global risk appetite, which can have a ripple effect on emerging markets like India. China’s economic releases and European growth indicators will also contribute to the overall global sentiment.

## Bottom line
Indian markets are poised for a flat to marginally positive open, guided by mixed global cues and a positive GIFT Nifty signal. The consistent FII buying provides a strong underlying support, but the market will need to contend with immediate resistance levels, particularly around Nifty 25050-25100. Investors should remain cautious of potential global tech sector volatility and the upcoming domestic and international inflation data, which could introduce volatility later in the week.
