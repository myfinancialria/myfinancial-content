## Story 1: New RBI Rules for Your Bank Account: What to Know About Cyber Fraud and Debit Holds

**Topic signal:** RBI issues new rules for banks to freeze suspicious accounts linked to cyber fraud, money mules

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has introduced new guidelines for banks to manage suspicious transactions and accounts linked to cyber fraud and money laundering. These rules aim to protect customers while also combating financial crime. If your account is flagged, banks can now place a temporary debit hold on the suspicious amount or the entire account.

Banks are required to notify you promptly about any debit hold, explaining the reason, the process for its removal, and contact details for assistance. You will generally have seven calendar days to provide an explanation and supporting documents. The bank then has another seven days to review your response and decide whether to remove the hold or refer the matter to the police.

These new directions are set to take effect from July 1, 2027, though individual banks may implement them earlier. The goal is to create a more structured and time-bound process for dealing with potential fraud, ensuring that genuine customers face minimal inconvenience.

## Story 2: EPFO's New Systematic Withdrawal Plan: A Flexible Retirement Income Option

**Topic signal:** EPF withdrawal rules: How retirees can generate monthly income from PF interest without dipping into their savings

The Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO) has approved a new Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) that could change how retirees access their provident fund savings. Instead of withdrawing the entire EPF corpus as a lump sum, eligible members may soon have the option to receive periodic payouts—monthly, quarterly, or half-yearly.

Under this proposed plan, the unwithdrawn portion of your EPF balance would continue to earn interest at the prevailing rate. This offers a way to create a steady income stream after retirement while keeping a part of your savings invested within the EPF framework.

The SWP is designed to be voluntary and would complement existing Employees' Pension Scheme (EPS) benefits. While the Central Board of Trustees approved the proposal, it will become operational only after formal notification from the Ministry of Labour and Employment.
