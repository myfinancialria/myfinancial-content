# UPI Transaction Limits Explained: From ₹1 Lakh to ₹5 Lakh

**TL;DR**
- The standard daily UPI transaction limit for most payments is ₹1 lakh per bank account.
- Certain specific categories, like payments to hospitals and educational institutions, allow transactions up to ₹5 lakh.
- Subscribing to Initial Public Offerings (IPOs) and purchasing Mutual Funds via UPI also permits a higher limit of ₹5 lakh.
- While the overall daily limit is set by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI), individual banks may impose lower per-transaction limits.
- These limits are designed to balance user convenience with security and fraud prevention measures.

## What this means in plain terms

The Unified Payments Interface (UPI) has revolutionised digital payments in India, making it easy to transfer money instantly using just a mobile phone. However, for security and regulatory reasons, there are limits on how much money you can send via UPI. While most everyday transactions are capped at ₹1 lakh per day, the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) and the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) have introduced higher limits for specific types of payments. This means you can now use UPI for larger transactions, such as paying significant hospital bills, school fees, or even investing in the stock market through IPOs, up to ₹5 lakh in certain cases. Understanding these different thresholds is crucial for managing your finances effectively and leveraging UPI's full potential.

## Understanding the Standard UPI Limits

For the vast majority of day-to-day transactions, the standard UPI limit is set at ₹1 lakh per day per bank account. This applies to payments made to friends, family, merchants for groceries, utilities, and most other services. This daily aggregate limit means that even if your bank allows individual transactions of, say, ₹50,000, you cannot exceed a total of ₹1 lakh in transfers from that account within a 24-hour period.

### Per-Transaction Limits

While the daily aggregate limit is ₹1 lakh, individual banks may also impose their own per-transaction limits, which could be lower than the ₹1 lakh ceiling. For instance, some banks might cap a single UPI transaction at ₹25,000 or ₹50,000. It's advisable to check with your specific bank for their exact per-transaction limits, as these can vary. These limits are primarily in place to mitigate potential fraud and ensure a layer of security for users.

## Enhanced Limits for Specific Transaction Categories

Recognising the need for higher transaction values in certain essential and investment-related categories, the RBI and NPCI have permitted elevated UPI limits. These special categories allow users to transfer more than the standard ₹1 lakh.

### Payments to Hospitals and Educational Institutions

One significant enhancement is for payments made to hospitals and educational institutions. For these critical services, the UPI transaction limit has been increased to ₹5 lakh. This is particularly beneficial for individuals needing to pay large medical bills or substantial school/college fees, offering greater convenience without resorting to other payment methods for larger sums.

### Capital Market Transactions and IPO Subscriptions

Investing in the capital markets, particularly subscribing to Initial Public Offerings (IPOs) and purchasing Mutual Funds, also benefits from higher UPI limits. For these transactions, the limit is set at ₹5 lakh. This allows retail investors to apply for larger allotments in IPOs or make more substantial lump-sum investments in mutual funds directly through their UPI apps, streamlining the investment process considerably.

### Government and Tax Payments

Payments to the government, including income tax and other statutory dues, can also be made via UPI with an enhanced limit of ₹5 lakh. This makes it easier for individuals and small businesses to fulfil their tax obligations digitally.

## Bank-Specific and Per-Transaction Limits

While the NPCI sets the overarching limits for UPI transactions, individual banks have the discretion to implement their own per-transaction and daily limits, provided they do not exceed the NPCI's maximums. This means that while the maximum allowed by NPCI for general transactions is ₹1 lakh, your bank might set a lower limit, such as ₹25,000 or ₹50,000, for a single transaction.

### How to Check Your Bank's Limits

It's always a good practice to check the specific UPI limits imposed by your bank. This information is usually available on your bank's official website, within their mobile banking app, or by contacting their customer service. Being aware of these limits can help you plan your larger payments and avoid transaction failures.

## Why UPI Limits are Important

The implementation of UPI transaction limits serves several crucial purposes, primarily focusing on security, fraud prevention, and responsible financial behaviour.

### Security and Fraud Prevention

Limits act as a safeguard against potential fraud. In the event of unauthorised access to a UPI account, transaction limits restrict the amount of money that can be siphoned off. While UPI transactions are generally secure with multi-factor authentication (MPIN), limits add an extra layer of protection. This is particularly important given the instant and irreversible nature of UPI transfers.

### Promoting Responsible Usage

By setting limits, regulators also encourage users to be mindful of their transactions. For very large sums, alternative payment methods that might offer additional layers of verification or dispute resolution mechanisms could be more appropriate. The tiered limits for different categories reflect a balanced approach, allowing higher value transactions where they are genuinely needed (like hospital bills) while maintaining stricter controls for everyday peer-to-peer transfers.

## A real example

Priya, 34, earns ₹22L CTC and lives in Gurugram. This month, she has several payments to make using UPI.

1.  **Grocery Shopping:** Priya spends ₹8,500 on groceries for the month. This falls well within the standard ₹1 lakh daily limit for general transactions.
2.  **Friend's Birthday Gift:** She sends ₹5,000 to her friend for a joint birthday gift. Again, this is covered by the standard ₹1 lakh limit.
3.  **Hospital Bill:** Her mother had a minor procedure, and the hospital bill is ₹1.5 lakh. Priya can comfortably pay this using UPI, as payments to hospitals are eligible for the enhanced limit of ₹5 lakh.
4.  **Investing in an IPO:** Priya decides to apply for a new IPO, investing ₹3 lakh. Since IPO subscriptions fall under the enhanced capital market limits, she can make this payment via UPI, as it is within the ₹5 lakh threshold for such investments.
5.  **Child's School Fees:** She needs to pay her child's quarterly school fees of ₹75,000. This payment can also be made via UPI, benefiting from the ₹5 lakh limit for educational institutions.

In this scenario, Priya's total UPI outflows for the day could be ₹8,500 + ₹5,000 + ₹1,50,000 + ₹3,00,000 + ₹75,000 = ₹5,38,500. While her general transactions (groceries, gift) are within the ₹1 lakh limit, the special category payments (hospital, IPO, school fees) allow her to exceed the ₹1 lakh daily aggregate, leveraging the higher ₹5 lakh limits for those specific transaction types.

## FAQ

### What is the standard daily UPI transaction limit?
The standard daily UPI transaction limit for most general payments is ₹1 lakh per bank account. This applies to peer-to-peer transfers and merchant payments for everyday purchases.

### Are there higher UPI limits for specific types of payments?
Yes, for certain categories like payments to hospitals, educational institutions, tax payments, and subscriptions to Initial Public Offerings (IPOs) or Mutual Funds, the UPI limit is enhanced to ₹5 lakh.

### Can my bank set lower UPI transaction limits than the NPCI?
Yes, while NPCI sets the maximum limits, individual banks have the discretion to impose lower daily or per-transaction limits for their customers. It's advisable to check your bank's specific policies.

### Is there a limit on the number of UPI transactions I can make in a day?
While there isn't a strict universal limit on the *number* of transactions, exceeding a certain frequency might flag your account for review by your bank. The primary constraint is the aggregate daily monetary limit.

### Do merchant payments have different UPI limits?
For most retail merchant payments, the standard ₹1 lakh daily limit applies. However, specific merchant categories like hospitals or educational institutions are eligible for the enhanced ₹5 lakh limit.

### How do UPI limits affect IPO applications?
You can apply for IPOs via UPI with a limit of up to ₹5 lakh. This allows retail investors to subscribe to larger amounts directly through their UPI-enabled apps.

### What happens if I try to exceed my UPI limit?
If you attempt a transaction that exceeds your daily or per-transaction limit, the payment will typically fail, and you will receive an error message from your bank or UPI app.
