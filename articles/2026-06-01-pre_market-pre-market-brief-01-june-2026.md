# Pre-Market Brief — 01 June 2026

**TL;DR**
*   Global markets presented a mixed picture, with US and key Asian indices closing higher, while European and Shanghai markets saw declines.
*   Crude oil prices surged significantly overnight, with Brent up over 2% and WTI over 3%, potentially impacting India's import bill and inflation outlook.
*   The Indian Rupee strengthened against the US Dollar, falling over 1% to ₹94.95, even as the Dollar Index saw a slight uptick.
*   Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) continued their substantial selling, offloading over ₹21,000 crore in cash equities yesterday, largely absorbed by Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs).
*   NSE is set to extend F&O trading by 10 minutes from August 3, 2026, introducing a Closing Auction Session framework.

## Where global markets closed
Global markets closed with a mixed performance yesterday. In the US, the Dow Jones Industrial Average rose by 0.72% to 51032.46, the Nasdaq Composite gained 0.20% to 26972.62, and the S&P 500 advanced by 0.22% to 7580.06. Across the Atlantic, the FTSE 100 in London saw a slight dip of 0.23% to 10385.20. Asian markets, however, largely trended positive, with Japan's Nikkei 225 climbing 0.91% to 66934.33 and Hong Kong's Hang Seng index up 1.02% to 25439.95. China's Shanghai Composite bucked the trend, closing down 0.27% at 4057.74. The overall global tone suggests a cautious optimism, particularly in US and some Asian markets, despite some regional weakness.

## Currency, commodities and yields
The Indian Rupee saw significant appreciation against the US Dollar, with the USD/INR pair falling 1.14% to 94.95. This strengthening of the Rupee is generally positive for India, potentially easing import costs and providing some relief from inflationary pressures. The Dollar Index, which measures the US Dollar against a basket of major currencies, edged up slightly by 0.06% to 98.97.

In the commodities market, crude oil prices witnessed a sharp rally. Brent Crude surged by 2.06% to $93.95 per barrel, while WTI Crude jumped 3.49% to $90.41 per barrel. This significant increase in crude oil prices is a key concern for India, a major oil importer, as it could lead to higher fuel prices, increased inflation, and a widening trade deficit. Gold prices dipped by 0.58% to $4534.20, while Silver saw a modest gain of 0.47% to $75.97. The US 10-year Treasury Yield eased slightly by 0.04% to 4.45%, which could provide some minor support to equity markets globally.

## Recent FII/DII flows
Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) were significant net sellers in the Indian cash market yesterday, offloading equities worth ₹-21,106 crore. Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs), however, provided strong counter-support, buying equities worth ₹16,764 crore. ## Overnight news that matters
**Explained: NSE extends F&O trading by 10 minutes. What changes for traders?**
The National Stock Exchange (NSE) will extend its equity derivatives trading hours by 10 minutes, introducing a Closing Auction Session (CAS) framework from August 3, 2026, pushing the market closing time to 3:40 pm. This change will alter the trading window for futures and options, potentially impacting intraday trading strategies and liquidity dynamics in the final minutes of the session for Indian traders.

**From Fed signals to rupee pressure: Key events shaping global markets this week**
Global markets are set for a pivotal week, influenced by upcoming US jobs data, monetary policy decisions from the European Central Bank (ECB) and the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), ongoing AI-driven tech rallies, rupee volatility, and geopolitical tensions. These macro-economic and geopolitical events, particularly the RBI's policy stance and US jobs data, will be crucial in shaping investor sentiment and market direction for Indian equities this week.

**Inflation and input costs threaten earnings momentum going forward: Dhananjay Sinha**
Despite strong March-quarter earnings from India Inc. that surpassed market expectations, concerns are emerging about moderating future earnings due to rising inflation and input costs. This expert view highlights potential headwinds for corporate profitability, suggesting that the impressive earnings momentum seen recently might face challenges from increasing operational expenses, which could impact valuations across various sectors.

**Pankaj Pandey identifies defence, pharma and consumption as core stock picking themes**
Amidst a mixed outlook for Indian equities, potentially influenced by a monsoon deficit, market expert Pankaj Pandey has identified defence, pharma, and consumption as key stock-picking themes. This provides investors with sectoral insights, pointing towards areas that could offer resilience or growth opportunities even if broader market conditions remain uncertain.

**Markets in limbo as US–Iran uncertainty clouds global outlook: Geoff Dennis**
Global markets are currently grappling with uncertainty stemming from the US-Iran standoff, with conflicting signals leaving investors hesitant, though oil prices remain below extreme forecasts. Geopolitical tensions, especially involving major oil-producing regions, can introduce significant volatility and impact crude oil prices, which are a critical factor for India's economy given its high reliance on oil imports.

## What to watch in today's session
1.  **Crude Oil Prices:** Given the significant overnight jump in Brent and WTI crude, monitoring their trajectory today will be crucial. Sustained high oil prices could fuel inflation concerns and impact sectors reliant on crude derivatives.
2.  **FII/DII Flows:** The heavy FII selling yesterday, despite DII support, indicates a cautious sentiment. Observing the continuation or moderation of these flows will be key to understanding market direction.
3.  **NSE F&O Extension Impact:** While the change is effective from August, any early market commentary or trader reactions to the extended F&O trading hours could influence sentiment, particularly for derivatives-heavy stocks.
4.  **Sectoral Performance:** Watch for the performance of IT stocks, which have seen a recent rally, and sectors sensitive to input costs, given the expert warnings about inflation impacting future earnings.
5.  **Global Cues and Macro Events:** Investors should keep an eye on global developments, especially ahead of key US jobs data and central bank policy decisions (ECB, RBI) later this week, which will set the tone for global and domestic markets.

## Bottom line
Indian markets are likely to open on a mixed to cautious note today, influenced by the divergent global cues and the significant FII outflows seen yesterday. While DIIs continue to provide a crucial cushion, the sharp rise in crude oil prices and lingering concerns about inflation and input costs could act as headwinds. Investors should remain vigilant, focusing on sector-specific developments and the evolving macroeconomic landscape, particularly the upcoming RBI policy decision.
