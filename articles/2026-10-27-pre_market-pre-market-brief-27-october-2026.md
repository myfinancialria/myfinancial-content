# Pre-Market Brief — 27 October 2026

**TL;DR**
*   Global markets saw a mixed close, with US and European indices positive, while Asian markets were split, led by a strong Nikkei and weaker Chinese/Hong Kong bourses.
*   GIFT Nifty is signalling a cautious start for Indian equities, indicating a potential gap-down opening.
*   Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) were net sellers yesterday, while Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) continued to provide strong buying support.
*   The upcoming US Federal Reserve meeting and the highly anticipated Jio Platforms listing tomorrow are key events on investors' radar.
*   Nifty's immediate resistance is seen around 22800, with strong support at 22700-22600 levels.
*   Global tech sector weakness and sustained FII outflows remain a potential risk for market sentiment today.

## How global markets traded — and what it means for India
Overnight, US markets closed higher, with the Nasdaq leading gains, driven by optimism around AI and semiconductor stocks. European indices also ended in positive territory. However, Asian markets this morning are mixed; Japan's Nikkei surged on AI optimism, while Chinese and Hong Kong stocks slipped due to weakness in technology shares. This divergence suggests a cautious global sentiment, with specific sector trends at play.

## Currency, commodities and yields
The Indian Rupee (USD/INR) weakened slightly against the US Dollar, while the Dollar Index also saw a modest uptick. Brent crude oil prices declined significantly, though WTI crude saw a minor rise. Gold and Silver prices softened. The US 10-year Treasury yield saw a marginal dip.

*   **USD/INR:** 95.17 (+0.24%)
*   **Dollar Index:** 102.17 (+0.21%)
*   **Brent Crude:** 111.14 (-4.03%)
*   **WTI Crude:** 102.13 (+0.36%)
*   **Gold:** 4011.30 (-0.67%)
*   **Silver:** 52.88 (-2.26%)
*   **US 10Y Yield:** 4.99 (-0.04%)

**Interpretation:** A weakening Rupee (USD/INR up) can be a tailwind for Indian IT and pharma exporters, but it increases import costs and inflationary pressures. The significant drop in Brent crude prices is a positive for India, potentially easing input cost pressures for sectors like paints, aviation, and oil marketing companies (OMCs), and could also help manage inflation. A stable to slightly lower US 10-year yield is generally supportive for emerging markets like India, potentially reducing the risk of FII outflows.

## GIFT Nifty — what it's signalling
The GIFT Nifty is currently trading at 22698.00, down by 0.33%. This suggests a potential gap-down opening for the Nifty 50 when the Indian markets commence trading.

## Stock & sector news that matters

*   **Jio Platforms IPO Buzz & Listing Tomorrow**
    Jio Platforms is set to list on BSE and NSE tomorrow, October 28, with grey market premium (GMP) signalling a 22% premium. The ₹30,213-crore IPO was subscribed 22.19 times.
    **Why it matters:** This is a significant event for the Indian market. While the listing is tomorrow, the buzz today could impact sentiment, especially for Reliance Industries (RIL) and Jio Financial Services (JFS). A strong debut could boost market confidence, particularly in the telecom and digital services space.
*   **US Fed Decision: What to expect from Warsh's October meeting**
    The US Federal Reserve is widely expected to keep interest rates unchanged at its October meeting. Investors will closely watch Chairman Kevin Warsh's statements for clues on future policy.
    **Why it matters:** While the decision is expected to be a 'hold', any hawkish or dovish commentary from the Fed Chairman could influence global liquidity and FII flows into emerging markets like India. This will be a key global event to monitor.
*   **HSBC third-quarter profit jumps 32% to $9.6 billion**
    HSBC reported a 32% rise in pretax profits for Q3, reaching $9.6 billion, driven by higher interest rates and reduced credit loss provisions.
    **Why it matters:** Strong results from a global banking major like HSBC indicate resilience in the financial sector globally. This could provide a positive sentiment spillover for Indian banking stocks, especially private sector banks.
*   **Mankind Pharma shares jump 8% as Q2 profit rises 38% to Rs 716 crore**
    Mankind Pharma saw its shares jump after reporting a 38% year-on-year rise in Q2 profit to ₹716 crore, with revenue growing 17% and improved EBITDA margins.
    **Why it matters:** This strong performance from a prominent pharma company could set a positive tone for the pharmaceutical sector today, potentially leading to buying interest in other pharma stocks.
*   **Jefferies says Vodafone Idea shares can soar 143% in bull case scenario**
    Jefferies has issued a report on Vodafone Idea, projecting a potential upside of up to 143% in its bull case scenario, citing network expansion, improving subscriber trends, and tariff hikes as key reasons.
    **Why it matters:** Such a significant target from a global brokerage could generate considerable interest and volatility in Vodafone Idea shares today. It might also bring the broader telecom sector into focus.
*   **Why are investors selling Neocloud stocks despite AI demand?**
    AI-focused neocloud stocks are facing pressure due to concerns over high debt, rising financing costs, mounting losses, and doubts about sustainable growth, despite strong AI demand.
    **Why it matters:** This global trend of selling in AI-related tech stocks, particularly those with high debt, could impact Indian IT services companies or any domestic firms with significant AI exposure, especially if global tech sentiment remains weak.
