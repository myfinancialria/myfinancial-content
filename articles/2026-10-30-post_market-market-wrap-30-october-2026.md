## What moved the market — the factors
The Indian market's sentiment today was primarily shaped by a combination of negative global cues and the flow of institutional money. Overnight, US markets witnessed significant losses, with the Nasdaq sinking 2.2%. This sharp decline was fueled by concerns over rising capital expenditures at Amazon and cash burn warnings from Intel, leading to fresh fears about AI spending. Such a substantial drop in global tech giants often spills over into Asian markets, including India, setting a cautious tone for the trading day.

On the domestic front, Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) were net sellers, offloading equities worth ₹-944 crore in the cash segment. This continued selling pressure from FIIs, after a mixed week, likely added to the market's subdued mood. However, Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) emerged as strong buyers, pumping in ₹1,924 crore, which likely provided a significant cushion against a sharper decline. This consistent DII support has been a recurring theme, helping to absorb FII outflows.

The ongoing Q2 earnings season also played a pivotal role, with several major companies announcing their results. Reliance Industries, a heavyweight, reported record Q2FY27 profit and announced plans to raise ₹15,000 crore through Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs). While positive for the individual stock, the broader market reaction to such results depends on overall sentiment. Other notable earnings included strong performances from Bank of Baroda, Lodha Developers, Syrma SGS, Spandana Sphoorty, and NTPC Green Energy, while Zee Entertainment and Reliance Retail reported profit declines. The mixed bag of corporate results, against a backdrop of global uncertainty and FII selling, contributed to the overall market dynamics.
