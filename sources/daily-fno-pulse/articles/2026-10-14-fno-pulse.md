## Decoding today's open interest

Open Interest (OI) represents the total number of outstanding derivative contracts, such as options or futures, that have not yet been settled. When we look at the option chain, the strike price with the highest Call OI indicates a significant level where Call option writers (sellers) expect the index price to face resistance or act as a ceiling. Conversely, the strike with the highest Put OI suggests a level where Put option writers expect the index to find support or act as a floor. These levels are often seen as "walls" that the index might struggle to cross.

For **NIFTY 50**, the aggregate flow today saw a substantial net build-up in Call Open Interest (59.3M) compared to Put Open Interest (12.0M). This indicates that fresh Call writing was significantly heavier than fresh Put writing. This fresh writing was particularly noticeable near the 22,600 and 22,700 Call strikes, suggesting that new sellers believe the upside for Nifty might be capped around these levels.

For **SENSEX**, the aggregate flow also showed a greater net build in Call OI (15.5M) versus Put OI (7.6M). The heaviest fresh Call writing today was observed near the 73,733 strike, implying that Call sellers are positioning for the index to remain below this level.

In **BANK NIFTY**, while there was some fresh Call writing near 56,333, indicating a potential cap, there was also significant fresh Put writing near the 55,033 strike. This suggests that Put sellers are establishing a strong floor around the 55,000 mark, which is quite close to the current spot price.

## PCR — what the put-call ratio is whispering

The Put-Call Ratio (PCR) is a widely watched indicator that compares the total Open Interest (OI) of Puts to Calls. A PCR(OI) above 1 typically suggests more Put writing (bullish sentiment from writers expecting a floor), while below 1 suggests more Call writing (bearish sentiment from writers expecting a cap). We also look at the PCR based on today's change in Open Interest (ΔOI), which tells us about the sentiment of fresh positions taken during the day.

For **NIFTY 50**, the PCR(OI) stands at 0.72, which is considered a balanced book, not strongly bullish or bearish on its own. However, today's flow ΔPCR is at 0.2, indicating that fresh writing during the day was heavily skewed towards Calls. This combination suggests that while the overall book might be balanced, the new positions taken today reflect a more cautious or bearish sentiment from option writers.

**SENSEX** has a PCR(OI) of 1.05, which also suggests a relatively balanced book, leaning slightly towards Put writing. But its today's flow ΔPCR is 0.49, meaning that fresh writing was predominantly Call-heavy. This divergence points to a day where new bearish bets were placed, despite the existing overall balance.

**BANK NIFTY** recorded a PCR(OI) of 0.91, again indicating a largely balanced options book. Interestingly, its today's flow ΔPCR is -3.7, which suggests a mixed or balanced flow of fresh writing, not strongly favouring either Calls or Puts on a net basis, despite the individual heavy writing observed.

## Max pain and the magnet effect

"Max Pain" is a concept in options theory that refers to the strike price at which option writers, who are typically large institutional players, would experience the least amount of financial loss if the index were to expire at that level. It's often seen as a magnet, with the underlying index tending to gravitate towards this level as expiry approaches, as option writers try to manage their positions.

For **NIFTY 50**, the Max Pain level is at 22,900, which is approximately 1.2% above the current spot price of 22,622.70. This suggests that option writers might be positioned for an upward pull towards this level by expiry.

**SENSEX** has its Max Pain at 72,700, which is very close to its current spot price of 72,781.89. When Max Pain is near the spot, it often indicates that the index might consolidate around current levels as expiry nears.

**BANK NIFTY**'s Max Pain is at 55,600, about 0.5% above its spot price of 55,316.00. This, similar to Nifty, points to a potential upward magnetic pull towards the Max Pain level. The closer we get to expiry, the stronger this "magnet effect" tends to become.

## FII positioning — what the big money did today

Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) are significant participants in the Indian derivatives market, and their positioning can offer insights into broader market sentiment.

Today, FIIs held an Index futures long ratio of **9.4%**, meaning that only 9.4% of their total Index futures positions were on the long side, while the vast majority were short. This is a relatively low long ratio, and historically, such low FII Index futures long ratios have often coincided with a cautious or bearish outlook from these institutional players on the broader market.

In Stock futures, FIIs held a long ratio of **55.8%**. This indicates a more balanced to slightly bullish stance on individual stocks compared to their notably bearish positioning in index futures.

## How to read this yourself daily

Understanding these market signals can be a valuable skill for any investor. You can access much of this data directly from the National Stock Exchange (NSE) website under the "Derivatives" section, specifically the "Option Chain" for Nifty, Sensex, and Bank Nifty. Many stockbrokers also provide this data through their trading platforms or analytical tools.

To interpret it, first, look at the Option Chain to identify the highest Call OI and Put OI strikes, which act as immediate resistance and support levels. Then, monitor the "Change in Open Interest" column to see where fresh positions are being added – a large build-up in Call OI at a particular strike indicates new sellers expecting a cap, while a build-up in Put OI suggests new sellers expecting a floor. Calculate the Put-Call Ratio (PCR) for both total OI and today's change in OI to gauge overall and fresh market sentiment. Finally, keep an eye on the FII derivative statistics, also available on the NSE website, to understand how institutional players are positioning themselves. Combining these data points daily will help you form a more informed perspective on market sentiment.
