## Decoding today's open interest

Open Interest (OI) in options refers to the total number of outstanding contracts that have not yet been settled. For Call options, the strike with the highest OI often acts as a **resistance level** or a "ceiling," as many Call writers (sellers) believe the price will not go above this point. Conversely, for Put options, the strike with the highest OI typically serves as a **support level** or a "floor," as Put writers expect the price to stay above this level.

For **Nifty 50**, the heaviest Call OI is observed at 24,500, indicating a potential ceiling just above the current spot. On the Put side, the heaviest Put OI is at 24,450, suggesting a strong base close to where the index closed. Today's fresh Call writing was most concentrated near 24,500, reinforcing the expectation of a cap at this level. Meanwhile, fresh Put writing was significant around 24,383, establishing a potential new floor slightly lower than the current spot.

In **SENSEX**, the option chain shows substantial Call writing near 78,666, which suggests that option sellers anticipate the index might struggle to move beyond this point. On the Put side, fresh writing was notable around 77,400, indicating that participants are building a support base at this level. The aggregate flow for Sensex today showed a net Call OI build of 10.0 million contracts, significantly higher than the 5.0 million net Put OI build, which could be interpreted as a more cautious sentiment among option writers.

For **BANK NIFTY**, the heaviest Call OI and Put OI are both positioned around 58,000, creating a significant pivot point. Today's heaviest fresh Call writing was seen near 58,266, suggesting potential resistance slightly above 58,000. Conversely, fresh Put writing was concentrated around 56,333, establishing a robust support level much lower than the current spot.

## PCR — what the put-call ratio is whispering

The **Put-Call Ratio (PCR)** is a widely watched indicator calculated by dividing the total Open Interest of Put options by the total Open Interest of Call options. A PCR above 1 generally suggests a more bullish sentiment (more Put writing than Call writing), while a ratio below 1 often indicates a more bearish sentiment (more Call writing than Put writing).

For **Nifty 50**, the PCR based on total Open Interest (PCR(OI)) stands at 0.76, which is generally considered balanced. The PCR based on today's fresh flow (ΔOI) is -0.07, indicating a mixed or balanced activity in new option writing for the day. This combination suggests that while the existing book is relatively neutral, today's fresh activity didn't lean strongly in either direction.

**SENSEX** has a PCR(OI) of 0.74, also indicating a balanced existing book. However, its PCR based on today's fresh flow (ΔOI) is 0.5, which suggests that today's new option writing was relatively heavier on the Call side compared to Puts. This shift in fresh writing leans towards a more cautious or bearish sentiment for the immediate future.

**BANK NIFTY** also shows a PCR(OI) of 0.76, similar to Nifty, pointing to a balanced options book. The PCR based on today's fresh flow (ΔOI) is -0.1, again indicating a mixed or balanced flow of new option writing, not showing a strong directional bias from fresh positions.

## Max pain and the magnet effect

**Max Pain** is the strike price at which option writers (sellers) would experience the minimum cumulative loss if the underlying asset were to expire at that price. It's often considered a "magnet" for the underlying index, especially as the expiry date approaches, as option writers tend to adjust their positions to minimise their losses, potentially pulling the index towards this level.

For **Nifty 50**, the Max Pain level is at 24,550, which is approximately +0.3% above the current spot price. This suggests a slight upward pull for the index towards this level as expiry nears.

**SENSEX** has its Max Pain level significantly higher at 78,800, about +0.8% above the current spot. This indicates a potential upward magnetic pull, although today's fresh Call writing activity might temper this pull in the very short term.

**BANK NIFTY** has a Max Pain level at 58,000, which is +1.0% above its current spot. This level also coincides with the heaviest Call OI, suggesting a strong potential for the index to gravitate towards 58,000 as expiry approaches.

## How to read this yourself daily

Understanding these data points can empower you to form your own perspective on market movements. You can access options Open Interest data, including the Put-Call Ratio, from the National Stock Exchange (NSE) website under the "Derivatives" section, specifically the "Option Chain" for each index. Many brokerage platforms also provide this data in an easily digestible format.

To interpret it yourself, start by identifying the strikes with the highest Call OI and Put OI; these are your immediate resistance and support levels. Then, look at the change in OI for the day (ΔOI) to see where fresh positions are being built – heavy fresh Call writing at a strike suggests new resistance, while heavy fresh Put writing suggests new support. Calculate or observe the PCR(OI) and PCR(ΔOI) to gauge overall sentiment and the sentiment from today's fresh activity. Finally, identify the Max Pain strike; this often acts as a magnetic pull, especially as expiry approaches. Combining these elements provides a comprehensive, data-driven overview of potential market behaviour without relying on speculative forecasts.
