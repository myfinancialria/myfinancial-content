Ever wonder how your government salary keeps up with rising prices? There's a special number called AICPI-IW that helps decide your Dearness Allowance (DA). It tracks how much everyday things cost for workers. When this number goes up, it often means your DA will also go up, helping you manage your money better against inflation.
