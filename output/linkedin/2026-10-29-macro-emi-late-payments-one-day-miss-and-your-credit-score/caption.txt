Ever worried about missing an EMI payment by just a day? Many of us do! The good news is, a one-day delay usually won't wreck your credit score immediately. Banks typically report it only after 30 days. But consistent delays *will* hurt your future borrowing. Always talk to your bank if you think you'll be late.
