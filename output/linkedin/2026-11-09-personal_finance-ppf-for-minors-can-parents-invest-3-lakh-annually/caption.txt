Thinking of opening a PPF for your child? Many parents wonder if they can put ₹1.5 lakh each, totaling ₹3 lakh, into their child's account. The simple answer is no. You can only put a maximum of ₹1.5 lakh per year into a minor's PPF account, no matter who contributes. Amounts above this limit won't earn interest or give tax benefits.
