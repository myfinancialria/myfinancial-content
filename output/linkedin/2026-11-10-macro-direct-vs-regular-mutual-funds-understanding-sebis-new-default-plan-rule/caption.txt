Ever wondered if you're paying too much for your mutual funds? There are two types: regular and direct. Regular plans cost you more because someone earns a commission. Direct plans are cheaper, meaning more money stays in your pocket and grows over time! SEBI now encourages direct plans, making them easier to choose. This small change can make a big difference to your savings. Learn how in our full article.
