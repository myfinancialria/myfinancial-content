Is your NPS money getting credited late? Good news! The government now says if your employer delays depositing your NPS contributions, they must pay you interest. This interest will be at the same rate as PPF (currently 7.1%). This means your hard-earned retirement money won't lose out due to delays. Read the full article to understand more.
