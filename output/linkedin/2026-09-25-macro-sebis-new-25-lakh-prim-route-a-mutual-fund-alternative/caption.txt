Ever wished investing in mutual funds felt simpler? SEBI has a new way for you! It's called PRIM. If you have ₹25 lakhs to invest, professional managers can now pick and manage mutual funds for you, just like they do for very rich people. This could make investing easier and more diverse for busy professionals. Learn more about how PRIM works.
