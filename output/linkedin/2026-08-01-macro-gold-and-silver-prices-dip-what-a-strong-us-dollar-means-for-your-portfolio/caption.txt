Ever wondered why gold prices jump or dip? Well, a strong US dollar just made gold and silver a bit pricier for many, causing a dip on July 31st. Think of it like this: if the dollar is strong, you need more local money to buy the same amount of gold. But don't worry, gold still had a good July overall.
