Thinking about investing in certain funds? There's a special rule you should know. For some funds (called SIFs), you need to keep at least ₹10 lakhs invested. This total is checked using your PAN number, even if you spread your money across different plans from the same company. If your investment drops below this, your fund house might sell off your remaining units. Learn more about this important rule in our latest article.
