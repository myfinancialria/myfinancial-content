Thinking about switching your tax plan? If you earn money from a business or profession, there's a special rule you need to know. You can't just jump between the old and new tax systems every year. There's a form (10-IEA) and a once-in-a-lifetime limit for switching back if you leave the new system. This helps keep things stable for your tax planning. Learn more about how this affects you.
