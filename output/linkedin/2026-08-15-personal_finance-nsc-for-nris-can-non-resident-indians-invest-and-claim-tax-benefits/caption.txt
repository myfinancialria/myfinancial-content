Many NRIs wonder if they can invest in National Savings Certificates (NSC) and save on taxes. The simple answer is: generally, no, you can't open new ones. If you already have an NSC and become an NRI, you can keep it till it matures, but you won't get tax benefits anymore, and the interest is taxable. Find out exactly what this means for your money.
