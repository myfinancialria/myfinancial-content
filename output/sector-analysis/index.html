01

Financial services

Credit, savings, risk transfer and market intermediation remain central to domestic growth.

Collapse

Banks

Constructive

Credit demand is still supported by formalisation, housing, infrastructure and consumption, but the next leg depends on deposit growth, asset quality and the cost of funds rather than loan growth alone.

Economic impact

Banks transmit monetary policy into household EMIs, working capital and capex. Stronger credit can lift investment; weaker deposits or stressed borrowers can amplify a slowdown.

News & performance drivers
  • RBI policy stance and liquidity operations → funding costs and net interest margins
  • Quarterly slippages, credit costs and deposit competition → earnings quality
  • Public-sector bank capital and infrastructure-loan updates → system risk appetite
SWOT snapshot
Strength

Large, increasingly formal domestic customer base

Weakness

Deposit mobilisation and cycle-sensitive credit costs

Opportunity

MSME, housing, digital payments and capex financing

Threat

Asset-quality reversal, fraud and margin compression

Policy tailwinds to track

RBI prudential, digital-lending and payment-system rules — Union Budget 2026–27 speech · Public infrastructure and formalisation priorities — PM India · 2026–27 capital expenditure and railways

NBFCs and housing finance

Watch

Specialist lenders retain an edge in underserved retail, vehicle, microfinance and property-linked credit. The trade-off is higher sensitivity to wholesale funding, collections and regulatory tightening.

Economic impact

NBFCs widen credit access for households and small businesses, especially where banks lack distribution. A funding shock can quickly reduce consumption and construction activity.

News & performance drivers
  • Borrowing spreads, securitisation volumes and bond-market liquidity
  • Microfinance collections and rural cash flows
  • Housing sales, property prices and affordable-housing demand
SWOT snapshot
Strength

Specialist underwriting and last-mile distribution

Weakness

Funding concentration and rollover dependence

Opportunity

Affordable housing, vehicle finance and MSME formalisation

Threat

Liquidity stress, collection weakness and tighter norms

Policy tailwinds to track

Housing, financial-inclusion and digital-credit frameworks — Union Budget 2026–27 speech

Insurance

Constructive

Low penetration, rising protection awareness and formal employment support a long runway. Near-term profitability still varies sharply by product mix, distribution cost and claims inflation.

Economic impact

Insurance converts household and business risks into investible long-term pools, supporting capital formation while improving resilience after health, crop or catastrophe losses.

News & performance drivers
  • IRDAI product, commission and foreign-investment decisions
  • Health and motor claims inflation
  • Life premium growth, persistency and solvency ratios
SWOT snapshot
Strength

Under-penetrated market with recurring premium pools

Weakness

Complex products and high distribution costs

Opportunity

Health, term life, crop and embedded insurance

Threat

Mis-selling, catastrophe losses and claims inflation

Policy tailwinds to track

Insurance-penetration and financial-inclusion reforms — Union Budget 2026–27 speech

Capital markets and fintech

Constructive

Retail participation, SIPs, demat accounts and digital rails have deepened market access. Valuations and revenue quality deserve more attention as trading activity normalises and compliance costs rise.

Economic impact

Efficient markets lower the cost of capital for companies and give households more ways to save. Excess leverage or speculative activity can increase household losses and volatility.

News & performance drivers
  • SEBI market-structure, derivatives and investor-protection changes
  • Mutual-fund flows, SIP growth and IPO pipeline
  • UPI, account-aggregator and fraud-prevention adoption
SWOT snapshot
Strength

World-scale digital public infrastructure

Weakness

Retail behaviour can be pro-cyclical

Opportunity

Wealth management, pensions and transparent fintech

Threat

Cyber incidents, regulatory compression and market sell-offs

Policy tailwinds to track

Digital public infrastructure and investor-protection reforms — Union Budget 2026–27 speech

02

IT, digital and communications

Export services are moving from labour arbitrage towards AI-enabled transformation and owned platforms.

Collapse

IT services and GCCs

Watch

Demand is bifurcated: cost takeout and cloud/AI work remain resilient, while discretionary transformation budgets and traditional application work are more uneven. Currency and utilisation matter for margins.

Economic impact

IT exports bring foreign exchange, high-value jobs and productivity gains to client economies. A global tech-spending slowdown can affect urban employment, office demand and services exports.

News & performance drivers
  • US and European tech budgets, deal wins and vendor consolidation
  • Generative-AI productivity claims versus billing and headcount
  • GCC announcements, hiring and campus expansion
SWOT snapshot
Strength

Deep talent pool, scale and global delivery

Weakness

Concentration in a few geographies and large clients

Opportunity

AI engineering, cybersecurity and vertical platforms

Threat

Automation, pricing pressure and visa/geopolitical risk

Policy tailwinds to track

IndiaAI, skilling and digital-infrastructure initiatives — PIB · Semicon 2.0

Software, AI and cybersecurity

Constructive

AI adoption is expanding demand for compute, data engineering, model governance and security, but monetisation is still being tested. Winners should pair proprietary data or workflow depth with lower inference costs.

Economic impact

Productivity gains can lift output without proportional labour growth, while cyber resilience reduces disruption across banks, hospitals, factories and government systems.

News & performance drivers
  • Model releases, enterprise AI budgets and data-centre capacity
  • IndiaAI compute and sovereign-model announcements
  • Cyber breaches, privacy enforcement and responsible-AI rules
SWOT snapshot
Strength

Large engineering base and digital adoption

Weakness

Limited global-scale product ownership

Opportunity

Vertical AI, public digital rails and cyber defence

Threat

Fast obsolescence, compute cost and data regulation

Policy tailwinds to track

IndiaAI Mission and semiconductor ecosystem support — PIB · Semicon 2.0

Telecom and data centres

Constructive

Data usage, 5G monetisation and cloud demand support capex, but the sector remains capital-intensive with high spectrum, energy and right-of-way costs. Consolidation has improved pricing discipline.

Economic impact

Connectivity lowers transaction costs and enables digital services, remote work and financial inclusion. Data-centre build-out also creates demand for power, cooling, construction and network equipment.

News & performance drivers
  • Tariff revisions, 5G enterprise use cases and spectrum policy
  • Data-centre leases, power availability and AI-compute demand
  • Right-of-way, cybersecurity and data-localisation rules
SWOT snapshot
Strength

Large data market and improving network scale

Weakness

High capex and balance-sheet leverage

Opportunity

Fibre, edge compute, private 5G and cloud

Threat

Price wars, energy constraints and cyber outages

Policy tailwinds to track

Digital India, data infrastructure and electronics policy — PIB · Semicon 2.0 · Capital-investment focus on enabling infrastructure — PM India · 2026–27 capital expenditure and railways

03

Industrials and infrastructure

Public capex, private manufacturing and logistics upgrades are creating a multi-year order-cycle, with execution as the key filter.

Collapse

Capital goods and EPC

Constructive

Order books benefit from power, rail, defence, factory and urban-infrastructure spending. The healthier businesses are those converting orders into cash without stretching working capital.

Economic impact

Capital formation raises productivity and creates upstream demand for metals, engineering, logistics and skilled labour. Delays can lock up capital and reduce the multiplier.

News & performance drivers
  • Fresh order awards, execution milestones and working-capital days
  • Private capex intentions and capacity-utilisation data
  • Commodity, freight and project-finance costs
SWOT snapshot
Strength

Domestic demand and technical capability

Weakness

Execution, receivables and tender concentration

Opportunity

Factory build-out, grid, rail and exports

Threat

Cost overruns, cancellations and commodity spikes

Policy tailwinds to track

Capital-expenditure and manufacturing push — PM India · 2026–27 capital expenditure and railways · PLI and strategic manufacturing support — PIB · Manufacturing momentum

Roads, rail, ports and logistics

Constructive

Multimodal connectivity and freight capacity remain strong structural themes. Financial performance depends on traffic, concession terms, land availability and whether public projects are awarded and executed on time.

Economic impact

Lower logistics costs improve the competitiveness of farms, factories and exporters; construction also supports employment and regional development.

News & performance drivers
  • Road awards, toll growth, rail capex and dedicated freight corridors
  • Port volumes, shipping rates and container availability
  • Land acquisition, environmental clearances and state execution
SWOT snapshot

Threat

Cement and building materials

Constructive

Demand tracks housing, roads and industrial construction. Volume growth can be healthy while margins remain sensitive to coal, petcoke, freight, regional oversupply and consolidation.

Economic impact

Construction materials are a direct bridge from public/private capex to jobs and fixed-asset creation, but price competition affects contractor and developer viability.

News & performance drivers

  • Coal/petcoke, freight and electricity costs
  • Housing launches, road awards and monsoon-led construction pace

Demand is broadening beyond passenger cars as two-wheelers, commercial vehicles and electric mobility compete on affordability and total cost.

Passenger vehicles, two-wheelers and commercial vehicles

Watch

Urban income, rural recovery, replacement demand and financing costs drive volumes. Premiumisation helps margins, but entry-level affordability and fleet utilisation remain important stress points.

Economic impact

Auto creates a large manufacturing and services ecosystem, from steel and components to finance, fuel, repair and logistics.

News & performance drivers

Economic impact

EVs can reduce oil dependence and local pollution while shifting value towards batteries, power electronics, software and grid services.

News & performance drivers

  • Battery-cell capacity, mineral prices and safety recalls

Opportunity

05

Energy and utilities

India needs more reliable, affordable power while simultaneously reducing import dependence and carbon intensity.

Collapse

Oil, gas and refining

Watch

Refiners and gas distributors balance domestic demand with crude differentials, shipping, currency and administered pricing. Energy security remains a policy priority, but transition risk changes long-duration valuations.

Economic impact

Energy prices flow through transport, fertiliser, chemicals, inflation and household budgets. Supply disruptions can widen the current-account deficit and slow growth.

News & performance drivers
  • Crude and gas prices, OPEC+ decisions and shipping disruption
  • Refining margins, fuel pricing and inventory gains/losses
  • Gas allocation, city-gas volumes and import dependence
SWOT snapshot
Strength

Weakness

Opportunity

Threat

Power generation and distribution

Constructive

Load growth from manufacturing, cooling, rail and data centres supports generation and grid investment. The investability of distribution still depends on tariffs, losses, payment discipline and state reform.

Economic impact

Reliable electricity is an economy-wide input: it raises factory utilisation, supports digital infrastructure and reduces the productivity cost of outages.

News & performance drivers
  • Peak demand, power-market prices and fuel availability
  • DISCOM dues, tariff orders and loss-reduction progress
  • Transmission tenders, storage and grid-balancing needs

Renewables, storage and green hydrogen

Solar and wind deployment are moving from project growth to system integration: transmission, firm power, batteries, domestic modules and bankable offtake are the bottlenecks to watch.

Economic impact

India remains important to global generic supply, while domestic branded generics benefit from rising healthcare spend. US pricing, inspections, compliance and input costs separate durable franchises from volume-only businesses.

Economic impact

News & performance drivers
