How the market behaved today

Indian equity benchmarks ended Tuesday's session in the red, with selling pressure resurfacing towards the close. The Nifty 50 settled at 23329.00, down 0.36%, after trading in a range between 23489.00 and 23285.75. Broader markets, however, showed more resilience, with the Nifty Midcap 100 declining marginally by 0.08% and the Nifty Smallcap 100 by 0.23%.

What moved the market — the factors

The Indian market witnessed a day of modest declines, primarily driven by persistent selling pressure from Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) and a late-session dip. Despite positive cues from US markets, where the Nasdaq hit an intraday record high on the back of tech stock momentum, Indian benchmarks struggled to hold gains. Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) continued to provide crucial support, absorbing a significant portion of the FII outflows.

A key factor influencing sentiment was the retreat in global crude oil prices, with Brent crude falling below $100 a barrel. This development calmed inflation jitters in India, a major oil importer, leading to a rally in Indian bonds. However, this positive macro development wasn't enough to counteract the broader selling in equities, especially in sectors like IT and FMCG. The IT sector, in particular, saw declines despite a global tech rally, suggesting specific domestic or sector-specific concerns. On the other hand, sectors like Media and Realty showed strength, with news of land acquisitions boosting real estate players.

Market breadth

The market breadth was negative today, with 190 advances against 307 declines out of a total of 497 stocks traded. This indicates that more stocks fell than rose, suggesting a broader underlying weakness despite the relatively contained index declines.

FII / DII flows

Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) continued their selling streak, offloading Indian equities worth ₹-576 crore today. In contrast, Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) remained net buyers, injecting ₹2,797 crore into the market, providing crucial support.

Looking at the 5-day history, FIIs have been consistent net sellers, with today marking their fifth consecutive day of outflows. Over this period, FIIs have cumulatively sold over ₹8,000 crore. DIIs, on the other hand, have been robust buyers, consistently cushioning the FII selling pressure, with cumulative inflows exceeding ₹13,000 crore in the last five sessions. This sustained DII buying has been a key factor in preventing sharper market corrections.

Event breakdown — what happened today and how it hit the market

Today's session was largely driven by a combination of global cues and domestic institutional support against FII selling. There wasn't a single defining event that dramatically altered the market's trajectory. The positive sentiment from US markets, particularly the Nasdaq's record high driven by tech stocks, provided some early optimism. However, this was counteracted by the continued FII outflows.

A significant macro development was the fall in global crude oil prices below $100 a barrel, which led to a rally in Indian bonds as inflation concerns eased. This was a positive for the broader economy but didn't translate into broad-based equity gains. In the real estate sector, news of Puravankara's land acquisition in Greater Noida, with an estimated ₹5,200 crore revenue potential, provided a boost to realty stocks. Meanwhile, the IT sector faced headwinds despite the global tech rally, suggesting sector-specific profit booking or concerns.

Setup for tomorrow

As we head into Wednesday, Indian markets will likely continue to monitor global cues, particularly the performance of US markets overnight. The Nasdaq's recent record high and the easing of oil prices below $100 are generally positive signals. However, the persistent FII selling remains a key overhang. Investors will also keep an eye on any further developments regarding SEBI's plans for derivatives settlement prices and corporate news, including stocks like HCL Technologies and Persistent Systems, which are in focus for tomorrow. Key levels for Nifty 50 and Bank Nifty will be crucial to watch for directional cues.
