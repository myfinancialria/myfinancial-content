Your Travel Just Got More Rewarding: Air India and IHG Join Hands

TL;DR

  • Air India and IHG Hotels & Resorts have partnered to make your travel more rewarding.
  • If you're an Air India Maharaja Club member, you can now earn Maharaja Points when you stay at IHG hotels worldwide.
  • If you're an IHG One Rewards member, you can convert your hotel points into Air India Maharaja Points for future flights.
  • This deal gives you more flexibility and ways to gather points for free flights or hotel stays.
  • There's even a special offer to earn triple Maharaja Points on stays until the end of 2026.

What's this about?

India's leading global airline, Air India, has teamed up with IHG Hotels & Resorts, a big international hotel company that owns brands like InterContinental, Holiday Inn, and Crowne Plaza. This partnership means that loyal customers of both companies can now get more value from their travel. Essentially, your hotel stays can help you earn flight points, and your flight points can be boosted by converting hotel points.

Why it's in the news right now

This new partnership was announced on September 4, 2026. It's a fresh development that expands the benefits for travelers who are part of Air India's Maharaja Club or IHG's One Rewards loyalty programs. The aim is to offer more choice and flexibility across the entire travel journey, from booking flights to staying in hotels.

How it actually works

This collaboration links the loyalty programs of both companies:

  1. Earning Maharaja Points at IHG Hotels: If you are a member of Air India's Maharaja Club, you can now earn Maharaja Points when you stay at most IHG Hotels & Resorts properties around the world. For example, you will earn 2 Maharaja Points for every US$1 (around ₹83, as of early September 2026) you spend at most IHG hotels. For stays at specific brands like Candlewood Suites or Staybridge Suites, you'll earn 1 Maharaja Point per US$1 spent.
  2. Converting IHG Points to Maharaja Points: If you are a member of IHG One Rewards, you have the option to convert your IHG points into Air India Maharaja Points. For every 1,000 IHG One Rewards Points you convert, you will receive 200 Maharaja Points. There's a minimum conversion of 1,000 IHG One Rewards Points.
  3. Special Launch Offer: To celebrate the partnership, Maharaja Club members can earn three times (3x) the Maharaja Points on eligible stays between September 15 and December 31, 2026. You need to register for this promotion to get the extra points.

A simple way to think about it

Imagine your local kirana store and your favourite restaurant decide to team up. Usually, when you buy groceries at the kirana, you get 'Kirana Koin' points. When you eat at the restaurant, you get 'Foodie Fund' points.

Now, with this partnership, if you buy groceries (fly Air India), you can get Kirana Koins. But also, if you eat at the restaurant (stay at an IHG hotel), you can choose to earn Kirana Koins instead of Foodie Funds. And if you have a lot of Foodie Funds, you can swap some of them for Kirana Koins to help you get that free grocery basket faster! It just gives you more ways to earn and use your rewards.

Why it matters to you

  1. More Ways to Earn Rewards: If you travel frequently, this partnership means you can accumulate loyalty points faster by combining your flight and hotel activities. This could lead to free flights or discounted stays sooner.
  2. Greater Flexibility: You now have more options for how you use your hard-earned points. If you have a lot of IHG points but need Air India points for a flight, you can convert them.
  3. Potential Savings on Travel: By earning and converting points efficiently, you can reduce the out-of-pocket cost of future flights or hotel bookings, making your travel more economical.
  4. Enhanced Travel Experience: For those who frequently fly Air India and stay at IHG properties, this streamlines the rewards process, making your overall travel journey more rewarding.

Common confusions cleared up

  • Myth: My existing Air India and IHG points will automatically merge.

    • * Fact: No, your points won't merge automatically. You will need to select Air India Maharaja Club as your earning preference for eligible IHG stays, or actively convert your IHG One Rewards points to Maharaja Points.

  • Myth: I can directly use my Maharaja Points for free stays at any IHG hotel.

    • Fact: The partnership allows Maharaja Club members to earn Maharaja Points on IHG stays, and IHG One Rewards members to convert* their IHG points into Maharaja Points. It doesn't mean you can directly redeem Maharaja Points for IHG hotel stays. You're building up Air India points, which are typically used for flights or other airline-related benefits.

FAQ

What are Maharaja Club and IHG One Rewards?

Maharaja Club is the frequent flyer program for Air India, rewarding customers for flying with the airline. IHG One Rewards is the loyalty program for IHG Hotels & Resorts, offering benefits and points for stays at their various hotel brands globally.

Do I need to do anything special to earn points?

Yes, if you are a Maharaja Club member, you will likely need to select Air India Maharaja Club as your earning preference when booking or checking into an IHG hotel. For the 3x points promotion, you must register for it.

Can I earn points at all IHG brands?

You can earn Maharaja Points at most IHG Hotels & Resorts worldwide. The earning rate might vary slightly for certain brands, like Candlewood Suites or Staybridge Suites.

Is there a minimum number of IHG points I need to convert?

Yes, you need a minimum of 1,000 IHG One Rewards Points to convert them into Air India Maharaja Points.

How long will this partnership last?

The announcement does not specify an end date for the overall partnership, suggesting it is a long-term collaboration. The special 3x points offer is valid until December 31, 2026.
