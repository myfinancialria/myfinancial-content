Market Wrap — 18 September 2026

TL;DR

  • Indian equities closed higher today, with Nifty 50 and Bank Nifty posting modest gains.
  • Midcap and Smallcap indices significantly outperformed, indicating broader market participation.
  • Falling crude oil prices and supportive global cues provided a tailwind for the market.
  • Adani Total Gas Ltd. was the top Nifty 500 gainer, surging over 12%.
  • Tata Chemicals Ltd. was the top Nifty 500 loser, dropping over 11%.
  • Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) continued their selling streak, while Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) provided strong buying support.
  • India VIX cooled down, suggesting a reduction in market volatility.

How the market behaved today

Indian benchmark indices ended the day in positive territory, largely driven by broad-based buying in mid and small-cap segments, coupled with favourable global cues. The Nifty 50 closed at 23346.40, up by 0.33%, while the Bank Nifty advanced by 0.54% to settle at 56358.70. The day saw indices trading within a relatively tight range after a positive start, with mid and small-caps showing significant strength throughout the session.

What moved the market — the factors

The Indian market's positive close today was primarily influenced by a combination of supportive global cues and robust domestic institutional buying. US markets opened higher, and a significant drop in crude oil prices provided a positive sentiment boost for India, a major oil importer. Domestically, while Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) continued their selling trend, Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) stepped in with substantial buying, effectively absorbing the FII outflows and providing a floor to the market. This DII support was crucial in sustaining the positive momentum. Sector-wise, the IT sector faced headwinds, while Metal, Media, and Realty sectors witnessed strong buying interest, contributing to the overall market uplift. The significant outperformance of the Nifty Midcap 100 and Nifty Smallcap 100 indices suggests a broad-based positive sentiment beyond just the large-cap counters.

Sectors — winners and losers

Today saw a clear rotation in sectors, with Metal, Media, and Realty leading the gains, while IT, FMCG, and Auto sectors lagged.

Top 3 Gainer Sectors:

  • METAL: +1.51% - The sector saw strong buying interest today.
  • MEDIA: +1.34% - Media stocks performed well, contributing to market gains.
  • REALTY: +1.19% - The real estate sector continued its positive momentum.

Top 3 Loser Sectors:

  • IT: -1.03% - The Information Technology sector was the biggest drag on the market today.
  • FMCG: -0.23% - Fast Moving Consumer Goods stocks saw some profit booking.
  • AUTO: -0.11% - The Automobile sector closed marginally in the red.

News that moved stocks

  • US Stocks Open Higher as Oil Prices Fall

    • The S&P 500 and Nasdaq indexes opened higher today, while the Dow remained flat, as investors reacted to declining oil prices. This positive sentiment from US markets provided a supportive global cue for Indian equities.

  • Bhumika Realty Leases 24,000 sq ft Retail Space to Lifestyle in Faridabad

    • Bhumika Realty announced a significant lease agreement with Lifestyle for 24,000 sq ft in their upcoming project 'The Icon' in Faridabad. This news is positive for the Realty sector, which was among the top gainers today.

  • Indian Government Bonds Face Fifth Weekly Loss Amid Fed Hike, RBI Moves

    • Indian government bonds have experienced their fifth consecutive weekly decline, influenced by a Federal Reserve rate hike and liquidity measures from the RBI. This indicates ongoing pressure in the bond market, though it did not significantly deter equity market sentiment today.

    • Hi-Tech Flow Solutions has filed its Draft Red Herring Prospectus (DRHP) for an Initial Public Offering (IPO) aiming to raise ₹300 crore through a fresh issue and offer-for-sale. This news highlights activity in the primary market.

Market breadth

The market breadth was strongly positive today, with 361 advances against 136 declines out of a total of 497 stocks, indicating broad participation in the day's upward move.

FII / DII flows

Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) continued their selling spree today, offloading Indian equities worth ₹3,209 crore. However, Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) provided strong counter-buying, injecting ₹3,618 crore into the market.

Investor TypeNet Flow (₹ crore)
FII-3,209
DII+3,618

Over the past five trading sessions, FIIs have been consistent net sellers, with cumulative outflows. In contrast, DIIs have consistently been net buyers, absorbing the FII selling pressure and providing crucial support to the Indian markets. This trend suggests that domestic liquidity is playing a significant role in stabilizing the market amidst foreign outflows.

Event breakdown — what happened today and how it hit the market

Today's market session was primarily driven by a combination of supportive global cues and strong domestic institutional buying. The positive opening of US markets and a notable fall in crude oil prices globally provided a favourable backdrop for Indian equities. Domestically, the consistent buying by DIIs proved to be a critical factor, offsetting the continued selling by FIIs. This strong domestic support, coupled with the positive global sentiment, helped the Nifty and Bank Nifty close higher, while the broader market, particularly mid and small-caps, saw significant gains. There was no single defining event like a major policy announcement or economic data release that singularly moved the market today; rather, it was a confluence of these factors.

Setup for tomorrow

Looking ahead to tomorrow, global cues will continue to be important. US markets opening higher and the significant drop in Brent Crude to $100.14 and WTI Crude to $97.84 are positive signals for the Indian market. The Dollar Index saw a slight increase to 100.50, while the USD/INR closed marginally lower at 95.86. The US 10-year yield rose to 5.00%. Investors will watch for further developments in global bond markets and any fresh cues from international commodity prices. Domestically, the sustained DII buying trend will be key, and attention will remain on sector-specific movements, especially in IT given today's decline, and the continued momentum in Metal, Media, and Realty.
