Pre-Market Brief — 29 October 2026

TL;DR

  • Global markets ended mixed, with US and European indices closing positive, while some Asian markets saw declines.
  • GIFT Nifty signals a strong positive opening bias for Indian equities.
  • Both FIIs and DIIs were net buyers in the cash segment yesterday, indicating broad support.
  • The US Federal Reserve held rates steady, but bond market concerns over inflation persist, while the ECB rate decision is awaited.
  • Nifty's key levels to watch are 23000 as immediate resistance and 22700 as crucial support.
  • Rising US bond yields and potential global inflation concerns remain key risks for today's session.

How global markets traded — and what it means for India

Overnight, US markets closed with modest gains, with the Dow Jones, Nasdaq, and S&P 500 all inching higher. European markets also saw positive momentum, led by the FTSE 100. However, Asian markets presented a mixed picture this morning, with Japan's Nikkei 225 rising, but Hong Kong's Hang Seng and China's Shanghai Composite declining, largely due to a tech selloff in China.

Currency, commodities and yields

The Indian Rupee weakened slightly against the US Dollar, with USD/INR trading at ₹95.33, up 0.16%. The Dollar Index also saw a marginal gain of 0.06% to 102.25. In commodities, crude oil prices saw a notable decline, with Brent Crude down nearly 4% to $110.73 and WTI Crude falling over 1% to $101.60. Gold and Silver also traded lower. The US 10-year Treasury yield rose by over 1% to 5.05%.

Traders should respect these option-chain derived levels in conjunction with the technical levels for today's trading range.

Stock & sector news that matters

  • ACME Solar shares rally 5% after Q2 profit jumps over 100%, signs 400MV agreement with NTPC

    • ACME Solar reported a substantial 133.5% jump in Q2 FY27 profit to ₹269 crore and a 67.5% rise in revenue to ₹1,007 crore, further boosted by a 25-year power purchase agreement with NTPC. Why it matters: This news is highly positive for ACME Solar, potentially driving its stock performance today. It also highlights strong growth in the renewable energy sector, which could positively impact other players.

  • Jio Platforms is becoming more than a telecom company: Dolat Capital

    • Dolat Capital suggests Jio Platforms is evolving beyond traditional telecom, driven by tariff hikes, 5G monetisation, home broadband, and enterprise digital services. Why it matters: This outlook provides a positive long-term narrative for Jio Platforms, potentially influencing investor sentiment ahead of its Q2 results (due tomorrow) and for the broader telecom and digital services sector.

  • US Market: Fed holds rates steady, but bond investors remain concerned over inflation

    • The US Federal Reserve maintained interest rates, but the Treasury yield curve steepened as investors weighed inflation risks, fiscal concerns, and the future rate outlook. Why it matters: While a rate pause is generally seen as positive, persistent inflation concerns in the US bond market could lead to higher US yields, potentially impacting FII flows into emerging markets like India.

  • HUL shares rise 3% after Q2 results. Jefferies, Nomura cut target price but forecast 29% upside potential

    • Hindustan Unilever reported a 4% year-on-year increase in Q2 FY27 net profit to ₹2,790 crore and 15% revenue growth to ₹18,226 crore, despite margin pressures from rising commodity prices. Why it matters: HUL's results provide insights into the FMCG sector's health. While revenue growth is strong, margin compression due to commodity prices is a key concern that could affect other FMCG players.

  • Indian bonds track US Treasuries higher amid oil-price relief

    • Indian government bonds saw gains, supported by a rally in US Treasuries and a drop in crude oil prices, although a large upcoming bond sale and a weak rupee limited the extent of the gains. Why it matters: Lower crude oil prices are a significant positive for the Indian economy and bond markets, potentially easing inflation and fiscal concerns. This could lead to a more stable interest rate environment domestically.

  • Vedanta Aluminium more than doubles profit; cost cuts in focus to drive earnings

    • Vedanta Aluminium's September-quarter net profit more than doubled, with revenue up 41% and EBITDA surging 83% year-on-year, driven by higher production volumes and cost efficiencies. Why it matters: This strong performance from Vedanta Aluminium could set a positive tone for the metals sector, highlighting the impact of operational efficiencies and commodity price movements.

Events & data to watch — and how markets usually react

  • European Central Bank (ECB) Rate Decision: The ECB is expected to hold interest rates steady. Central bank policy announcements are typically met with increased market volatility as investors react to the decision and accompanying commentary on the economic outlook and future policy path.
  • US Bond Market Sentiment: Continued concerns over inflation and US Treasury yields could influence global risk appetite. Markets often react to rising US yields by re-evaluating investments in emerging markets, potentially leading to FII outflows or increased caution.
  • Jio Platforms Q2 Results (tomorrow): While not today, the anticipation of results from a major listed entity like Jio Platforms can influence market sentiment, particularly for the telecom and digital services sectors.

What to watch in today's session

  1. GIFT Nifty's strong signal: The significant gap-up indicated by GIFT Nifty will set the initial tone for the Indian market open.
  2. Crude Oil Prices: The notable fall in Brent and WTI crude prices is a key positive for India and will be closely monitored for its impact on oil marketing companies, paint, and aviation sectors.
  3. FII/DII Flows: After both institutional categories were net buyers yesterday, their continued stance will be crucial for market direction.
  4. Key Technical & Option Chain Levels: Nifty's 23000 Call OI resistance and 22700 Put OI support, along with the narrow CPRs, will define the day's trading range.
  5. Corporate Action Stocks: Companies going ex-dividend today, such as CRISIL, SHRIRAMFIN, and KAJARIACER, might see price adjustments and increased trading activity.
  6. Global Cues: The US 10-year yield movement and the outcome of the ECB rate decision will continue to influence global risk sentiment and, consequently, Indian markets.

Bottom line

Indian markets are poised for a positive start today, largely driven by a strong GIFT Nifty signal and supportive global cues from the US and European markets, coupled with a significant drop in crude oil prices. The Nifty's ability to sustain above the 22900-23000 zone will be key for further upside. However, rising US bond yields and lingering global inflation concerns could introduce volatility, urging traders to remain cautious and vigilant of key support and resistance levels.
