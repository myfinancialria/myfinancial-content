Pre-Market Brief — 03 November 2026

TL;DR

  • Global Tone: Mixed cues from global markets; US tech indices closed lower while Dow gained, and Asian markets were largely subdued.
  • GIFT Nifty Bias: Signalling a positive open for Indian markets, with a notable gap-up indicated.
  • FII/DII Stance: Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) were net buyers yesterday, continuing their supportive trend, while Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) were net sellers.
  • Key News: Reliance Industries' significant surge post Q2 results and GRSE securing a large ₹32,000 crore Indian Navy contract are major market movers.
  • Nifty Level to Watch: The 23700-23800 range will be crucial, acting as immediate support and resistance based on option chain data.
  • Day's Main Risk: Rising crude oil prices and global geopolitical tensions could weigh on sentiment, alongside caution ahead of US Midterm elections.

How global markets traded — and what it means for India

Overnight, global markets presented a mixed picture. In the US, the Dow Jones Industrial Average managed a positive close, gaining 0.41%, but technology-heavy Nasdaq and the broader S&P 500 both ended in negative territory, down 1.07% and 0.51% respectively. European markets like the FTSE 100 also saw declines. This morning, Asian markets are trading with a mixed bias; Japan's Nikkei 225 is down, while Hong Kong's Hang Seng posted gains, and Shanghai traded lower.

Index% Change
Dow Jones+0.41%
Nasdaq-1.07%
S&P 500-0.51%
FTSE 100-0.60%
Nikkei 225-0.33%
Hang Seng+0.57%
Shanghai-0.82%

Interpretation: The divergence in global markets, particularly the weakness in US tech while the Dow showed resilience, suggests a cautious but not entirely negative global backdrop. The mixed Asian cues indicate that Indian markets might open with some uncertainty, but the strong GIFT Nifty signal points to a potentially positive start.

Currency, commodities and yields

The Indian Rupee (USD/INR) strengthened slightly against the US Dollar, closing at 94.82, a marginal decrease of 0.08%. The Dollar Index, which measures the greenback against a basket of major currencies, saw a minor uptick of 0.02% to 101.77. Crude oil prices witnessed a significant rebound, with Brent Crude jumping 1.57% to $106.42 and WTI Crude rising 1.63% to $98.03. Gold and Silver both declined, with Gold down 1.29% to $3918.40 and Silver falling 3.68% to $48.85. The US 10-Year Treasury Yield eased by 0.71% to 4.89%.

Interpretation: A slightly stronger Rupee is generally positive for Indian importers and helps in managing imported inflation. However, the notable surge in crude oil prices is a significant concern for India, a major oil importer. This could put pressure on sectors like oil marketing companies (OMCs), paints, and aviation due to higher input costs. The fall in US 10-Year yields is a positive sign, potentially making emerging markets like India more attractive for foreign capital.

GIFT Nifty — what it's signalling

The GIFT Nifty is trading at 23762.50, up by a significant 1.54%.

Interpretation: This strong positive movement in GIFT Nifty indicates a substantial gap-up opening for the Nifty 50 today, suggesting a bullish start to the trading session for Indian equities.

Yesterday, Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) were net buyers in the Indian cash market, injecting ₹3,115 crore. Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs), however, were net sellers, pulling out ₹-392 crore.

Option-chain signals (OI) — where support & resistance sit

NIFTY: The Put-Call Ratio (PCR) for Nifty is 1.05, leaning towards a neutral-to-supportive sentiment. The highest Call OI is concentrated at 23800 (2.98 crore contracts), followed by 23750 (2.25 crore contracts), indicating these levels could act as strong resistance. On the Put side, the highest OI is at 23700 (2.98 crore contracts), followed by 23650 (2.18 crore contracts), suggesting these are key support zones.

BANK NIFTY: The PCR for Bank Nifty is 0.9, suggesting a slightly cautious-to-neutral sentiment. Major Call OI is seen at 57000 (12.15 lakh contracts), 56000 (10.76 lakh contracts), and 55000 (10.23 lakh contracts), which will act as resistance. For support, 55000 (12.55 lakh contracts) holds the highest Put OI, followed by 54000 (7.31 lakh contracts).

SENSEX: Sensex's PCR is 0.71, indicating a cautious or slightly bearish bias. Significant Call OI is at 78000 (13.87 lakh contracts), 77500 (10.34 lakh contracts), and 77000 (10.32 lakh contracts), forming resistance. Key Put OI is at 76000 (11.80 lakh contracts) and 76500 (10.86 lakh contracts), providing support.

Interpretation: Combining technicals and OI, for Nifty, the 23700 level is a strong immediate support, while 23800 is a significant resistance. A move past 23800 could see further upside. For Bank Nifty, 55000 is a critical pivot, acting as both resistance and support based on OI. The narrow CPR suggests a trending move, so a decisive break above 55700 or below 55100 will be key. Sensex faces resistance around 77000-77500, with support at 76000. Traders should respect these levels today, as a break in either direction could lead to accelerated moves.

Corporate actions today

  • SASKEN: Trades ex-dividend for an Interim Dividend of ₹12 per share. Shareholders on record date will receive this dividend.
  • RRKABEL: Trades ex-dividend for an Interim Dividend of ₹6.50 per share. The stock price will adjust downwards by this amount on the ex-date.
  • MAZDOCK: Trades ex-dividend for an Interim Dividend of ₹5 per share. Shareholders on record date will be eligible for this dividend.
  • IRBINVIT: Trades ex-distribution for a distribution of ₹1.625 per unit, comprising ₹1.475 per unit as interest and ₹0.15 per unit as return of capital.
  • INFY: Trades ex-dividend for an Interim Dividend of ₹26 per share. The stock price will typically reflect this adjustment.
  • GRAVITA: Trades ex-dividend for an Interim Dividend of ₹7.70 per share. Shareholders on record date will receive this dividend.

Stock & sector news that matters

  • GRSE shares jump 9% after signing Rs 32,000 crore Indian Navy contract for five next-generation corvettes.

    • * Garden Reach Shipbuilders & Engineers (GRSE) shares surged after securing a massive ₹32,000 crore contract from the Indian Navy for five Next Generation Corvettes. * Why it matters: This is a significant order for the defence sector and specifically for GRSE, ensuring a strong order book for the next nine years. It highlights the government's push for indigenous defence manufacturing and could bring the entire defence shipbuilding sector into focus.

  • 4 reasons why Reliance Industries shares can rally after Monday magic sparked historic 12% surge.

    • * Reliance Industries shares saw their biggest single-day gain since April 2020, adding nearly ₹2 lakh crore to its market capitalisation, following strong Q2 results. * Why it matters: RIL is a market heavyweight, and such a significant move can influence the broader indices. Its strong performance, driven by Q2 results, could provide a positive sentiment boost across the market, especially for large-cap stocks.

  • SBI Q2 Results: Standalone profit rises 14% YoY to Rs 23,038 crore, NII jumps 15%.

    • * State Bank of India (SBI) reported a 14% year-on-year increase in standalone profit to ₹23,038 crore for Q2 FY27, with Net Interest Income (NII) rising 15% to ₹49,143 crore. * Why it matters: As the largest public sector bank, SBI's robust results are a positive indicator for the entire banking sector. Strong NII growth suggests healthy loan book expansion and improved profitability, which could lift sentiment for other PSU banks and the broader financial segment.

  • Bajaj Finserv Q2 Results: Net profit jumps 23% YoY to Rs 2,758 crore, shares rise 2%.

    • * Bajaj Finserv reported a 23% year-on-year jump in net profit to ₹2,758 crore for Q2 FY27, with revenue from operations increasing by 17% to ₹43,416 crore. * Why it matters: Strong results from a major NBFC like Bajaj Finserv indicate resilience in the financial services sector beyond traditional banking. This could positively impact other diversified financial companies and insurance players.

  • Angel One, CDSL, BSE, other capital market stocks jump up to 8%. 3 reasons behind the rally.

    • * Capital market stocks, including Angel One, CDSL, and BSE, rallied up to 8% on hopes of Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG) tax relief, renewed FII buying, and a broader market recovery. * Why it matters: This news highlights a strong sectoral tailwind for capital market intermediaries. Any positive news regarding LTCG tax relief could significantly boost investor sentiment and trading activity, directly benefiting these companies.

  • German bond yields rise as oil climbs ahead of US Midterm elections.

    • * German government bond yields increased due to rising oil prices and caution surrounding the upcoming US midterm elections. * Why it matters: This is a global macro factor. Rising bond yields in major economies can impact global capital flows, potentially making emerging markets less attractive. The US Midterm elections are a key event that could introduce global political uncertainty and market volatility.

Events & data to watch — and how markets usually react

  • US Midterm Elections (today/this week): While not directly an Indian event, the outcome of the US midterm elections can significantly influence global market sentiment, US policy direction, and the strength of the US dollar. Markets typically react with increased volatility around such major political events, as investors assess potential impacts on economic policy and geopolitical stability.
  • Crude Oil Price Movement: The recent rebound in Brent and WTI crude oil prices is a critical factor to watch. Sustained high crude prices can lead to inflationary pressures in India, impacting corporate margins (especially for OMCs, paint, and aviation companies) and potentially influencing RBI's monetary policy stance. Markets generally react negatively to sharp increases in crude oil prices.
  • NSE International Exchange 23.5-hour Trading: The NSE International Exchange has extended its trading hours to nearly 23.5 hours, aiming to provide continuous market access across global time zones. While this is a structural change, it could gradually increase liquidity and global participation in Indian derivatives, potentially leading to more efficient price discovery and reduced overnight gaps over time.
  • Corporate Earnings Season: Although no Nifty 500/large-cap results are scheduled for today, the ongoing earnings season continues to be a key driver. Strong results from major companies like RIL, SBI, and Bajaj Finserv have already set a positive tone. Markets will continue to react to individual company results as they are announced throughout the week, leading to stock-specific movements.

What to watch in today's session

  1. Nifty 50's Open and Initial Price Action: Given the strong GIFT Nifty signal, how Nifty handles the gap-up and sustains above the 23700-23750 zone will be crucial.
  2. Crude Oil Price Movement: Monitor international crude oil prices closely. Any further significant rise could temper positive sentiment.
  3. Key Levels for Nifty and Bank Nifty: Watch Nifty's 23800 resistance and 23700 support, and Bank Nifty's 55000 pivot and the R1/S1 levels for trending moves.
  4. Corporate Actions: Keep an eye on stocks going ex-dividend today, especially Infosys (INFY), as their prices will adjust.
  5. Sectoral Performance: Defence stocks (like GRSE) and capital market intermediaries (like Angel One, CDSL, BSE) could see continued momentum based on recent news. Banking stocks, especially PSU banks, might also be in focus after SBI's strong results.
  6. FII Flows: Continue to monitor FII activity; sustained buying would be a positive sign.

Bottom line

Indian markets are poised for a positive open today, largely driven by a strong GIFT Nifty signal and supportive FII flows. The key will be for Nifty to sustain above the 23700-23750 range, with 23800 acting as immediate resistance. While strong corporate earnings from heavyweights like RIL and SBI provide a tailwind, rising crude oil prices and global political uncertainties (US Midterms) warrant caution. Traders should watch for a trending move in Bank Nifty given its extremely narrow CPR.
