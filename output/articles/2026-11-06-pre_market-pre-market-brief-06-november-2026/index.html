Pre-Market Brief — 06 November 2026

TL;DR

  • Global markets saw a significant sell-off overnight, particularly in US tech and Asian indices.
  • GIFT Nifty is signalling a positive start for Indian markets, defying the global weakness.

How global markets traded — and what it means for India

Global markets experienced a broad-based decline overnight, with US indices leading the fall amid concerns over AI infrastructure spending and valuations. The Nasdaq plummeted over 5.5%, while the Dow Jones and S&P 500 also saw significant corrections. Asian markets followed suit this morning, with the Nikkei 225 dropping nearly 6% and Hang Seng and Shanghai also closing lower. This widespread global weakness suggests a cautious opening for Indian equities, despite some domestic positive factors.

FII/DII flows — who's driving the tape

Yesterday, Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) were significant net buyers in the Indian cash market, injecting ₹14,975 crore. Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs), on the other hand, were net sellers, offloading ₹10,445 crore. This trend of FII buying and DII selling has been consistent over the last five sessions.

Bottom line

Indian markets are poised for an interesting session, with a positive signal from GIFT Nifty potentially countering the significant global market weakness. The sustained FII buying remains a strong domestic tailwind, but the global tech sell-off and concerns over AI valuations present a notable risk. The Nifty's ability to hold above its CPR pivot of 24759.93 will be crucial in determining the day's direction, with 25000 acting as a psychological resistance. Investors should remain cautious and monitor global developments closely.
