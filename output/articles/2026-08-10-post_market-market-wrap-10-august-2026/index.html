Market Wrap — 10 August 2026

TL;DR

  • Indian benchmark indices, Nifty 50 and Sensex, ended largely flat today amidst mixed global cues and elevated oil prices.
  • The broader market, however, saw Nifty Midcap 100 post decent gains, while Nifty Smallcap 100 declined.
  • Realty emerged as the top-gaining sector, while PSU Bank was the biggest laggard.
  • Hitachi Energy India Ltd. was the top Nifty 500 gainer, surging over 10%.
  • Power Finance Corporation Ltd. (PFC) led the Nifty 500 losers, dropping over 8%.
  • Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) and Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) were net buyers in the cash market today.
  • Futures & Options (F&O) build-up data is unavailable for this session and will be populated from the next wrap.

How the market behaved today

The Indian equity market witnessed a largely range-bound session today, with benchmark indices ending nearly flat. The Nifty 50 closed marginally higher at 24583.80, up by a mere 0.05%, after trading in a narrow band. Similarly, the Bank Nifty ended slightly in the red, closing at 57686.95 with a change of -0.10%. The day was characterized by cautious trading, with both indices failing to establish a clear directional trend.

What moved the market — the factors

The Indian market's largely flat performance today was primarily influenced by a combination of elevated global crude oil prices and ongoing geopolitical uncertainties in the Middle East, which weighed on investor sentiment. Despite softer US jobs data supporting expectations of lower US rates, rising crude prices capped gains in the bond market and contributed to a cautious mood in equities.

Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) and Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) both turned net buyers in the cash market today, providing some support. However, this was largely offset by profit-booking and specific stock-related news. While the frontline indices struggled for direction, the Nifty Midcap 100 showed resilience, closing with a healthy gain of 0.62%, suggesting continued interest in the broader market. Conversely, the Nifty Smallcap 100 ended in negative territory. Sectoral rotation was evident, with Realty, Media, and Metal sectors seeing buying interest, while PSU Bank, Pharma, and FMCG faced selling pressure. Heavyweight stock moves, particularly the significant drops in Power Finance Corporation (PFC) and Bharat Forge, also contributed to the overall market dynamics.

News that moved stocks

  • Bharat Forge reports Q1 net loss: Bharat Forge shares plunged nearly 9% after the company posted an ₹89.89 crore consolidated net loss in Q1 FY27, compared to a profit a year earlier, primarily due to exceptional items related to restructuring and VRS. This news directly impacted the stock, making it one of the top Nifty 500 losers.
  • Astra Microwave shares tank on weak Q1 results: Astra Microwave shares fell nearly 9% after reporting a 24% decline in Q1FY27 profit and a 12% drop in revenue year-on-year. The weak earnings report led to significant selling pressure on the stock.
  • Gland Pharma's Q1 net profit jumps 47% YoY: Gland Pharma reported a robust 47% year-on-year increase in Q1 FY27 net profit to ₹317 crore, driven by strong performance in its CDMO and B2B businesses. This positive earnings news could influence sentiment for the pharma sector.
  • SEBI steps up AI use to police finfluencers: SEBI is intensifying its use of artificial intelligence through 'Project Sudarsan' to monitor misleading financial advice on social media, with 'Sebi R(AI)DAR' reviewing advertisements. This regulatory development highlights SEBI's focus on investor protection and could impact the financial advisory landscape.
  • Indian bonds steady as soft US data offsets oil rise: Indian government bonds remained largely steady as softer US jobs data supported expectations of lower US rates, even as rising crude prices capped gains. This indicates a cautious approach in the debt market, influenced by both global and commodity factors.

Event breakdown — what happened today and how it hit the market

Today's session was primarily driven by broad market flows and global cues rather than a single defining event. The persistent uncertainty surrounding the Strait of Hormuz, which kept crude oil prices elevated, was a significant factor weighing on overall market sentiment. This global concern, coupled with mixed signals from international markets (European shares holding steady), contributed to the range-bound and cautious trading seen in India.

Specific stock-related news, such as the Q1 FY27 results of Bharat Forge and Astra Microwave, led to sharp movements in their respective shares, impacting the Automobile & Auto Components and Capital Goods sectors. Conversely, positive results from Gland Pharma offered some cheer to the Pharmaceutical sector. The regulatory news about SEBI's 'Project Sudarsan' to monitor finfluencers highlighted ongoing efforts to enhance market integrity, though its immediate impact on the broader market was limited.

Setup for tomorrow

As we head into tomorrow's session, global cues will continue to play a crucial role. Elevated crude oil prices, with Brent Crude closing at $84.53 and WTI Crude at $79.10, will remain a key factor to watch, as they can influence inflation expectations and corporate margins. The Dollar Index edged up slightly to 99.72, while the US 10-year yield saw a marginal dip to 4.66. Investors will also keep an eye on overnight developments in US and Asian markets. Domestically, the performance of the PSU Bank sector, which was a significant laggard today, and continued interest in the Realty and Capital Goods sectors will be important. Key levels for Nifty 50 and Bank Nifty will be closely monitored for directional cues.
