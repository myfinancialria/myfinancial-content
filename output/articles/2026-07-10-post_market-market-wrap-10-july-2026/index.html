Market Wrap — 10 July 2026

TL;DR

  • Indian equities witnessed a strong rally today, with all major indices closing significantly higher.
  • Broad-based buying, particularly in Realty, PSU Bank, and IT sectors, fueled the market's upward momentum.
  • Godrej Industries Ltd. emerged as the top Nifty 500 gainer, surging over 16%.
  • Schneider Electric Infrastructure Ltd. was the biggest Nifty 500 loser, declining nearly 5%.
  • Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) were strong net buyers, while Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) turned net sellers.

How the market behaved today

Indian equity markets concluded the trading session on a strong note, extending their winning streak with broad-based gains. The Nifty 50 closed at 24206.90, up 1.02%, while the Bank Nifty surged 1.39% to settle at 58045.90. The day saw a positive trend throughout, with indices maintaining their upward trajectory after opening higher. Midcap and Smallcap indices also outperformed, reflecting a healthy market breadth.

What moved the market — the factors

Today's market rally was primarily driven by a combination of positive sentiment, strong Domestic Institutional Investor (DII) buying, and favourable global cues. Despite mixed US stock futures ahead of SK Hynix's US debut, easing Middle East tensions and falling oil prices contributed to a positive global backdrop. On the domestic front, DIIs injected a substantial ₹2,058 crore into the market, offsetting the ₹533 crore outflow from Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs).

Sector-specific strength was evident, with Realty, PSU Bank, and IT sectors leading the charge. Optimism surrounding banking stocks and positive earnings expectations, as highlighted by the mention of TCS earnings in market wrap reports, further lifted investor sentiment. Heavyweight stocks, particularly those in the financial services and IT sectors, played a crucial role in pushing the benchmark indices higher. The broad-based nature of the rally, with both midcap and smallcap indices also performing strongly, indicated widespread participation.

Sectors — winners and losers

The day witnessed robust performance across several sectors, with Realty, PSU Bank, and IT leading the gains.

  • PSU BANK: +3.03% - Public sector banks rallied significantly, possibly on the back of positive banking sentiment.
  • IT: +1.96% - Technology stocks continued their upward momentum, potentially buoyed by global tech trends and earnings expectations.

Top 3 Loser Sectors:

  • FMCG: -0.08% - The Fast-Moving Consumer Goods sector remained largely flat, showing marginal weakness compared to the broader market.
  • PHARMA: +0.07% - The pharmaceutical sector also traded sideways, with negligible gains.
  • MEDIA: +0.10% - The media sector saw minimal positive movement, underperforming the broader market.

News that moved stocks

  • Primary Market Buzz: SEBI Approves Multiple IPOs

    • Sebi has given its nod to IPO proposals from Tonbo Imaging India, Marri Retail, Zetwerk, and Gujarat Victory Forgings, signaling a robust pipeline for the primary market. Additionally, Milky Mist Dairy Food Ltd. is reportedly preparing for a $213 million IPO this month, aiming to be India's largest dairy offering. Kusumgar Limited's IPO also saw immense demand, being oversubscribed 136 times. Lord's Mark Industries Limited successfully listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange today. This flurry of IPO activity indicates strong investor appetite and a healthy primary market.

  • Global Market Dynamics and Middle East Tensions

    • US stock futures showed mixed signals ahead of SK Hynix's US debut, which could impact its valuation against Micron. Meanwhile, renewed hostilities in the Middle East spurred caution, causing the Indian Rupee to slip on the week, although it ended largely unchanged today. Easing Middle East tensions, however, contributed to a positive global backdrop for equities.

  • Midcap Stocks Hit 52-Week Highs

    • Nykaa, Godrej Industries, and four other BSE MidCap stocks reached fresh 52-week highs, demonstrating strong momentum in the broader market. This highlights specific pockets of strength within the midcap segment.

  • Analyst Coverage Boosts Vedanta Aluminium

    • Nuvama Institutional Equities and Motilal Oswal Financial Services initiated 'Buy' ratings on Vedanta Aluminium shares, citing strong earnings growth and cost reduction, with target prices of ₹540. This positive analyst coverage could drive investor interest in the stock.

Event breakdown — what happened today and how it hit the market

Today's session was primarily driven by broad-based positive flows and supportive global cues, rather than a single defining event. The positive sentiment was bolstered by consistent buying from Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs), who pumped over ₹2,000 crore into the market. Positive global cues, including easing Middle East tensions and a generally positive outlook for US stocks despite mixed futures, also contributed to the bullish mood.

Sector-specific strength was notable, with Realty, PSU Bank, and IT sectors witnessing significant rallies. Reports of positive earnings expectations, such as those for TCS, likely added to the overall optimism, particularly benefiting the IT sector. The approval of several IPOs by SEBI and the listing of Lord's Mark Industries also kept the primary market in focus, reflecting healthy investor interest.

Setup for tomorrow

Looking ahead to tomorrow, global cues will continue to play a significant role. US stock futures are currently mixed, and investors will be watching for any developments in the Middle East, which has recently impacted the Rupee. The debut of SK Hynix in the US market could also influence global tech sentiment. On the domestic front, the consistent DII buying trend will be crucial to watch. Key levels for Nifty 50 would be around its day's high of 24228 and the psychological 24000 mark for support. Sectors like IT and Financial Services, which saw strong momentum today, could remain in focus.
