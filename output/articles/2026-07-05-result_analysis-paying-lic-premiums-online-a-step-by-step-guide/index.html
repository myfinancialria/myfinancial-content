Paying LIC Premiums Online: A Step-by-Step Guide

TL;DR

  • Paying your Life Insurance Corporation (LIC) premiums online offers unparalleled convenience, allowing payments anytime, anywhere.
  • LIC provides multiple digital channels for premium payment, including its official website, the 'Pay Direct' option, and its dedicated mobile app.
  • Policyholders can use various payment methods like UPI, Net Banking, Debit Cards, and Credit Cards for their online transactions.
  • E-receipts are generated instantly upon successful payment and are valid for income tax purposes, eliminating the need for physical receipts.
  • It is crucial to ensure policy details are correct and to avoid duplicate payments, especially if standing instructions are already in place.

What this means in plain terms

In today's fast-paced world, managing financial commitments efficiently is key. For millions of Indians, paying life insurance premiums to the Life Insurance Corporation (LIC) has traditionally involved visiting branches or designated collection centres. However, LIC has significantly streamlined this process by offering robust online payment facilities.

This means you no longer need to take time out of your busy schedule to stand in queues. Whether you're at home, in the office, or even travelling, you can easily pay your LIC premiums using your computer or smartphone. This digital shift not only saves time but also provides instant confirmation and a digital record of your payments, making financial management much simpler. The Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI) oversees the insurance sector, ensuring policyholder protection and transparent practices, which extends to secure online transactions.

Understanding LIC's Online Payment Options

LIC offers several convenient digital avenues for policyholders to pay their premiums, catering to both registered and non-registered users. These options are designed for flexibility and ease of use.

The Official LIC Website

The primary online channel is the official LIC website (www.licindia.in). Here, policyholders can access the 'Customer Portal' for a comprehensive suite of services, including premium payment. Registered users can log in to their accounts to view policy details and make payments. The website is available 24x7, offering real-time updates of policy master data upon successful payment.

LIC Pay Direct (Quick Pay)

For those who prefer not to register or log in, LIC provides the 'Pay Direct' option. This facility allows for quick payment of renewal premiums, advance premiums, loan interest, and loan repayments without requiring a user ID or password. You simply need your policy number and other basic details.

The LIC Mobile App

LIC also offers a dedicated mobile application, "LIC Digital," available on both Google Play Store and Apple App Store. This app brings many functionalities of the customer portal to your smartphone, enabling premium payments on the go. Beyond LIC's official app, various third-party mobile payment apps like PhonePe, Paytm, Google Pay, and Amazon Pay also facilitate LIC premium payments.

Step-by-Step Guide: Paying via Website

Paying your LIC premium through the official website is a straightforward process, whether you are a registered user or opting for a quick payment.

For Registered Users

  1. Visit the Official Website: Go to www.licindia.in.
  2. Access Customer Portal: Click on 'Customer Portal' and then 'Registered User'.
  3. Log In: Enter your user ID/email/mobile number, password, and date of birth to sign in.
  4. Select Policy: Navigate to the 'Self/Policies' tab to view your policies.
  5. Initiate Payment: Confirm the due date and click on the 'Pay Premium' button.
  6. Choose Payment Mode: Select your preferred payment method (UPI, Net Banking, Debit Card, Credit Card).
  7. Complete Transaction: Follow the prompts to complete the payment. An e-receipt will be generated and emailed to your registered ID.

For Non-Registered Users (Guest Payment via Pay Direct)

  1. Visit the Official Website: Go to www.licindia.in.
  2. Select 'Pay Premium Online': On the homepage, find and click on 'Pay Premium Online'.
  3. Choose 'Pay Direct': Select the 'Pay Direct (without login)' option.
  4. Select Transaction Type: From the dropdown, choose 'Renewal Premium/Revival', 'Loan Repayment', or 'Loan Interest Payment' as applicable.
  5. Enter Details: Fill in your policy number, instalment premium amount (without GST), date of birth, email ID, and mobile number.
  6. Verify and Proceed: Review the details carefully and click 'Submit' or 'Proceed'.
  7. Select Payment Mode: Choose your preferred payment option (UPI, Net Banking, Debit Card, Credit Card).
  8. Complete Transaction: Follow the instructions to finalise the payment. An e-receipt will be sent to the email ID provided.

Step-by-Step Guide: Paying via Mobile App

Using mobile apps for LIC premium payments offers maximum flexibility. You can use LIC's official app or popular third-party payment platforms.

Using the LIC Digital Mobile App

  1. Download the App: Search for "LIC Digital" on Google Play Store or Apple App Store and install it.
  2. Register/Log In: If you're a new user, register your policy details. Existing customer portal users can log in with their credentials.
  3. Select Premium Payment: Navigate to the premium payment section within the app.
  4. Enter Policy Details: Input the policy number and other required information.
  5. Choose Payment Method: Select from available options like UPI, Net Banking, Debit Card, or Credit Card.
  6. Confirm and Pay: Complete the transaction. An e-receipt will be available in the app and sent to your registered email.

Using Third-Party Payment Apps (e.g., PhonePe, Paytm, Google Pay)

  1. Open the App: Launch your preferred payment app (e.g., PhonePe, Paytm, Google Pay).
  2. Navigate to Insurance/Bill Pay: Look for the 'Insurance' or 'Bill Payment' section.
  3. Select LIC: Choose 'Life Insurance Corporation (LIC)' from the list of insurers.
  4. Enter Policy Details: Input your policy number and other necessary details like date of birth.
  5. Verify Amount: The app will display your premium due amount.
  6. Select Payment Method: Choose your desired payment option (UPI, linked bank account, debit/credit card).
  7. Complete Payment: Authorise the payment. You will receive an instant confirmation and a digital receipt within the app and via email.

Essential Tips for Smooth Online Premium Payments

To ensure a hassle-free experience when paying your LIC premiums online, keep these points in mind:

Accepted Payment Methods

LIC's online platforms accept a variety of payment options:

  • UPI (Unified Payments Interface): A popular and often free method for instant payments.
  • Net Banking: Directly debit funds from your bank account.
  • Debit Cards: Use your debit card for direct payment.
  • Credit Cards: Accepted for premium payments. Notably, LIC has waived convenience fees on credit card payments to promote digital transactions.
  • Bharat QR Code: Another convenient option for quick payments.

Checking Payment Status and Receipts

Upon successful payment, an e-receipt is generated immediately and sent to your registered email ID. This e-receipt is valid for income tax purposes. If you don't receive it, you can often download it from the 'View Transaction Status' section on the LIC website (under 'Pay Direct') or your customer portal. In case of debit but no receipt, wait for 48 working hours, then contact LIC's support if still unresolved.

Avoiding Late Fees and Duplicate Payments

  • Grace Period: Be aware of the grace period for your policy. For monthly mode, it's 15 days; for quarterly, half-yearly, or yearly modes, it's one month but not less than 30 days.
  • Reminders: Set up automated reminders for premium due dates. Many online platforms allow you to do this.
  • Single Payment Mode: If you have standing instructions (e.g., NACH or Bill Pay) with your bank, avoid making separate online payments to prevent duplicate transactions. If a double payment occurs, LIC typically refunds it within 6-8 days.
  • Eligible Policies: Online payment is generally available for 'in-force' ordinary policies and those eligible for spot revival. It is typically not available for policies under the Salary Savings Scheme (SSS), ULIP, Health, or e-term plans.

A real example

Meet Anjali, 32, who works as a software engineer in Bengaluru with an annual CTC of ₹18 lakhs. She has two LIC policies: one for herself (Jeevan Anand, ₹25,000 annual premium, due in July) and another for her parents (Jeevan Labh, ₹15,000 half-yearly premium, due in June and December).

Historically, Anjali would visit a premium collection centre during her lunch break, which was often inconvenient. Now, she uses the LIC Pay Direct option and the PhonePe app for her payments.

Anjali's Payment for Jeevan Anand (Annual Premium):

  1. In early July, she receives an SMS reminder from LIC.
  2. She opens the PhonePe app, navigates to 'Insurance', and selects 'LIC'.
  3. She enters her Jeevan Anand policy number and her date of birth.
  4. The app fetches the premium due: ₹25,000.
  5. She selects UPI as her payment method and authorises the transaction using her UPI PIN.
  6. Within seconds, she receives a confirmation message on PhonePe and an e-receipt in her registered email for ₹25,000.

Anjali's Payment for Jeevan Labh (Half-Yearly Premium for Parents):

  1. In mid-June, she uses her laptop to visit the official LIC website (www.licindia.in).
  2. She clicks on 'Pay Premium Online' and then 'Pay Direct (without login)'.
  3. She selects 'Renewal Premium/Revival' and enters her parents' policy number, the premium amount (₹15,000), her mother's date of birth (as the policyholder), her email ID, and mobile number.
  4. After verifying the details, she proceeds to payment.
  5. She chooses Net Banking from her bank and completes the transaction.
  6. An e-receipt for ₹15,000 is immediately displayed on the screen, which she downloads, and a copy is also emailed to her.

By using these online methods, Anjali saves valuable time and ensures her policies remain active without any hassle.

FAQ

Can I pay my LIC premium online without registering on the LIC portal?

Yes, you can use the 'Pay Direct' option on the official LIC website, which allows you to pay renewal premiums and other dues without needing to log in or register.

Are there any extra charges for paying LIC premiums online?

No, LIC does not levy extra charges for online payments made via UPI, Debit Card, Net Banking, or Bharat QR Code. While credit card payments were historically subject to a convenience fee, LIC has waived these charges to promote digital transactions.

How do I get a receipt after paying my LIC premium online?

Upon successful online payment, an e-receipt is instantly generated and sent to your registered email ID. You can also download it from the 'View Transaction Status' section on the LIC website or from your customer portal.

Can I pay premiums for multiple LIC policies in one transaction online?

Yes, if you are a registered user on the LIC Customer Portal, you can typically add and manage multiple policies under your profile and pay their premiums in a single transaction.

What if my account is debited but the payment status is not updated or I don't receive a receipt?

If your account is debited but the receipt isn't generated, wait for up to 48 working hours. Often, the payment is processed, and the receipt is sent. If it's still unresolved, contact LIC support at bo_eps1@licindia.com with your policy number and transaction details.

Can I pay my LIC premium in advance?

Yes, you can pay your LIC premium in advance. Policyholders can deposit premiums up to the end of the current financial year. Additionally, premiums due in the next financial year can be paid up to three months in advance of their due date.

Are all types of LIC policies eligible for online premium payment?

Generally, online payment is available for ordinary 'in-force' policies and those eligible for spot revival. However, policies under the Salary Savings Scheme (SSS), ULIP, Health, and e-term plans are typically not eligible for online premium payments.
