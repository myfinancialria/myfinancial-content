In Asia, the Hang Seng and Shanghai Composite showed modest gains, while Japan's Nikkei 225 edged lower. This mixed global sentiment, with a slight negative lean from the US, suggests that Indian markets might open with some underlying caution, though Asian cues could provide some support.
