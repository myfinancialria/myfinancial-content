News that moved stocks

  • CleanMax beats Q2 EBITDA estimates, reports strong H1 growth

    • CleanMax surpassed analyst estimates for the third consecutive quarter, driven by portfolio expansion and strong business performance, reporting a 58% rise in H1 EBITDA. This positive earnings report would likely have generated investor interest in the company.

  • Bagmane REIT declares Rs 527cr distribution to unitholders for September quarter

    • Bagmane Prime Office REIT announced a significant distribution of ₹527 crore to unitholders for the September quarter, having leased 1.2 million square feet and achieving a committed occupancy of 90%. This news is positive for unitholders and could impact the REIT's valuation.

  • Sebi levies Rs 40 lakh penalty on four entities in Axis MF front-running case

    • SEBI imposed a total penalty of ₹40 lakh on four entities, including Woodstock Broking and Marfatia Stock Broking, for front-running trades in the Axis Mutual Fund case. Regulatory actions like these reinforce market integrity and can impact the reputation and operations of the involved entities.

  • Utkarsh Small Finance Bank reports sixth consecutive quarterly loss

    • Utkarsh Small Finance Bank reported a loss of ₹70 crore in the second quarter, marking six consecutive quarters of decline, primarily due to high provisions. This consistent underperformance is a significant concern for the bank and its investors.

  • Loan growth, fall in provisions help SBI profits in Q2

    • State Bank of India (SBI) reported a robust 14% year-on-year rise in Q2 FY27 net profit to ₹23,038 crore, driven by 18% loan growth, higher net interest income, and reduced loan loss provisions. This strong performance from a banking heavyweight like SBI is a positive signal for the broader banking sector.

  • MCX Q2 Results: Profit rockets 130% YoY to Rs 455 crore, revenue jumps 114%

    • Multi Commodity Exchange of India (MCX) reported an exceptional Q2 FY26 consolidated net profit, surging 130% year-on-year to ₹454.57 crore, with revenue jumping 114%. This strong earnings report, including a 151% rise in EBITDA, indicates robust operational performance and would likely have been a significant positive for MCX shares.

  • BLS International Q2 results: Record revenue on Digital Services growth

    • BLS International reported record Q2 FY27 revenue of ₹973 crore, up 32% year-on-year, driven by growth in Visa and Consular Services and Digital Services. This strong revenue growth highlights the company's expanding business and could attract investor attention.
