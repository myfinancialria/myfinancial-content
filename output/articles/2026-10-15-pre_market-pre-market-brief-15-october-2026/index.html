Pre-Market Brief — 15 October 2026

TL;DR

  • Global markets presented a mixed picture, with US indices closing lower and Asian markets mostly in the red, while European markets were flat.
  • GIFT Nifty is signalling a significant gap-up open for the Indian benchmark Nifty 50, diverging from global cues.
  • Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) continued their selling streak, while Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) provided strong buying support.
  • Nestle India reported robust Q2 results, and the IT sector saw a notable rebound over the past few days.
  • Nifty 50's key resistance sits around 23000-23150, with strong support at 22700-22760.
  • The expectation of a potential US Fed rate hike in December, following hotter-than-expected inflation, remains a key global risk factor.

How global markets traded — and what it means for India

Overnight, US markets closed lower, with the Nasdaq seeing a significant decline of 1.88% and the S&P 500 down by 1.11%. The Dow Jones also registered a marginal dip. In Asia this morning, markets are largely trading in the red, with Japan's Nikkei 225 down nearly 5% and Hong Kong's Hang Seng and China's Shanghai Composite also showing losses. The UK's FTSE 100, however, managed a slight gain.

Currency, commodities and yields

The Indian Rupee strengthened against the US Dollar, with USD/INR closing at 94.96, down 0.34%. The Dollar Index also saw a decline of 0.46% to 102.08. Crude oil prices softened, with Brent Crude down 2.20% to $103.73 and WTI Crude falling 1.41% to $93.75. Gold and Silver both saw gains, rising 1.38% and 1.87% respectively. The US 10-Year Treasury Yield decreased by 0.52% to 5.19%.

Interpretation: A stronger Rupee (lower USD/INR) is generally positive for importers and helps to curb imported inflation, while potentially reducing tailwinds for IT and pharma exporters. Falling crude oil prices are a significant positive for India, easing pressure on oil marketing companies (OMCs), paint, and aviation sectors. The decline in US 10-Year yields is a relief for global bond markets and could potentially reduce the risk of FII outflows from emerging markets like India. Rising gold and silver prices often indicate a flight to safety amidst global uncertainties.

GIFT Nifty — what it's signalling

The GIFT Nifty is currently trading at 23043.00, showing a significant gain of +1.86%.

Interpretation: This strong positive movement in GIFT Nifty suggests that the Indian Nifty 50 index is likely to open with a substantial gap-up today, potentially defying the negative cues from broader Asian markets.

FII/DII flows — who's driving the tape

Yesterday, Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) remained net sellers in the Indian cash equity market, offloading shares worth ₹-1,857 crore. Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs), however, continued to provide strong support, buying equities worth ₹2,670 crore. This trend of FII selling being absorbed by DII buying has been consistent over the last five sessions.

Corporate actions today

  • SHIVAMAUTO: Trades ex-rights today for its Rights Issue in the ratio of 19:25 at a premium of ₹10 per share. This means shareholders on record date will be eligible to apply for new shares at the specified price.
  • SHRADDHA: Trades ex-rights today for its Rights Issue in the ratio of 3:20 at a premium of ₹150 per share. Similar to SHIVAMAUTO, the share price will adjust to reflect the rights entitlement.
  • ANANDRATHI: Trades ex-dividend today for an Interim Dividend of ₹4 per share. On the ex-date, the stock price typically adjusts downwards by the dividend amount, and shareholders buying today will not be eligible for this dividend.

Events & data to watch — and how markets usually react

  • US Federal Reserve Rate Hike Expectations: Citigroup now expects the US Federal Reserve to raise interest rates by 25 basis points in December, following hotter-than-expected September inflation data. Markets typically react with caution and increased volatility around expectations and actual announcements of interest rate hikes from major central banks like the Fed, as it impacts global liquidity and capital flows.
  • Indian Bond Market Movement: The easing of US Treasury yields is providing some relief to Indian government bonds. Bond markets are sensitive to global yield movements and domestic liquidity conditions, with declining yields generally seen as positive for equity markets, especially rate-sensitive sectors.
  • FII Flow Trends: The continued FII selling trend, despite DII buying, will be closely watched. Sustained FII outflows can put pressure on the market, while a reversal could provide a significant boost.

What to watch in today's session

  1. GIFT Nifty's lead: Monitor if the Indian market open truly reflects the strong gap-up indicated by GIFT Nifty, or if global negative cues temper the initial enthusiasm.
  2. Nifty 50's 23000-23200 resistance: Watch how Nifty reacts to the crucial 23000-23200 zone, which has high Call OI and technical resistance. A sustained move above this could signal further strength.
  3. FII vs. DII dynamics: Observe the FII and DII cash market activity. Continued DII support will be crucial to absorb any further FII selling pressure.
  4. IT Sector Momentum: After the recent rally, watch if IT stocks can sustain their upward trajectory, especially given the mixed global tech sentiment.
  5. Nestle India and FMCG: Keep an eye on Nestle India's stock performance following its strong Q2 results, and its potential impact on the broader FMCG sector.
  6. Crude Oil Prices: Any further decline in crude oil prices would be a positive for India and could provide sector-specific tailwinds.

Bottom line

The Indian market is poised for a potentially gap-up open, as indicated by GIFT Nifty, despite a largely negative global backdrop. While strong domestic institutional buying has been a consistent support, the persistent FII selling and global concerns around a potential US Fed rate hike remain key factors to monitor. The Nifty's ability to sustain above the 23000-23200 resistance zone will be critical in determining the day's direction.
