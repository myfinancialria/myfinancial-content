Market Wrap — 01 July 2026

TL;DR

  • Indian markets started July on a positive note, with Nifty 50 closing above 24,000 and Bank Nifty gaining over 0.8%.
  • Realty, FMCG, and Media sectors led the charge, while IT stocks faced significant selling pressure.
  • Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) provided strong support, with substantial net buying, offsetting Foreign Institutional Investor (FII) outflows.
  • Midcap and Smallcap indices also ended in the green, indicating a broad-based positive sentiment, albeit with some sector-specific weakness.
  • Global cues were mixed, with US futures slipping due to geopolitical tensions, while the rupee weakened against the dollar.

How the market closed

Indian equities kicked off the new month with gains, as the Nifty 50 closed above the psychological 24,000 mark, advancing by 0.59%. The Bank Nifty outperformed, rising by 0.85% to settle above 58,000. Broader markets also saw positive momentum, with both the Nifty Midcap 100 and Smallcap 100 indices closing higher.

Sectors — winners and losers

The day's trading saw a clear divergence in sectoral performance, with Realty leading the pack, while the IT sector faced a significant downturn.

Top 3 Gainer Sectors:

  • REALTY: +3.58%. The sector surged on news of significant investment plans by major developers and the launch of a new REITs index by BSE, boosting investor confidence.
  • FMCG: +2.08%. This defensive sector saw strong buying interest, contributing positively to the broader market.
  • MEDIA: +2.07%. Media stocks also performed well, indicating a positive sentiment shift towards the sector.

Top 3 Loser Sectors:

  • IT: -2.01%. The information technology sector experienced a sharp decline, with several prominent IT stocks featuring among the top losers.
  • METAL: -0.99%. Metal stocks also ended in the red, contributing to the day's negative sectoral performance.
  • PHARMA: -0.57%. The pharmaceutical sector saw a moderate decline, closing lower for the session.

Market breadth

The market breadth was positive today, with 290 advances against 210 declines out of a total of 500 stocks. This indicates that more stocks gained than lost ground, suggesting a generally healthy underlying sentiment despite some sectoral weakness.

FII / DII flows

Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) continued their strong buying spree, providing crucial support to the Indian markets, while Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) remained net sellers.

What moved markets today

1. Realty Sector Boosted by Investments and New Index Launch M3M Group announced a significant investment of ₹10,000 crore for construction and land acquisition in FY27, primarily around Gurugram, signalling robust growth prospects for the real estate sector. Further boosting sentiment, BSE Index Services launched India's first REITs and Commercial Real Estate Index, designed for passive investment products, which is expected to attract more capital into the sector. These developments collectively contributed to the Realty sector's strong performance today.

2. RBI's Tighter Capital Market Norms Kick In New Reserve Bank of India (RBI) regulations, effective from today, aim to enhance financial stability by restricting bank exposure to real estate and securities. While intended to bolster the financial system, the market will be watching for any potential short-term impact on credit availability for these sectors.

3. Rupee Weakens Amid Global Cues The Indian rupee depreciated to a near three-week low, closing at 95.24 against the US dollar. This decline mirrored broader weakness in Asian currencies, influenced by investor caution ahead of US Federal Reserve Chair's remarks and ongoing geopolitical uncertainties surrounding US-Iran tensions. A weaker rupee can impact import costs and foreign investment flows.

4. SEBI Cracks Down on Pump-and-Dump Operations Market regulator SEBI took decisive action against a large-scale pump-and-dump scheme, banning 221 entities for up to seven years and imposing a ₹10 crore fine on the alleged ringleader. This move underscores SEBI's commitment to market integrity and protecting retail investors from manipulative practices.

Setup for tomorrow

Indian markets are likely to take cues from global developments and the ongoing FII/DII flow dynamics. With Nifty 50 holding above 24,000, the focus will be on sustaining this momentum. Investors will also monitor the impact of RBI's new capital market norms.

Globally, US stock futures are showing weakness due to heightened US-Iran tensions, which could weigh on sentiment. Asian markets will react to these geopolitical developments overnight. Key data releases and any further news on crude oil prices will also be important factors to watch.
