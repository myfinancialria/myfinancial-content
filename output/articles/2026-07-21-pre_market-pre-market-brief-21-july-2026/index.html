Pre-Market Brief — 21 July 2026

TL;DR

  • Global markets presented a mixed picture overnight, with US and European indices closing lower, while Asian markets, particularly Japan, showed some recovery.
  • GIFT Nifty indicates a mildly positive opening bias for the Indian benchmark Nifty 50 today.
  • Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) remained net sellers for the fifth consecutive session, while Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) continued to provide strong support.
  • The widening performance gap between HDFC Bank and ICICI Bank is a significant concern for the banking sector, potentially impacting broader market sentiment.
  • Nifty 50's immediate resistance stands around 24250, with key support seen at 24100.
  • The primary risks for today include sustained FII selling pressure and cautious global market sentiment.

How global markets traded — and what it means for India

Overnight, US markets closed with a negative bias, with the Dow Jones, Nasdaq, and S&P 500 all registering declines. European markets also ended in the red. However, Asian markets this morning are showing a mixed to positive trend, with Japan's Nikkei rebounding strongly after a recent sell-off, while China's Shanghai Composite also posted gains. This suggests a cautious but somewhat recovering sentiment in parts of Asia, contrasting with the overnight weakness in Western markets.

Currency, commodities and yields

The Indian Rupee (USD/INR) saw a slight depreciation against the US Dollar, while the Dollar Index edged lower. Crude oil prices (Brent and WTI) softened, which is generally a positive for India as a major oil importer. Gold and Silver prices, however, rallied significantly, supported by hopes of US-Iran peace talks. The US 10-Year Treasury Yield rose, indicating a shift towards risk-off sentiment or expectations of higher interest rates globally.

  • USD/INR: 96.33 (+0.05%)

    • Interpretation: A minor depreciation of the Rupee could offer a slight tailwind for export-oriented sectors like IT and pharmaceuticals, but it also increases import costs for the broader economy.

  • Dollar Index: 100.94 (-0.05%)

    • Interpretation: A marginally weaker Dollar Index could provide some relief for emerging market currencies, including the Rupee, by reducing global dollar strength.

  • Brent Crude: 88.69 (-0.59%)

    • Interpretation: Falling crude oil prices are a positive for India, potentially easing inflationary pressures and benefiting sectors like oil marketing companies (OMCs), paints, and aviation, which rely heavily on crude derivatives.

  • WTI Crude: 82.31 (-1.11%)

    • Interpretation: Similar to Brent, a decline in WTI crude prices is favourable for India's economy and corporate margins across various sectors.

  • Gold: 4059.60 (+1.23%)

    • Interpretation: The significant rise in gold prices suggests increased safe-haven demand, possibly due to global uncertainties or geopolitical developments like the US-Iran diplomatic efforts.

  • Silver: 58.28 (+2.61%)

    • Interpretation: A sharp jump in silver prices, mirroring gold, indicates strong investor interest in precious metals as a hedge against inflation or market volatility.

  • US 10Y Yield: 4.60 (+1.26%)

    • Interpretation: A rising US 10-year yield can make US dollar-denominated assets more attractive, potentially leading to capital outflows from emerging markets like India, posing a risk for FII flows.

The GIFT Nifty is trading at 24169.00, up by 0.23%. This suggests a mildly positive opening for the Nifty 50 today.

Interpretation: The positive indication from GIFT Nifty points towards a potential gap-up or a flat-to-positive start for the Indian benchmark index, despite the mixed global cues.

FII/DII flows — who's driving the tape

Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) continued their selling streak, offloading equities worth ₹-376 crore yesterday. In contrast, Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) remained net buyers, injecting ₹1,018 crore into the market, providing crucial support. This trend of FII selling and DII buying has been consistent over the last five trading sessions.

  • RAINBOW: Ex-dividend for Rs 3.50 Per Share. The stock price will adjust for this dividend.
  • ANGELONE: Ex-interim dividend for Re 1 Per Share. Eligible shareholders will receive this dividend.
  • LXCHEM: Ex-dividend for Rs 0.30 Per Share. The share price will adjust accordingly.
  • POCL: Face Value Split (Sub-Division) - From Rs 5/- Per Share To Rs 2/- Per Share. This means each existing share of Rs 5 face value will be split into 2.5 shares of Rs 2 face value. The total value of holdings remains the same, but the number of shares increases, and the price per share adjusts downwards proportionally.
  • PSB: Ex-dividend for Re 0.39 Per Share. The stock price will adjust for this dividend.

Stock & sector news that matters

HDFC Bank is lagging ICICI Bank where it matters as investors count Rs 5 lakh crore loss HDFC Bank's stock has seen a significant decline this year, leading to substantial investor value erosion. Analysts are noting a widening operational gap between HDFC Bank and ICICI Bank, with HDFC Bank facing margin pressure. Why it matters: This news is critical for the banking sector, particularly private banks. HDFC Bank is a heavyweight in Indian indices, and its underperformance compared to ICICI Bank could weigh on the broader Nifty Bank and Nifty Financial Services indices. Investors will be closely watching the performance of both banks today.

SBI Funds Management IPO: Shares list at 7% premium, but target prices go up to Rs 750. Time to buy? SBI Funds Management debuted with a premium of nearly 7% over its IPO price, although it fell short of grey market expectations. Brokerages, however, remain bullish, with some assigning a target price of ₹750. Why it matters: The listing performance of SBI Funds Management provides a gauge of investor appetite for new issues in the financial services sector. While the premium was modest, brokerage bullishness could attract further interest. This also reflects the broader sentiment in the primary market.

Jaiprakash Power shares surge 8% after Q1 profit jumps 69%, revenue rises 12% YoY Jaiprakash Power shares rallied 8.4% following strong Q1FY27 results, reporting a 12.2% rise in revenue and a 68.6% surge in profit year-on-year. Why it matters: This is positive news for Jaiprakash Power and could lead to continued upward momentum for the stock. Strong quarterly results often act as a catalyst for individual stock performance and could bring the power sector into focus.

UltraTech Cement shares gain 2% after Q1 results. Why Nuvama, other brokerages raised target? UltraTech Cement shares rose after the company posted a 17% year-on-year rise in Q1 FY27 consolidated net profit to ₹2,599 crore and a 16% increase in revenue to ₹24,648 crore. Why it matters: Strong results from a major player like UltraTech Cement are positive for the entire cement sector. Brokerage upgrades following good earnings can attract investor interest, suggesting potential tailwinds for other cement stocks.

Paytm shares gain 3% after Q1 results. What are Goldman Sachs, Citi and CLSA saying? Paytm shares rose 2.7% after the fintech firm reported a 79% year-on-year jump in Q1 consolidated net profit to ₹220 crore and a 28% increase in revenue from operations to ₹2,448 crore. Why it matters: Positive quarterly results from Paytm, a prominent fintech player, could boost sentiment for other companies in the digital payments and broader fintech space. Strong revenue and profit growth indicate improving business fundamentals.

CDSL share price falls after Sebi fines ₹1 crore over 2022 malware attack CDSL's share price declined after SEBI imposed a ₹1 crore fine on the company concerning a 2022 malware attack. CDSL stated that the financial and operational impact, apart from the penalty, was not material. Why it matters: Regulatory actions, even if the financial impact is stated as non-material, can create negative sentiment around a stock. Investors will be watching for any further developments or implications for other market infrastructure institutions.

Events & data to watch — and how markets usually react

  • Alphabet Q2 Earnings: Alphabet (Google's parent company) is scheduled to release its Q2 earnings. Investors will be watching for insights into AI investments, cloud growth, and overall guidance.

    • * Market Reaction: Major US tech earnings can significantly influence global technology stocks, including Indian IT companies. Strong results could provide a positive sentiment boost, while disappointing numbers might lead to caution in tech-heavy sectors.

  • US-Iran Diplomatic Efforts: Renewed diplomatic efforts between the US and Iran are being watched closely, as they have influenced commodity prices like gold and silver.

    • * Market Reaction: Geopolitical developments, especially those affecting major oil-producing regions, can cause volatility in crude oil prices, which directly impacts India's import bill and inflation outlook. It also influences safe-haven assets like gold.

  • Upcoming IPOs (Lohia Corp, Indo-MIM, Xtranet Technologies): Three new IPOs are set to launch on July 23.

    • * Market Reaction: Increased activity in the primary market, especially with positive grey market trends, indicates healthy investor appetite and liquidity. Successful IPOs can boost overall market sentiment, while weak listings might temper enthusiasm.

What to watch in today's session

  1. Nifty 50 and Bank Nifty Key Levels: Monitor Nifty's immediate resistance at 24250 and support at 24100. For Bank Nifty, watch 58000 as a crucial pivot, with 59000 as resistance and 57000 as support. The narrow CPRs suggest a trending day is likely.
  2. FII/DII Flow Trends: Keep an eye on the FII and DII activity. Continued FII selling, if not adequately absorbed by DIIs, could put pressure on the market.
  3. Banking Sector Performance: The news regarding HDFC Bank's underperformance relative to ICICI Bank will keep the banking sector in focus. Any significant moves in these heavyweights could sway the broader indices.
  4. Commodity Price Movements: Fluctuations in crude oil and gold prices, influenced by global factors and geopolitical news, will be important to track for their impact on inflation and sector-specific stocks.
  5. Global Cues: The performance of other Asian markets throughout the day, following the mixed overnight global close, will provide further direction.

Bottom line

The Indian market is poised for a potentially cautious but mildly positive open, as indicated by GIFT Nifty, despite mixed global cues and persistent FII selling. The key battleground for Nifty will be around the 24100-24250 range, with a narrow CPR suggesting a trending move if either side is decisively broken. Investors should remain vigilant about continued FII outflows and specific sector-related news, particularly from the banking space.
