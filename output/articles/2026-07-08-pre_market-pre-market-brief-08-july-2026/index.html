Events & data to watch — and how markets usually react

  • RBI Policy Meeting: The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) is scheduled to announce its monetary policy decision this week. Markets typically react with volatility around policy announcements, as investors gauge the impact of rate changes and policy statements on the economy and inflation.
  • US Fed Meeting: The US Federal Reserve is also holding its policy meeting this week. Global markets often react to US Fed decisions, especially regarding interest rates and forward guidance.
  • F&O Expiry: The monthly F&O expiry is approaching, which can lead to increased market volatility and churn as positions are squared off.
