Pre-Market Brief — 17 August 2026

TL;DR

  • Global markets closed mixed, with US indices slightly down and Asian markets showing a mixed trend.
  • GIFT Nifty indicates a slightly negative opening bias for the Indian market.
  • FIIs were net buyers yesterday after two sessions of selling, while DIIs continued their buying streak.
  • Nifty's profit growth hit a 10-quarter high, though driven by a few heavyweights.
  • Nifty 50's key levels to watch are 24300 as immediate support and 24500 as resistance.
  • The primary risk factor remains crude oil prices approaching $90 amidst geopolitical tensions.

How global markets traded — and what it means for India

Global markets presented a mixed picture overnight. Major US indices, the Dow Jones, Nasdaq, and S&P 500, closed slightly in the red, indicating a cautious sentiment. European markets, represented by the FTSE 100, also saw a minor dip. In Asia this morning, the Nikkei 225 was largely flat, while the Hang Seng and Shanghai Composite posted decent gains. This mixed global tone suggests that Indian markets might open without strong directional cues from international peers, potentially leading to a subdued start.

Currency, commodities and yields

The Indian Rupee (USD/INR) saw a marginal depreciation against the US Dollar, trading at ₹95.42. The Dollar Index, measuring the dollar's strength against a basket of currencies, dipped slightly. Brent Crude oil prices edged higher to $88.71, while WTI Crude saw a minor decline. Gold prices surged over 1.5%, and Silver also gained, indicating a flight to safety amidst global uncertainties. The US 10-Year Yield rose by 1.19% to 4.70%.

GIFT Nifty — what it's signalling

The GIFT Nifty is currently trading at 24394.50, down by 0.11%. This suggests a slightly negative to flat opening for the Nifty 50 today, likely indicating a gap-down or a subdued start for the Indian benchmark index.

Interpretation: The GIFT Nifty's slight dip points towards a cautious or marginally lower open for the Nifty 50 in India, aligning with the mixed global cues.

FII/DII flows — who's driving the tape

After two consecutive sessions of net selling, Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) turned net buyers on Friday, injecting ₹508 crore into the Indian equity market. Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) continued their strong buying momentum, adding ₹356 crore. Over the last five sessions, DIIs have been consistent net buyers, providing crucial support to the market, while FII flows have been volatile.

Events & data to watch — and how markets usually react

  • IPO Listings: Molbio Diagnostics and Dhoot Transmission are listing today. New listings often bring volatility to the specific stocks and can influence broader market sentiment depending on their debut performance. Strong listings typically create positive sentiment, while weak listings can dampen it.
  • IPO Openings: Lalithaa Jewellery Mart IPO opens today. Large IPOs can absorb significant market liquidity. Subscription numbers are usually watched closely as a gauge of investor confidence.
  • Crude Oil Prices: Brent crude approaching $90 a barrel due to geopolitical tensions. Historically, rising crude oil prices tend to be a negative for Indian markets, leading to concerns about inflation, corporate margins, and the current account deficit. Markets often react with caution to sustained upward movements in crude.
  • FII/DII Flows: The daily FII and DII flow data will continue to be crucial. Consistent FII selling can put pressure on the market, while DII buying often provides a floor. Markets react sensitively to shifts in these institutional flows.

What to watch in today's session

  1. GIFT Nifty's Open: Confirm the actual Nifty 50 open against the GIFT Nifty signal for immediate direction.
  2. Nifty 50 Levels: Watch if Nifty respects the 24300 support and 24500 resistance levels. A sustained break on either side could dictate intraday trends.
  3. Crude Oil Movement: Any further escalation in crude prices will be a key monitorable, impacting oil-sensitive sectors.
  4. IPO Performance: The listing performance of Molbio Diagnostics and Dhoot Transmission, and the subscription rates for Lalithaa Jewellery Mart IPO, will be in focus.
  5. FII/DII Flows: Monitor institutional activity; a continuation of FII buying or strong DII support will be crucial.
  6. Sectoral Action: Keep an eye on sectors like IT and Pharma due to USD/INR, and OMCs/Aviation/Paints due to crude oil.

Bottom line

The Indian market is poised for a cautious to slightly negative open, influenced by mixed global cues and a marginally lower GIFT Nifty. While Nifty's strong profit growth is a positive, the concentration of this growth and the rising crude oil prices pose underlying concerns. The key will be how Nifty 50 reacts to its immediate support around 24300 and resistance at 24500, with institutional flows and crude oil prices remaining critical factors to watch throughout the day.
