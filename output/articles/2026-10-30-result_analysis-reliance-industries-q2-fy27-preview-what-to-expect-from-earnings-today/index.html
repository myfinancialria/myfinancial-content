Reliance Industries Q2 FY27 Preview: What to Expect from Earnings Today

TL;DR

  • Reliance Industries is set to announce its Q2 FY27 results today, October 30, 2026, with analysts anticipating a robust performance.
  • The Oil-to-Chemicals (O2C) business is expected to be the primary growth driver, benefiting from stronger refining margins and a weaker rupee.
  • Digital Services (Jio Platforms) is projected to show steady growth, supported by subscriber additions and stable Average Revenue Per User (ARPU).
  • Reliance Retail's performance, however, might be relatively subdued due to festive season shifts and potential margin pressures.
  • Investors will closely monitor management commentary on future outlook, particularly regarding refining and petrochemical margins, and updates on the New Energy business.

What this means in plain terms

Today, India's largest conglomerate, Reliance Industries Limited (RIL), will release its financial results for the second quarter of the fiscal year 2026-27 (July-September 2026). This announcement is keenly watched by investors, analysts, and the market at large, as it provides crucial insights into the health of diverse sectors, from energy and petrochemicals to retail and digital services.

In simple terms, market experts are largely optimistic about RIL's overall performance this quarter. The company's traditional oil refining and petrochemical business, known as O2C, is expected to be the star performer, driven by favourable market conditions. Its digital arm, Jio, is also anticipated to continue its steady growth trajectory. However, the retail segment might face some headwinds, leading to a more moderate performance. The focus will not just be on the numbers, but also on what the management says about the company's future plans and outlook across its various businesses.

Reliance Industries Q2 FY27: Headline Expectations

Brokerage firms are forecasting a healthy increase in RIL's consolidated revenue and profitability for the September quarter. Consolidated revenue is estimated to rise by approximately 1.4% quarter-on-quarter (QoQ) to around ₹3,13,700 crore, from ₹3,09,468 crore in the preceding quarter.

Consolidated Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortisation (EBITDA) is projected to see a more significant jump, with estimates ranging from a 9% sequential increase to ₹51,942 crore (NDTV Profit) to as high as ₹53,700 crore (Nuvama Research), representing a 17% year-on-year (YoY) growth. The EBITDA margin is also expected to expand, potentially reaching 16.6% from 15.4% in the previous quarter.

Net profit is forecast to grow sequentially by about 2% to ₹21,368 crore (NDTV Profit), while some brokerages like Kotak Institutional Equities (KIE) anticipate a 30% YoY rise, with profit after tax (PAT) potentially reaching ₹23,621.7 crore. This double-digit growth in key financial metrics is primarily attributed to a strong recovery in the O2C business.

Segmental Performance: O2C, Digital, and Retail in Focus

Oil-to-Chemicals (O2C) Business: The Growth Engine

The O2C segment is widely expected to be the primary driver of RIL's improved performance. Analysts project an 18% sequential increase in O2C EBITDA to approximately ₹20,082 crore. Some estimates, like Kotak's, even suggest a 47% YoY jump. This robust growth is anticipated due to several factors:

  • Stronger Refining Margins: Favourable global refining earnings and higher throughput are contributing significantly.
  • Petrochemical Operations: Contributions from US ethane-based petrochemical operations are also expected to boost the segment.
  • Currency Movements: A weaker Indian rupee is likely to provide an additional tailwind.
  • Windfall Tax Exemption: RIL's special economic zone (SEZ) refinery is expected to largely avoid the impact of windfall taxes, further aiding profitability.

Digital Services (Jio Platforms): Steady Momentum

RIL's digital services business, Jio Platforms, is anticipated to deliver steady and healthy growth. The segment's EBITDA is projected to rise by about 3% sequentially to ₹21,889 crore. Nomura estimates Jio Platforms' EBITDA at ₹22,100 crore, a 4% sequential increase. This growth is underpinned by:

  • Subscriber Additions: Continued expansion of its subscriber base, with Nomura expecting an addition of 80 lakh subscribers, bringing the total to 54.1 crore.
  • ARPU Improvement: A gradual increase in Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) is also expected, with projections ranging from ₹218 to ₹219 per month.

    • Investors will be keen to hear updates on JioFiber expansion and any indications of potential tariff increases, especially following Jio Platforms' recent stock market debut.

Reliance Retail: Muted Growth and Margin Pressure

The retail segment is expected to show a relatively subdued performance compared to its peers. While Kotak estimates retail revenue to increase by 11.7% YoY, EBITDA could see a marginal decline of 0.8% YoY or a modest sequential improvement of 7% to ₹6,784 crore. The primary reasons for this muted outlook include:

  • Festive Season Shift: The timing of the festive season is expected to impact growth, shifting some demand to the subsequent quarter.
  • Margin Pressure: Increased scale-up of hyper-local deliveries and a higher comparison base from the previous year could lead to lower EBITDA margins.

Oil and Gas (Exploration & Production): Flat to Weak

The upstream oil and gas exploration business is generally expected to report flat or slightly weaker EBITDA, around ₹4,973 crore, largely unchanged from the previous quarter. Declining production volumes are likely to weigh on this segment's contribution.

Management Commentary and Future Outlook

Beyond the headline numbers, investors will be keenly listening to the management's commentary during the post-earnings call. Key areas of focus will include:

  • Crude Sourcing Strategy and Margin Outlook: Insights into how RIL plans to navigate global crude markets and maintain strong refining and petrochemical margins will be crucial.
  • Jio Platforms' Future: Updates on subscriber trends, JioFiber expansion plans, and any hints regarding potential tariff hikes will be closely watched, particularly in the context of the recent Jio IPO.
  • Reliance Retail's Growth Strategy: Management's perspective on how to accelerate retail growth and sustain profitability, especially in the face of seasonal demand patterns, will be important.
  • New Energy Business: Any updates on the progress and financial contribution of RIL's ambitious New Energy initiatives, including solar manufacturing, batteries, and green hydrogen, will be significant. Nuvama projects New Energy to become a substantial earnings contributor in the coming years.
  • Fundraising Plans: The board is also expected to consider raising funds via debentures, and any details on this will be important for future capital allocation.

Peer and Industry Context

Reliance Industries operates across a diverse set of industries, making a direct peer comparison complex. However, within its core segments, industry trends play a significant role. In the O2C segment, RIL's performance is heavily influenced by global crude oil prices, refining margins (cracks), and petrochemical product spreads. The current environment, with stronger refining earnings and a weaker rupee, is generally favourable for integrated players like RIL. Other refining and marketing companies in India include Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Ltd, Mangalore Refinery and Petrochemicals Ltd, and Chennai Petroleum Corporation Ltd. For Digital Services (Jio Platforms), the competitive landscape involves other major telecom players like Bharti Airtel and Vodafone Idea. Key metrics like subscriber additions and ARPU are industry-wide indicators. Jio's consistent subscriber growth and stable ARPU are generally seen as strong performance within the sector. In Retail, the market is highly competitive, with both organised and unorganised players. While RIL Retail has shown significant expansion, managing margins amidst festive season shifts and increased operational costs is a challenge faced by many large retail chains. RIL's diversified structure often provides a hedge against volatility in any single sector, allowing for a more stable consolidated performance compared to pure-play companies in its respective segments.

A real example

Let's consider Priya, 38, a software engineer with ₹35L CTC, based in Bengaluru, who holds 100 shares of Reliance Industries in her portfolio. She bought these shares at an average price of ₹2,500 per share.

Ahead of the Q2 FY27 results, Priya is assessing the impact of the expected performance on her investment. Based on analyst estimates, RIL's Q2 FY27 consolidated net profit is expected to be around ₹21,368 crore. Reliance Industries has approximately 6.77 billion (677 crore) outstanding shares. This would translate to an estimated Earnings Per Share (EPS) of: EPS = Net Profit / Number of Shares Outstanding EPS = ₹21,368 crore / 676.5 crore shares ≈ ₹31.59 per share for the quarter.

Priya knows that a strong EPS, especially if it beats analyst expectations, can lead to positive market sentiment and potentially a rise in share price. Conversely, a miss could cause a decline.

If RIL announces results broadly in line with or slightly better than these expectations, particularly driven by the strong O2C performance and steady Jio growth, Priya might decide to hold her shares, anticipating continued long-term growth. The expected EBITDA margin expansion to 16.6% would signal improved operational efficiency, which is a positive indicator for her long-term investment.

However, if the retail segment's underperformance is more severe than anticipated, or if management commentary on future growth in New Energy is cautious, Priya might consider booking partial profits or re-evaluating her investment thesis, especially if the stock has run up significantly prior to the results. For now, the analyst consensus suggests a stable to positive outlook, which supports a 'hold' strategy for her.

What to do this week

  1. Review RIL's Official Announcement: As soon as the results are out today, check the official filings on the BSE/NSE websites or RIL's investor relations page for the actual figures. Compare them against the analyst expectations discussed here.
  2. Listen to the Earnings Call: If possible, tune into the post-results analyst call (usually streamed online). Management commentary on future guidance, segment outlooks, and capital expenditure plans is often more insightful than just the numbers.
  3. Assess Segmental Performance: Pay close attention to the individual performance of O2C, Jio, and Retail. Understand which segments are driving growth and which are facing challenges, as this indicates the underlying health of RIL's diverse businesses.
  4. Evaluate Your Portfolio Allocation: Based on the results and management outlook, reconsider if your current allocation to RIL (or the sector it represents in your portfolio) aligns with your risk appetite and investment goals.
  5. Don't React Impulsively: Avoid making immediate buy/sell decisions solely based on short-term market reactions to the results. Instead, integrate the new information into your long-term investment thesis for RIL.
