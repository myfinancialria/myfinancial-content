Pre-Market Brief — 19 June 2026

TL;DR

  • Global markets presented a mixed picture, with US indices closing higher on Thursday, though US markets will be closed today for Juneteenth.
  • Indian IT sector faces significant headwinds following Accenture's weak outlook, leading to a sharp decline in the Nifty IT index.
  • Reliance Industries' Annual General Meeting (AGM) today is a key event, with investors keenly awaiting updates on the Jio IPO roadmap.
  • Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) were net sellers of Indian equities yesterday, while Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) provided strong buying support.
  • Several prominent companies, including HDFC Bank, Sanofi India, Polycab, HDFC Life, and Tata Communications, are trading ex-dividend today.

Where global markets closed

Global markets closed with a mixed performance on Thursday. US indices saw a positive close, led by a strong rally in technology stocks. The Nasdaq recorded significant gains, while the S&P 500 also rose over 1%. European markets remained largely flat, with the FTSE 100 closing marginally higher. In Asia, the Nikkei 225 posted modest gains, but Chinese markets, including the Hang Seng and Shanghai Composite, ended lower. It is important to note that US stock markets (NYSE and Nasdaq) will be closed today for the Juneteenth federal holiday.

Currency, commodities and yields

The Indian Rupee strengthened against the US Dollar, with USD/INR trading at ₹94.48, down 0.40%. A stronger Rupee is generally positive for India as it makes imports cheaper and can help in managing inflation. The Dollar Index remained flat at 100.86.

In the commodities space, Brent Crude saw a marginal uptick of 0.03% to $79.87 per barrel, while WTI Crude declined by 0.74% to $76.03 per barrel. Mixed crude prices may not have a significant immediate impact but sustained movements can influence India's import bill and inflation outlook. Gold prices fell by 1.04% to $4180.00, and Silver also saw a decline of 2.01% to $64.93. A fall in precious metal prices often indicates a shift towards riskier assets, though the US market holiday today might temper this interpretation.

Recent FII/DII flows

Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) were net sellers in the Indian cash equity segment yesterday, offloading equities worth ₹1,025 crore. Conversely, Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) continued to provide strong support, emerging as net buyers with purchases totaling ₹3,517 crore.

In contrast, DIIs have been consistent net buyers, injecting over ₹13,600 crore into the market during the same period, indicating robust domestic institutional support.

Overnight news that matters

All eyes on Reliance AGM as Street awaits Jio IPO roadmap Reliance Industries' 49th Annual General Meeting (AGM) today is a highly anticipated event, with investors expecting significant announcements, particularly regarding the potential IPO of Jio Platforms. This could be a major catalyst for Reliance's stock and the broader market.

Indian IT is a trade, not an investment, and the market just got the memo: Sandip Sabharwal Following Accenture's weak guidance, market expert Sandip Sabharwal suggests that Indian IT stocks are currently more suited for trading than long-term investment due to macroeconomic headwinds and threats from AI. This view underscores the ongoing pressure on the IT sector.

Nifty IT crashes 6% to 3-year low as Infosys, HCL Tech, other IT stocks crash up to 9%. Time to buy the dip? The Nifty IT index experienced a sharp decline of over 6% to a three-year low after Accenture's cautious outlook triggered a sell-off in major Indian IT stocks like Infosys, TCS, and HCLTech. This highlights significant concerns about demand and client spending in the IT services industry.

Multibagger HFCL shares hit 5% upper circuit for second session. What’s triggering the surge? HFCL shares surged after the company secured a substantial contract from Rail Vikas Nigam Limited, bolstering its position in the telecom sector and indicating growth opportunities in infrastructure and defence.

Stock market holiday: Are US stock markets closed for Juneteenth today? US stock markets, including the NYSE and Nasdaq, will be closed today for the Juneteenth federal holiday. This means there will be no fresh cues from the largest global market, potentially leading to lower trading volumes and increased volatility in other markets.

What to watch in today's session

  1. IT Sector Performance: The Indian IT sector will be under intense scrutiny today following the significant sell-off yesterday, triggered by Accenture's weak guidance. Investors should watch for any signs of stabilisation or further declines in major IT stocks.
  2. Reliance Industries AGM: The outcome of Reliance Industries' AGM will be a major market mover. Any announcements regarding the Jio IPO or other strategic plans could significantly impact the stock and broader market sentiment.
  3. Corporate Actions: With numerous companies trading ex-dividend today, including large-caps like HDFC Bank, Polycab, and HDFC Life, their stock performance might see some adjustments.
  4. FII/DII Flows: The continued trend of FII selling and DII buying will be crucial to monitor. Sustained domestic institutional support could help cushion any negative global cues, especially with US markets closed.
  5. Specific Stock Movements: Keep an eye on stocks like HFCL and Paras Defence, which have seen recent surges due to specific contract wins and optimism in the defence sector.

Bottom line

The Indian market is likely to open with mixed cues today. While positive momentum from US tech stocks could provide some support, the significant pressure on the IT sector and continued FII selling are key concerns. The absence of US market activity due to the Juneteenth holiday might lead to reduced liquidity and potentially higher volatility. Investors should remain cautious and closely monitor the Reliance AGM for fresh triggers.
