Market Wrap — 06 August 2026

TL;DR

  • Indian benchmark indices witnessed a mixed closing, with Nifty 50 barely holding onto gains.
  • Strong buying by Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) largely offset selling pressure from Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs).
  • PSU Bank and Banking sectors led the gains, while Realty and Media sectors saw significant declines.
  • Navin Fluorine International emerged as the top Nifty 500 gainer, surging over 13%.
  • Firstsource Solutions was the biggest Nifty 500 loser, dropping more than 13%.

How the market behaved today

The Indian market closed with a mixed performance today, with the Nifty 50 managing a marginal gain while the Bank Nifty showed stronger momentum. The day saw indices trading within a relatively narrow range, with the Nifty 50 hovering near its high for most of the session. The broader market, however, presented a mixed picture with midcap stocks declining and smallcap stocks posting gains.

What moved the market — the factors

Today's market movement was a blend of mixed global cues, significant institutional flows, and selective heavyweight stock action. While US stock futures held steady, reports of tech stocks tumbling in the US and investors awaiting a Mideast peace deal created a cautious sentiment. Conversely, European shares hit record highs on peace hopes and strong corporate earnings, providing some positive global backdrop.

Domestically, the Nifty 50's marginal gain was largely supported by a rally in heavyweights like Reliance Industries (RIL) and State Bank of India (SBI), which surged up to 3.5% on heavy trading volumes. The banking sector, particularly PSU Banks, also contributed positively. However, this was counteracted by declines in sectors like Realty, Media, and Auto, and a dip in the Nifty Midcap 100 index.

Institutional activity showed DIIs as strong net buyers, injecting ₹2,883 crore into the market, which helped absorb the FII selling of ₹943 crore. This DII support was crucial in preventing a sharper decline. The new closing auction session also continued to cause "wild moves" and "distortions" in Indian stocks, as noted by Zerodha co-founder Nithin Kamath, impacting the final minutes of trading.

News that moved stocks

  • Heavyweights Rally on Volumes: Reliance Industries and SBI shares rallied up to 3.5% on Thursday amid heavy trading activity, with over 41.65 million shares worth ₹4,294 crore changing hands. This rally in index heavyweights significantly supported the Nifty 50.
  • LIC Q1 Results Impress: Life Insurance Corporation (LIC) reported a robust 23% year-on-year (YoY) growth in its standalone net profit to ₹13,492 crore for the first quarter of FY27, with Value of New Business (VNB) surging 61.3%. This strong earnings report could positively impact investor sentiment towards the insurance giant.
  • Chavda Infra Declares Bonus Shares: Chavda Infra announced a 1:1 bonus share issue for existing shareholders, pending approvals, and also approved increasing its authorized capital to ₹70 crore. Such corporate actions typically generate positive investor interest in the stock.
  • Indo-MIM Shares Surge Post-IPO: Indo-MIM's shares skyrocketed by 82% above their IPO price, extending gains to 82% over the issue price, driven by robust investor interest and strong financial growth in FY26. This indicates strong post-listing performance and investor confidence.
  • New Closing Auction Session Causes Distortions: Zerodha co-founder Nithin Kamath highlighted that the sharp swings observed under the new Closing Auction Session (CAS) reflect structural weaknesses in Indian markets, causing "wild moves" and "distortions" in stock prices. This new mechanism is impacting end-of-day price discovery.
  • Profit-taking Stalls India Bond Rally: Indian government bonds experienced slight fluctuations as profit-taking coincided with substantial debt issuance, despite the Reserve Bank of India's dovish stance providing some stability. This suggests a cautious approach in the debt market.

Market breadth

The market breadth was negative today, with 210 advances against 282 declines out of a total of 492 stocks traded. This indicates that more stocks declined than advanced, suggesting a cautious underlying sentiment despite the benchmark indices' mixed performance.

FII / DII flows

Today, Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) were net sellers in the Indian cash market, offloading equities worth ₹943 crore. In contrast, Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) continued their buying spree, with a net inflow of ₹2,883 crore.

Event breakdown — what happened today and how it hit the market

Today's session was primarily driven by a combination of mixed global cues, significant institutional flows, and specific corporate developments. The rally in heavyweight stocks like Reliance Industries and SBI provided a floor for the Nifty 50, while the strong buying by DIIs helped absorb the FII selling.

A notable event was the release of LIC's Q1 FY27 results, which showed a 23% YoY increase in net profit and a 61% jump in Value of New Business. This positive earnings report could bolster investor confidence in the insurance sector.

Furthermore, the new Closing Auction Session (CAS) continued to be a talking point, with market experts like Nithin Kamath pointing out its role in causing "wild moves" and "distortions" in Indian stock prices during the closing minutes. This mechanism is influencing daily price discovery and adding an element of unpredictability to the market close.

The Indian bond market also saw some action, with profit-taking and substantial debt issuance stalling a rally, despite the RBI's dovish stance. This indicates a cautious approach by bond investors.

Setup for tomorrow

Looking ahead, global cues remain mixed. While US S&P 500 and Dow futures are holding steady, the tech sector in the US saw a tumble, and investors are awaiting a Mideast deal. European markets, however, closed at record highs. Crude oil prices saw an uptick today, with Brent crude closing at $80.80 and WTI crude at $76.39. The Dollar Index strengthened slightly to 99.76, and the USD/INR pair also edged up to 95.21. These factors will likely influence market sentiment tomorrow. Investors will be watching for any further developments on the global geopolitical front and the performance of key global indices overnight. Domestically, the momentum in banking and PSU stocks, along with the performance of index heavyweights, will be crucial.
