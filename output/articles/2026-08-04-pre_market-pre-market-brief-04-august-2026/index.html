Pre-Market Brief — 04 August 2026

TL;DR

  • Asian markets are trading with a negative bias this morning, following a mixed global tone.
  • GIFT Nifty indicates a slightly positive open for the Indian markets.
  • Both FIIs and DIIs were net buyers in the cash segment yesterday, continuing a positive trend.
  • Anticipation of tomorrow's RBI monetary policy decision is influencing banking stocks.
  • Nifty's immediate resistance sits around 24600-24650, while 24500 is a key support level.
  • The primary risk for today remains global market sentiment and the lead-up to the RBI policy.

Asian markets are largely trading in the red this morning, with Japan's Nikkei 225, Hong Kong's Hang Seng, and China's Shanghai Composite all registering declines. This follows a period where data for major US indices (Dow Jones, Nasdaq, S&P 500) was unavailable. The cautious sentiment in Asia could set a subdued tone for the Indian market open.

Currency, commodities and yields

The Indian Rupee (USD/INR) strengthened slightly against the US Dollar, closing at ₹95.30, down 0.11%. The Dollar Index, however, saw a minor uptick of 0.22% to 100.02. Crude oil prices witnessed a significant decline, with Brent Crude falling 5.58% to $85.09 and WTI Crude down 3.97% to $81.31. Gold and Silver both saw gains, with Gold up 1.70% to $4118.00 and Silver up 2.59% to $59.08. Data for the US 10-Year Yield is unavailable.

Interpretation: A stronger Rupee is generally positive for the Indian economy, easing import costs. The sharp drop in crude oil prices is a significant positive for India, which is a major oil importer, potentially reducing inflation pressures and benefiting sectors like oil marketing companies (OMCs), paints, and aviation. A rising Dollar Index, however, can sometimes lead to FII outflows. The rise in gold and silver suggests some flight to safety amidst global uncertainties.

Interpretation: This indicates a mildly positive opening for the Nifty 50, suggesting a potential gap-up of around 50-60 points from yesterday's close of 24586.8.

Traders should respect these levels today; a break above a major Call OI strike could signal a short covering rally, while a break below a major Put OI strike could indicate further downside.

Despite this, brokerages like Nomura maintained 'Buy' ratings.     • Banking stocks experienced declines ahead of the RBI's monetary policy announcement, driven by inflation worries and geopolitical tensions. Investors anticipate the RBI to maintain current interest rates. Why it matters: The banking sector is highly sensitive to interest rate decisions. The pre-policy caution suggests that the RBI's announcement tomorrow will be a major market mover, and banking stocks could see continued volatility today.

Events & data to watch — and how markets usually react

  • RBI Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) decision (tomorrow): While the decision is tomorrow, anticipation will drive market sentiment today, especially for banking and financial stocks. Markets typically react with increased volatility around RBI policy days, with rate hikes or hawkish commentary generally seen as negative, and status quo or dovish stances as supportive.
  • Manipal Health Enterprises IPO listing (tomorrow): The listing of this significant IPO will be watched closely. IPO listings can sometimes create sector-specific buzz or draw liquidity from other parts of the market.
  • Dhoot Transmission IPO opening (August 10): The announcement of the price band for upcoming IPOs provides a pipeline of primary market activity.
  • Global crude oil prices: The significant drop in Brent and WTI crude prices yesterday is a positive for India. Sustained lower crude prices can ease inflationary pressures and improve trade balances, generally seen as positive for equity markets.

What to watch in today's session

  1. GIFT Nifty's lead: Monitor the GIFT Nifty for sustained positive bias or any shift in sentiment as the Indian market opens.
  2. Key Nifty levels: Watch Nifty's ability to hold above 24600 and 24500. A break above 24670 (R1) or below 24535 (S1) could dictate the day's trend.
  3. Banking sector performance: With the RBI policy due tomorrow, banking stocks will be in focus. Any significant moves in large-cap banks like SBI, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, and Axis Bank could influence the broader market.
  4. Crude oil price action: While crude prices fell sharply yesterday, any reversal or continued decline will be important for sectors sensitive to oil.
  5. Stock-specific reactions: Keep an eye on KEI Industries, GE Shipping, Ather Energy (positive results), and Nazara Technologies (loss, CEO change) for their individual stock movements.

Bottom line

The Indian market is poised for a mildly positive open, as indicated by GIFT Nifty, despite a cautious tone from Asian peers. The significant drop in crude oil prices offers a tailwind, but the looming RBI monetary policy decision tomorrow will keep banking and financial stocks on edge. Nifty's ability to sustain above the 24600-24650 resistance zone will be crucial for any upward momentum, while 24500 acts as a critical support. Traders should remain vigilant for volatility ahead of the RBI policy and global cues.
