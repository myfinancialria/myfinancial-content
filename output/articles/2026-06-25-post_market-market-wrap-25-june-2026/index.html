Market Wrap · 2026-06-25

Market Wrap — 25 June 2026

TL;DR

  • Indian benchmark indices, Nifty 50 and Bank Nifty, closed marginally higher, extending their winning streak.
  • Broader markets, represented by Nifty Midcap 100 and Smallcap 100, ended the day in negative territory.
  • Auto, FMCG, and Realty sectors led the gains, while Metal, IT, and Energy sectors faced selling pressure.
  • Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) were net sellers, while Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) provided strong buying support.
  • Global cues like a surge in chip stocks and falling crude oil prices influenced market sentiment.

How the market closed

Indian equity benchmarks witnessed a mixed session today, with the Nifty 50 closing above the 24,050 mark and the Bank Nifty also registering a slight gain. However, broader markets saw some profit booking.

Sectors — winners and losers

The session saw a clear divergence in sectoral performance, with defensive and consumption-oriented sectors leading the charge, while commodity-linked and IT sectors faced headwinds.

Top 3 Gainer Sectors:

  • AUTO (+2.25%): The automobile sector was the top performer today, likely boosted by strong individual stock performances and positive sentiment.
  • FMCG (+0.68%): Fast-Moving Consumer Goods stocks showed resilience, closing with decent gains.
  • REALTY (+0.33%): The real estate sector saw positive movement, supported by recent data showing a significant rise in housing sales.

Top 3 Loser Sectors:

  • METAL (-1.37%): The metals sector was the biggest laggard, particularly impacted by a bearish outlook on aluminium stocks from a brokerage firm.
  • IT (-0.86%): Despite positive global cues for chip stocks, the Indian IT sector ended in the red.
  • ENERGY (-0.68%): The energy sector also witnessed selling pressure during the session.

Market breadth

The market breadth leaned towards declines today, with 324 stocks falling against 174 advances out of a total of 498 traded stocks. This indicates that while benchmark indices held firm, a larger number of individual stocks faced selling pressure.

FII / DII flows

Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) were net sellers in the Indian equity markets today, offloading shares worth ₹1,843 crore. However, Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) provided strong counter-buying, with a net inflow of ₹3,637 crore, significantly cushioning the market from FII outflows.

Over the past five trading sessions, FIIs have largely been net sellers, with net outflows on four out of five days, except for a positive inflow on June 19th. In contrast, DIIs have consistently remained net buyers, demonstrating strong domestic support for the markets.

What moved markets today

1. Global Chip Rally and AI Optimism Global chip stocks surged today, driven by strong financial results and an optimistic outlook from Micron Technology, which reignited confidence in the AI sector. This positive sentiment from the US market, particularly the Nasdaq futures jumping 2%, contributed to a positive opening for Indian markets.

2. Falling Crude Oil Prices Crude oil prices continued their downward trend, which has been a significant tailwind for the Indian economy. The slide in oil prices has powered Indian stock benchmarks to their longest weekly winning run in seven months and helped the Rupee end slightly higher.

3. Strong Housing Sales Data Despite global economic uncertainties, Indian housing sales recorded a robust 19% increase in April-June across nine major cities, exceeding 1.12 lakh units. This positive data likely provided a boost to the Realty sector, which was among the top gainers today.

4. Bearish Outlook on Aluminium Sector InCred Equities issued a bearish report on the aluminium sector, advising investors to exit these stocks due to a potential 30-40% downside. This outlook significantly impacted metal stocks, with the Metal sector being the top loser and National Aluminium Co. Ltd. (NALCO) featuring among the top Nifty 500 losers.

Setup for tomorrow

Indian markets are expected to watch global cues closely for tomorrow's session. The positive momentum from US futures, particularly the Nasdaq's strength, could provide some support. However, the broader market's negative breadth today suggests caution. Key data releases overnight and any further developments in crude oil prices will be important.

Global Cues: US futures are pointing higher, with Nasdaq futures jumping 2%. Brent Crude and WTI Crude are slightly down, while the USD/INR closed a tad higher at 94.39.
