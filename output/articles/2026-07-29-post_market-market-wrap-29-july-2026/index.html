Market Wrap — 29 July 2026

TL;DR

  • Indian benchmark indices rallied today, with Nifty 50 closing over 1% higher and Bank Nifty also posting solid gains.
  • The market was buoyed by strong domestic institutional and a turnaround in foreign institutional buying, coupled with positive global cues ahead of the US Fed decision.
  • PCBL Chemical Ltd. was the standout gainer among Nifty 500 stocks, surging nearly 15%.
  • Jammu & Kashmir Bank Ltd. was the biggest loser, declining almost 13%.
  • Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) turned net buyers after several days of outflows, while Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) continued their buying spree.
  • Futures & Options (F&O) build-up data is unavailable for this session.

How the market behaved today

Indian benchmark indices witnessed a strong upward trend today, with the Nifty 50 rallying over 1% to close above the 24,250 mark. The Bank Nifty also posted healthy gains, advancing nearly 0.8%. The market opened with a gap-up and maintained its positive momentum throughout the session, closing near its day's high. Broader markets also participated in the rally, with both Midcap and Smallcap indices registering significant gains.

The India VIX, a measure of market volatility, declined by 4.38% to 12.01, indicating an easing of market jitters.

What moved the market — the factors

The Indian market's robust performance today was primarily driven by a combination of factors. Domestically, strong Industrial Production (IIP) data and renewed optimism over Foreign Institutional Investor (FII) inflows played a crucial role. After several sessions of net selling, FIIs turned net buyers today, complementing the consistent buying by Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs). This strong institutional support provided a significant boost to market sentiment.

Globally, cautious optimism prevailed ahead of the US Federal Reserve's policy decision, with US stock futures inching higher. This positive global backdrop, coupled with a firming rupee, further supported the domestic rally. Sector-wise, the Information Technology (IT) and Metal sectors led the charge, contributing significantly to the Nifty's upward movement. The decline in India VIX also suggested reduced uncertainty among investors.

Sectors — winners and losers

Today saw broad-based buying interest, with several sectors ending in the green.

Top 3 gainer sectors:

  • IT: +2.32% - The sector witnessed strong buying interest, contributing significantly to the overall market rally.
  • METAL: +2.31% - Metal stocks performed robustly, indicating positive sentiment in commodity-related businesses.
  • FMCG: +1.66% - Fast-Moving Consumer Goods companies saw good gains, with some major players reporting strong quarterly results.

Top 3 loser sectors:

  • REALTY: -0.33% - The realty sector experienced a slight dip, making it the top sectoral loser for the day.
  • AUTO: -0.06% - Auto stocks traded mostly flat to negative, despite some corporate restructuring news in the sector.
  • ENERGY: +0.02% - While technically positive, the energy sector's minimal gain placed it among the bottom performers for the day.

News that moved stocks

  • Nithin Kamath warns on MTF boom: Zerodha co-founder Nithin Kamath cautioned that the rapid growth in margin trading funding could amplify market downturns, particularly in small- and mid-cap stocks, leading to forced selling and liquidity issues. This warning could influence investor sentiment towards riskier segments of the market.
  • Strong Q1 Earnings Reports:

    • - Dabur India reported a 15% rise in Q1 consolidated net profit to ₹591 crore, with revenue up 11%, driven by price hikes and broad-based FMCG growth. This positive result likely boosted sentiment for FMCG stocks. - Bajaj Housing Finance announced a 23% year-on-year jump in Q1 net profit to ₹715 crore, with Assets Under Management (AUM) surging 24%. This strong performance could have positively impacted financial services stocks. - Vedanta Oil & Gas swung to a profit of ₹945 crore in Q1 FY27, a significant turnaround from a previous net loss, with revenue up 9% YoY. This news provided a boost to the energy sector. - Dhanlaxmi Bank saw its Q1 net profit more than double to ₹24.9 crore, attributed to higher income and lower provisions, alongside improved asset quality. This positive earnings report supported banking sector sentiment.

  • SBI raises funds via Tier 1 bonds: State Bank of India successfully raised ₹4,691 crore through Basel III-compliant Additional Tier 1 bonds at a 7.75% annual coupon, indicating strong demand from investors. This reflects confidence in the banking giant and the broader financial system.
  • SML Mahindra to acquire Mahindra Truck and Bus division: SML Mahindra is set to acquire the Mahindra Truck and Bus Division from Mahindra & Mahindra, consolidating commercial vehicle operations and expanding its portfolio. This news could lead to re-rating of the involved auto sector stocks.
  • Commodity stocks hit 52-week highs: Pidilite Industries was among seven commodity stocks that touched fresh 52-week highs, surging up to 25% in a month. This highlights strong momentum in certain commodity and chemical-related businesses.

Market breadth

The market breadth was overwhelmingly positive today, with 376 advances against 124 declines out of the 500 stocks traded. This indicates a broad-based participation in the rally, suggesting underlying strength across various segments.

FII / DII flows

Today marked a significant shift in Foreign Institutional Investor (FII) activity, as they turned net buyers after four consecutive days of net selling. Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) continued their strong buying trend.

In contrast, DIIs have been consistent net buyers, with inflows ranging from ₹1,664 crore to ₹5,454 crore over the past five sessions. This reversal in FII flows, combined with sustained DII support, is a positive signal for the market.

Event breakdown — what happened today and how it hit the market

Today's session was significantly influenced by the anticipation surrounding the US Federal Reserve's policy decision, expected later tonight. Global cues reflected this, with US stock futures showing modest gains as investors awaited clarity on interest rates. The Indian rupee firmed up against the dollar, hitting a three-week peak, as traders adjusted positions ahead of the Fed announcement. US Treasury yields also rose, ending a three-day decline, as markets braced for the Fed's stance.

Domestically, strong Industrial Production (IIP) data provided a positive impetus to market sentiment. Additionally, a slew of Q1 corporate earnings reports shaped stock-specific and sectoral movements. Positive results from Dabur India boosted the FMCG sector, while strong performances from Bajaj Housing Finance and Dhanlaxmi Bank supported the financial services space. Vedanta Oil & Gas swinging to profit also contributed to positive sentiment in the energy sector. The news of SBI raising funds through Tier 1 bonds further underscored confidence in the banking system.

Setup for tomorrow

Tomorrow's market opening will largely hinge on the outcome of the US Federal Reserve's policy decision, expected overnight. While US stock futures were cautiously higher today, any surprises from the Fed could trigger significant reactions across global markets. Brent crude oil and WTI crude oil prices surged over 6% today, which could have implications for energy-related stocks and inflation concerns. Gold and silver also saw gains, while the Dollar Index softened.

Investors will be watching global market reactions to the Fed's announcement closely. Domestically, the momentum in IT, Metal, and FMCG sectors, driven by today's strong performance and earnings, will be key. Key levels for Nifty 50 to watch would be around its day's high of 24283.55, with support around 24136.75.
