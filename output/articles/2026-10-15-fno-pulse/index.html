Decoding today's open interest

Open Interest (OI) represents the total number of outstanding derivative contracts that have not yet been settled. In options, the strike with the highest Call OI typically acts as a significant resistance level, as many call writers (sellers) expect the price not to rise above this point. Conversely, the strike with the highest Put OI often serves as a strong support level, as put writers (sellers) anticipate the price will not fall below it.

For Nifty 50, today's heaviest fresh Call writing was observed near the 23,266 level, indicating that new call sellers expect the index to face a cap around this point, which is slightly above the current spot. On the Put side, fresh writing was concentrated near 22,800, suggesting new put sellers believe this level could act as a floor.

In the Sensex, the heaviest Call OI wall is positioned at 75,000, which is just about 0.9% above the current spot, indicating a close ceiling. Today's fresh Call writing was most significant near 74,433, reinforcing the expectation of a cap. Meanwhile, fresh Put writing was heaviest around 73,000, suggesting a potential floor.

For Bank Nifty, the heaviest Call OI is at 56,000, a mere 0.3% above the spot, highlighting a very tight resistance level. Fresh Call writing today was concentrated near 55,900, with new sellers anticipating a cap close to the current levels. On the Put side, fresh writing was heaviest near 55,733, indicating a potential nearby floor.

PCR — what the put-call ratio is whispering

The Put-Call Ratio (PCR) is a widely watched sentiment indicator that compares the total Open Interest (OI) of Puts to the total OI of Calls. A PCR(OI) greater than 1 typically suggests more puts are open than calls, often interpreted as a bullish signal (as put writers expect prices to rise or stay stable). A PCR(OI) less than 1 suggests more calls are open, often seen as bearish. PCR(today flow ΔOI) looks at only the change in OI for the day, giving insight into fresh positioning.

Nifty 50 has a PCR(OI) of 1.36, which is above 1, indicating that put open interest significantly dominates call open interest. This suggests that option writers, on aggregate, have positioned for downside protection, often seen as a bullish undertone. However, today's flow ΔPCR was -5.52, signifying that today's fresh writing was more balanced or even slightly call-heavy, providing a mixed signal.

For Sensex, the PCR(OI) stands at 1.1, suggesting a relatively balanced book with a slight tilt towards puts. Today's flow ΔPCR was 1.44, indicating that fresh writing was predominantly in puts, which could be interpreted as a bullish signal for today's activity.

Bank Nifty shows a PCR(OI) of 0.95, which is just below 1, suggesting a slightly higher concentration of call open interest. Today's flow ΔPCR was -1.18, indicating that fresh writing was mixed, leaning slightly towards calls, which points to a neutral to mildly cautious sentiment from today's activity.

Max pain and the magnet effect

"Max Pain" refers to the strike price at which option writers, who are typically large institutional players, would experience the minimum possible loss if the index were to expire at that level. It's often considered a 'magnet' for the underlying index, especially as the weekly or monthly expiry date approaches.

For Nifty 50, the Max Pain point is at 22,650, which is approximately 1.5% below the current spot of 22,997.35. This suggests a potential gravitational pull downwards towards this level as expiry nears.

The Sensex Max Pain is at 73,600, about 1.0% below its spot of 74,315.61. This also indicates a potential downward pull towards this strike.

Bank Nifty has its Max Pain exactly at the spot level of 55,800. When Max Pain aligns with the current spot, it suggests option writers are comfortable with the index expiring around the current levels, indicating a neutral short-term outlook from this specific data point.

FII positioning — what the big money did today

Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) are significant participants in the Indian derivatives market, and their positioning can offer insights into broader market sentiment.

What this combination historically tends to mean for tomorrow

When we observe a combination of strong spot gains alongside significant fresh Call writing near current levels and Max Pain points below the spot, as seen in Sensex and Bank Nifty, it has historically tended to suggest potential resistance overhead. This often implies that despite today's positive momentum, the market participants who sell options are positioning for a cap on further upward movement, and there might be a gravitational pull towards lower levels as expiry approaches.

For Nifty, the mixed signals – a high PCR(OI) suggesting underlying support, but fresh call writing and Max Pain below spot – historically tend to coincide with a period of consolidation or range-bound movement, where the index might test both support and resistance levels. The low FII Index futures long ratio also historically tends to align with a cautious sentiment from these large players, which can contribute to overall market hesitation.

How to read this yourself daily

Understanding these market dynamics doesn't require complex software; you can access most of the raw data yourself. To start, visit the National Stock Exchange (NSE) website (nseindia.com) and navigate to the "Derivatives" section. Here, you can find the "Option Chain" for Nifty, Bank Nifty, and Sensex. Look for the "Open Interest" column for both Calls and Puts at various strike prices. The strikes with the highest OI are your key support and resistance levels.

To calculate the Put-Call Ratio (PCR), sum up the total Open Interest for all Puts and divide it by the total Open Interest for all Calls. For "Max Pain," you'll need to look at the change in OI across strikes and identify the strike where the total value of outstanding options (both calls and puts) would result in the least loss for option writers. Many brokerage platforms also provide this data in an easily digestible format. By regularly tracking these data points and observing how they shift day-to-day, especially fresh writing activity, you can begin to form your own informed perspective on potential market movements.
