How the market behaved today

Indian equities concluded Tuesday's session in the red, breaking a four-day winning streak, as investors engaged in profit-booking and adopted a cautious stance ahead of the Reserve Bank of India's upcoming policy decision. The Nifty 50 closed at 24614.90, down -0.64%, after trading in a range between 24703.90 and 24427.95. Similarly, the Bank Nifty ended at 57907.20, shedding -0.58%, with its day's high at 58068.95 and low at 57352.65.

What moved the market — the factors

The Indian market witnessed a day of consolidation and profit-booking, snapping a four-day gaining streak, primarily driven by investor caution ahead of the Reserve Bank of India's monetary policy announcement. While global cues were mixed, with US stock futures rising on strong AI-linked earnings, the domestic market remained subdued. Heavyweight stock moves also played a significant role, particularly the sharp decline in Life Insurance Corporation of India (LICI) following its oversubscribed Offer For Sale (OFS).

Sectoral rotation was evident, with Realty, FMCG, and IT sectors experiencing selling pressure, pulling down the broader indices. Conversely, Media and Metal sectors showed resilience. Despite a generally negative market, both Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) and Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) were net buyers, providing some underlying support. The rise in India VIX also suggested increased uncertainty among traders.

News that moved stocks

  • Institutional investors over-subscribe LIC OFS, govt opts for green-shoe option

    • The government's share sale in Life Insurance Corporation of India garnered overwhelming interest from institutional investors, with bids reaching ₹36,400 crore. This led to the government exercising the green-shoe option, which likely contributed to the stock's significant decline today.

  • Bharti Airtel Q1 Results: Net profit surges 37% YoY to Rs 8,167 crore; ARPU rises to Rs 264

    • Telecom major Bharti Airtel reported a robust consolidated net profit of ₹8,167 crore for the April-June quarter of FY27, marking a more than 37% year-on-year increase, driven by higher subscriber upgrades and robust data usage.

  • Nykaa Q1 Results: Net profit spikes 243% YoY to Rs 80 crore; revenue jumps 29%

    • Nykaa's quarterly profit more than tripled year-on-year to ₹80 crore, with revenue jumping 29%, fueled by strong growth in its beauty business and accelerated fashion sales.

  • Kalyan Jewellers Q1 Results: Net profit jumps 32% YoY to ₹349 crore; margins contract

    • Kalyan Jewellers reported a 32% year-on-year increase in net profit to ₹349 crore for the June quarter, although margins contracted.

  • India bonds rise on position building ahead of RBI policy

    • Indian government bonds saw a slight increase as traders positioned themselves ahead of the Reserve Bank of India's policy announcement, with expectations for the central bank to maintain its key policy rate. This sentiment also kept the rupee nearly flat.

  • US stock futures rise as strong AI-linked earnings lift sentiment

    • US stock futures showed gains, indicating positive sentiment in global markets, driven by strong earnings reports from AI-linked companies.

Event breakdown — what happened today and how it hit the market

Today's session was largely influenced by two key factors: anticipation surrounding the Reserve Bank of India's upcoming policy decision and the aftermath of the Life Insurance Corporation of India (LICI) Offer For Sale (OFS).

The market saw traders building positions in Indian government bonds ahead of the RBI policy, widely expected to maintain its key policy rate. This cautious stance also kept the rupee relatively stable. The broader market, particularly the Nifty 50 and Bank Nifty, reflected this caution with profit-booking, leading to a break in the four-day winning streak.

Separately, the news of institutional investors oversubscribing the LICI OFS, leading the government to opt for a green-shoe option, directly impacted LICI's stock price, which emerged as the top Nifty 500 loser, shedding over 8%. This significant move in a financial services heavyweight contributed to the overall negative sentiment in the market.

Setup for tomorrow

Tomorrow's market will be keenly watching the Reserve Bank of India's monetary policy announcement, which is expected to be the primary driver of sentiment. Global cues appear mixed, with US stock futures showing gains on strong AI-linked earnings, but crude oil prices declining and gold prices rising. Investors will also monitor the USD/INR movement and the Dollar Index. Key sectors and stocks to watch will include those sensitive to interest rate changes, as well as companies that announced Q1 results today, such as Bharti Airtel and Nykaa.
