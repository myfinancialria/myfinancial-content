Market Wrap — 22 June 2026

TL;DR

  • Indian benchmark indices, Nifty 50 and Bank Nifty, closed higher for the day, extending their positive run.
  • Broader markets, including Midcap 100 and Smallcap 100, also witnessed healthy gains.
  • FIIs turned net buyers today, infusing ₹4,859 crore, while DIIs were net sellers.
  • Media, Pharma, and IT sectors led the gains, while FMCG saw some selling pressure.
  • Capital Goods stocks like Kirloskar Oil Eng Ltd. and Triveni Turbine Ltd. were among the top performers.

How the market closed

Indian equities continued their upward momentum today, with the Nifty 50 closing above the 24,100 mark and the Bank Nifty also registering positive gains. The broader market indices, Nifty Midcap 100 and Nifty Smallcap 100, outperformed the benchmarks, indicating a positive sentiment across market segments.

Sectors — winners and losers

Today's session saw a mixed performance across sectors, with Media, Pharma, and IT leading the charge, while FMCG experienced some downward pressure.

Top 3 Gainer Sectors:

  • MEDIA: +1.42%. The Media sector emerged as the top gainer, reflecting positive investor interest.
  • PHARMA: +1.24%. The Pharma sector continued its healthy run, attracting buying interest.
  • IT: +0.74%. The Information Technology sector also posted decent gains, ahead of the upcoming Q1 earnings season.

Top 3 Loser Sectors:

  • FMCG: -0.41%. The Fast Moving Consumer Goods sector was the only one to close in the red today, experiencing some profit booking.
  • REALTY: +0.40%. Despite being among the lower performers, the Realty sector still managed to close with a positive gain.
  • BANK: +0.43%. Similar to Realty, the Banking sector, while not among the top gainers, ended the day in positive territory.

Market breadth

The market breadth was largely positive today, with 341 advances against 160 declines out of a total of 501 traded stocks. This suggests that the upward movement was broad-based, with more stocks participating in the rally than those falling.

FII / DII flows

Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) turned net buyers in the Indian equity markets today, infusing a significant ₹4,859 crore. Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs), on the other hand, were net sellers, offloading equities worth ₹1,160 crore.

| Investor Type | Net Cash Position (₹ crore) | | :------------------ | :-------------------------- | | FII | +4,859 | | DII | -1,160 |

Looking at the 5-day history, FII flows have been somewhat volatile, with today's strong buying contrasting with net selling on June 18th and 16th. DIIs have largely been net buyers over the past five days, except for today's selling and a neutral position on June 16th. This indicates a shift in FII sentiment today, providing a strong boost to the market.

What moved markets today

Several key developments influenced market sentiment and stock movements today:

RBI's Forex Intervention in April The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) actively intervened in the forex market in April, selling a net $8.9 billion to prevent the rupee from depreciating significantly amidst the U.S.-Iran crisis. This proactive measure by the central bank helped stabilize the Indian currency and likely contributed to overall market confidence by mitigating external risks.

TCS to Kickstart Q1 Earnings Season IT major Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) is set to announce its Q1FY27 earnings on July 9, potentially declaring an interim dividend. As the first major company to report, TCS's results and management commentary, especially on AI-driven growth, will be closely watched by investors for cues on the broader IT sector's performance and future outlook.

India's Manufacturing Export Boom Predicted A top strategist has highlighted India's brightening economic outlook, predicting a multi-year manufacturing export boom. This positive long-term view on India's economic fundamentals, despite past currency challenges, could attract further foreign investment and support domestic manufacturing stocks.

Bharat Electronics Secures Large Order Bharat Electronics (BEL) announced securing additional orders worth ₹1,081 crore, enhancing its revenue visibility. Such significant order wins for defence-related companies typically generate positive investor interest and can lead to increased focus on the defence sector.

SEBI Proposes Revamp of Technology Rules for Exchanges The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has proposed a major overhaul of technology and cybersecurity regulations for stock exchanges, clearing corporations, and depositories. This move aims to simplify existing rules, eliminate overlaps, and strengthen the market infrastructure, which could enhance investor confidence in the long run.

Setup for tomorrow

Indian markets are likely to open with cautious optimism tomorrow, building on today's positive momentum and strong FII inflows. Investors will continue to monitor global cues, particularly developments in the Middle East and their impact on crude oil prices.

Globally, Brent Crude closed lower at $77.46, down by 2.99%, and WTI Crude also fell by 4.09% to $73.47, which could provide some relief on the inflation front for India. The US 10-year yield saw a slight uptick, closing at 4.49%. The USD/INR pair closed at 94.67, with the rupee weakening by 0.35% against the dollar. Gold and Silver also saw minor declines. Specific pointers for US futures or Asian market openings are unavailable at this time. Sectors like IT, ahead of TCS earnings, and Capital Goods, given today's strong performance, might remain in focus.
