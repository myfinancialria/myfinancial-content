Market Wrap — 24 June 2026

TL;DR

  • Indian benchmark indices, Nifty 50 and Bank Nifty, closed significantly higher, with Nifty reclaiming the 24,000 mark.
  • Realty, IT, and Banking sectors led the gains, while Energy, Auto, and Metal sectors faced selling pressure.
  • Financial Services stocks were prominent among the top gainers, reflecting positive sentiment in the banking space.
  • Capital Goods stocks saw a broad-based sell-off, dominating the list of top losers.
  • Market breadth was positive, with advances slightly outnumbering declines across the Nifty 500.
  • Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) continued their strong buying streak, while Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) were marginal net buyers.

How the market closed

Indian equities witnessed a strong rebound today, with the Nifty 50 surging past the crucial 24,000 level and the Bank Nifty posting robust gains. The broader market also closed in the green, albeit with more modest increases.

Market breadth

The market breadth leaned positive today, with 259 advances against 241 declines out of the Nifty 500 stocks. This indicates that while the benchmarks saw strong gains, the broader market participation was evenly spread, suggesting a healthy underlying sentiment.

FII / DII flows

Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) were marginal net buyers today, injecting ₹18 crore into the Indian equity market. Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) continued their robust buying spree, with a net inflow of ₹680 crore.

What moved markets today

1. Nifty 50 Reclaims 24,000 Mark; Experts Bullish on Banking and Defence The Nifty 50 index surged past the crucial 24,000 level, driven by short covering and lower crude oil prices. Market experts are increasingly optimistic, with some predicting Nifty could aim for 25,000 if it holds the 23,800 support. Banking and defence sectors are highlighted as potential outperformers in the next market rally. This positive outlook on key sectors contributed significantly to the day's gains, especially in Bank Nifty and related financial stocks.

2. Robust Pre-Sales Boost Realty Sector India's top 28 listed real estate firms achieved impressive sales bookings of ₹1.95 lakh crore last fiscal year, fueled by robust housing demand. Godrej Properties led the pack, followed by Prestige Estates. This strong performance in the real estate market directly translated into the Realty sector being the top gainer today.

3. Indian Rupee Holds Steady Amidst Dollar Strength The Indian rupee defied regional weakness today, supported by likely intervention from the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) against a strengthening US dollar. While other Asian currencies faltered, the rupee held firm. Globally, the US dollar hit a 13-month peak, fueled by a selloff in tech stocks and growing expectations for interest rate hikes from the Federal Reserve. The RBI's actions helped cushion the rupee, preventing a sharper depreciation which could have impacted market sentiment.

4. Motilal Oswal Initiates Coverage on Textile Stocks Motilal Oswal has started coverage on eight textile firms, including Arvind and Gokaldas Exports, predicting growth due to global demand recovery and capacity expansions, with potential upsides of up to 39%. This positive brokerage report likely contributed to the strong performance of textile stocks like K.P.R. Mill Ltd., which was among the top Nifty 500 gainers.

Setup for tomorrow

Indian markets will likely take cues from global developments and continue to monitor FII flows. The Nifty 50's ability to sustain above the 24,000 mark will be crucial. Investors will also keep an eye on sector-specific news, particularly in banking and real estate, which showed strength today.

Globally, US stock futures edged higher overnight after a recent sell-off in technology stocks, with S&P 500 futures up 0.2%. Brent crude slipped below $75, closing at $74.63, while the Dollar Index strengthened to 101.69. The US 10-year yield closed at 4.45%.
