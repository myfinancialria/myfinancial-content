Market Wrap — 30 June 2026

TL;DR

  • Indian benchmark indices, Nifty 50 and Bank Nifty, closed in the red for the second consecutive session.
  • Broader markets, represented by Nifty Midcap 100 and Smallcap 100, showed resilience and ended with healthy gains.
  • The IT sector was the biggest drag, hitting a multi-year low, while Realty stocks led the pack of gainers.
  • Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) remained net sellers, while Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) continued their buying streak.
  • Market breadth was positive, with more advancing stocks than declining ones, indicating underlying strength in the broader market.

How the market closed

Indian equities concluded the trading day on a subdued note, with frontline indices Nifty 50 and Bank Nifty registering declines. The Nifty 50 closed below the 23,900 mark at 23865.75, down 0.34%, while the Bank Nifty also shed 0.32% to settle at 57542.90. Despite the weakness in benchmarks, the broader market displayed strength, with midcap and smallcap indices posting gains.

| Index | Close | Change | High | Low | |---|---|---|---|---| | Nifty 50 | 23865.75 | -0.34% | 24035.55 | 23829.20 | | Bank Nifty | 57542.90 | -0.32% | 58011.95 | 57456.65 | | Midcap 100 | 61797.70 | +0.37% | 62052.60 | 61398.35 | | Smallcap 100 | 18863.10 | +1.02% | 18909.60 | 18697.05 |

The India VIX, a measure of market volatility, stood at 13.60, registering a marginal decrease of 0.07%. This indicates a slight easing in market apprehension, despite the benchmark indices closing lower.

Sectors — winners and losers

The session saw a clear divergence in sectoral performance, with IT stocks facing significant selling pressure while Realty and Auto sectors found favour.

Top 3 Gainer Sectors:

  • REALTY: +1.31% - The sector witnessed strong buying interest, pushing it to be the top performer of the day.
  • PHARMA: +0.39% - Pharma stocks posted moderate gains, contributing positively to the market breadth.
  • AUTO: +0.24% - The Automobile sector closed in the green, showing some resilience despite mixed individual stock performance.

Top 3 Loser Sectors:

  • IT: -2.73% - The Information Technology sector was the biggest laggard, with the Nifty IT index hitting an over 3-year low and crashing 30% year-to-date amid cautious client spending and economic uncertainties.
  • MEDIA: -0.77% - Media stocks experienced a decline, contributing to the overall market weakness.
  • PSU BANK: -0.72% - Public Sector Undertaking (PSU) Banks also ended the day in negative territory.

Market breadth

The market breadth leaned towards the positive side today, with 285 advances against 215 declines out of 500 stocks traded. This indicates that despite the headline indices being down, a larger number of individual stocks managed to close in the green, suggesting underlying strength in the broader market.

FII / DII flows

Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) were net sellers in the Indian equity markets today, offloading shares worth ₹-1,350 crore. In contrast, Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) continued to provide crucial support, with net purchases amounting to ₹2,801 crore.

| Investor Type | Net Position (₹ crore) | |---|---| | FII | -1,350 | | DII | 2,801 |

Looking at the 5-day history, FIIs have largely been net sellers, with today marking their fourth day of outflows in the last five trading sessions. Meanwhile, DIIs have consistently been net buyers, absorbing the selling pressure from foreign investors and providing a strong domestic cushion to the market.

What moved markets today

1. IT Sector Plunges to Multi-Year Lows The Nifty IT index experienced a significant downturn, hitting an over 3-year low and showing a year-to-date crash of 30%. This decline was attributed to cautious client spending and ongoing economic uncertainties. The market impact was evident as IT stocks like KPIT Technologies and Tata Technologies were among the top Nifty 500 losers, dragging down the benchmark indices.

2. Monthly Expiry Day Volatility Weighs on Benchmarks Indian equities ended lower for the second straight session, with benchmarks slipping on expiry-day volatility. Despite the fall in Sensex and Nifty, broader markets advanced, indicating a divergence in performance. This suggests that while derivatives expiry might have influenced the frontline indices, underlying sentiment in the broader market remained relatively positive.

3. Rupee Achieves First Quarterly Gain in Five The Indian rupee recorded its first quarterly gain since March 2025, supported by declining oil prices and policy measures aimed at attracting dollar inflows. Although the currency saw a slight dip today, its overall resilience and quarterly performance could be a positive signal for the economy and potentially influence FII sentiment in the medium term.

4. Nomura Anticipates Muted Impact of Below-Normal Monsoon on Rural Demand Despite concerns about a weaker monsoon, Nomura's Mihir Shah expects a limited impact on rural demand, citing healthy reservoir levels and improved irrigation. This positive outlook on rural demand could provide support to sectors reliant on rural consumption, potentially cushioning any broader economic slowdown fears.

Setup for tomorrow

Tomorrow's session will likely see investors reacting to today's mixed market signals and global cues. The resilience of midcap and smallcap segments, coupled with positive market breadth, suggests selective buying interest. The IT sector will be closely watched for any signs of a rebound or continued pressure, given its significant decline today. Sectors like Realty and Auto, which showed strength, might continue their momentum.

Globally, the USD/INR closed higher at 94.66, while the Dollar Index also strengthened. Brent Crude saw a notable increase of 1.80% to close at $74.47, and WTI Crude also moved up. Gold and Silver prices edged higher, while the US 10-Year Yield saw a slight dip. These global factors, particularly crude oil prices and currency movements, will be key pointers for the Indian market tomorrow.
