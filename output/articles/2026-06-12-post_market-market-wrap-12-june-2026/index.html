Market Wrap — 12 June 2026

TL;DR

  • Indian benchmark indices witnessed a strong rally today, with Nifty 50 gaining nearly 2% and Bank Nifty surging almost 3%.
  • Broader markets, including Midcap and Smallcap indices, significantly outperformed, rising over 2.4%.
  • Realty, Banking, and PSU Banking sectors led the charge, while IT and Oil & Gas stocks saw some selling pressure.
  • IFCI Ltd. soared nearly 20% on buzz surrounding the NSE IPO, while oil marketing companies faced headwinds from falling crude prices.
  • Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) continued their selling streak, but Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) provided robust buying support.

How the market closed

Indian equities closed sharply higher today, with the Nifty 50 reclaiming the 23,600 mark and the Bank Nifty surging past 56,800. The Nifty 50 ended the session up 1.99%, while the Bank Nifty recorded an impressive gain of 2.97%. Broader markets also participated enthusiastically in the rally, with both the Nifty Midcap 100 and Nifty Smallcap 100 indices posting gains of over 2.4%.

The India VIX, a measure of market volatility, declined by 5.70% to settle at 14.72, indicating a reduction in market uncertainty and increased investor confidence.

Sectors — winners and losers

Today's session saw broad-based buying, with most sectors ending in the green.

Top 3 Gainer Sectors:

  • REALTY: +3.53% - The sector witnessed strong buying interest, though a US realty firm Opendoor announced winding down its India operations, impacting nearly 250 employees.
  • BANK: +2.97% - Banking stocks surged, likely buoyed by positive sentiment from the bond market rally and news of top Indian state lenders eyeing dollar bonds with RBI's new subsidy.
  • PSU BANK: +2.71% - Public sector banks also saw significant gains, benefiting from the broader banking rally and the prospect of accessing dollar bonds with RBI's subsidised hedging mechanism.

Top 3 Loser Sectors:

  • IT: -0.09% - The IT sector remained largely flat to negative, with some major players experiencing declines.
  • PHARMA: +0.30% - The Pharma sector saw marginal gains, barely staying in positive territory.
  • FMCG: +0.63% - Fast Moving Consumer Goods stocks posted modest increases, underperforming the broader market.

Market breadth

The market breadth was overwhelmingly positive today, with 447 advances against just 53 declines out of 500 stocks. This strong advance-decline ratio indicates that the buying interest was widespread across a vast majority of stocks, suggesting a healthy underlying sentiment for the day's rally.

FII / DII flows

Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) remained net sellers in the Indian equity market today, offloading shares worth ₹-1,987 crore. However, Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) continued to provide strong counter-support, injecting ₹4,225 crore into the market.

| Investor Type | Net Position (₹ crore) | | :------------ | :--------------------- | | FII | -1,987 | | DII | 4,225 |

Looking at the 5-day history, FIIs have been consistent net sellers, with outflows ranging from ₹1,987 crore to ₹8,776 crore. Conversely, DIIs have consistently been net buyers, providing substantial liquidity and absorbing the FII selling pressure, with inflows ranging from ₹3,124 crore to ₹9,134 crore over the past five trading sessions. This trend highlights the continued domestic institutional confidence in the Indian market despite foreign outflows.

What moved markets today

Several key developments influenced market sentiment and specific sectors today:

  • Hopes for US-Iran Peace Deal Drags Down Crude Oil Prices: Optimism surrounding potential peace talks between the US and Iran led to a sharp decline in crude oil prices, with Brent and WTI crude both falling over 2%. This development positively impacted Indian government bonds and the Rupee, which rallied against the dollar. However, it weighed heavily on oil exploration and production companies like ONGC and Oil India.
  • SpaceX IPO Creates Global Buzz, Elon Musk Becomes Trillionaire: Elon Musk's SpaceX made a historic debut on Nasdaq with a massive $75 billion IPO, valuing the company at $1.77 trillion and making Musk the world's first trillionaire. This landmark listing generated significant global excitement and contributed to a generally positive risk-on sentiment in markets.
  • NSE IPO Buzz Fuels IFCI Rally: Shares of IFCI Ltd. surged nearly 20% to a 52-week high amidst increasing expectations of the National Stock Exchange (NSE) filing its IPO papers soon. IFCI's indirect exposure to NSE through SHCIL was cited as the primary driver for this sharp rally.
  • SEBI Approves New IPOs, Signalling Primary Market Momentum: The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) granted approval for the IPOs of Bombay Coated and Special Steels, Bonfiglioli Transmissions, and Swaraj Green Power and Fuel. These approvals underscore the sustained momentum in India's primary market, indicating robust investor appetite for new listings.
  • RBI Subsidy for Dollar Bonds Boosts PSU Banks: Top Indian state lenders, including State Bank of India and Bank of Baroda, are reportedly planning to issue dollar bonds, leveraging the Reserve Bank of India's new subsidised hedging mechanism. This move is seen as a positive for the banking sector, particularly PSU banks, as it provides a new avenue for fundraising.

Setup for tomorrow

Indian markets are likely to open with positive global cues tomorrow, following the strong performance today. US stock futures rose on hopes for a US-Iran peace deal, which also led to a significant slump in crude oil prices overnight. This decline in crude is generally favourable for India, a major oil importer. The Rupee also strengthened against the dollar, closing at 95.10. Investors will continue to monitor global developments, especially around crude oil, and the momentum in banking and realty sectors.
