Market Wrap — 27 July 2026

TL;DR

  • Indian equities staged a strong rebound, ending a five-session losing streak.
  • Easing geopolitical tensions between the US and Iran, leading to a sharp fall in crude oil prices, was the primary driver.
  • Nifty 50 closed near its day's high, gaining almost 1% to settle at 23,995.95.
  • Cartrade Tech Ltd. emerged as the top Nifty 500 gainer, while Cemindia Projects Ltd. was the biggest loser.
  • Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) were strong net buyers, offsetting significant selling by Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs).
  • Futures & Options (F&O) build-up data is unavailable for today's session.

How the market behaved today

Indian equity markets witnessed a robust recovery today, snapping a five-day losing streak. The Nifty 50 opened higher and maintained its upward trajectory throughout the session, closing near its day's high at 23,995.95, up by 0.96%. The Bank Nifty also contributed to the positive sentiment, closing at 57,087.20 with a gain of 0.69%. Broader markets outperformed, with the Nifty Midcap 100 rising 1.11% and the Nifty Smallcap 100 gaining 1.31%.

The India VIX, a measure of market volatility, plunged by 9.76% to 12.66, indicating a significant reduction in market fear and uncertainty.

What moved the market — the factors

The primary catalyst for today's strong market rally was the significant de-escalation of tensions between the United States and Iran, which led to a sharp decline in global crude oil prices. Brent crude fell over 6% and WTI crude over 5.5%, providing a major relief to import-dependent economies like India. This positive global cue translated into a broad-based buying spree across Indian equities.

Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) showed strong conviction, pumping in ₹5,454 crore, which effectively absorbed the ₹3,893 crore net selling by Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs). This robust DII support was crucial in sustaining the market's upward momentum. Positive Q1 earnings reports from several companies, including Tata Power, BEL, and Coal India, also contributed to the upbeat sentiment. Sector-wise, Media, IT, and Realty led the gains, reflecting a broad-based recovery.

Event breakdown — what happened today and how it hit the market

Today's market session was predominantly driven by a significant positive shift in global sentiment. The news of a pause in hostilities between the US and Iran immediately led to a sharp decline in international crude oil prices. Brent crude, a key benchmark, tumbled over 6%, while WTI crude fell over 5.5%. This development was a major relief for India, a net oil importer, as lower crude prices ease inflationary pressures and improve the current account deficit outlook.

The positive global cues translated into a strong rebound across Indian equities, with the Nifty 50 gaining nearly 1%. The India VIX, a measure of market volatility, also cooled off significantly, dropping almost 10%, reflecting reduced investor anxiety. The bond market reacted positively, with India's 10-year bond yield logging its biggest plunge in two months. While no single domestic event dominated, the positive global backdrop, coupled with robust DII buying and a slew of Q1 earnings reports (both positive from Tata Power, BEL, Coal India, Tamilnad Mercantile Bank, and negative from Tata Chemicals), contributed to the broad-based market rally.

Setup for tomorrow

Tomorrow's session will likely take cues from the overnight performance of US markets, which saw S&P 500 and Nasdaq futures jump up to 1.7% on easing US-Iran tensions and falling oil prices. Asian markets will also provide an early indication. Key factors to watch include the continued trajectory of crude oil prices, the Dollar Index, and any further developments on the geopolitical front. Investors will also keep an eye on the remaining Q1 earnings reports. On the charts, Nifty 50 will need to sustain above the 24,000 mark for further upward momentum.
