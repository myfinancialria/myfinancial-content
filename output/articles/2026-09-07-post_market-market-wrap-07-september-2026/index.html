Market Wrap — 07 September 2026

TL;DR

  • Indian benchmark indices, Nifty 50 and Bank Nifty, ended Monday's session in negative territory, extending their losses from the previous week.
  • Heavy selling by Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) coupled with weakness in IT and Media sectors primarily weighed on market sentiment.
  • Syrma SGS Technology Ltd. emerged as the top gainer in the Nifty 500 universe, surging over 12%.
  • Zee Entertainment Enterprises Ltd. was the biggest loser among Nifty 500 stocks, declining over 6% amidst broad sector weakness.
  • FIIs were net sellers for the second consecutive session, offloading ₹3,112 crore, while Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) provided strong support with net buying of ₹8,930 crore.
  • F&O positioning data is unavailable for this session and will be populated from the next wrap.

How the market behaved today

Indian equities witnessed a subdued trading session on Monday, with benchmark indices closing lower. The Nifty 50 opened with a slight gap down and trended downwards throughout the day, closing near its intraday low at 23779.15, marking a decline of 0.50%. The Bank Nifty also followed suit, ending at 57088.30, down 0.49%. Midcap and Smallcap indices showed mixed performance, with the Nifty Midcap 100 falling 0.46% while the Nifty Smallcap 100 managed a marginal gain of 0.02%.

What moved the market — the factors

The Indian market's downward trajectory today was primarily influenced by a combination of factors. A significant driver was the sustained selling pressure from Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs), who offloaded a substantial ₹3,112 crore from the cash segment. This selling was partially absorbed by strong buying from Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs). Global cues, including elevated crude oil prices, also contributed to the cautious sentiment. Geopolitical uncertainty further weighed on investor confidence. On the sectoral front, heavy-weight sectors like IT and Media faced considerable selling pressure, pulling down the broader indices. For instance, the IT sector declined by 2.28% and the Media sector by 2.86%, as highlighted in market wrap reports.

Sectors — winners and losers

The market witnessed a clear divergence in sectoral performance today, with Pharma stocks showing resilience while Media and IT sectors bore the brunt of selling pressure.

Top 3 gainer sectors:

  • PHARMA: +0.75% - The healthcare sector showed strength, possibly due to defensive buying amidst broader market weakness.
  • AUTO: -0.04% - While technically in negative territory, the auto sector showed relative resilience compared to the broader market decline.
  • ENERGY: -0.25% - The energy sector also managed to limit its losses, outperforming the benchmark indices.

Top 3 loser sectors:

  • MEDIA: -2.86% - This sector experienced the sharpest decline, with key constituents like Zee Entertainment and PVR INOX seeing significant falls.
  • IT: -2.28% - Technology stocks faced heavy selling, contributing significantly to the Nifty's decline.
  • REALTY: -1.70% - The real estate sector also saw considerable profit-taking today.

News that moved stocks

  • 360 One Group CEO Bhagat buys 1.3 lakh shares of Novartis India for Rs 22 cr

    • Karan Bhagat, CEO of 360 One Group, acquired 1.3 lakh shares of Novartis India for nearly ₹22 crore, while his firm Kyrush Investments sold an equal number of shares, indicating an internal transfer of ownership. This news could be seen as a positive signal for Novartis India.

  • Sebi eases compliance norms for FPIs investing only in government securities

    • SEBI has relaxed compliance requirements for Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPIs) that invest exclusively in government securities, removing the need to furnish investor group details. This move aims to simplify the investment process for such FPIs and could potentially attract more foreign investment into Indian government bonds.

  • Jio Platforms said to target IPO in November, roadshows from next week

    • Jio Platforms is reportedly targeting a November IPO, with roadshows expected to begin next week, though details like size, valuation, and dates are still under deliberation. This news is likely to keep Jio Platforms and related entities in focus for investors.

  • Vodafone Idea among 4 stocks hitting 52-week highs; rallied up to 22% in a month

    • Despite a fall in the broader market, Vodafone Idea, along with Lenskart Solutions, Solar Industries India, and Divi's Laboratories, hit fresh 52-week highs today. This indicates strong individual momentum for these stocks, possibly driven by specific company news or sector tailwinds.

  • AI penny stock under ₹10 hits 5% upper circuit as IndiaAI Mission boosts infrastructure demand

    • Empower India Limited's shares hit a 5% upper circuit as the company expands into sustainable digital infrastructure, aiming to meet the rising demand for AI-related infrastructure. This news highlights investor interest in companies aligning with the IndiaAI Mission.

  • Indian bonds end flat as traders gauge RBI liquidity approach

    • Indian government bonds experienced little movement today as the Reserve Bank of India implemented temporary liquidity management strategies, withdrawing substantial amounts from the system. This suggests that the RBI's actions are closely watched by bond traders and can influence market liquidity.

Market breadth

The market breadth was negative today, with 325 declines significantly outweighing 171 advances out of a total of 496 traded stocks. This indicates that the selling pressure was fairly broad-based across the market, rather than concentrated in just a few stocks.

FII / DII flows

Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) continued their selling spree for the second consecutive session, offloading a net of ₹3,112 crore from the Indian equity market today. In contrast, Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) provided strong support, pumping in a net of ₹8,930 crore.

DIIs, however, have been consistent net buyers throughout this period, providing crucial stability to the market.

Event breakdown — what happened today and how it hit the market

Today's session was primarily driven by a combination of broad market flows and specific sectoral weakness rather than a single defining event. The persistent selling by Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) was a key factor contributing to the overall negative sentiment. This FII outflow, coupled with elevated crude oil prices and prevailing geopolitical uncertainty, created a cautious environment for investors. Notably, the IT and Media sectors experienced significant selling pressure, with stocks like Zee Entertainment Enterprises Ltd. and PVR INOX Ltd. seeing substantial declines. On the other hand, the Pharma sector showed resilience, indicating some defensive buying. The Reserve Bank of India's liquidity management strategies also kept the Indian bond market flat, as traders assessed the central bank's approach.

Setup for tomorrow

Looking ahead to tomorrow's session, global cues will continue to play a significant role. The USD/INR closed at 94.47, while Brent Crude remained elevated at $96.28, and WTI Crude at $91.48. Gold and Silver saw gains of over 1%, and the US 10-year yield edged up to 4.78%. Investors will be watching for any overnight developments in global markets. Domestically, the performance of IT and Media sectors will be crucial, and any reversal in FII selling could provide a boost. Key levels for Nifty 50 and Bank Nifty will be closely monitored, with potential support and resistance levels being eyed by traders.
