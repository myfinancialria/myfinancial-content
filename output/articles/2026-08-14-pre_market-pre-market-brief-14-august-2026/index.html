Pre-Market Brief — 14 August 2026

TL;DR

  • Global markets presented a mixed picture overnight, with US indices closing higher while most Asian markets traded with a negative bias.
  • GIFT Nifty is signalling a cautious to negative start for the Indian benchmark Nifty 50 today.
  • Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) continued their strong buying streak, significantly offsetting Foreign Institutional Investor (FII) selling.
  • A notable trend saw FIIs offloading nearly ₹1 lakh crore from ten Indian stocks, yet eight of these managed to defy the selloff.
  • For Nifty, the 24300-24330 zone appears to be a crucial support, while 24400-24500 could act as immediate resistance.
  • The day's main risk could be sustained FII selling pressure and the mixed global sentiment.

How global markets traded — and what it means for India

Overnight, US markets closed positively, with the tech-heavy Nasdaq leading the gains, indicating some risk-on sentiment in the Western hemisphere. However, Asian markets this morning are largely trading in negative territory, suggesting a cautious start to the day across the region. The FTSE 100 in the UK also closed lower. This mixed global backdrop indicates that Indian markets might open with some uncertainty, balancing positive cues from the US with negative sentiment from Asia.

Currency, commodities and yields

The Indian Rupee (USD/INR) saw a marginal depreciation against the US Dollar, trading at ₹95.39. The Dollar Index, which measures the dollar's strength against a basket of major currencies, edged down slightly. Crude oil prices, both Brent and WTI, saw minor upticks, indicating persistent demand. Gold prices moved higher, often seen as a safe-haven asset, while Silver saw a notable dip. The US 10-Year Yield declined, which is generally a positive sign for equity markets.

GIFT Nifty — what it's signalling

GIFT Nifty is currently trading at 24390.00, down by 0.30% from its previous close. This suggests that the Indian Nifty 50 index could open with a slight gap-down or on a cautious note today.

FII/DII flows — who's driving the tape

Yesterday, Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) were net sellers in the cash market, offloading equities worth ₹511 crore. In contrast, Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) continued to provide strong support, buying equities worth ₹4,353 crore. This trend of DII buying offsetting FII selling has been prominent over the last few sessions.

Stock & sector news that matters

  • FIIs dumped nearly Rs 1 lakh crore worth of these 10 stocks but 8 defied the selloff

    • Foreign investors sold a substantial ₹1 lakh crore from ten Indian stocks, yet eight of these companies saw their stock prices increase despite the selling. Bajaj Finance led the gains with a twenty-five percent rise. Why it matters: This highlights the resilience of certain Indian stocks and the strong domestic buying interest that is capable of absorbing significant FII outflows. It suggests that while FII selling is a factor, it's not universally detrimental to all stocks, and specific company fundamentals or DII support can override it.

  • Optimystix Entertainment shares to debut today. Here's what GMP suggests ahead of listing

    • Optimystix Entertainment is set to list on the NSE SME platform today after its ₹108.5-crore IPO received 2.05x subscription. With shares trading at zero Grey Market Premium (GMP), a muted debut is expected. Why it matters: This is a listing of a new SME IPO. A muted debut, as suggested by zero GMP, could influence sentiment for other upcoming SME listings and the broader IPO market.

  • Skyways Air sets price band for Rs 582.8 crore IPO; issue to open on Aug 24

    • Skyways Air Services will launch its ₹582.8 crore IPO on August 24, with the price band set at ₹131–138 per share. The issue comprises a ₹398.8 crore fresh issue and ₹184 crore offer-for-sale. Why it matters: This adds to the pipeline of upcoming IPOs, indicating continued activity in the primary market. Investors will be watching subscription figures for this and other ongoing IPOs like Behari Lal Engineering and Shiprocket.

  • JSW Steel Share Price Live Updates: JSW Steel dips below its 20-day Simple Moving Average

    • JSW Steel's stock price has dipped below its 20-day Simple Moving Average. Why it matters: For traders and technical analysts, a breach below a key moving average like the 20-DMA can be seen as a bearish signal, potentially indicating a short-term downtrend or increased selling pressure for the stock. This could put JSW Steel and potentially other metal stocks in focus.

  • Gold and silver prices today: Rates drop up to 1% on MCX as inflation fears fuel rate hike speculations

    • MCX gold October futures were down 0.69% at ₹1,52,406 per 10 grams, while MCX silver September futures were down 0.85% at ₹2,33,455 per kg. This drop is attributed to inflation fears fueling rate hike speculations. Why it matters: While gold and silver are commodities, their price movements linked to inflation fears and rate hike speculations reflect broader macroeconomic concerns. If central banks are perceived to be more aggressive on rate hikes, it could impact liquidity and investor sentiment across equity markets.

Events & data to watch — and how markets usually react

  • IPO Listings/Allotments: Today sees the listing of Optimystix Entertainment on the NSE SME platform. Additionally, allotment for Milky Mist Dairy Food IPO is likely to be finalised. Ongoing IPOs like Behari Lal Engineering and Shiprocket are in their final bidding days. Markets typically show increased activity and sometimes volatility around IPO listings, with investor focus shifting to the performance of newly listed entities and the subscription levels of ongoing issues.
  • Inflation Fears and Rate Hike Speculations: News reports linking falling gold and silver prices to inflation fears and potential rate hikes highlight a key macroeconomic theme. Markets generally react with caution and increased volatility when there are heightened expectations of interest rate hikes, as this can impact borrowing costs for companies and overall economic growth prospects.

What to watch in today's session

  1. Global Cues: The mixed signals from US and Asian markets will be crucial in setting the initial tone for Indian indices.
  2. FII/DII Flows: Continued DII support will be key to absorbing any further FII selling pressure.
  3. Nifty & Bank Nifty Key Levels: Watch Nifty's CPR pivot at 24336 and the 24300-24500 range for directional cues. For Bank Nifty, 57556 (CPR pivot) and 58000 (resistance) are important.
  4. IPO Activity: The listing performance of Optimystix Entertainment and the subscription updates for Behari Lal Engineering and Shiprocket will be on investors' radar.
  5. Corporate Actions: Stocks going ex-dividend today, especially large caps like HINDPETRO and APOLLOHOSP, might see some price adjustments and trading interest.
  6. Sectoral Action: Keep an eye on the metal sector following JSW Steel's technical dip, and IT/Pharma for any tailwinds from USD/INR movement.

Bottom line

Indian markets are likely to open with a cautious bias today, influenced by mixed global cues and a slightly negative signal from GIFT Nifty. While DIIs continue to provide strong domestic support, sustained FII selling remains a factor to monitor. The Nifty's ability to hold above the 24300-24330 zone will be crucial, with 24400-24500 acting as immediate resistance. Investors should remain vigilant of global developments and FII flow patterns, especially given the narrow CPRs suggesting potential for trending moves if key levels are breached.
