Pre-Market Brief — 24 June 2026

TL;DR

  • Global markets closed mostly lower, with US tech indices seeing significant declines, signalling a cautious start.
  • Indian markets, however, saw a strong rebound yesterday, driven by optimistic investor outlook and likely RBI intervention.
  • FIIs were marginal net buyers yesterday at ₹18 crore, while DIIs continued their strong buying trend with ₹680 crore.
  • Crude oil prices eased, and gold saw a slight dip, which could offer some relief on the inflation front.
  • Several corporate actions are lined up today, including buybacks and bonus issues, which may see specific stock movements.

Where global markets closed

Global markets presented a mixed to negative picture as they closed yesterday. US indices, particularly the tech-heavy Nasdaq and the S&P 500, experienced notable declines, reflecting a broader risk-off sentiment. European and Asian markets, however, showed more resilience, with the FTSE 100 and Nikkei 225 registering minor losses, while the Hang Seng and Shanghai Composite managed to close in positive territory. The overall global tone appears cautious, primarily influenced by the significant sell-off in US technology stocks.

Currency, commodities and yields

The Indian Rupee (USD/INR) remained stable against the US Dollar, closing at 94.70 with no significant change. This stability comes amidst reports that the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) likely intervened by selling dollars and conducting buy-sell swaps, which aided the rupee's recovery from its session lows yesterday. The Dollar Index saw a marginal uptick of 0.04% to 101.45, indicating a slight strengthening of the greenback globally.

In commodities, both Brent Crude and WTI Crude oil prices eased, falling by 0.77% to $76.49 and 0.72% to $72.68 respectively. This decline in crude prices could be a positive factor for India, which is a major oil importer, potentially helping to manage imported inflation. Gold prices also saw a slight dip of 0.64% to $4103.50, while Silver was nearly flat at $62.01. The US 10-year Treasury Yield decreased by 0.27% to 4.45%, suggesting a slight shift towards safer assets or expectations of stable interest rates.

Corporate actions today

Today marks the ex-date for several corporate actions that could influence specific stock movements:

  • BAJAJ-AUTO: The stock will trade ex-buyback today. Shareholders who wish to participate in the buyback offer must have held the shares before today.
  • BUILDPRO: The company will trade ex-dividend for a dividend of ₹5 per share.
  • KOTYARK: The stock will trade ex-bonus for a 10:1 bonus issue, meaning shareholders will receive 10 new shares for every 1 share held.
  • ZFCVINDIA: This stock will also trade ex-bonus for a 5:1 bonus issue, where shareholders will get 5 new shares for every 1 share held.
  • WHEELS: The company will trade ex-dividend for a dividend of ₹9.14 per share.

Recent FII/DII flows

Yesterday, June 23, 2026, Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) were marginal net buyers in the Indian equity cash segment, with a net inflow of ₹18 crore. Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) continued their robust buying spree, recording a significant net inflow of ₹680 crore.

Over the last five trading sessions, FII flows have been volatile, showing a mixed trend with both significant buying and selling days. DIIs, on the other hand, have consistently been net buyers, providing strong domestic support to the markets.

Overnight news that matters

RBI likely selling dollars, conducting FX swaps via state banks, traders say India's central bank likely intervened in the foreign exchange market yesterday by selling dollars and conducting buy-sell swaps through state-run banks. Why it matters to Indian markets: This intervention likely supported the Indian Rupee, preventing sharper depreciation against the dollar. A stable rupee is crucial for managing imported inflation and maintaining investor confidence in Indian assets.

Why is market rising today? Sensex rallies over 650 pts, Nifty nears 24K. Key factors driving D-St rebound Indian stock markets experienced a significant upswing yesterday, with both the Sensex and Nifty registering robust gains, fueled by a more optimistic investor outlook. Why it matters to Indian markets: This indicates strong domestic resilience and positive sentiment despite mixed global cues. It suggests that underlying factors are supporting a rebound in Indian equities, which could carry over into today's session.

Bitcoin falls to $62,600 as investors trim risk exposure after AI stock selloff; technical indicators signal caution Bitcoin slipped to around $62,600, extending losses amid a broader selloff in AI and technology stocks that triggered risk-off sentiment. Why it matters to Indian markets: The global sell-off in AI and technology stocks, which impacted Bitcoin, could spill over into the Indian IT sector. Indian IT companies, heavily reliant on global tech spending, might face pressure if this risk-off sentiment persists.

India's textile industry poised for long-term growth as global sourcing shift gathers pace: Siddhartha Khemka India's textile sector is poised for significant growth, driven by a global shift in sourcing away from China and a cyclical recovery in demand. Why it matters to Indian markets: This positive outlook for the textile sector could lead to increased investor interest in textile stocks. Brokerages like Motilal Oswal have also initiated bullish coverage on several textile companies, indicating potential upside.

Vedanta Power, Oil & Gas shares fall 3%; Vedanta Aluminium, Iron & Steel jump up to 5%. What lies ahead? Vedanta's demerger saw mixed fortunes for its listed entities, with Vedanta Power and Oil & Gas shares dipping, while Vedanta Aluminium and Iron & Steel surged. Separately, analysts suggest Vedanta shares may bounce back in 1-2 weeks after a significant drop following a large block deal, with promoter entity Twin Star Holdings reportedly behind the sale to reduce debt. Why it matters to Indian markets: The demerger and promoter selling activity will keep Vedanta group stocks in focus. Investors should monitor the individual performance of the demerged entities and the impact of further potential promoter selling on Vedanta's share price.

What to watch in today's session

  1. Global Cues and US Tech Performance: Given the significant sell-off in US tech stocks yesterday, the performance of global markets, especially the Nasdaq and S&P 500 futures, will be crucial for setting the tone for Indian equities, particularly the IT sector.
  2. FII/DII Activity: While DIIs have been consistent buyers, FII flows have been volatile. Their net positions today will provide further direction on institutional sentiment towards Indian markets.
  3. Crude Oil Prices: The recent easing of Brent and WTI crude prices is a positive for India. Any further movement in crude oil will be watched closely for its impact on inflation and oil marketing companies.
  4. Corporate Actions: Stocks like BAJAJ-AUTO (buyback), KOTYARK and ZFCVINDIA (bonus issues), and BUILDPRO and WHEELS (dividends) will be in focus due to their ex-date corporate actions.
  5. Textile Sector: With a positive outlook and bullish brokerage coverage, the textile sector could see increased activity. Investors might watch stocks in this space for potential momentum.

Bottom line

Indian markets are likely to open with a cautious bias, influenced by the significant tech-led sell-off in US markets. However, the strong domestic buying by DIIs and yesterday's robust rebound in Indian indices suggest underlying resilience. Investors should remain vigilant about global developments, especially in the technology space, while also monitoring specific corporate actions and sector-specific news that could drive individual stock movements.
