Pre-Market Brief — 17 June 2026

TL;DR

  • Global markets displayed a mixed sentiment, with US tech indices closing lower while Asian markets were largely positive.
  • The Indian Rupee strengthened to a six-week high, supported by falling crude oil prices.
  • Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) were net sellers yesterday, continuing a recent trend of outflows.
  • Several corporate actions are scheduled for today, including a bonus issue from Brigade and a buyback offer from Cyient.
  • IDBI Bank and Yes Bank witnessed significant rallies in the previous session on specific news and technical factors.

Where global markets closed

Global markets presented a mixed picture in yesterday's trading. In the US, the Dow Jones Industrial Average closed higher, gaining 0.64%, but the tech-heavy Nasdaq and the broader S&P 500 both ended in negative territory, down 1.15% and 0.57% respectively. European markets saw the FTSE 100 decline by 0.19%. Across Asia, the Nikkei 225 in Japan rose by 0.72%, and China's Shanghai Composite gained 0.40%, while Hong Kong's Hang Seng index closed lower by 0.83%. The overall global tone suggests a cautious sentiment, with some pressure on technology stocks.

Currency, commodities and yields

The Indian Rupee strengthened against the US Dollar, with USD/INR trading at 94.42, down 0.34%. This move was largely supported by a slump in crude oil prices. The Dollar Index, which measures the US dollar against a basket of major currencies, saw a marginal increase of 0.01% to 99.54.

In the commodities market, crude oil prices declined, offering some relief. Brent crude was down 0.53% at $78.54 per barrel, while WTI crude fell 1.33% to $75.04 per barrel. Lower crude prices are generally positive for India, as the country is a major oil importer, potentially easing inflationary pressures and improving trade balances. Gold and Silver both saw modest gains, with Gold up 0.36% at $4346.30 and Silver up 0.35% at $70.14.

The US 10-year Treasury yield rose by 0.54% to 4.49%. A rising US yield can sometimes impact foreign investment flows into emerging markets like India, as higher returns in safe-haven assets become more attractive.

Corporate actions today

Several companies have corporate actions scheduled for today:

  • BRIGADE: The company has announced a Bonus Issue in the ratio of 1:3. This means for every three shares held, shareholders will receive one additional share.
  • KRISHANA: This stock will trade ex-dividend today for a dividend of Re 0.50 per share. Shareholders who buy the stock today or later will not be eligible for this dividend.
  • MBAPL: Similar to Krishana, MBAPL will trade ex-dividend today for a dividend of Re 0.50 per share.
  • CYIENT: The company's share buyback offer will be ex-date today. This means shareholders who wish to participate in the buyback must have held the shares before today.

Recent FII/DII flows

Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) were net sellers in the Indian cash equity segment yesterday, offloading shares worth ₹-749 crore. Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) remained neutral with a net position of ₹0 crore.

Over the last five trading sessions, FIIs have largely been net sellers, with significant outflows observed on four out of five days. In contrast, DIIs have consistently been net buyers, providing support to the market during this period.

Overnight news that matters

IDBI Bank share price rockets 19%. What’s triggering the massive surge? IDBI Bank shares saw a significant rally yesterday, extending a four-session upward trend with heavy trading volumes. This surge is attributed to renewed optimism surrounding the bank's long-delayed privatisation process, with reports suggesting government progress on the matter. This news is significant for the banking sector and could indicate potential value unlocking in other public sector undertakings (PSUs) undergoing divestment.

Rupee touches six-week high on oil slump, RBI effect lingers The Indian Rupee strengthened to a six-week high, primarily driven by the fall in Brent crude prices below $80 a barrel. Supportive measures from the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) to attract dollar inflows, including tax reforms, also contributed to the Rupee's appreciation. A stronger Rupee and lower crude prices are generally positive for the Indian economy, potentially reducing import bills and inflation.

OMCs get a breather from cooling oil prices, but the coast is not all clear Indian Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs) are finding some relief from cooling crude oil prices, with Brent crude falling below $80 per barrel. This development is positive for their refining margins and profitability, as lower input costs can improve their financial health. However, the article suggests that while there's optimism, the situation is not entirely clear, implying continued monitoring of global oil dynamics is needed.

HDFC Bank raises $750 million via offshore bond issue through GIFT City unit HDFC Bank successfully raised $750 million through an offshore bond issue via its GIFT City unit. This move demonstrates the growing importance of GIFT City as an international financial hub for Indian institutions to raise capital. For HDFC Bank, this capital infusion can support its growth plans and strengthen its balance sheet, which is positive for the broader banking sector.

Clay Craft India's Rs 110-crore IPO to open on June 17, price band fixed at Rs 193-203 Clay Craft India Limited, a ceramic tableware manufacturer, is launching its Initial Public Offering (IPO) today, aiming to raise ₹110.11 crore. The IPO is priced between ₹193-203 per share, with funds intended for expansion. This marks another entry into the primary market, offering new investment opportunities for retail investors.

What to watch in today's session

  1. Banking Sector Momentum: Keep an eye on IDBI Bank and Yes Bank, which saw significant rallies yesterday. The sentiment around IDBI Bank's privatisation and Yes Bank's technical levels could continue to drive movement. HDFC Bank's successful bond issue also adds to the positive sentiment for large private banks.
  2. Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs): With crude oil prices declining, OMCs like IOCL, BPCL, and HPCL could see positive sentiment. Investors should monitor their performance and any further news regarding crude price stability.
  3. Corporate Actions: Today is ex-date for several corporate actions. Brigade's bonus issue and Cyient's buyback could see increased activity in these stocks. Krishana and MBAPL going ex-dividend will also be watched.
  4. Primary Market Activity: The IPO of Clay Craft India opens for subscription today. Investors interested in new listings will be monitoring its initial reception.
  5. FII/DII Flows: The continued trend of FII outflows and DII inflows will be a crucial factor. Sustained DII support has been cushioning the market, but significant FII selling could still exert pressure.

Bottom line

The Indian market is likely to open with a mixed bias, influenced by a cautious global environment, particularly the weakness in US tech stocks. However, positive domestic cues such as a strengthening Rupee, falling crude prices, and strong DII support could provide some resilience. Investors should remain cautious and monitor global developments, FII activity, and specific corporate news for directional cues.
