Events & data to watch — and how markets usually react

The events and data to watch are:

  • RBI policy: Markets usually react positively to a rate cut and negatively to a rate hike.
  • US Fed: Markets usually react positively to a dovish tone and negatively to a hawkish tone.
  • CPI/GDP prints: Markets usually react positively to a low inflation print and negatively to a high inflation print.
  • F&O expiry: Markets usually react with high volatility on F&O expiry days.
  • Auto/GST monthly data: Markets usually react positively to a strong auto sales data and negatively to a weak auto sales data.
